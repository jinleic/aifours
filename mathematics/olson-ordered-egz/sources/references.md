## Ordered baseline and novelty gate

5. Vesselin Dimitrov, **On the Strong Davenport Constant of Nonabelian Finite p-Groups**, *Mathematica Balkanica*18 (2004),129–140. [Official PDF](https://www.math.bas.bg/infres/MathBalk/MB-18/MB-18-129-140.pdf), [retained PDF](dimitrov-2004.pdf), [OCR of pp129–131](dimitrov-2004-ocr.txt). Page131 visually checked against the scan. Theorem2 proves ordered Olson for every finite p-group; for noncyclic groups of order n it gives the stronger threshold s>=(1+(2p-1)/p^2)n-1. The rank-dependent refinement requires 1+(p-1)r<=epsilon*p^r. Corollary2 about soluble groups is explicitly the weak/unordered version. Thus finite Q8 or order-eight dihedral checks add no new coverage beyond this theorem.
6. Ankit Agarwal and Robert Lou, **Application of the Ordered Erdos Ginzburg Ziv Theorem to the Non-Abelian Dihedral Group**, [IMSA2016 primary abstract](https://digitalcommons.imsa.edu/sir_presentations/2016/oralsession1/55/), presentation2016-04-28, page posted2016-05-12. It reports odd-dihedral lower bound4m-1 for groups of order2m and a different even-case lower bound. Full proof and construction are not exposed. Our r^(2m-1)s^(2m-1) argument and Lean proof therefore reproduce a bound already claimed publicly; no novelty is claimed.

## Recent work that does NOT settle the ordered target

7. Naveen K. Godara, Renu Joshi, Eshita Mazumdar, **Combinatorial invariants for certain classes of non-abelian groups**, [arXiv2408.13558](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.13558), [v2 full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.13558v2), revision2026-01-02. The introduction separately defines sequences in a free abelian monoid, ordered sequences in a free nonabelian monoid, the ordered Davenport constant D_o(G) for arbitrary nonempty subword lengths, and the unordered Gao constant E(G) for length|G|. Its special-family D_o and E results must not be conflated into an ordered length|G| theorem. The paper also explains that “strong Davenport constant” is ambiguous in current literature.
8. Yang Zhao and Guoqing Wang, **The Erdos-Ginzburg-Ziv theorem constant of finite groups**, [arXiv2603.21052](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21052),2026-03-22. The abstract defines E(G) using n distinct selected terms whose product is the identity “in some order.” Its noncyclic |G| not divisible by4 bound E(G)<=3|G|/2 is unordered, not a solution here.
9. Yongke Qu, Guoqing Wang, Yuanlin Li, **The equality between the Erdos-Ginzburg-Ziv constant and the short product-one constant for finite nonabelian groups**, [arXiv2608.15568](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15568),2026-08-16. The abstract concerns exponent-length constants and groups with cyclic subgroup of index the smallest prime. It does not assert the exact all-groups increasing-index statement. Ordering conventions of its full text were not independently audited here; no ordered consequence is inferred.
10. Weidong Gao and Yuanlin Li, **The Erdos-Ginzburg-Ziv Theorem for Finite Solvable Groups**, [author-hosted full text, dated2009-04-03](https://cfc.nankai.edu.cn/_upload/article/files/d2/e7/02ad99784f4bbe19008160bb92ce/cad4f1ea-5d2e-46ac-99c5-e2fb33887cf7.pdf). Its first page defines product-one using a permutation and differentiates a “main subsequence” preserving order. Lemma5 concatenates disjoint quotient-zero blocks, which may interleave in the original sequence; the argument is not an ordered proof.

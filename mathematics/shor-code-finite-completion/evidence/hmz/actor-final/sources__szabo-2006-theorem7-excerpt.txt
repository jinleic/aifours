Readable mathematical statement (paraphrase; typography normalized):
If C' is a finite completion of Shor's code and a^n belongs to C', then
there exist pairwise distinct primes p1,p2,p3 such that
    330*p1*p2*p3 divides n.
Consequently n >= 9900.

This theorem is a necessary condition, not an all-order noncompletion
result. The 19800 partial corollary in proof.md combines it with Section
3's 8 dividing n; its novelty is unaudited.

## Exact full goal

diagonal entries 2, nonpositive off-diagonal entries, and symmetric zero
pattern, let ell be rank(A). Work over C with **independent**
\(h_1,\ldots,h_{2n-\ell}\) and a rank-n matrix C whose first n rows are A.
In the preliminary contragredient Lie algebra impose

\[
[e_i,f_j]=\delta_{ij}h_i,\quad [h_p,h_q]=0,\quad
[h_p,e_i]=c_{pi}e_i,\quad [h_p,f_i]=-c_{pi}f_i.
\]

Let tau be the maximal ideal meeting the Cartan trivially. Let J be the
ideal generated by all positive and negative Serre relations
\((\operatorname{ad}e_i)^{1-a_{ij}}e_j\) and
\((\operatorname{ad}f_i)^{1-a_{ij}}f_j\) for distinct i,j. Prove **J=tau for
every such A and full realization**, or provide an exact characteristic-zero
counterexample with nonmembership and all descents checked. Neither a
finite-characteristic group example nor a truncation answers this goal.

The full realization, arbitrary-rank quantifier and characteristic-zero
requirement have not been weakened. TASK.md and verify.py retain their

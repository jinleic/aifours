## 2. Sources and actual reading scopes

### A. Classical companion and PH sources

* **Ellard–Smigoc**, arXiv:1702.02650v1, 8 Feb2017, [official HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/1702.02650v1). Read the exact Theorem1.2 statement, Theorem2.1's prescribed diagonal construction, Lemma2.2's characteristic polynomial, and the trace/JLL discussion. The all-q proof uses this as a named theorem import, not a re-proved general result. The source's long §5 proof uses additional elementary-symmetric-function inequalities and has NOT been independently reconstructed in this lane.
* **Commault–Mocanu2003**, DOI10.1080/0020717031000114986, [accessible primary PDF](https://people.smp.uq.edu.au/YoniNazarathy/AMSIschool2016/CommaultMocanu_forAssignment.pdf). ### C. Positive-realization lower bounds

* **Benvenuti2020**, *The NIEP and the Positive Realization Problem*, ELA36:367–384, [primary PDF](https://journals.uwyo.edu/index.php/ela/article/download/5039/5065/6317). ## 3. A matched check against logarithmic complete monotonicity

**Taghavian–Drummond–Johansson**, arXiv:2302.08773v1, 17 Feb2023, [official primary HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2302.08773v1). Read Introduction, CM/LCM definitions, external-positivity distinction and Lemma1 with its proof. LCM is explicitly a proper subset of complete monotonicity/external positivity. Lemma1 gives the exponential pole-zero sum criterion for LCM; it is not a necessary criterion for every PH transfer function.

For our denominator and no finite zeros, that source's criterion becomes exactly

    exp(-ct)+4exp(-at)cos(bt)>=0 for every t>=0,

or, with k=d/b and x=bt,

    1+4exp(-kx)cos x>=0 for every x>=0.

The most negative damped-cosine excursion is the first one, at x=pi-arctan k, since all subsequent excursions have an additional positive exponential decay factor. Thus this is equivalent to

    4exp[-k(pi-arctan k)]/sqrt(1+k^2)<=1.

This is not the E manuscript's density criterion involving tan xi=xi and m_k(xi). The two cannot be silently equated: they apply to log H and H, respectively.

An exact separation can be shown without root numerics. At k=1/4, the E manuscript's m_k(x) decreases for x>=xi>6. At x6,

    m_(1/4)(6)=(173/8)exp(-3/2)/sqrt37<1.

For a simple rational check, its square is29929/[64·37·exp3]; the rational factor is below14, while exp3>1+3+9/2+27/6+81/24=131/8>16. Hence E gives k_*<1/4, so beta_*>4 and H with beta4 is PH at that characterization boundary.

But its LCM expression is

    16exp[-(pi-arctan(1/4))/4]/sqrt17>16/(e sqrt17)>1,

using pi<4, e<3 and sqrt17<5. Thus that PH example is not LCM. This is a matched scope check showing why the 2023 LCM results do not by themselves settle or refute our candidate's full positivity boundary. It is not a separate novelty claim or a new active target.

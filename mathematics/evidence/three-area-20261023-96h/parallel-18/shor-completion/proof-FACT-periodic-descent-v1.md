## 2. Degenerate projections cannot escape the seed constraints

**Lemma.** Let m>1. Suppose P+Q=Z_m is a direct factorization, 0,1 belong to Q, and P contains the residues of0,3,8,11. Repetitions among the four residues are initially ALLOWED. Suppose Q also contains the residues of7,13,14. Then

    330 divides |Q|,
    m >= 330,
    all nine named seed residues are distinct WITHIN their respective factors,
    2 divides |P|,
    m >= 1320.

Proof. Because m>1, 0 and1 in Q are distinct. Each following collision uses two different Q-preimages, so it remains a collision in the PUSHFORWARD even if the multiplier identifies those two images or if some P seed residues coincide.

- Multiplier8 on Q: (P residue8,Q preimage0) and (P residue0,Q preimage1) both sum to8. If |Q| were odd, M would make the coefficient-counting sum direct, a contradiction. Thus 2 divides |Q|.
- Multiplier3: (P residue3,Q preimage0) and (P residue0,Q preimage1) both sum to3. Thus3 divides |Q|.
- Multiplier5: (P residue8,Q preimage0) and (P residue3,Q preimage1) both sum to8. Thus5 divides |Q|.
- Multiplier11: (P residue11,Q preimage0) and (P residue0,Q preimage1) both sum to11. Thus11 divides |Q|.

So330 divides |Q|, whence m=|P||Q|>=330. This bound now proves that the integer seeds0,3,8,11 have distinct residues in P, and0,1,7,13,14 have distinct residues in Q. It was not used prematurely in the multiplier argument.

Apply multiplier2 to P. The distinct preimage pairs (P residue3,Q residue1) and (P residue0,Q residue7) both sum to7. Hence |P| is even. Already |P|>=4, so m>=1320. This lower bound does NOT assert8|m: it is an ordinary inequality, and m can still a priori lie in660Z minus1320Z. QED.

## 3. Quotienting a period of A

Suppose A has a nonzero period h in Z_n. Let H be the subgroup generated by h. Then A+H=A and |H|>1. Let pi:Z_n->Z_n/H, and identify the quotient with Z_m where m=n/|H|.

### Injectivity on B

If b,b' in B have pi(b)=pi(b'), then b-b' is in H. For any a in A, a+(b-b') is in A. Thus

    a+b = (a+b-b')+b'.

Uniqueness in A+B gives b=b'. Therefore pi is injective on B, and |pi(B)|=|B|>=5. In particular m>1.

### The quotient factorization

Surjectivity of pi(A)+pi(B) follows by projecting A+B=Z_n. As A is a union of H-cosets,

    |pi(A)| = |A|/|H|,
    |pi(A)||pi(B)| = (|A|/|H|)|B| = n/|H| = m.

Every residue has at least one representation and there are exactly m pairs, so every residue has exactly one. Thus pi(A)+pi(B)=Z_m directly.

The images contain all the seed residues from §1, though the A seed images have not yet been assumed distinct. Section2 applies, since pi(B) contains distinct0,1 and m>1. It gives m>=1320 and therefore distinctness of ALL the seed images. Taking their canonical integer representatives gives the literal containments in §1. This is a strictly smaller FACT solution.

## 4. Quotienting a period of B

If B+H=B with |H|>1, the symmetric uniqueness argument makes pi injective on A. So |pi(A)|=|A|>=4 and m>1.

Projecting and counting gives the direct factorization pi(A)+pi(B)=Z_m. Its B images include0 and1; they are distinct simply because m>1. Again §2 applies without assuming that the other B seed images were distinct. It forces m>=1320 and thereby restores every literal seed point. This is again a strictly smaller FACT solution.

## 5. Descent and what it does NOT preserve

Each nontrivial-period quotient strictly reduces the positive integer modulus. Repeated quotienting must terminate. At termination neither factor has a nonzero period, and all seed containments hold literally at the final modulus. Equivalently, a least-modulus FACT solution must already have two aperiodic factors.

The same reasoning preserves full generated subgroups: every descendant A contains3 and8 and every descendant B contains1. The cardinality restrictions 330||B| and2||A| are re-established by §2 at EACH step, rather than silently inherited through a quotient.

**Important non-preservation:**
- This does not quotient a raw code M or its actual delays/bridge words.
- A descendant FACT modulus need NOT still meet the full-completion condition1320|n or the inherited conditional19800 threshold. In particular a full-code candidate can project to an endpoint factorization at an order in660Z minus1320Z. Such endpoint cases cannot be dropped from an all-order not-FACT proof.
- It does not preserve or construct C_M(b), a common cbc family, or any STAB solution.
- It supplies no minimum-order FACT witness and no contradiction for aperiodic factors.

Thus a sufficient negative route may focus on aperiodic FACT solutions, but it must still handle ALL their orders and unknown additions. A positive FACT construction may also seek an aperiodic pair first, but must then solve every actual raw-word bridge condition. Neither task is completed here.

## 2. New uniform theorem OG: a growing one-gap provider across R

**Theorem OG (candidate).** Let

and let w be ANY actual word at these counts. If SOME actual cyclic reflection gap contains at least2d+1 rotation occurrences, then w has an identity2p selection in original order. Values may repeat or vanish, every other gap is arbitrary, and no restriction is placed on reflection values or blocks.

The result is a growing-R word theorem, not a claim that every remaining R band satisfies its gap hypothesis. ### 2.1 Actual marked pairs in ONE initial gap

Cut after the reflection preceding the stated gap, writing

    X1 f1 X2 f2 ... XF fF,  f_t=sigma_(y_t).

From the first2d+1 actual rotations in X1 choose its first2d as d disjoint consecutive pairs, and name the next actual rotation r0. Let s_j be the sum of the two actual values in pair j. It may be zero. There are exactly

    R-2d-1=p-d

other named rotation occurrences r1,...,r_(p-d).

Use Boolean reflection colours u2,...,uF, with u1=u_(F+1)=0, selecting f_t iff u_t!=u_(t+1). Independently use d Boolean marks m1,...,md: mark1 selects BOTH actual occurrences of its pair, mark0 selects neither. Fix an arbitrary EVEN subset K of the unpaired rotations, selecting precisely K among those occurrences.

Each pair is in gap1, so its actual selected contribution is rho_(s_j*m_j), with no reflection prefix before it. Other rotations in that same gap cause no problem: rotations in a gap add in their actual local product, and no factor is commuted through a reflection. Reflection parity is even; marked rotations add even size; |K| is even.

Direct multiplication gives the exact coordinate and size polynomials

    V_K= sum_(r in K) x_r
           +sum_(t=2)^F (y_(t-1)-y_t)u_t
           -2sum_(r in K)x_r*u_(g(r))
           +sum_(j=1)^d s_j*m_j,

    Z_K=|K|+sum_(t=1)^F(u_t+u_(t+1)-2u_t*u_(t+1))
              +2sum_(j=1)^d m_j.                         (1)

The number of parameters is

    M=(F-1)+d=3p-3.

The degree-two homogeneous part of Z_K is

    Z2=-2sum_(t=2)^(F-1)u_t*u_(t+1),                      (2)

which contains NO mark variable. This is different from pairing in an arbitrary noninitial gap: here its colour is actually fixed zero.

### 2.2 First box: covering all mark variables consumes degree

For any polynomial in M variables of degree<=M, its alternating Boolean sum is (-1)^M times its full squarefree coefficient. This follows monomial by monomial: omitted variables cancel; a degree<=M monomial containing every variable must have every exponent1. Also, a polynomial of degree<M has alternating sum zero.

Apply this to

    Q_K=(1-V_K^(p-1))(1-Z_K^(p-1)).

Fermat makes Q_K the indicator of identity coordinate and size divisible by p. Its parameter degree is at most3p-3=M. Its full squarefree coefficient is exactly

    [u2...uF m1...md] (L_K+sum_j s_j*m_j)^(p-1)*Z2^(p-1),  (3)

where L_K is the colour-linear part of (1), excluding marks and the constant term. Constant terms of V_K and all lower-degree parts of Z_K cannot contribute at total parameter degree M. M is even.

Crucially, every mark in (3) must be supplied by the first factor: Z2 has no marks. A contributing monomial must therefore spend at least d of its p-1 first-factor terms on mark variables. Its remaining dependence on rotation statuses has total degree at most

    p-1-d.                                                (4)

No nonzero pair-sum assumption is needed. A zero s_j can only make the coefficient vanish sooner.

### 2.3 Second box and actual parity completion

Introduce p-d formal statuses z_i for r_i and replace K's colour-linear contribution by

    -2sum_(i=1)^(p-d) z_i*x_i*u_(g(r_i)).

    deg_z G<=p-1-d < p-d.

The actual unpaired rotation r0 lies in colour-zero gap1, so adding it affects neither L_K nor (3). For a Boolean subset A of the other p-d occurrences, take K=A if |A| is even, and K=A union{r0} otherwise. These are actual EVEN subsets, empty exactly when A is empty.

If w had no identity2p selection, then at every nonempty even K, Q_K would vanish at every parameter point. For K empty the only surviving indicator point would be the genuinely empty selection u=m=0, contributing1. Injectivity of reflection colours and the actual two-position marks makes that empty selection unique.

The first box would therefore give

Its alternating sum on the (p-d)-dimensional status box would be1. But its total degree is strictly below p-d, so that sum is0. Contradiction. This proves OG on the cut word, and §5 restores original order.

### 3.1 A first-box polynomial at degree M+1

Cut with two named actual rotation anchors r0,r1 in initial gap1. There are p other rotations. Use only the M=F-1=3p-4 reflection colours and, formally, rotation statuses z for ALL R occurrences. Put

    S(u)=sum_t(u_t+u_(t+1)-2u_t*u_(t+1)),
    C(z)=sum_r z_r*x_r,
    L(z;u)=sum_(t=2)^F(y_(t-1)-y_t)u_t
                    -2sum_r z_r*x_r*u_(g(r)),
    k(z)=sum_r z_r.

Define the ordinary polynomial

    H(z)=sum_(u in {0,1}^M)(-1)^(sum u)
          (1-(C(z)+L(z;u))^(p-1))
          (1-(k(z)+S(u))^(p-1)).                         (5)

For a Boolean status point, this is the actual fixed-subset indicator sum. Odd rotation subsets are permitted in this FORMAL definition, but no avoidance conclusion will be imposed on their values.

Here the indicator has colour degree at most3p-3=M+1. The previous exact degree-M coefficient shortcut must NOT be used unchanged. Instead apply strict-degree cancellation only to terms whose colour degree is below M:

1. The terms1, V^(p-1) and Z^(p-1) alone have colour degree below M and vanish under the colour sum.
2. In V^(p-1)(k+S)^(p-1), any use of the colour-CONSTANT k lowers maximal colour degree by at least2, to at most3p-5=M-1. All such terms vanish.
3. In (C+L)^(p-1) S^(p-1), any use of C at least twice also lowers maximal colour degree to at most3p-5<M. All those terms vanish.

Consequently the exact surviving formula is

    H(z)=sum_u (-1)^(sum u) [
            L(z;u)^(p-1)*S(u)^(p-1)
            +(p-1)C(z)*L(z;u)^(p-2)*S(u)^(p-1)].         (6)

This formula retains the degree-M+1 terms honestly, without a false multilinear coefficient claim.

Both C and L have total status degree at most1, so

    deg_z H<=p-1.                                         (7)

Because g(r0)=g(r1)=1 and u1=0, L is independent of BOTH anchor statuses. The anchors enter (6) only through the LINEAR C. Thus H is affine in their two statuses, jointly: there is no anchor-product term. For every fixed choice A of outside statuses,

    H(A,0,0)+H(A,1,1)=H(A,1,0)+H(A,0,1).                (8)

This is an exact polynomial fact. It does not say the actual odd and even selected products or their sizes agree.

### 3.2 Parity sums, not a nonlinear parity substitution

Suppose no identity2p selection exists. At a nonempty EVEN rotation subset, its actual indicator sum H is zero. At the empty subset it is1, because only the empty reflection selection remains. No value for an odd rotation subset is asserted: such a selection could have odd size p or3p.

Define the polynomial in the p outside statuses

    G(A)=H(A,0,0)+H(A,1,1).

It has total degree<=p-1. If |A| is even, both subsets on the right are even, giving G(0)=1 and G(A)=0 for all nonempty even A. If |A| is odd, use (8): the two subsets on its right are nonempty EVEN subsets, so again G(A)=0. This pointwise use of the affine relation avoids substituting a high-degree parity polynomial anywhere.

Thus G would be the Boolean point mass at0 on p variables, impossible for degree<p by the signed-box lemma. Contradiction. This proves TA, modulo the same original-cut restoration in §5.

## 7. Why the naive whole-middle extension is NOT a proof

For general R=p+1+d and no marked initial pairs, there are F-1=3p-3-d colour variables. The Fermat indicator can exceed this degree by d. Constant coordinate terms survive as soon as d>=1, and size constants can survive as soon as d>=2. Thus an actual initial-gap parity rotation no longer disappears merely because its colour is zero.

A useful upper bound is that a contributing term using j constant size factors and l constant coordinate factors must have2j+l<=d; its status degree can be as high as p-1+j. In particular d>=2 can raise the status degree beyond p-1. This is an obstruction to the OLD argument, not a proof that all larger-band methods fail.

OG repairs the degree by spending p-1-degree budget on d actual initial mark variables; TA repairs the first d=1 defect by two-anchor affinity. The necessary anchors or paired marks are actual gap resources, not resources every word has. On the zero-free singleton-gap R=p+2 class neither theorem supplies them. No rearrangement of nonidentity rotations or fictitious pair is licensed to fill that gap.

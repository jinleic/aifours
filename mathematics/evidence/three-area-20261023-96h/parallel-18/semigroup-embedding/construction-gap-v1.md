## 1. Precise remaining obligation

Let S satisfy the broad book hypotheses. Let xi be its least completely simple congruence, whose existence for regular S is proved in proof-v1.md Lemma2.3. In the finite case, the **existing** theorem X implies xi is idempotent pure. In arbitrary infinite S that purity remains an independent unresolved prerequisite except for the covered classes of proof-v1.md. Do not substitute existence of some quotient for this purity requirement.

For q:S --> C=S/xi, when q is idempotent pure, choose normalized Rees coordinates

    C=M[G;I,Lambda;P],
    p_(lambda0,i)=p_(lambda,i0)=1,
    q(s)=(i_s,g_s,lambda_s).

The next concrete task is:

> Build the canonical locally regular band B_q below and prove that its canonical map is injective on **each idempotent fibre** q^(-1)(c), c in E(C).

A proof of this one separation property gives the full actual P embedding for S, including arbitrary nonorthodox S. In particular it would finish the **finite** source case without another finite-purity search. No claim that the separation property already follows from the book hypotheses is made.

A collapse would be a nonembedding obstruction only when correctly tied to the **least** quotient q, not to an arbitrarily coarser quotient. Section5 proves that this canonical choice loses no P embedding, so the obligation is not an accidental over-strong fixed-quotient ansatz.

## 2. The actual canonical band and group action

Take the alphabet A=G cross S with formal letters X_(u,s). Start with the free locally regular band F_V(A), where V is the exact band variety from the book, defined by band identities and (LR) in proof-v1.md. Impose the congruence generated by

    X_(u,st)
       = X_(u,s) X_(u g_s p_(lambda_s,i_t),t)
                  for every u in G and s,t in S.   (M)

Call the quotient B_q. It is a locally regular band because it is a quotient in the band variety V. It is nonempty even if the relations collapse it: the one-element band is always a model. This nonemptiness is not a claim of injectivity.

For v in G define on generators

    alpha_v(X_(u,s))=X_(vu,s).

Left shifts preserve the band identities. They send (M) at u to (M) at vu, so preserve the generated congruence. Their inverse shifts are alpha_(v^(-1)), and alpha_v alpha_w=alpha_(vw). Thus they descend to an actual group action by automorphisms of B_q.

Put beta(s)=[X_(1,s)]. Then (M) at u=1 gives exactly

    beta(st)= beta(s) alpha_(g_s p_(lambda_s,i_t))(beta(t)).

Consequently

    kappa_q(s)=(beta(s),q(s)) : S --> B_q odot C    (2.1)

is multiplicative for the genuine Pastijn formula, including its sandwich entry in the action. It is not merely a map into an ordinary semidirect product, and its injectivity has not been assumed.

This construction has the expected universal property over the **fixed** q. If theta(s)=(b_s,q(s)) is any homomorphism to D odot C with D in V and action gamma of G, the assignment

    X_(u,s) |---> gamma_u(b_s)

respects (M), since theta is multiplicative and gamma is an action. It extends to a homomorphism B_q --> D. Therefore any collapse by kappa_q occurs in every homomorphism of this P type over q. Conversely, if kappa_q is injective it is itself the required embedding over q. No claim is made that a universal-property reformulation is a solution of the missing separation property.

## 3. Full injectivity reduces to the literal idempotent-fibre condition

Here is a complete algebraic reduction, not an assumption of a faithful functor.

**Lemma.** Let S be regular, let q:S --> C be idempotent pure, and let k:S --> T be a homomorphism such that q(s)=q(t) whenever k(s)=k(t). If k is idempotent separating, then k is injective.

**Proof.** Suppose k(s)=k(t), hence q(s)=q(t). Choose s' in V_S(s). Since q(s')q(s) and q(s)q(s') are idempotent,

    q(s't)=q(s')q(t)=q(s')q(s),
    q(ts')=q(t)q(s')=q(s)q(s')

are idempotent. Purity of q makes s't and ts' actual idempotents of S. Also s's and ss' are idempotents, and

    k(s't)=k(s's),   k(ts')=k(ss').

Idempotent separation gives

    s't=s's,   ts'=ss'.                            (3.1)

Thus

    t(s's)=(ts')s=(ss')s=s,
    (ss')t=(ts')t=t(s't)=t(s's)=s.

Both ss' and s's are idempotent, so s=(ss')t=t(s's) means s<=t in the exact book natural order. Interchanging s,t and choosing an inverse of t yields t<=s. The natural partial order on a regular semigroup is antisymmetric, hence s=t. QED.

Apply the lemma to kappa_q. Its second coordinate is q, so the kernel-containment hypothesis is automatic. Two idempotents e,f with kappa_q(e)=kappa_q(f) already have q(e)=q(f). Therefore kappa_q is injective if and only if

    [X_(1,e)]=[X_(1,f)], q(e)=q(f), e^2=e,f^2=f
        implies e=f.                              (SEP)

By the shift action, it is equivalent to require the same at every u, or just at u=1. Thus checking only the fibres in §1 does not silently discard the nonidempotent elements: the written proof above recovers them using purity, idempotent separation and natural-order antisymmetry.

## 4. What can already be proved about the fibre amalgam

For c in E(C), let E_c=q^(-1)(c). Because q is surjective and pure, this is a nonempty band. It is **locally regular**: for e in E_c, the band e E_c e is a subband of E(eSe), and the latter is a regular band by the book hypothesis.

For s,t in E_c, with c=(i,p_(lambda,i)^(-1),lambda), we have i_s=i_t=i, lambda_s=lambda_t=lambda and g_s=p_(lambda,i)^(-1). Hence the shift factor in (M) is

    g_s p_(lambda_s,i_t)=1.

So these particular relations are just

    X_(u,s) X_(u,t)=X_(u,st).                      (F)

First impose **only** the fibre relations (F), and call the resulting V-band B_0. Concretely it is the coproduct in V of one copy of each E_c for every pair (u,c), together with free singleton-generator factors for the remaining letters (u,s) having q(s) nonidempotent.

Each E_c copy embeds into B_0. To see this without importing an amalgamation theorem, fix one distinguished copy E_c and choose a point a in it. Map its own letters identically to E_c; map every other letter constantly to a. All (F) relations are respected: in every other factor the two sides map to a^2=a; in the distinguished factor they are its multiplication law. The target satisfies V's identities. Hence this assignment induces a retraction B_0 --> E_c of the canonical factor map. The factor map is therefore injective.

The full B_q is obtained from B_0 by adding all remaining **mixed** relations (M). Those relations, not a defective local band or an omitted pure fibre, are the sole unresolved source of a collapse in (SEP). The single missing construction can now be stated sharply:

> Show that the quotient B_0 --> B_q preserves every fibre factor E_c, under the book's local r-orthodoxy and weak E-unitarity hypotheses on S.

This is not a generic request for a faithful functor. It is an exact injectivity problem for a displayed band presentation with a displayed actual action. No preservation under those mixed relations has been proved here.

The obvious section attempt does not already solve it. In the orthodox group-grading special case, choose r_u with q(r_u)=u and a mutual inverse r_u'. The candidate

    F(u,s)=r_u s r_(u q(s))'

is an idempotent by purity, but

    F(u,s) F(u q(s),t)
      =r_u s (r_(u q(s))' r_(u q(s)))
                         t r_(u q(s)q(t))'

has an inserted idempotent not present in F(u,st). Local regularity does not license deleting it when s,t range outside its local monoid. That precise mixed-relation defect is why the naive section construction is not promoted to a solution.

## 5. Why the least quotient is legitimate: pulling an actual embedding back to q

This section only establishes the claimed scope of the single remaining construction; it does not prove (SEP).

Suppose S is regular and already embeds in a P target B odot C' by

    theta(s)=(b_s,r(s)).

Its projection image r(S) is completely simple by proof-v1.md Lemma2.1. Thus ker r is a completely simple congruence, and the least one xi is contained in ker r. Consequently r=f q for a homomorphism f:C=S/xi --> C'.

Use normalized source coordinates C=M[G;I,Lambda;P] as in §1 and the existing target coordinates C'=M[G';I',Lambda';P']. A homomorphism f has row map a:I --> I', column map b:Lambda --> Lambda', a group homomorphism h:G --> G', and labels u_i,v_lambda in G' such that

    f(i,g,lambda)=(a(i),u_i h(g) v_lambda,b(lambda)),
    h(p_(lambda,i))=v_lambda p'_(b(lambda),a(i)) u_i. (5.1)

For completeness these formulas do not require a surjective f or a faithful h. Green R,L relations in the source give row/column maps. Put k=p'_(b(lambda0),a(i0)). Write the middle coordinate of f(i0,g,lambda0) as m(g). Restriction to this H-class gives m(gh)=m(g) k m(h), m(1)=k^(-1); hence h(g)=k m(g) is a group homomorphism. If f(i,1,lambda0) has middle label u_i and f(i0,1,lambda) has middle label z_lambda, set v_lambda=k z_lambda. The source factorization

    (i,g,lambda)=(i,1,lambda0)(i0,g,lambda0)(i0,1,lambda)

gives the first formula in (5.1). Applying f to

    (i0,1,lambda)(i,1,lambda0)=(i0,p_(lambda,i),lambda0)

gives the second. Thus all Rees twists used below are accounted for.

Let gamma be the original G' action on B. Define the G action delta_g=gamma_(h(g)), and put

    b_hat_s=gamma_(u_(i_s)^(-1))(b_s).

Since the row of q(st) is i_s, multiplicativity of theta and (5.1) give

    b_hat_(st)
      = b_hat_s gamma_(h(g_s) v_(lambda_s)
                              p'_(b(lambda_s),a(i_t)) u_(i_t))(b_hat_t)
      = b_hat_s delta_(g_s p_(lambda_s,i_t))(b_hat_t).

The first equality follows by applying gamma_(u_(i_s)^(-1)) to the original base multiplication, then replacing b_t by gamma_(u_(i_t))(b_hat_t); it does not commute any group factors. Therefore

    s |---> (b_hat_s,q(s))

is a homomorphism to B odot C for the actual P formula and the new action delta. It is injective: equality gives equal q, hence the same i_s and equal r=fq; applying gamma_(u_(i_s)) recovers equal original b_s, and theta's injectivity gives equal s.

So every P embedding of S can be replaced by one over its least completely simple quotient q. Combining this with the universal property in §2 shows: when q is pure, **P embeddability is equivalent to (SEP)**. Failure over a randomly chosen coarser quotient would not suffice; the construction deliberately uses q=S/xi.

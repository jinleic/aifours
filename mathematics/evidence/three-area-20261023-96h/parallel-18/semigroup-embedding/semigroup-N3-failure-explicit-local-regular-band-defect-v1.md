`kl=p`, `kr=k`, `lr=r`, `rl=l`.

Let v,f,g∈D and assume

`vr=r`, `v f p f g=f p f g`.

**Explicit defect lemma.** If H≠K, then the original regular-band identity FAILS at these THREE displayed corner idempotents:

`L R L Z L != L R Z L`.  (DEFECT)

*Proof.* First

`pY=p f p f g=(pf)^2 g=pfg=H`,

`KH=k f H H=k f H=K`,

using only band idempotency. Also H=k lY because kl=p, and K=k rY because kr=k.

For all t∈vD, the map t↦tv is a band homomorphism into vDv: (t v)(u v)=t(vu)v=tuv because vu=u. In particular

`L R L Z L=(l r l Y l)v`,

`L R Z L=(l r Y l)v`.

If these corner words were equal, right multiplication by l would remove the appended v (vl=l). Since lrl=l and lr=r, this would give

`lYl=rYl`.

Right-multiplying by Y and using (lY)^2=lY yields

`rYlY=lY`.

Left-multiply by k, and use kr=k, kl=p and pY=H, to obtain

`K(lY)=H`.

Since K is idempotent, KH=K²(lY)=K(lY)=H. But the earlier direct calculation gave KH=K. Therefore H=K, contradiction. This proves DEFECT. No theorem about global Green congruences or regularity of a principal ideal was assumed. ∎

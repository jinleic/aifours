## 2. Two elementary weak-E-unitarity facts

**Lemma2.1 — stabilized square.** If b^3=b^2 in S satisfying WEU, then b is idempotent.

Indeed b^4=b^2, so d=b^2 is idempotent, and db=bd=d. Apply WEU to d=d b=b d, with both outer idempotents d. This proves b in E(S). No cancellation or compatible natural order is assumed.

**Lemma2.2 — cyclic idempotence.** For arbitrary a,b in S^1 with ab and ba in S,

`ab in E(S) IFF ba in E(S)`.                            (CYC)

If ab is idempotent, `(ba)^3=b(ab)^2a=b(ab)a=(ba)^2`; Lemma2.1 makes ba idempotent. Exchange a,b for the converse. Cases with an absent factor are tautological. This does not assert ab=ba or global orthodoxy.

**Lemma2.3 — sandwich removal with an actual left support.** If f is idempotent, fv=v, and vf is idempotent, then v is idempotent.

Since fv=v, `v^2=vfv`; hence `v^3=vfvfv=(vf)^2v=vfv=v^2`. Lemma2.1 applies. In the other direction, if fv=v and v is idempotent, then `(vf)^2=vfvf=v^2f=vf`, so vf is idempotent. Thus under this ACTUAL support, v is idempotent IFF vf is. No unrelated common unit is adjoined.

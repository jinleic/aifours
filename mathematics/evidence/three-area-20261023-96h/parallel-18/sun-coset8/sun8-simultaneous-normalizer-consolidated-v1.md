## 2. Fixed original fibres and normalizer seven-retention

Fix i and left-translate ALL original cosets by g_i^-1. Write U=U_i and the others a_jU_j. Let V_j=U∩U_j. Intersection-index divisibility and product-set cardinality give

    [G:V_j]=7r_i r_j lambda_j,
    [U:V_j]=r_j lambda_j,
    |UU_j|/|G|=lambda_j/7,
    1≤lambda_j≤7.

The value seven would give UU_j=G and make U intersect a_jU_j. Therefore lambda_j≤6. These fibre subgroups and factors are fixed BEFORE choosing an overgroup.

### Lemma NR

Every normal overgroup U◁L≤G has7|[G:L]. Suppose instead d=[G:L] lacks7. It then divides r_i and is coprime to every7r_j. The elementary coprime-index lemma makes L meet each a_jU_j and gives [L:L∩U_j]=7r_j.

Project to P=L/U. Exact index multiplication gives c_j lambda_j=7, where c_j is the index of the projected subgroup. Since lambda_j<7, lambda_j=1,c_j=7. Every projected intersection coset avoids identity. If two projected supports overlapped, their pieces in the common U-fibre would be disjoint LEFT V_j/V_k cosets inside U of coprime indices r_j,r_k, impossible. Thus seven disjoint projected cosets, each of size |P|/7, exhaust P while all avoiding identity. Contradiction.

Take N=N_G(U). Then U◁N, [G:N]=7s and

    Q=N/U, q=|Q|, r_i=sq.

In particular q divides r_i. No normality of N in G is assumed.

## 5. Every elementary-abelian slice has a blind subgroup

For three, none can be a hyperplane by density. All three annihilators are two-dimensional and intersect pairwise.

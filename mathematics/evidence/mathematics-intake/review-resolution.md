## Uniformly curved M-estimation theorem

The second Claude review checked 23 claims and found no substantive logical error. Its qualifications were incorporated by the owning agent:

- Replicate errors explicitly have the same law as the original errors and are independent of them.
- Existence, uniqueness, finite-sample bounds and limits explicitly use n>=n0.
- The constant-error equality of pooled/original minimizers is almost sure.
- The nonquadratic example now controls the population minimizer between complete design cycles, not only along a subsequence.

The parent replayed both exact-arithmetic checkers and the actual nonquadratic score-bisection scenario. These finite computations support implementation correctness; the written influence/concentration argument, not the numerical experiment, proves the theorem. It assumes a fixed C2 loss with global bounds 0<m<=rho''<=M and finite error variance. LAD, Huber, arbitrary convex losses and infinite-variance errors are not silently included.

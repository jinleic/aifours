## 0. Labels and feasibility at an unmarked v in O
For either family x, write x' for the opposite family and work in the configuration with alpha-matrix x. Put
- a_u := x_vu for u in O minus {v}, each in {1,2,4};
- z := x_(v i*) in {1,2,3,4,5};
- b_u := 3^(f_u - 1) 5^(h_u) w_u, odd and pairwise coprime;
- lambda_u = b_u a_u, lambda_(i*) = 2 * 5^(h*) w_* z (f* = 1), lambda_d = 2^(e_d - 1) 5^(h_d) w_d;
- val_x(y) := sum over u in O minus {v} of y_u a_u + y_(i*) z + 3 y_d.
The conditional row bound says val_x(y) <= 5 for every feasible y. Feasibility means that every lambda-allowed set has
weight <= 1, and is checked on its positive-weight support (allowed sets are hereditary).

**Odd parts that matter.**
- Among the O-labels and the d-label there are no shared odd primes (unique 3-marker t, unique 5-marker, no 3 in d).
- The i*-label can share an odd prime with an O-label only as follows:
  - z = 3: the shared prime is 3 and the neighbour is t;
  - z = 5: the shared prime is 5 and the neighbour is p, if p is an O-vertex;
  - z = 1: no shared odd prime.
If p = t, this is one vertex carrying both markers; each of the rules still has only one exceptional neighbour. If p is
outside O, its marker is absent from the O-labels. The i*- and d-labels may share 5 when p = d and z = 5; certificates
below assigning positive weight to i* in that case always have y_d = 0.

## 1. Lemma D4 (the R4 obstruction survives marked neighbours)
If all four O-entries at unmarked v belong to {1,2}, the row violates the conditional 5/6 bound.

*Proof.* Set y_u = 1/a_u (1 for entries 1, 1/2 for entries 2), y_d = 1/2 and y_(i*) = 0. The positive 1-labels are coprime
to every other positive label. The other positive labels are O-2-labels and lambda_d, with pairwise gcd 2, so no positive
allowed triple exists (Sun(3)). Feasible. The value is 4 + 3/2 = 11/2 > 5. The marker on t (and on p if it is in O) does
not spoil feasibility because i* has zero weight. QED

## 2. Lemma D3 (the R3 obstruction survives, with repaired certificates)
If at unmarked v exactly one O-entry equals 4 and the other three belong to {1,2}, **z is even**.

*Proof.* Suppose z is odd: z in {1,3,5}. The baseline O-weights are
  beta_u := 1 if a_u = 1, and beta_u := 1/2 if a_u in {2,4}.
Their O-contribution is 1 + 1 + 1 + 2 = 5. All certificates below are nonnegative; their values exceed 5.

### Case z = 1
Take y_u = beta_u, y_(i*) = 1/2 and y_d = 0. The positive 1-labels are isolated. All other positive labels (O-2-labels,
the single O-4-label and the i*-label) have pairwise gcd 2: at most one has 2-part 4, the rest have 2-part 2, and there
is no shared odd prime on the positive support. Thus the certificate is feasible (Sun(2), Sun(3)). Value 5 + 1/2 = 11/2.

### Case z = 3, with a_t in {1,2}
Take y_t = 0, y_u = beta_u for u != t, y_(i*) = 1/2 and y_d = 0. Deleting t removes the only O-label sharing 3 with
i*. On positive support, 1-labels are isolated and the half-weight labels (including the single 4-label and i*) have
pairwise gcd 2. Feasible. The deleted t-contribution is 1, so the value is 5 - 1 + 3/2 = 11/2.

### Case z = 3, with a_t = 4
The previous deletion would not give enough value. Instead take y_t = 0, y_u = 1/a_u for all three u != t,
y_(i*) = y_d = 1/2. All other O-entries are 1 or 2.
- The positive 1-labels are isolated from all other positive labels: their odd parts have no shared 3 after t is removed,
  and no shared 5 because there is at most one 5-marker.
- The positive half-weight labels are O-2-labels, i* and d. They have pairwise gcd exactly 2: lambda_(i*) has 2-part 2
  (z = 3), lambda_d has 2-part >= 8 and no 3, and neither can share 5 with an O-label or with each other (z != 5).
So the certificate is feasible. Its value is 3 + 3/2 + 3/2 = 6 > 5.

### Case z = 5, with p not in O
Take the baseline weights, y_(i*) = 1/2 and y_d = 0. There is no O-label with a common 5 with i*, and z has no 3.
As in the z = 1 case, positive 1-labels are isolated and half-weight labels have pairwise gcd 2. Feasible. Value 5 + 5/2.
The possible shared 5 between i* and d when p = d is irrelevant because y_d = 0.

### Case z = 5, with p in O
Take y_p = 0, y_u = beta_u for u != p, y_(i*) = 1/2 and y_d = 0. Deleting p removes the only O-label sharing 5 with i*;
there is no shared 3 because z = 5 and f* = 1. The remaining positive 1-labels are isolated, and the remaining
half-weight labels have pairwise gcd 2. Feasible. If a_p in {1,2} the value is 5 - 1 + 5/2 = 13/2; if a_p = 4 the
value is 5 - 2 + 5/2 = 11/2. This includes p = t. QED

## 3. Local graph consequences (not the G-only global conclusions)

  contradicting D4 in the x'-row.

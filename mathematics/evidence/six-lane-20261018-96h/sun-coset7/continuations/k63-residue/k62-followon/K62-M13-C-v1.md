## 1. Lemma C1w (a chain with a small-entry witness)
Let v be an unmarked O-vertex. **If there is c_0 in O minus {v} with alpha*_(i* c_0) <= 3**, then
  **gcd(n_v, T_alpha*(V minus {v})) = 6.**
This is a candidate implication proved from accepted arithmetic imports; existence of the witness for every v is
obtained in C2, conditionally on M13-B B3.

*Proof.* Work in the alpha*-family and write T_m for the running intersection index. For an added vertex e,
T_(m+1) = lcm(n_e, T_m) k_e with k_e a positive integer and
  k_e / gcd(n_e, T_m) = mu(U*_e W_m) <= mu(U*_e U*_j)
for every earlier j, by L0.1 and Lagrange.
- **First d, t (exact).** Their pair entry is alpha*_dt = 1 and gcd(n_d, n_t) = 2, so k_t = 1 and
  T_2 = lcm(n_d, n_t). In particular 3 divides T_2 (indeed v3 T_2 = f_t >= 2) and v2 T_2 >= e_d >= 4.
- **Then p, if it is a distinct O-vertex, then the unmarked vertices of O minus {v,t,p}.** Every one of these vertices e
  has f_e = 1. Its gcd with T_m is exactly 6:
  - 2-part 2; 3-part 3;
  - 5-part 1 (if e = p, all earlier vertices are 5-free and all earlier k are <= 3; otherwise h_e = 0);
  - w-part 1 (inductively no k introduces a prime > 5, and w_e is coprime to the earlier w's).
  Comparing with d gives k_e <= 6 mu(U*_e U*_d) = 6 (1/2) = 3. This proves the induction and includes the coincident,
  absent and outside-O 5-marker cases. Denote the resulting index by T_0: it is the index over {d} union (O minus {v}).
- **Add i* last.** gcd(n_(i*), T_0) = 12:
  - the 2-part is 4 (v2 T_0 >= e_d);
  - the 3-part is 3 (f* = 1 and 3 divides T_0);
  - the 5-part is 1 (if h* >= 1 every included vertex is 5-free and every earlier k <= 3; otherwise h* = 0);
  - the w-part is 1 (w_* has not entered and no earlier k has a prime > 5).
  Since U*_c_0 is one of the groups already intersected, the small-entry witness gives
    k_(i*) <= 12 mu(U*_(i*) U*_c_0) = 12 alpha*_(i* c_0) / 6 <= 6.
  Every integer <= 6 has prime factors only in {2,3,5}. Thus the final T = T_alpha*(V minus {v}) has prime-to-30 part
  equal to the product of the w_j for j != v, and that part is coprime to w_v. Since n_v = 6 w_v and 6 divides T, the
  gcd is exactly 6. QED

## 3. Lemma CI13 (cherry isolation in both families) [CONDITIONAL ON CAP/DT VERDICT]
Let y be either family, y' its opposite, v in O unmarked and a != b in O minus {v}. In the Phi^y-configuration at v put
X^v_j := Phi^y_v Phi^y_j. The following are inconsistent:
  y_va = y_vb = 4,  X^v_a = X^v_b,  and both va, vb tight (so y'_va = y'_vb = 2).
**This includes y = alpha*, whose distinguished entry is 3.** It does not apply the original CI statement outside its
x_(d i*) = 1 scope; it replays that proof using the new granularity in C2 instead of the original chain hypothesis.

*Proof (the CI proof with its replacement hypothesis written out).* Normalize the Phi^y-configuration at v. Let D'_j
be its VFC sets, of measure y'_vj / g_vj, lying outside X^v_j, with local indices lambda'_j = n_j y'_vj / g_vj (V1-V3,
INV (b)). Put W := intersection of Phi^y_j over j != v. Whether y = alpha (CAP (C1)) or y = alpha* (C2 above),
gcd(n_v, T_y(V minus {v})) divides 6. So the light set Phi^y_v W has measure >= 1/6 (L0.1, Lagrange), lies in every
X^v_j and misses every D'_j. Consequently mu(union_j D'_j) <= 5/6.
1. Tightness and X^v_a = X^v_b give D'_a = D'_b =: D, of measure 2/6.
2. D misses D'_j for j in {d} union (O minus {v,a,b}). Otherwise a common VFC right fibre would carry a,b,j. Their
   local indices have pairwise gcd <= 2:
   - lambda'_a = 2 * 3^(f_a - 1) 5^(h_a) w_a and likewise b have 2-part exactly 2;
   - for another O-vertex j, lambda'_j = y'_vj * 3^(f_j - 1) 5^(h_j) w_j, with y'_vj in {1,2,4};
   - lambda'_d = n_d/2 = 2^(e_d - 1) 5^(h_d) w_d (y'_vd = 1).
   No two of these labels share an odd prime (one 3-marker, one 5-marker, the entries contribute no odd prime, the w's
   are coprime). The three-piece VFC condition then violates Sun(3).
3. D'_d has measure 1/2, so D union D'_d already has measure 5/6. Therefore every D'_j is contained in D union D'_d.
   By step 2, for each of the two remaining O-vertices c, c', D'_c and D'_c' are contained in D'_d.
4. A VFC fibre in D'_c is shared by c,d, so Sun(2) forces gcd(lambda'_c, lambda'_d) >= 2. Their odd parts are coprime,
   so y'_vc is even; also mu(D'_c) = y'_vc/6 <= 1/2. As y'_vc in {1,2,4}, this forces y'_vc = 2, and likewise y'_vc' = 2.
5. A fibre in D'_c cap D'_c' would carry c,c',d with pairwise gcd 2 (as in step 2), violating Sun(3). Thus D'_c and D'_c'
   are disjoint inside D'_d, but their measures add to 2/6 + 2/6 = 2/3 > 1/2. QED

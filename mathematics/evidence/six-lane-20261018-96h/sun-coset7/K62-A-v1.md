## 2. Step 1: alpha_di* = alpha*_di* = 1 (LFC at d)

The inverted configuration has the same indices (INV (a), E6), so alpha*_di* = 1.

## 4. Step 3: contradiction

Steps 1-2 apply verbatim to the inverted configuration, so the alpha*-entries of i* towards C also form {2, 2, 4, 4, 4}.
The three positions with alpha_i*c = 4 have alpha*_i*c <= 2, and the three positions with alpha*_i*c = 4 have
alpha_i*c <= 2 (INV (b)); these are disjoint triples among five positions. Contradiction. QED

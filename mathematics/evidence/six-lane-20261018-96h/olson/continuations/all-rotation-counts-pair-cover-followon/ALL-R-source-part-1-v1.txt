3:## Exact target
4:
5:Let G be a finite group and n=|G|. Given every word a_1,...,a_(2n-1), select indices i_1<...<i_n such that a_(i_1)...a_(i_n)=1, without rearrangement. Equivalently, every list xs of length 2|G|-1 has a List.Sublist ys of length |G| and ordered product 1. No all-groups proof or counterexample has been established by this dossier.
6:
7:## 1. Sharp lower bound for every odd dihedral parameter (known result, reproduced)
8:
9:Let m>=1 be odd. In D_(2m)=<r,s | r^m=s^2=1, srs=r^(-1)>, consider the word
10:
11:    W = r repeated (2m-1) times, then s repeated (2m-1) times.
12:
13:Its length is 4m-2=2|G|-2, ONE TERM SHORT of the conjecture's hypothesis.
14:
15:An ordered 2m-term subword of W is r^k s^l with k+l=2m and 0<=k,l<=2m-1. In particular 0<k,l<2m. If its product were 1, projection to D_(2m)/<r> forces l even. Then s^l=1 and r^k=1, so m divides k. The only positive multiple of m below 2m is m itself. Thus k=m and l=m, contradicting oddness. Therefore W has no ordered |G|-term identity-product subword.
16:
17:The theorem `OlsonPartial.odd_dihedral_sharp_lower_bound` in `work/DihedralLowerBound.lean` proves the full all-m assertion with Mathlib's actual DihedralGroup, including its cardinality, the precise word length, and the universal List.Sublist quantifier. It is not a theorem about the 2|G|-1 bound. Its known-result label is essential: Agarwal–Lou's 2016 primary abstract already reports the odd-dihedral lower bound 4m-1 for the forcing constant. The abstract does not expose their construction or full proof, so this is a reproduced bound, not a claim that their exact proof was recovered.

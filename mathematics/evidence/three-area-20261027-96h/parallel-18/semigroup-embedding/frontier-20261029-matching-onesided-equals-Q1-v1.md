# The matching one-sided host problem is equivalent to originalQ1 (conditional on Fact1)

**Status: NEW AUTHOR WRITTEN corollary; UNREVIEWED / UNADOPTED.** It neither proves nor refutes Q1. It shows that the separately tracked "matching one-sided" obligation is not an independent difficulty.

Inputs: the seminormal Kimura decomposition `frontier-20261029-SN-kimura-decomposition-v1.md`, SHA `06abe46dff1144a855f4fd78133a8b3b9c72053438bc69909687365be30eb5b6`; the source reduction `frontier-20261029-source-onesided-reduction-v1.md`, SHA `40668a70f0d86c7d5634b52c850797278cbc01e89efa987223c4a57d2c97ca63`; and, where marked, the printed **Fact1** (every weakly E-unitary locally inverse semigroup embeds in a Pastijn product of a normal band by a completely simple semigroup; Billhardt–Szendrei 2003), imported and not reproved. Call an original source RRL if all its local idempotent bands are right regular.

## 1. Statement

The following are equivalent:

1. (Q1) every original source has a faithful physical SN-band-by-CS host;
2. every RRL original source has a faithful physical SN host;
3. [with Fact1] every RRL original source has a faithful physical RIGHTSN host (matching one-sided form);
4. [with Fact1] in every RRL original source, distinct R-related idempotents in one actor fibre are separated by the canonical RIGHTSN host.

## 2. Proof

1⇔2 is the source reduction plus duality (no Fact1). 3⇒2 because RIGHTSN⊆SN. 4⇔3 is the source reduction's §3 (with Fact1) together with the decomposition §3(b).

2⇒3. Let S be RRL with a faithful SN host `φ:S→P(B,D)`. The congruence ρ_B (generated by local L-pairs of B) is characteristic, hence invariant under the group action, so `P(B,D)→P(B/ρ_B,D)` is a host homomorphism with RIGHTSN band. If distinct idempotents e,f of S have the same actor d and ρ_B-related band coordinates, then ρ_B⊆L gives `b_eb_f=b_e`, `b_fb_e=b_f`; the trivial weight at the idempotent actor gives `φ(ef)=φ(e)`, `φ(fe)=φ(f)`, so `e L f` in S. Such a pair is not λ_S-related, because `λ_S⊆R` and distinct L-related idempotents are not R-related. For RRL S, `S/λ_S` has local bands that are left regular (source reduction) and right regular (images), i.e. semilattices, so it is weakly E-unitary locally inverse and, by Fact1, has a faithful normal-band host N. Normal bands are RIGHTSN. The product host with band `B/ρ_B×N` is RIGHTSN, its actor is pure, and it separates all idempotents: pairs not collapsed in B/ρ_B are separated there, and the collapsed ones are separated through `S/λ_S`. A homomorphism to a physical host with pure actor that is injective on idempotents is injective (source reduction §3). □

## 3. Meaning and limits

The previously open "matching" form (right-regular-local sources should embed with RIGHT-seminormal bands) is therefore exactly as hard as Q1, given Fact1. Combined with the source reduction, the sole remaining obligation for Q1 is item4: separating same-fibre R-related idempotents of RRL sources. All finite examples examined (S45, S68 and the random sources pinned earlier) are consistent with all four items; no proof of item4 and no counterexample is claimed. Fact1's proof and interface with this physical-host convention have not been independently reconstructed here.

# Two canonical one-sided local-band quotients

**Status: NEW AUTHOR WRITTEN STRUCTURAL THEOREM and bounded controls; UNREVIEWED / UNADOPTED.** This introduces exact one-sided quotients for the original separation problem. It does NOT claim they always give a subdirect decomposition of the original source or solve either quotient's physical embedding.

## 1. Explicit least quotients

For a nonempty regular S, define χ_L as the congruence generated by

`(efe,ef)` for idempotents e,f in a common local monoid zSz,

and χ_R by `(efe,fe)` over the same pairs. Then S/χ_L is the least quotient of S whose local idempotents form **left regular bands**, and S/χ_R the least quotient whose local idempotents form **right regular bands**. These descriptions use the exact identities `efe=ef` and `efe=fe`, not potentially differing one-sided terminology conventions.

Proof. Every map to a regular semigroup with the indicated local property kills its generating pairs. Conversely, lift an arbitrary quotient local idempotent to an idempotent in an actual source local monoid by the regular-corner `x y x` lift (y inverse to x²). Given any two idempotents a,b in the same quotient local monoid, lift both to e,f in the same source local monoid. The generating identity gives aba=ab for χ_L, or aba=ba for χ_R. In either case ab is idempotent: for χ_L, `(ab)²=(aba)b=(ab)b=ab`; for χ_R, `(ab)²=a(bab)=a(ab)=ab` using the same law on the reversed pair. Hence the quotient local idempotents are closed and satisfy the stated band identity. The quotient is regular, so these are the desired least quotients. No orthodoxy or purity of S is assumed.

Let χ be the least locally-inverse congruence from `frontier-20261028-localinverse-residual-kernel-v1.md`, SHA `1a98319b9cea8e65116cebc6c2876c6b5966ceb2c560b85518ef2228a116ca90`. Then

`χ_L∨χ_R=χ`.

The two generating identities together identify ef and fe, so χ⊆χ_L∨χ_R. Conversely every locally inverse quotient has commuting local idempotents and satisfies both one-sided identities; hence χ_L,χ_R⊆χ. This proves equality.

## 2. Pure-CS inheritance and exact subdirect criterion

Every homomorphism from S to a CS semigroup sends common-local idempotents e,f to the same actor idempotent. Thus it kills both generating pairs; `χ_L,χ_R⊆ν`, where ν is the least CS congruence. If ν is pure, the induced quotient maps of S/χ_L and S/χ_R onto S/ν are pure as well. Both quotients are regular, weakly E-unitary and locally r-orthodox because left/right regular bands are regular bands. Therefore both quotients remain within the original source class.

The diagonal map

`S→S/χ_L × S/χ_R`

has kernel `χ_L∩χ_R`; it is a subdirect embedding **iff this intersection is equality**. Consequently, if the intersection is equality and both one-sided quotient sources have physical SN-band-by-CS hosts, their product gives a faithful host of S. This is a conditional reduction, not a proof of either antecedent. In particular **join=χ does not imply intersection=equality**, and neither purity nor local r-orthodoxy is being silently used to infer that.

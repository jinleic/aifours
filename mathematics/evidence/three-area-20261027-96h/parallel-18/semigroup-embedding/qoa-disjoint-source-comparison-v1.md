## 4. Definitions: ordinary versus generalized and classical versus product

### 4.1 Ordinary physical QLS and pair orthogonality

[Definition2.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2111.04055v1#S2.Thmtheorem5), P1:9–12: a d-by-d array of vectors in C^d, each row and each column an ONB. Thus every cell vector is unit.

[Definition2.8](https://arxiv.org/html/2111.04055v1#S2.Thmtheorem8), P1:54–59: two such arrays are orthogonal exactly when their d² same-cell **tensor products** form an ONB of C^d tensor C^d. The defining scalar identity is the product of the two member inner products equalling delta_(ii')delta_(jj'). It preserves all complex phases; it is not a rule about abstract labels or independent pairwise choices.

The bound original successor target is **four pairwise ordinary** QLS(8), including mixed classical/nonclassical member patterns. Its quantifiers are supplied by the target packet, not inferred from an entangled-design example.

### 4.2 Classical member is a global-ray condition

[Definition2.6](https://arxiv.org/html/2111.04055v1#S2.Thmtheorem6), P1:21–32, allows one unitary U on a member's entire C^d, row/column permutations and arbitrary modulus-one **cell phases**. P1:37–39 then calls a QLS classical if equivalent in that sense to a computational-basis Latin square.

This matches the target's **per-member global ONB-ray** notion. Each of four members may have a different U/frame; no common output frame is imposed. Cell factorization across different members is not the same condition as classicality of each member. Lemma2.7's supplied proof establishes the necessary magnitude0-or1 condition for a classical member; no original four-family conclusion is inferred from it.

### 4.3 Generalized cells may be entangled

[Definition4.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2111.04055v1#S4.Thmtheorem2), P2:33–90, has d² **unit t-party states** psi_ij in (C^d)^{tensor t}, t>=2, not a priori t separated vectors. It requires:

1. Eq8: all psi_ij mutually orthonormal.
2. Eqs9/10: for each surviving output party, the two off-diagonal address sums of reduced operators equal delta times I_d.
3. Eq11: for every surviving output pair, the sum of diagonal-cell two-party reductions equals I_(d²).

The cross terms psi_ij psi_i'j'^* in Eqs9/10 are load-bearing. Replacing them by only diagonal densities or ignoring their phases would change the definition.

## 5. QOA coherent sum and exact normalization

[Definition4.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2111.04055v1#S4.Thmtheorem1), P2:12–26, defines QOA(r,N,d,k) by the **double sum**

sum_(alpha,beta) Tr_(N-k parties)(|varphi_alpha><varphi_beta|)
   =(r/d^k) I_(d^k)

for each surviving k-subsystem choice. The rows are N-party pure states and may themselves be entangled.

Put Phi=sum_alpha varphi_alpha. The displayed left side is exactly the k-party reduction of |Phi><Phi|, not a classical mixture of row projectors. Taking its trace gives ||Phi||²=r. Therefore

|Omega>=r^(-1/2)|Phi>

is normalized and has every k-party reduction I/d^k. This proves the k-uniform **caller** directly from the admitted definition without importing the paper's external QOA references.

Relative row phases of a general QOA change cross terms in |Phi><Phi| and are not freely discarded without proof. In particular a projective list or incoherent ensemble of entangled rows is not automatically the same QOA certificate.

The phrase 'minimal support, i.e. r=d^k' at P2:30 is used here only to identify the paper's special parameter regime. It is **not** adopted as a universal minimum of scalar nonzero coefficients in an arbitrary physical basis, nor as a proved r-lower-bound for every entangled-row interpretation of Definition4.1. Row count, address expansion length and physical product-basis support are distinct notions.

## 6. Complete square proposition: direct proof and converse

[Proposition4.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2111.04055v1#S4.Thmtheorem4), P2:102–184, says QOA(d²,t+2,d,2) generates t-GMOQLS(d), and vice versa. Its proof is fully present in the admitted fragment.

### 6.1 QOA to generalized square, with actual conditional states

Here ||Phi||²=d². Choose the **actual first two physical subsystems** and their fixed product computational bases. Expand the same coherent sum as

Phi=sum_(i,j)|i,j> tensor Phi_ij.

The surviving two-address reduction is I_(d²), so <Phi_i'j'|Phi_ij>=delta_(ii')delta_(jj'). Thus these are unit, orthogonal conditional t-party vectors. No entangling change of the output tensor factorization or arbitrary separately chosen cell completion is used.

Every surviving physical pair falls into exactly one of three cases:

- **Two addresses:** its reduction is the conditional Gram matrix; equality to I_(d²) is Eq8.
- **One address and one output:** tracing the other address leaves either sum_j Tr_(other outputs)(|Phi_ij><Phi_i'j|) or the corresponding sum_i. Equality of this address-output matrix to I_(d²) is exactly Eq10 or Eq9, including off-diagonal address entries.
- **Two outputs:** tracing both addresses kills all different-address cross terms, leaving sum_(i,j) Tr_(other outputs)(|Phi_ij><Phi_ij|). Equality to I_(d²) is Eq11.

This pays all source cases, not only diagonal marginals. After normalization Omega=Phi/d, the same reductions are I/d².

### 6.2 Generalized square to QOA/uniform tensor

Conversely supply actual unit Phi_ij satisfying Eqs8–11. Let the QOA rows be |i,j> tensor Phi_ij, each unit, and let their sum be Phi. The same three-case computation, reversed, gives all pair reductions I_(d²), so these rows satisfy Definition4.1 at r=d²,k=2. Phi/d is the normalized 2-uniform tensor.

For fixed ordered addresses and their fixed bases, the coefficient expansion relates the generalized array and the aggregate tensor uniquely. I do **not** certify a literal bijection between all arbitrary original QOA row decompositions and arrays: Definition4.1 controls their coherent sum, and the proof generates the address-structured decomposition from that sum. Proposition's 'generates' supports the existence/state correspondence; the introductory 'one-to-one' language is not used to assert preservation or injectivity of arbitrary input row lists.

## 7. Fully separated specialization: exact ordinary-square equivalence

[Remark4.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2111.04055v1#S4.Thmtheorem3), P2:94–98, assumes

psi_ij = v^1_ij tensor ... tensor v^t_ij

in the **actual t separated physical factors**, with each factor chosen unit (a global cell phase can be absorbed into one factor). Here is the full two-direction verification against the target's pairwise ordinary convention.

### 7.1 Generalized plus separation implies ordinary

The diagonal-address cases of Eqs9/10 give, for each member s, sums of d unit rank-one projectors equal to I_d along every row and every column. A square unit-vector frame whose projector sum is I is an ONB. Hence every member is a genuine QLS.

For each distinct output pair s,u, Eq11 says that the sum of the d² projectors onto v^s_ij tensor v^u_ij is I_(d²). Again a square unit frame is an ONB. Thus every member pair is orthogonal in Definition2.8's exact sense.

Eq8 also follows: two distinct full-cell products have zero inner product because any selected pair-product ONB already has zero inner-product product on those distinct cells. This requires t>=2, exactly the definition's range.

### 7.2 Ordinary pairwise MOQLS implies the generalized equations

Suppose each member is a QLS and every pair's cell products are an ONB. Define psi_ij as their actual product.

- Eq8 follows from pairwise ONB orthogonality as above.
- For Eqs9/10, diagonal address indices reduce to a member's row/column ONB sum. For different address indices, at least one traced member remains because t>=2, and its within-row/column inner product is zero, term by term. Every cell phase is retained but multiplies a zero term.
- Eq11 is exactly the ONB resolution of each surviving pair.

Therefore fully separated t-GMOQLS arrays and the supplied ordinary pairwise MOQLS systems correspond at these exact physical factors. No member classicality is implied or imposed by this equivalence.

## 8. Application to the original ordinary four-family target

Set t=4 and d=8 **because these are the bound target parameters**, not a selected diagnostic. The formulas above give:

ordinary four-family -> fully separated 4-GMOQLS(8)
 -> QOA(8²,6,8,2) -> normalized 2-uniform tensor on six dimension-eight parties.

The normalizing factor is1/d on the d²-term addressed sum. This is 2-uniform, **not** an asserted maximally-uniform six-party AME object.

The reverse implication from a generic QOA or 2-uniform tensor stops at generalized conditional cells. To obtain the original family one must additionally prove every address-conditional cell is a tensor product over the **same four actual output parties**, and then check member nonclassicality if seeking the negative classicality alternative. A generalized entangled cell is not a genuinely nonclassical **ordinary member**; it is a different object.

Even a fully separated generalized array does not by itself choose the four members' global ONB-ray status. All mixed patterns remain allowed. Independent member-local unitaries preserve the ordinary system, but an entangling output unitary or independent per-cell frame replacement is not a legal common-frame WLOG.

Thus neither the generalized equivalence nor existence of a k-uniform state supplies the required ordinary four-family witness with at least one nonclassical member, or a universal theorem that all four members are classical. The supplied sections settle the conversion **at its extra hypotheses**, not the registered four-family alternative.

# Conditional literature bridge for the orthodox side of Q1

**Status: WRITTEN CONDITIONAL DEDUCTION; UNREVIEWED / UNADOPTED.** This record combines independent statements at their exact stated scopes. It is not a proof of the full original question, and the crucial seminormal embedding assertion is attributed in its source to private communication and work in preparation rather than proved there.

Let S be a regular, orthodox, weakly E-unitary, locally r-orthodox semigroup, with the ordinary definitions used in the registered physical target. The following conditional chain would establish a faithful embedding of S into a semidirect product `B⋊G` with B seminormal:

1. **SQ-E input (named import):** the actual idempotence-context quotient `q:S→D` is idempotent-pure with D completely simple for the original source class. This record does not independently establish SQ-E.
2. **Actor dichotomy (author candidate):** for regular S with actual pure-CS q, orthodoxy forces D to be a group and `E(S)=q⁻¹(1)`, hence S is E-unitary in the strong unitary-kernel sense. See `frontier-20261028-orthodox-actual-actor-dichotomy-v1.md`, SHA `e1cc0408a619f83ba8075b9ebff5c3b1cf78600486bb4897bad6cc491b85feeb`; unreviewed.
3. **Band identity (author candidate plus mandatory erratum):** since S is orthodox, `E(eSe)=eE(S)e`, so local r-orthodoxy makes E(S) a locally regular band. The candidate `frontier-20261028-local-regular-seminormal-equivalence-v1.md`, SHA `bf7af7afe0440fda2774231c99577fa1d36ebf4b12f70628eddc380a162663ee`, together with `frontier-20261028-local-seminormal-band-content-rationale-erratum-v1.md`, SHA `dfedbf04821a2e60259e2d34631f336178ed73f867e79373d888926f4cb1262a`, gives `E(S)∈SN`. Both are unreviewed.
4. **Literature assertion (unproved import):** Billhardt, *On Embeddability into a Semidirect Product of a Band by a Group*, J. Algebra 206 (1998), 40–50, §3, <https://www.academia.edu/53871668/On_Embeddability_into_a_Semidirect_Product_of_a_Band_by_a_Group>, explicitly reports that each E-unitary regular semigroup whose idempotent band is seminormal is **strictly embeddable**, meaning it embeds in B⋊G with `B∈var(E(S))`. The article attributes this claim to a Szendrei private communication and a Billhardt in-preparation result, and does NOT provide its proof there. We have not located a separate proof or verified that claim independently.

If all four inputs hold, `B∈var(E(S))⊆SN`, so this is a faithful physical P(B,G) host (group G is a completely simple actor). This pays the **orthodox subclass** of original Q1, not any nonorthodox case; the actual actor q transport is not needed to assert existence of some host. It also depends on verifying that the printed book uses the same weak-E, local-r, seminormal and physical product conventions. The 1998 author-attributed assertion is not a novelty finding or a result we have proved; it must be reviewed at the source-strength actually available.

Without input 4, the stronger globally-regular-E orthodox subclass follows from the published close-embedding theorem separately cited in `frontier-20261028-q1-finite-product-subsemigroup-v1.md`, SHA `8ee60ebf10ee69b6390b5b5e3299afaa2af2702f101bd62aa95a2bc809b2c790`. Nonorthodox same-q R/L separation and arbitrary-S Q1 remain open.

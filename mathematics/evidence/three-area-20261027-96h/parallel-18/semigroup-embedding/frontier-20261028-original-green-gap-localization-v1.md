## 2. Localization for an arbitrary original source

Let e,f be distinct R-related idempotents of an arbitrary original S. If `e` and `f` have different χ_L-classes, their images in `T_L=S/χ_L` are distinct R-related idempotents. T_L is again original and its local bands are LRB. By §1 its canonical SN product separates their images. Composing with the quotient S→T_L gives a physical SN-host map of S separating e,f. Its actor factors through η_S; the generic same-band row-gauge lift moves the map over η_S without losing any source distinction. The canonical universal property then says κ_(η_S) separates e,f. Therefore

`e R f and κ_(η_S)(e)=κ_(η_S)(f)  ⇒  e χ_L f`.            (R)

Dually,

`e L f and κ_(η_S)(e)=κ_(η_S)(f)  ⇒  e χ_R f`.            (L)

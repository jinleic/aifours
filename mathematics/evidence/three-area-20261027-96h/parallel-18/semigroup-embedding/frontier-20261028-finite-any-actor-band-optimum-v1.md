Its all-actor scope depends on the named finest-pure-actor and same-band lifting candidates; its fixed-actor component uses the named Green–Rees finite-free-band input.

## 1. The exact optimization formula

Let S be a finite nonempty regular semigroup whose actual syntactic quotient q is pure and completely simple. Let `q̂:S→D̂=M[G;Î,Λ̂;P̂]` be the finest pure CS quotient from `frontier-20261028-finest-pure-cs-actor-v1.md`, SHA `772cf39e2d5c6aab1c127ed73edaf3e7c2e617deda8f3113b8059df19f4c83ab`. Let V be the seminormal band variety and set `n=|G||S|`.

Form the canonical V-band C over the symbols `X_(u,s)` with u∈G,s∈S, modulo all V identities and the mixed source equations

`X_(u,st)=X_(u,s) X_(u g_s P̂_(λ_s,i_t),t)`.

All u∈G are included; the left shift of u gives an action of G on C. For any G-invariant congruence θ of C, let κ_θ:S→P(C/θ,D̂) be the source map. Then the exact minimum band cardinality among **all** faithful physical V-band-by-CS hosts is

`min { |C/θ| : θ a G-invariant congruence of C and κ_θ injective }`,

with minimum ∞ when the set is empty. In particular a host exists iff the unquotiented canonical map κ_id is injective.

## 2. Why the search is complete

The free band on n generators is finite by the classical Green–Rees theorem, so its V-quotient and C are finite. An explicit upper bound is

`F(n)=Σ_(k=1)^n binom(n,k)c_k`, with `c₁=1` and `c_k=k² c_(k−1)²`.

The named source and finite construction appear in `frontier-20261028-finite-source-canonical-decision-v1.md`, SHA `b6f54918e468755366b215d64ac7fe247fcd3d73be6357105c8817ab47123f47`; this finite-free-band fact is imported, not established by a sample. A finite multiplication table for C can be constructed by free-band word reduction, imposing the explicit SN identity `abcda=abcacda` for all substitutions, adding all mixed relations, and saturating the finite congruence. Enumerating all partitions of C, retaining precisely the congruences stable under all G-actions, and testing injectivity of κ_θ terminates. No efficiency is inferred from termination.

Now take any faithful host over any CS actor, possibly with an infinite band. The same-band lifting theorem `frontier-20261028-finest-actor-same-band-lift-v1.md`, SHA `fa8bcc7beb069fbe873380429415f9c90723420a241abd078fe206973d1d8c3c`, moves it faithfully to D̂ on the exact same band and some G-action. The subband B₀ generated by `{α_u(c_s):u∈G,s∈S}` is G-stable, lies in V, contains all source coordinates, and is finite with `|B₀|≤F(n)`. The canonical universal property gives an onto equivariant homomorphism `C→B₀`; its kernel is a G-invariant congruence θ and `C/θ≅B₀`. Thus the formula's value is no larger than the size of any host band. Conversely each listed θ constructs an actual faithful host, so the formula is exact. □

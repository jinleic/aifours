# Exactly two pure completely simple quotients of the 68-source

**Status: NEW AUTHOR WRITTEN SCOPE STRENGTHENING; UNREVIEWED / UNADOPTED.** This strengthens `frontier-20261028-quotient68-actor-image-classification-v1.md`, SHA `ab1ac0cf15127a7fd5bb0b5545eff543cec8c6c55dc3e0a213c079791a777749`: a faithful physical host is not needed to classify the pure CS quotients.

For the pinned regular 68-source S, every onto idempotent-pure homomorphism `r:S→T` with T completely simple is, up to a unique isomorphism of its target commuting with the map from S, either the actual syntactic quotient `q:S→D₈` or the explicit refined quotient `r₁₂:S→D₁₂`. Both are onto and pure, as independently checked from the literal source/actor tables in the cited predecessor artifacts. The two kernels are distinct: for example source elements 1 and17 have the same q value but distinct r₁₂ columns. The r₁₂ kernel is contained in the q kernel since q is obtained by identifying the two refined columns. Thus the poset of pure-CS quotient kernels on this source is exactly a two-element chain.

Proof: the classification proof's §3 uses a faithful host only to *establish* that its actor image map is pure. If r is pure by hypothesis, its remaining argument applies verbatim: actual q factors through r; the induced onto map `f:T→D₈` is pure and injective on local groups, so T has group C₂; surjectivity and the computed finest source row/column partitions force T to have exactly 2 rows and 2 or3 columns. At size8, f is an isomorphism. At size12, equality of r₁₂ values forces equal T rows, equal T columns and equal f-images; local-group injectivity makes r equal. Thus r factors through r₁₂ and the induced onto map D₁₂→T is bijective. □

The statement concerns **onto pure CS quotients**, not arbitrary non-pure CS images or arbitrary semigroup congruences. A pure map into a larger CS semigroup factors through its image, which is CS because S is regular and the regular-subsemigroup theorem applies. This observation does not by itself preserve a chosen band or prove a host embedding. All source, partition and q-purity inputs remain pending independent review.

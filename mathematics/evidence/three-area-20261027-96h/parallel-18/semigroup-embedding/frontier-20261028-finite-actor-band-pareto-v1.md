# Exact finite actor–band Pareto and whole-host optimization

**Status: NEW AUTHOR WRITTEN CONDITIONAL THEOREM; UNREVIEWED / UNADOPTED.** This is a finite, generally infeasible enumeration formula. It does not prove existence for every original source or an efficient general algorithm. Its assumptions and named dependencies are essential.

## 1. Setup and formula

Let S be a finite nonempty regular semigroup whose actual idempotence-context quotient q is pure and completely simple. Let V be the seminormal band variety. By the candidate pure-CS quotient-lattice theorem `frontier-20261028-pure-cs-quotient-lattice-v1.md`, SHA `21646707ddc27c5fe4a9e6ab2dc2ec118661ed123e5cd8e800fc8b800c0d59bf`, the onto pure CS quotients of S, up to unique S-compatible isomorphism, form a finite explicit list `r:S→D_r`, indexed by independent row/column partitions inside the q row/column fibres. Their local groups are all isomorphic to the q group G.

For each r, construct the finite canonical V-band `C_r` with generators `X_(u,s)` and the physical mixed source relations, and let

`m(r)=min{|C_r/θ| : θ is G-invariant and the induced source map over D_r is injective}`,

with m(r)=∞ if no such θ exists. Then the minimum cardinality of an entire faithful physical V-band-by-CS product over **all** actors is exactly

`min_r ( |D_r| · m(r) )`.                                      (1)

When every m(r)=∞ no host exists. The componentwise nondominated finite pairs `( |D_r|, m(r) )` form the exact actor/band Pareto frontier among finite faithful hosts, with any larger ambient actor or extra unused band elements discarded.

## 2. Proof and exact termination

Consider any faithful host `S→P(B,D₀)`, including one with infinite B or D₀. Its actor image T is finite because S is finite, regular as a homomorphic image, and CS as a regular subsemigroup of D₀. Its actor map `r:S→T` is pure, so it appears in the named finite quotient lattice. Apply the general same-band lifting lemma `frontier-20261028-finest-actor-same-band-lift-v1.md`, SHA `fa8bcc7beb069fbe873380429415f9c90723420a241abd078fe206973d1d8c3c`, to the inclusion T→D₀: S embeds into `P(B,T)` with the SAME band. This removes ambient actor elements without increasing any factor.

Let G act in the T-host. The subband B₀ generated by the finitely many group translates of source coefficients is G-stable, belongs to V and is finite by the Green–Rees finite-free-band theorem; replacing B by B₀ preserves faithfulness. The universal map from C_r onto B₀ has a G-invariant kernel θ, and the induced source map is injective. Thus every host is dominated in both factor cardinalities by a host accounted for in (1). Conversely every finite θ counted in m(r) explicitly gives a faithful `P(C_r/θ,D_r)`. Hence (1) and the Pareto statement follow. □

Each C_r is a quotient of the n-generator free band, `n=|G||S|`, of size at most the named Green–Rees bound `F(n)` in `frontier-20261028-finite-source-canonical-decision-v1.md`, SHA `b6f54918e468755366b215d64ac7fe247fcd3d73be6357105c8817ab47123f47`. Enumerating all partitions of C_r, checking congruence and G-invariance, and testing source injectivity terminates. Enumerating the quotient lattice terminates by its finite Bell-product description. Nothing here asserts feasibility of constructing C_r or its congruence lattice in practice.

## 3. The 68-source instance and scope

For S68 the only pure CS quotients are D₈ and D₁₂, as in `frontier-20261028-quotient68-pure-cs-quotients-v1.md`, SHA `8494ddd4f4091f3ef446018536c2273c77961f4e24fa1cbba310895a62135532`. Its pinned exact fixed-q band optimum is 10 and its pinned all-actor band optimum is 8, attained over D₁₂. Thus its Pareto pairs are `(8,10)` and `(12,8)`, and (1) yields `min(80,96)=80`, agreeing with the direct finite-source argument in `frontier-20261028-quotient68-global-host-optimum-v1.md`, SHA `d259e08d89fab9a4c2132e04ffd685cbf7bf226db69015f6756f6da826027dd2`.

This S68 conclusion is conditional on the independently unreviewed exact no-nine arc traces, both source/host witnesses, quotient classification and same-band gauge. The general formula is conditional on its named predecessor theorems and the canonical universal property. It says nothing about infinite S or about all-S sources when the actual q is not a pure CS quotient. No formal proof, independent review or novelty is claimed.

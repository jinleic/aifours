## 1. Exact band-source criterion

Let S be ANY band. Then

`S embeds in some seminormal-band-by-CS physical product  iff  S is seminormal`.                 (1)

The reverse implication is immediate: choose band S and the one-element group actor.

For the forward implication suppose `j:S→P(C,D0)` is faithful, with C seminormal and D0 completely simple. The actor image T is a band, since every source element is idempotent. In particular T is a regular subsemigroup of D0, and hence is completely simple by the elementary Green-inheritance argument in `frontier-20261028-regular-source-any-actor-characterization-v1.md`, SHA `73d3951b03ecdbc6574a21753665e612c28aa0570158e7936a7228e36deade5d`. A completely simple band is a rectangular band, so its maximal group is trivial.

Restrict the original host to T by the occupied-row Rees gauges (the same-band calculation in `frontier-20261028-all-cs-obstruction-actor-gauges-addendum-v1.md`, SHA `99a310d0b058de9a4fc754a0d05388f4fc1ed41a46f7fd439878a49fe9646bda`, §3; the calculation itself does not require the source to be finite). The resulting faithful host has trivial structure group and is exactly `C×T`. Rectangular bands and C both satisfy the seminormal identity, and identities are inherited by products and subsemigroups. Hence S is seminormal. This proves (1) for arbitrary finite or infinite bands.

## 2. A necessary condition for universal original-class embedding

Let V denote bands whose local corners are regular bands, and SN the seminormal band variety defined by

`x y z w x = x y z x z w x`.

Every B∈V is itself a regular weakly E-unitary locally r-orthodox semigroup: all elements are idempotent, and its local corners satisfy the required regular-band law. Thus if **every** original-class semigroup has a physical seminormal-band-by-CS host, (1) forces `V⊆SN`.

The reverse inclusion always holds: if B is seminormal, then in any local monoid eBe, substituting its identity e for z in the seminormal identity gives `xywx=xyxwx`, the regular-band identity. Therefore the universal physical positive assertion NECESSARILY implies

`V=SN`.                                                  (2)

This is a necessary condition only. Even if (2) is proved, the general source embedding problem remains unpaid; equality of varieties alone supplies no host for an arbitrary source.

## 3. A finite all-host route if the identity fails

Conversely, if `V⊄SN`, choose B∈V and elements x,y,z,w witnessing failure of the seminormal identity. The subband `F=⟨x,y,z,w⟩` remains in V and still violates the identity. By the named Green–Rees free-band finiteness/count theorem, F is finite with

`|F| ≤ F(4)=332380`.

As an idempotent semigroup, F is an actual original-class source. By (1) it cannot embed in ANY seminormal-band-by-CS physical host, for any CS actor and action. Its actual idempotence syntactic quotient is trivial, since every context is idempotent. Thus a valid explicit locally regular nonseminormal band is a self-contained FINITE original-source all-host obstruction at this physical target; it is not merely a failure of a chosen construction.

No such F is supplied here. Bounded SAT searches and samples do not establish `V⊆SN` or `V⊄SN`; Green–Rees, the standard Rees actor gauges and the regular-subsemigroup lemma are named inputs requiring review.

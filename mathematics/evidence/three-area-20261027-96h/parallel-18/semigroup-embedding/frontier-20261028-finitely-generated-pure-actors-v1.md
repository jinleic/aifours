# Finitely generated regular sources have finitely many pure CS quotients

**Status: NEW AUTHOR WRITTEN CONSEQUENCE; UNREVIEWED / UNADOPTED.** This is an infinite-source statement conditional on the named general pure-CS actor and quotient-lattice candidates; it is not a finiteness statement for S, G or the canonical band, and does not resolve Q1.

Let S be a regular semigroup generated as a semigroup by d≥1 elements. Assume its actual syntactic quotient `q:S→M[G;I,Λ;P]` is completely simple; q-purity then follows from `frontier-20261028-syntactic-purity-all-regular-v1.md`, SHA `20b839e181bc4fff3655cc22cf07675357c0e4ddedb2998fdd1949642d1c1714`. Subject to `frontier-20261028-pure-cs-quotient-lattice-v1.md`, SHA `21646707ddc27c5fe4a9e6ab2dc2ec118661ed123e5cd8e800fc8b800c0d59bf`, S has at most `Bell(d)²` pure CS quotient kernels, even if S and G are infinite. Its group G is finitely generated by at most `d+d²` displayed elements.

Proof. Let R and C be the finest source row/column equivalences in the named actor theorem, generated by `s~st` and `t~st`. Every source element is a nonempty word in the d generators. Repeatedly using `R(st)=R(s)` shows its R-class is the R-class of the word's first generator; similarly its C-class is that of its last generator. Hence `|R|≤d` and `|C|≤d`.

The pure quotient lattice is the product of partition lattices on the R-fibres over q rows and C-fibres over q columns. The product of Bell numbers for disjoint blocks of total size at most d is at most Bell(d), since every combination of within-block partitions is one partition of the total set. Thus their two products have size at most Bell(d)².

Write the q-image of generator x_j as `(i_j,g_j,λ_j)`. Any source word has group coordinate a product of the `g_j` and the sandwich entries `P_(λ_j,i_k)` for 1≤j,k≤d. Because q is onto and every Rees cell contains every element of G, every g∈G occurs as the group coordinate of some source word. Thus those at most d+d² elements generate G as a group. No claim is made that the resulting generating set is minimal, that G is finite, or that S is locally finite. □

For the explicit 68-source the actual finitely enumerable actor lattice has two elements; adjoining arbitrary completely simple factors as in `frontier-20261028-quotient68-cs-factor-family-v1.md`, SHA `bc8cab96779aed3b3072fc8fc17a5373064d1d822519d04faa12965e8ee5db3f`, can create infinite sources, but finite generation is asserted here only when explicitly verified for the factor chosen. All predecessor and source assumptions remain unreviewed; no formal proof or novelty is claimed.

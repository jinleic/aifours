# Every original source splits into its two one-sided local-band quotients

**Status: NEW AUTHOR WRITTEN GENERAL PROOF; UNREVIEWED / UNADOPTED.** This pays the previously unproved kernel-intersection step in `frontier-20261028-local-one-sided-quotients-v1.md`, SHA `3157a54a8e5a0320530fdb8eddd9fc47a2d44cdcd4348cf2ff77edbeabb31e1b`. ## 1. Exact theorem

Let S be a nonempty regular locally r-orthodox semigroup. Let χ_L be generated by `(efe,ef)` and χ_R by `(efe,fe)`, for idempotents e,f in a common local monoid zSz. Then

`χ_L⊆R_S`,   `χ_R⊆L_S`.                                (1)

If S in addition has any pure CS quotient (in particular, if S is weakly E-unitary by the separately reconstructed source-class bridge), then

`χ_L∩χ_R = equality`.

Consequently every original-class regular weakly-E locally-r S embeds subdirectly in

`S/χ_L × S/χ_R`,

where the two factors have respectively left-regular and right-regular local idempotent bands, remain regular/weakly-E/locally-r, and both map purely onto the least CS actor of S. No finiteness or global orthodoxy is required.

## 2. A regular-band R-congruence calculation

In a regular band A, if a,b are R-related idempotents, `ab=b,ba=a`, then for each h∈A, `ah R bh`. This follows directly from `xyxzx=xyzx`: substitute x=b,y=a,z=h to obtain

`b a b h b = b a h b`, hence `b h b = a h b`.

Multiplying right by h gives `bh=ahbh`, because `(bh)²=bh`. Interchanging a,b gives `ah=bhah`. Thus `(ah)(bh)=bh` and `(bh)(ah)=ah`, exactly the R-pair equations. The dual argument shows L is preserved under left multiplication in a regular band. This is a direct identity proof, not a Kimura-decomposition import.

## 3. Local R-pairs survive arbitrary source contexts

Let a,b be R-related idempotents in the SAME actual source local monoid zSz. Let y∈S be arbitrary and put t=zy. Choose a mutual inverse v of t and set `t'=vz`. Since zt=t,

`t t' t=t v z t=t v t=t`,
`t' t t'=v z t v z=v t v z=vz=t'`.

Thus t' is a mutual inverse and `h=t t'=z y v z` is an idempotent of zSz. Its local idempotents form a regular band by the source hypothesis. The calculation in §2 therefore gives `ah R bh`, witnessed by products inside that band.

Since a=az and b=bz, `ay=at` and `by=bt`. Also

`ay t'=ah`, `(ah)t=ay`, and similarly `by t'=bh`, `(bh)t=by`.

Hence `ay R ah R bh R by` in S. Arbitrary LEFT multiplication preserves Green R (the right-ideal witnesses remain valid after multiplying them on the left), so `xay R xby` for every source x. The same conclusion covers independently absent contexts, since R itself contains a,b and the one-sided arguments apply when only one context is present.

For each χ_L generator, `a=efe`, `b=ef` are idempotents in zSz and satisfy `ab=b`, `ba=a` because local E(zSz) is a band. Every contextual instance of every generator is therefore an R-pair. A congruence generated by a relation is the transitive equivalence closure of its actual/absent contextual instances; as R is an equivalence, `χ_L⊆R`. Dualize the entire argument to obtain `χ_R⊆L`. This proves (1) without assuming a global R or L congruence on S.

## 4. Purity removes the possible H-kernel

Let θ=χ_L∩χ_R. By (1), θ⊆H. Also χ_L,χ_R are contained in every CS quotient kernel: common-local source idempotents all map to the unique idempotent in the corresponding actor local group. Hence θ is contained in a pure CS kernel r, and is itself idempotent-saturating: s θ e∈E implies r(s)=r(e) idempotent, so s is idempotent.

Suppose s θ t and choose s'∈V(s). Then `s's θ s't`. The left member is idempotent; the saturation just proved makes s't idempotent. Since θ⊆H, the two are H-related idempotents and therefore equal. Also s R t, so t=sx for some x∈S¹ and `ss't=ss'sx=sx=t`. But `s't=s's` gives `ss't=ss's=s`. Hence t=s. Thus θ is equality.

The factor source classes and their purity follow from the explicit quotient/local-lift theorem in the predecessor: quotient idempotents lift within actual source corners, each one-sided identity forces local band closure, and both kernels lie within the least pure-CS kernel.

## 1. An explicit least locally inverse congruence

Let S be any nonempty regular semigroup. Let χ be the congruence generated by

`(ef,fe)` for all idempotents e,f belonging to a common local monoid zSz, z∈E(S).

Then S/χ is locally inverse and χ is the least congruence with that property. Every homomorphism from S to a locally inverse semigroup kills these pairs, because the images of e,f are idempotents of the inverse local monoid `π(z)Tπ(z)` and commute.

Therefore any two idempotents a,b of εTε lift to idempotents e,f in the same source zSz. The defining χ-pair makes `ab=ba`. The regular local monoid εTε consequently has commuting idempotents and is inverse. Hence T is locally inverse. This proves the minimality assertion with no local orthodoxy or purity assumption on S.

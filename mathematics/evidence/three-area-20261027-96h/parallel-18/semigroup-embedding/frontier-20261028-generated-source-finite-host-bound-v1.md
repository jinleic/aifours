## 1. The bound

Let S be ANY semigroup generated by d≥1 elements, and let `r:S→D=M[G;I,Λ;P]` be an onto CS actor with finite G. Put

`c_1=1`, `c_k=k²c_(k−1)²`, `F(n)=Σ_(k=1)^n binom(n,k)c_k`.

If S has a faithful physical host over r with a locally regular band, it has one with band size at most `F(d|G|)`. Moreover I and Λ have at most d elements each, so

`|S| ≤ |D| F(d|G|) ≤ d² |G| F(d|G|)`.                  (1)

The same bound applies to the actual canonical band, whether or not κ_r is injective. For an actual pure-CS q, this improves the predecessor's `F(|S||G|)` bound to `F(d|G|)`. An infinite S satisfying the hypotheses cannot have such a fixed-actor host. For pure q with finite G, Brown's theorem already implies locally finite S, so no claim is made that this last conditional supplies an actual infinite original counterexample.

## 2. Proof from mixed equations

Choose source generators `a_1,…,a_d`. In any physical r-model define B0 as the subband generated by the orbit

`{α_g(β_(a_i)):g∈G,1≤i≤d}`.

It is invariant under the G action. The mixed product law is

`β_(st)=β_s α_(w(s,t))(β_t)`.

Induction on source word length shows every β_s lies in B0: the induction hypothesis is stable under all α_g and products. By the named Green–Rees finite-free-band theorem from the predecessor, B0 is finite of size at most F(d|G|); it remains a V-band and the action restricts. All source images lie in its physical host, so an originally faithful model remains faithful. The same argument applies to the canonical band generators, with no assumption that κ_r is faithful.

A product in Rees D always has the row of its first factor and the column of its last factor. Since r is onto and S has d generators, every D row and column occurs among the d actor generators; thus |I|,|Λ|≤d. Hence D is finite of size at most d²|G|. Injectivity of the physical source map gives (1).

## 3. Finite table construction with fewer generators

For finite S, a word in the chosen d generators for every source element is computable by closure/BFS. Recursively expand its β coordinate using the ACTUAL group weights. This expresses each β_s as a word in only d|G| orbit symbols. One can construct the same canonical band by taking the finite free band on those orbit symbols, imposing all locally regular identities, and imposing all source mixed equations with each β_s replaced by its chosen expression. The maps in both directions are inverse: the expanded expressions hold in the original canonical presentation; conversely, its full generators map to the expanded expressions and satisfy every original mixed equation. The all-source-pair relations ensure chosen source-word representatives introduce no spurious ambiguity.

This is still an astronomically expensive finite algorithm. No incomplete bounded search, SAT UNKNOWN or source-rank optimization is a universal proof of embedding/nonembedding.

# Source-level reduction of Q1 to locally right-regular sources and same-fibre R-pairs

**Status: NEW AUTHOR WRITTEN reduction theorem with exact finite controls; UNREVIEWED / UNADOPTED.** It does NOT prove originalQ1. It reduces Q1 to one precise obligation. Named imports are stated where used.

Throughout, an *original source* is a regular semigroup S with an onto idempotent-pure homomorphism q to a completely simple semigroup D such that every local idempotent band E(gSg) is regular. (By the author bridge `frontier-20261028-weakE-localorth-pure-cs-proof-v1.md`, SHA `d339b3165ef3dce4a3b85bc3c74db447a504275aaa41195028f58c58008a7fe9`, this is the regular weakly-E-unitary locally-r-orthodox class.) For idempotents, `e R f` means `ef=f, fe=e`; `e L f` means `ef=e, fe=f`.

## 1. Theorem

Let λ_S (resp. ρ_S) be the congruence on S generated by all pairs of R-related (resp. L-related) idempotents lying in a common local band E(gSg). Then:

1. `λ_S ⊆ R`, `ρ_S ⊆ L` (Green's relations of S), and both lie in ker q;
2. `λ_S ∩ ρ_S = Δ`, so S embeds subdirectly in `S/λ_S × S/ρ_S`;
3. S/λ_S is an original source whose local idempotent bands are LEFT regular; S/ρ_S is one whose local bands are RIGHT regular.

## 2. Proof

(1) Let x,y∈E(gSg), `x R y`, z∈S. Put v=gz, choose v'∈V(v), h=vv'. Then `xz=xv R xh` (as `xh=xvv'`, `xv=xhv`), likewise `yz R yh`, and gh=h. Since q(x)=q(g) and q(h) is an idempotent in q(g)'s row, `q(xh)=q(h)` is idempotent, so xh, yh are idempotents (purity); also `h̃=ghg` is an idempotent of gSg because `q(h)q(g)=q(g)`. Now `xhy=xh̃y=yh̃y=yhy`, the middle step by "x R y in a regular band ⇒ xwy=ywy" applied in E(gSg) (`frontier-20261029-SN-kimura-decomposition-v1.md`, SHA `06abe46dff1144a855f4fd78133a8b3b9c72053438bc69909687365be30eb5b6`, Step1). Hence `(xh)(yh)=(yhy)h=yh` and symmetrically `(yh)(xh)=xh`; so `xh R yh` and `xz R yz`. R is a left congruence, so every translate `uxv R uyv`, and λ_S⊆R. The generators have equal q-values, so λ_S⊆ker q. Dually ρ_S⊆L.

(2) λ_S∩ρ_S ⊆ H∩ker q. If `s H t` and q(s)=q(t), pick s'∈V(s); by the standard inverse-location lemma (Petrich 2006, Lemma2.1, pinned `frontier-20261029-petrich-2006-one-sided-public-source.pdf`, SHA `0514fe1fe0239bcc06965713b0976d8573594ae1a7c360b66db206463b57ab09`) t has an inverse t'∈H_(s') with tt'=ss'. Purity makes s't idempotent (`q(s't)=q(s's)`); s't and s's are both R-related to s' and L-related to s, hence equal. Thus `t=ss't=ss's=s`.

(3) q factors through S/λ_S and stays onto and pure. Its idempotent fibres are images of the fibres of S; by the exact fibre formula `E(eSe)=eF_(q(e))e` (`frontier-20261029-exact-idempotent-fibre-class-v1.md`, SHA `aabc39e5354b05bef98bb012cbe1922c43eb66c1625f04c551b0d0c395856960`), each local band of S/λ_S is the image of some E(eSe) by a congruence containing its R-relation, hence a quotient of E(eSe)/R, which is left regular. Dually for ρ_S. □

## 3. Consequences

- **Hosts combine.** A product of physical hosts is a physical host over the product CS actor. Hence if S/λ_S and S/ρ_S have faithful hosts with bands in SN (for instance LEFTSN and RIGHTSN), S has one with band their product.
- **Duality.** S is original iff S^op is; opposite hosts exist with B^op, and SN is self-dual. Therefore **Q1 holds for all original sources iff it holds for all original sources with RIGHT-regular local bands.**
- **What is left inside that class.** Let S have right-regular local bands. Then ρ_S=Δ, and S/λ_S has local bands that are both left and right regular, i.e. semilattices: it is a weakly E-unitary LOCALLY INVERSE source. A homomorphism into a physical host with pure actor is injective iff injective on idempotents (if φ(s)=φ(t): φ(ss')=φ(ts') forces ss'=ts', likewise s's=s't, so s H t, then s=t by (2)). Distinct idempotents not λ_S-related are separated by S/λ_S. λ_S-related distinct idempotents are R-related and in one q-fibre. So, **conditional on the printed Fact1 for weakly E-unitary locally inverse semigroups (a normal-band host; Billhardt–Szendrei 2003, imported and not reproved here)**, Q1 is equivalent to: *for every original source with right-regular local bands, distinct R-related idempotents in one q-fibre are separated by some faithful-on-them SN host*; by `frontier-20261029-SN-kimura-decomposition-v1.md` §3(b) this is the same as separation by the canonical RIGHTSN host.

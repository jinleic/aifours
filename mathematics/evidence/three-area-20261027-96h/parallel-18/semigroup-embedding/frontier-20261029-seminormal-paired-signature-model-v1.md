## 1. Exact word theorem

For a nonempty word w let R(w) be the complete right-seminormal signature in `frontier-20261029-right-seminormal-normal-form-v1.md`, SHA `c2a5f4d9de295deed8e28282b2456f6d274886856224dccd68208bae0c2fe6be`: recursively `(head,earliest-last-occurrence letter,signature of suffix after it)`. Define the left signature `L(w)=R(reverse(w))`, interpreted with reversed multiplication.

**Theorem.** Two words u,v are equal in ALL seminormal bands (`abcda=abcacda`) iff

`R(u)=R(v)` AND `L(u)=L(v)`.                            (1)

The free SN band on an alphabet A is the subband generated by the diagonal generators in the direct product of the free rightSN band and the free leftSN band. Its elements are the correlated signature pairs `(R(w),L(w))`; not every arbitrary pair with matching contents must be realizable, so use generator closure rather than the whole cartesian product.

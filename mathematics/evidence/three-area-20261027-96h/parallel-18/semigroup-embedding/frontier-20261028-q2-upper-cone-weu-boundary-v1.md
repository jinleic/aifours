# Upper-cone obstruction is automatic in the original weak-E class

**Status: NEW AUTHOR WRITTEN SCOPE ADDENDUM; UNREVIEWED / UNADOPTED.** This accompanies `frontier-20261028-q2-upper-cone-obstruction-v1.md`, SHA `283fb012e02f730a86aaa72ecbb77f695de5bd572c73dfeed794a0ab77e7720a` and narrows its relevance; it does not modify that immutable record.

If S is weakly E-unitary, then for any idempotent z the upper cone `↑z={e∈E(S): z≤e}` is automatically a band. Indeed, if e,f∈↑z, then `z(ef)=(ze)f=zf=z` and `(ef)z=e(fz)=ez=z`. Thus z≤ef, and weak E-unitarity makes ef an idempotent. The same equations show ef∈↑z. Therefore the nine-element zero counterexample in the predecessor cannot be an original Q1 source and the upper-cone *closure* necessary condition cannot alone refute Q1.

If S is also regular and locally r-orthodox, every local band `e(↑z)e` lies inside the regular band `E(eSe)` and is therefore regular. Hence ↑z is locally regular. Using the separately unreviewed candidate `frontier-20261028-local-regular-seminormal-equivalence-v1.md`, SHA `bf7af7afe0440fda2774231c99577fa1d36ebf4b12f70628eddc380a162663ee`, **together with its mandatory rationale erratum** `frontier-20261028-local-seminormal-band-content-rationale-erratum-v1.md`, SHA `dfedbf04821a2e60259e2d34631f336178ed73f867e79373d888926f4cb1262a`, ↑z is even seminormal. Thus the predecessor's complete SN upper-cone obstruction is also automatic for the original WEU/local-r class if that separate identity candidate is accepted.

The necessary condition remains a genuine restriction on the wider non-WEU Q2 source class, demonstrated by the explicit locally inverse zero semigroup. It is not an all-source Q1 obstruction, a solution of the same-q R/L gap, a proof of Q2 sufficiency, or independently reviewed.

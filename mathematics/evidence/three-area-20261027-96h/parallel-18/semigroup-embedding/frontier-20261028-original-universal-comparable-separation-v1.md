## 1. Least locally-inverse quotient never collapses a comparable pair

Let S be ANY regular locally r-orthodox semigroup; weak E-unitarity is not needed in this subsection. Let χ_L,χ_R be the least one-sided local-band congruences and χ their join, the least locally-inverse congruence. The fresh proof `frontier-20261028-original-one-sided-subdirect-theorem-v1.md`, SHA `5bb5ecb15691e4c953eb6bc877af8c7a98e1444bebca9188da3bce89c42504b5`, establishes `χ_L⊆R_S`, `χ_R⊆L_S`, hence their intersection is contained in H_S. In particular their intersection is **idempotent-separating**, even without purity, because two H-related idempotents are equal.

Suppose e,f∈E(S), e≤f, and e χ f. Project to `T_L=S/χ_L`. This regular quotient has left-regular local idempotent bands. Its least locally-inverse congruence is exactly `χ/χ_L`: a locally-inverse quotient of T_L corresponds to one of S whose kernel contains χ_L, and χ is the least such kernel. On T_L the left one-sided congruence is equality, so its right one-sided congruence equals its least locally-inverse congruence. The Green-containment theorem applied to T_L gives

`χ/χ_L ⊆ L_(T_L)`.

The images of e,f are therefore L-related idempotents; they are also comparable, because the source equations `ef=fe=e` survive every homomorphism. Comparable L-related idempotents are equal, so e χ_L f. Dualizing with `T_R=S/χ_R` gives e χ_R f. Thus e,f lie in χ_L∩χ_R⊆H, and idempotent uniqueness in an H-class gives e=f.

We have proved:

`e≤f, e χ f  ⇒  e=f` for idempotents of every regular locally-r S.    (1)

The proof uses exact quotient universal properties and Green containments; it is not inferred from the finite S68 example.

## 3. A semilattice cocycle suffices for this part

In any band, Green D is its least semilattice congruence and its classes are rectangular bands (named standard band structure input). A distinct comparable pair cannot lie in one rectangular D-class; otherwise the two natural-order equations force identical row and column. The G-action preserves D and descends to the semilattice `B/D`. Thus the coefficient map to B/D gives a physical **semilattice-by-η map of S which separates every distinct comparable idempotent pair** simultaneously. This map is not claimed injective on R/L incomparable pairs.

The canonical seminormal-band source map over η therefore separates every distinct comparable idempotent pair: its universal map into this semilattice host would otherwise identify one. For any other onto pure CS actor r of S, the factor map goes **from η to r**, not in the reverse direction. It is onto and pure. Apply the explicit whole-host embedding in `frontier-20261028-compact-pure-onto-transport-v1.md`, SHA `196c384642f7ba0d3ef6fe1e502d6bc8372ddc53d6138fe235e3bfc99487cb4d`, to the semilattice-by-η host. Comparable source idempotents have the SAME r-actor, so their band coefficients remain distinct and comparable; reflect this new band once more to its maximal semilattice quotient. The resulting semilattice-by-r map still separates every distinct comparable pair. In particular the conclusion holds over the actual syntactic q.

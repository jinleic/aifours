## 1. Full setting and the candidate invariant

- g_(st)=w(s,t)g_t;
- w(s,tr)=w(s,t);

The canonical free V-band has letters X_(u,s), u in G, s in S, and every mixed relation

X_(u,s) X_(u w(s,t),t) = X_(u,st).                 (M)

V is defined by band idempotency and the **local** regular-band identity LOC printed in S3:15–19. No global right-regular identity is substituted.

A nonempty word W=X_(u1,s1)...X_(un,sn) is **coherent** when

at each adjacent join. For a coherent word define the partial value

This is only a proposed invariant for derivations; no total invariant is assumed. Parentheses are irrelevant by associativity. The mixed relations contract any coherent word to X_(u1,s1...sn).

Since i_t=i_(t1) and lambda_t=lambda_(tm), we have

Consequently,

UU is coherent iff g_t p_(lambda_t,i_t)=1_G

The last equivalence uses the actual purity of q in one direction and homomorphic preservation of idempotents in the other.

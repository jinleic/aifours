## 4. Reconstructing the actual syntactic pure CS actor

Candidate1 LF21–39; packet S2 LF15–104.
Define `a τ b` by equality of idempotence truth in EVERY actual/absent two-sided context. The relation is a congruence: multiplication merely shifts a context into another allowed S¹ context.
The empty context makes τ saturate E(S).

For a regular source and onto homomorphism p, if p(a) is idempotent, choose a mutual inverse t of a² and set b=ata. Then

`b²=a t a² t a=a(t a² t)a=ata=b`,

and p(b)=p(a). Thus idempotents lift, and a congruence saturating source idempotents has a PURE quotient. In particular the ACTUAL q:S→S/τ is onto and idempotent-pure, even for infinite S.

WEU gives the cyclic idempotence property used in the quotient proof. If c³=c², then d=c² is idempotent and d=dc=cd; WEU makes c idempotent. If ab is idempotent, `(ba)³=(ba)²`, so ba is idempotent; the reverse is symmetric.
If f²=f and fv=v, then v is idempotent iff vf is: one direction is direct multiplication, the other cyclicity.

For a local orthodox subsemigroup, e idempotent and ea idempotent imply ae idempotent by cyclicity; orthodoxy makes `(ae)(ea)=aea` idempotent, and `aea=(ae)a=a(ea)` makes a idempotent by WEU. This proves the exact local-unitarity step, without assuming E(S) globally closed.

If e≤f are source idempotents, the supplied all-context calculation is valid: forward, xey idempotent gives eyx idempotent; support gives eyxf idempotent, then local unitarity in fSf makes fyxf idempotent, support gives fyx idempotent, and cyclicity gives xfy idempotent. Reverse, xfy gives fyxf idempotent; product with e is idempotent in the SAME local orthodox monoid fSf, then support/cyclicity give xey idempotent.
The conclusion is `e τ f`, not equality of arbitrary contextual products.

For quotient idempotents ε≤φ, lift to source idempotents e,f. Purity makes `fef` idempotent, and it lies below f; the just-proved comparison gives `fef τ f`, hence ε=φ. Thus quotient idempotents are primitive.

## 8. Complete iff in the completely regular class

## 10. Infinite bicyclic example and its genuinely nontrivial shift host

Candidate2 LF41–77.
Let S=N_0² with

`(i,j)(k,l)=(i+max(0,k−j), l+max(0,j−k))`.

Associate to (i,j) the partial bijection on the tail n≥j taking n to n−j+i. Distinct labels give distinct domains/images, and composition in the usual order is exactly the displayed multiplication, so associativity holds without invoking a finite truncation.
The identity is (0,0), and (j,i) is the partial inverse of (i,j); uniqueness of partial inverses gives an inverse monoid.
Idempotents are precisely (a,a), and their product is `(max(a,b),max(a,b))`. Hence they form a semilattice; every actual local corner is inverse and locally r-orthodox.

The difference map q(i,j)=i−j is a homomorphism onto Z and q(i,j)=0 iff (i,j) is idempotent. It is pure.
For book WEU, if e=fa with e,f idempotent, applying q gives q(a)=0, so a is idempotent. This is stronger than needed for the given e=fa=ag implication.

The actual syntactic quotient is this Z, not merely a convenient pure quotient: equal grades give equal idempotence truth after EVERY two-sided context because idempotence is exactly grade zero. Conversely, if a τ b, use a mutual inverse a' as the left context. Since a'a is idempotent, a'b is idempotent too, giving `q(a')+q(b)=0=q(a')+q(a)` and q(b)=q(a).
Thus the syntax and shift actor really match.

For p0=(0,1),q0=(1,0), the actual products are p0q0=1 and q0p0=(1,1)≠1.
For any band homomorphism ρ, write a=ρ(p0),b=ρ(q0),d=ρ(1). d is identity on the image and ab=d. Since a,b are idempotent,

`a=a(ab)=a²b=ab=d`, `b=(ab)b=ab²=ab=d`.

So ρ(q0p0)=ba=d=ρ(1): it identifies two distinct source idempotents. No globally idempotent-separating trivial-action band map exists.

Now take the band of integer upper tails `A_i={z∈Z:z≥i}` with intersection, and action α_u(A_i)=A_(u+i).
It is a semilattice, hence locally regular and in V. Put β(i,j)=A_i.
For EVERY u,i,j,k,l,

`α_uβ(i,j) ∩ α_(u+i−j)β(k,l)`

Thus ALL mixed canonical relations are satisfied and the map extends to an equivariant band homomorphism.

It is an actual nontrivial shift-action host. Distinct idempotents are separated by their tails.

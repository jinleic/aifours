2. Named import (Main-authorized): rough-transfer, kernel side

f259 (parent-dispositioned rough finite-quotient transfer) is the adopted
sibling of the following kernel-side form, which is what Section 3 needs and
which is proved here inline rather than assumed.
Proof. Let D_l=x_lW_l, l=1..8, be pairwise-disjoint actual left cosets of
finite-index subgroups of X x K with all pairwise index gcds <=7. Write
pr:X x K->X, and let M={1} x K, a normal subgroup. By the index-product
argument of (L1) with N replaced by M, for each l
  [X x K:W_l] = a_l b_l,  a_l=[X:pr(W_l)],  b_l=[M:W_l cap M],
and b_l divides the finite order |K|. Suppose pr(D_i) and pr(D_j) meet at
a point s. Pick one t in pr^{-1}(s) and, for l in {i,j}, a point y_l in
D_l cap pr^{-1}(s); then D_l cap pr^{-1}(s) is an actual left coset of
W_l cap M (translated as in (L4); M is normal so the conjugated subgroup
still lies in M and has index b_l). The two slices are disjoint actual
left cosets in M, forcing gcd(b_i,b_j)>=2 by (L2). But b_i,b_j both
divide |K|, which is coprime to 210, so every prime divisor of
gcd(b_i,b_j) exceeds 7, whence gcd(b_i,b_j)>7; and gcd(b_i,b_j) divides
gcd(a_i b_i, a_j b_j)<=7, a contradiction. Hence the eight projections
pr(D_l) are pairwise disjoint actual left cosets in X, of indices a_l
with pairwise gcds dividing the original ones, hence <=7. This
contradicts B_7(X)<=7. □

Only finiteness of K and coprimality of |K| to 210 are used; K need not be
abelian, and the lemma is the kernel-side dual of the f259 pattern, kept as
the single named import of this correction.

3. THEOREM 2' (conditional overlap-density degeneration; rigorous)

HYPOTHESES. G is an arbitrary group, N normal, Q=G/N, B_7(N)<=7,
B_7(Q)<=7, and C_i=g_iU_i, i=1..8, is an actual bad family in G (eight
pairwise-disjoint actual left cosets, all pairwise index gcds in {2..7}).
Let p_1,...,p_8 be ANY distinct primes, each greater than 7 and dividing
neither |G| after finite-core reduction (Section 3, step 0) nor any of the
finitely many indices in sight; the tuple is universally quantified over
the infinitely many such choices, never selected.

STEP 0 (finite core). Replace G by G/R with R=intersection_i core_G(U_i)
as in (L5). The family, all indices n_i,a_i,b_i, both factor bounds
(quotients of N and of Q), the overlap graph and all subset intersections
of the images A_i, and all densities |A_i cap A_j|/|Q| are preserved. So
G, N, Q may be assumed finite for the rest of the proof; the conclusion
transfers back verbatim because it concerns the reduced data.

CONSTRUCTION. With additive notation for the cyclic factors, put

  G' = G x C_{p_1} x ... x C_{p_8},     N' = N x {0} x ... x {0},
  U_i' = U_i x {0}_i x (product of all C_{p_j}, j != i),
  g_i' = (g_i, 0, ..., 0),              C_i' = g_i' U_i'.

N' is normal in G' and N' is isomorphic to N; Q'=G'/N' is isomorphic to
Q x C_{p_1} x ... x C_{p_8}.

CONCLUSIONS.
(a) The C_i' are eight pairwise-disjoint actual left cosets of finite-index
    subgroups of G', with [G':U_i']=n_i p_i; since the p_i are fresh and
    distinct, gcd(n_i p_i, n_j p_j)=gcd(n_i,n_j)<=7. So C_i' is again a
    bad family (in a group that therefore itself fails Sun8; this is the
    point of the construction).
(b) Both factor bounds persist for the new extension: B_7(N')<=7 because
    N' is isomorphic to N, and B_7(Q')<=7 by LEMMA K applied with X=Q and
    K=C_{p_1} x ... x C_{p_8}, whose order is coprime to 210.
(c) Kernel indices and incidence are preserved: [N':U_i' cap N']=b_i for
    every i; writing pi':G'->Q', each image is
    A_i'=pi'(C_i')=A_i x {0}_i x (product of the other C_{p_j});
    consequently A_i' cap A_j' is nonempty exactly when A_i cap A_j is,
    so the overlap graph is unchanged, and for a point q'=(q,x_1..x_8) of
    Q x product C_{p_j} the active label set is
    S'(q')=S(q) cap {i : x_i=0},
    so every old active set is realized (take all x_i=0) and no new one
    arises; in particular max |S'|=max |S| and every point-multiplicity
    datum is preserved. Theorem 1 applied to the new family is consistent:
    it reads sum_{i in S'(q')} 1/b_i<=1 with S'(q') a subset of S(q).
(d) For every pair, |A_i' cap A_j'| = |A_i cap A_j| * (product of p_k,
    k != i,j) and |Q'| = |Q| * (product of all p_k), hence
    |A_i' cap A_j'| / |Q'| = (|A_i cap A_j| / |Q|) / (p_i p_j).
    Since |A_i cap A_j| <= |Q|, the total relative overlap satisfies
    sum_{i<j} |A_i' cap A_j'|/|Q'| <= 28 / min_{i != j}(p_i p_j);
    any universally admissible tuple with all p_i > sqrt(28/delta) makes
    the total strictly below any prescribed delta>0. No tuple is selected:
    the statement is that EVERY admissible tuple this large has the
    property.

EXACT CONSEQUENCE. If ANY bad eight-family exists in ANY group whose two
factors satisfy Sun8, then no uniform positive lower bound on the total
relative overlap density of the quotient images can hold for all such
families: any proposed closure step of the form "every bad family has
quotient-image total overlap density at least delta>0" is false or
vacuous. Equivalently, a measure-type forced-overlap premise can never be
a non-vacuous necessary condition of bad families under the two original
factor bounds.

EXPLICIT NON-CLAIMS. Theorem 2' does NOT refute any scale-free overlap
constraint: the overlap graph, all point multiplicities, all pairwise
index gcds and all kernel indices are preserved by the construction, so
clique-type or multiplicity-type lower bounds are untouched. It does NOT
establish that a bad family exists; everything is conditional on the
hypotheses, and if Sun8 holds universally the consequence is vacuous. It
does NOT prove logical non-implication of any constraint list, since it
conditions on existence rather than exhibiting one. It does not claim
B_7(G')<=7 — on the contrary, G' contains the bad family C_i'.

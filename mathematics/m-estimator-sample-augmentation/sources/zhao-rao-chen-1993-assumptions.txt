2 The Main Result
We make the following assumptions on rho:

(A1) rho is a convex function on R^1 with right and left derivatives psi_+(.) and psi_-(.). Choose psi(.) such that psi_-(u) <= psi(u) <= psi_+(u) for all u in R^1.

(A2) E psi(e_1) = 0 and there exist positive constants c_0, c_1 and Delta such that
(2.1) |E psi(e_1+u)| >= c_0 |u| for |u| < Delta
and
(2.2) E psi^2(e_1+u) <= c_1 < infinity for |u| < Delta.

(A3) S_n^(-1) -> 0 as n -> infinity.

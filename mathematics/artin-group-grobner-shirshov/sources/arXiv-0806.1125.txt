                                                  Abstract. In this paper, we give a Gröbner-Shirshov basis of the braid group
                                                  Bn+1 in the Artin–Garside generators. As results, we obtain a new algorithm
                                                  for getting the Garside normal form, and a new proof that the braid semigroup
                                                  B + n + 1 is the subsemigroup in Bn+1 .

                                             3. Corollaries
   Let S ⊂ khXi. A word u is called S-irreducible if u 6= asb, where s ∈ S, a, b ∈ X ∗ .
Let Irr(S) be the set of all S-irreducible words.
   Recall Shirshov’s Composition lemma ([8], [2], [3]) :
   Let S be a Gröbner-Shirshov set in khXi. If f ∈ ideal(S), then f = asb, s ∈ S.
The converse is also true.
   The Main corollary to this lemma is the following statement:
   Subset S ⊂ khXi is a Gröbner-Shirshov set iff the set Irr(S) is a linear basis
for the algebra khXi/ideal(S) = khX|Si generated by X with defining relations S.

   Let G = sgphX|Si be the semigroup generated by X with defining relations S.
Then S is called a Gröbner-Shirshov basis of G if S is a Gröbner-Shirshov basis of
the semigroup algebra k(G), i.e., S is a Gröbner-Shirshov set in khXi. It follows
from the Main corollary to the Composition lemma that in this case any word u in
X is equal in G to a unique S-irreducible word C(u), called the normal (canonical)
form of u.

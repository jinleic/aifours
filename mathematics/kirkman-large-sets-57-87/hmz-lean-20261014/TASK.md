## Original goal and honest acceptance

The book asks: for every integer t >= 1, with v = 6t+3, construct/prove existence of v-2 pairwise block-disjoint resolvable Steiner triple systems on Fin v. An STS is a family of unordered three-element subsets covering every two-element subset exactly once. A parallel class partitions the points, and a resolution partitions all blocks into parallel classes. An LKTS partitions all triples into v-2 such resolved systems.

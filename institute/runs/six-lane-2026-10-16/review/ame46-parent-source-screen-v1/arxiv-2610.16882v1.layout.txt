                                                              Seeking a pictorial representation of four-party absolutely maximally entangled (AME) states of local size
                                                           six, we independently construct a real AME(4, 6) √  state from a table of signed Pauli matrices. Its two-unitary
                                                           matrix has 60 nonzero entries, equal to 1 or ±1/ 2. A locally equivalent representative yields a genuine
                                                           geomagic square, where every row, column and main diagonal tiles a hexagon and all 36 pieces are pairwise
                                                           non-congruent. Colors, shapes and hatching retain the full tensor, allowing its reconstruction from the picture.

Source: Topalova, S.; Zhelezova, S. Point-Cyclic Resolutions of 2-(64,4,1) Designs. Axioms 15(10):762, 13 October 2026.
https://mdpi-res.com/d_attachment/axioms/axioms-15-00762/article_deploy/axioms-15-00762.pdf (read through functions.read; PDF pp.3–4).

Definition 3 (PDF p.4): "An automorphism of the design D = (V, B) is a permutation of the points of V preserving B."

Paragraph after Definition 3 (PDF p.4): "Two resolutions are isomorphic if there is an automorphism of the underlying design that maps each parallel class of the first resolution to a parallel class of the second one."

Definition 4 (PDF p.4): "A 2-(v,k,λ) design is cyclic if it is invariant under a cyclic automorphism group of order v."

Definition 5 (PDF p.4): "A resolution is point-cyclic if it has an automorphism permuting the points in one cycle. A 2-(v,k,λ) design is cyclically resolvable if it has at least one point-cyclic resolution."

The source has no separately numbered "automorphism of a resolution" definition. The proof reads it as a self-isomorphism of the resolution in the sense of the paragraph above: a design automorphism mapping each parallel class to a parallel class. Per the hostile review, any stronger reading (e.g. fixing every class) strengthens the hypothesis, so the nonexistence result covers it; the source's own usage (types T1–T3 and class orbits of lengths 1, 4, 16 at v = 64) presupposes this permutation action on classes.

original-LF791: ## 15. Lower bound M(7) ≥ 2: the published construction [U14]
original-LF792: 
original-LF793: Source: Ball–Simoens arXiv:2607.12933v2 (pinned PDF 2d60d45f…21afe; layout text df1d8b14…4219), Theorem 6 with
original-LF794: Lemmas 12–14, Example 15 and Corollary 7 (layout lines 57–62, 240–262, 296–336, 352–389). Alternative citation in
original-LF795: the accepted baseline verdict a39911fd…3ff2: Huang–Li Theorem 5.2.
original-LF796: 
original-LF797: Construction at q = 7 (R = F_7, generating character χ(x) = ω^x with ω = e^{2πi/7}):
original-LF798: - Corollary 7 with d = 3, the largest proper divisor of q − 1 = 6: H = {1, 2, 4} is the multiplicative subgroup of
original-LF799:   order 3. Example 15: f0(x) = x for x ∈ H and f0(x) = 0 otherwise. f0 determines q + 1 − |H| = 5 directions, so
original-LF800:   M0 = {2, 6} is the set of undetermined directions (x ↦ f0(x) − mx is a permutation exactly for m ∈ M0).
original-LF801: - As in the proof of Theorem 6, shift by m0 = 2: f(x) = f0(x) − 2x, that is (f(0), …, f(6)) = (0, 6, 5, 1, 3, 4, 2),
original-LF802:   a permutation, and M = M0 − 2 = {0, 4}.
original-LF803: - Ψ(f) has entries ψ_ij = (1/√7) Σ_{x∈F_7} ω^{i f(x) + j x} |x⟩ (i, j ∈ F_7). It is a QLS (Lemma 12, since f is a
original-LF804:   permutation) and non-classical (Lemma 13, since f is not additive plus a constant: f(0) = 0 and f(1) = 6 would
original-LF805:   force f(3) = 18 = 4, but f(3) = 1).
original-LF806: - Φ(4) has entries φ_ij = |4i + j⟩. It is a classical QLS, and Ψ(f) ⊥ Φ(4) by Lemma 14, because 4 ∈ F_7^* and
original-LF807:   x ↦ f(x) − 4x, that is (0, 2, 4, 3, 1, 5, 6), is a permutation.
original-LF808: - So {Ψ(f), Φ(4)} is a non-classical set of 2 MOQLS(7), and M(7) ≥ 2.

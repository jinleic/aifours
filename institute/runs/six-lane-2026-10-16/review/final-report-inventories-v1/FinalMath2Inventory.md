### Notation and verification layers

Unless stated otherwise, \(p\ge5\) is prime and the group is \(D_{2p}\).

- An **avoider** has length \(4p-2\) and no identity \(2p\)-subword in increasing original index order. The ordered-Olson conclusions concern words of length **\(4p-1\)**.
- \(F,R\): numbers of reflections and rotations. \(o=o_F\): odd blocks in the **maximal cyclic constant reflection-block decomposition**, **not** the number of reflection runs.
- \(H=(F-o)/2\), \(\kappa=p-1-H\). A terminal all-odd normal form has \(k\le o\), \(k\equiv o\pmod2\), and \(\widehat\kappa=\kappa(w)-(o-k)/2\).
- **RGR**: rotations lie in one nonzero pair \(\{\rho_c,\rho_{-c}\}\), and every maximal cyclic reflection run is constant; different runs may have different values.
- **Inherited** retains all five conditions: \(R\le3p-2\); \(F+\sum\lfloor r_i/2\rfloor\le3p-2\); \(R+H\le3p-2\); \(H\le p-1\); \(a_i,b_i\le p-1\). It is not shorthand for an arbitrary avoider.
- **W** below: parent-accepted written mathematics with scoped imports, Codex `gpt-6.1-sol` / Claude `claude-opus-5-5` reviews and the parent checks described in the cited verdict. **H**: historical reviewed partial result, with its narrower recorded review coverage. **F**: finite computer-assisted result.

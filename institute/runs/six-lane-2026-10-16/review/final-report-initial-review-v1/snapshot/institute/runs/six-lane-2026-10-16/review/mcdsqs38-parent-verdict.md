# mcDSQS(38): parent verdict

## Result

**An mcDSQS(38) exists.** The pinned certificate gives a Steiner quadruple system on 38 points (2,109 blocks; every one of the 8,436 three-subsets in exactly one block) and, at each of its 38 points, an explicit partition of the 222 derived triples into 19 partial parallel classes (class sizes 12^18 6 at every point).

- [Certificate](mcdsqs38-parent-inputs-v1/mcdsqs38_g342_s0.txt): SHA-256 `973f93e4646e48df64b70b3a64811afcdc87b10a1767222f54ffe65d3a303f1c`.
- Definitions (Tan–Zhou, arXiv:2503.11934v1, lines 89–130 of the [parent-read text](../../../../mathematics/kirkman-large-sets-57-87/sources/tan-zhou-2503.11934.txt)): an r-colouring partitions a design's blocks into r partial parallel classes; χ′(v) is the minimum over **all** STS(v); an mcDSQS(v) is an SQS(v) whose derived design at every point admits a χ′(v−1)-colouring.
- **Minimality is self-contained.** Every STS(37) has 222 triples and every partial parallel class on 37 points has at most 12, so every colouring needs at least ⌈222/12⌉ = 19 colours. The certificate's colourings attain 19, so χ′(37) = 19 and every derived design is minimum colourable. This agrees with, but does not depend on, Tan–Zhou (1.1).
- Primary open statement, Tan–Zhou §4(1): "Find more examples of mcDSQS(v)s, for example, an mcDSQS(38) and an RDSQS(52) (the smallest unknown mcDSQS(6n + 2)s and the smallest unknown RDSQS(6n + 4)s, respectively)." [Frozen contract](../screening/math-1-mcdsqs38-contract.json).

## Priority and significance

[Bounded priority audit](mcdsqs38-priority-audit.md): no earlier mcDSQS(38) and no impossibility proof found. **The underlying SQS(38) is already public**: the parent confirmed from the dataset bytes that the certificate's block set is record 143 of the Kiermaier–Krčadinac–Wassermann SQS(38) dataset (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.17153238, 2025-09-18) under an explicit point map ([receipt](mcdsqs38-dataset-isomorphism-v1/parent-result.json)). That dataset and its paper make no colouring claims. The contribution is the certified 19-colourings of all 38 derived designs of an already-published design.

Consequences from stated primary theorems:
- the first example asked for in Tan–Zhou §4(1);
- the n = 38 case of Shi–Xia–Krotov Conjecture 2 (arXiv:2212.00048v2, p.21), whose general statement stays open;
- q′₀(3,4,38) = 20 by Tan–Zhou Corollary 1.4(1) (hypotheses n ≡ 2 mod 6, n ≥ 20 hold).

No new infinite family or LKTS consequence follows through the source's recursive constructions [INFERENCE, audit derivation].

## Evidence-handling limits

The lane's original multi-sample producer deleted intermediate KM/colouring CNFs and mutated control files in several runs, and overwrote two buggy control certificates; this is recorded in the [incident record](../../../../mathematics/evidence/six-lane-20261016/mcdsqs-twisted-family/sources/incident-deletion-20261017.md). The pinned certificate, its sample-0 producer inputs and the parent's guarded replays are unaffected; certificate validity does not imply that the lost intermediates survived.

# gcDCQS(20⁴:0) and its mcDSQS family — parent verdict

## Result and exact scope

**A gcDCQS(20⁴:0) exists.** Two complete certificates give a candelabra quadruple system on 80 points in four groups of 20, empty stem, with good colourings of every derived design. This resolves the v = 20 component of Tan–Zhou's explicit Section 4(2) question; **v = 32 remains open here**. [Frozen target](../screening/math-1-gcdcqs20-contract.json).

Consequently, by the published **Corollary 2.6**, **mcDSQS(5·4ⁿ) exists for every integer n ≥ 3**. The imported inputs and their hypotheses are checked below. **Construction 2.1 separately gives mcDSQS(80)**; the parent has also directly verified and independently rebuilt a concrete 80-point certificate. Formal verification of these finite objects is in progress, not claimed by this verdict.

## Mathematical bridge and imported inputs

[Primary source](../../../../mathematics/kirkman-large-sets-57-87/sources/tan-zhou-2503.11934.txt), Tan–Zhou arXiv:2503.11934v1:

1. **Definitions, lines 211–220 and 281–288.** Here w = 79 and h = 19, so r = χ′(79) = 40 and s = 40 − (79 − 19)/2 = 10. Partial classes need not be full partitions of the point set. More than 10 hole-free classes is impossible: each of the 19 hole points appears in 30 triples and in at most one triple of each class, so 570 ≤ 19(40 − s).
2. **Known input mcDSQS(20), Lemma 3.4.** Phelps' cyclic SQS(20) II.A.1 is shown minimum-colourable in Shi–Xia–Krotov, JCTA 200 (2023) 105790, Proposition 3 (arXiv:2212.00048v2). Its source data are independently reconstructed and checked by the parent ([receipt](mcdsqs80-parent-fill-v1/parent-result.json)).
3. **Corollary 2.6, lines 684–693.** m = 20 ≡ 8 (mod 12); the required gcDCQS(20⁴:0) is now supplied. Theorem 2.5 gives 2-RSQS*(4ᵗ) for every t ≥ 2 and RDTD(3,4,20): gcd(20,4) = 4 ≠ 2 and gcd(20,18) = 2 ≠ 3. Construction 2.4 has 4m + s = 80 ≡ 2 (mod 6). These published constructions yield mcDSQS(20·4ᵗ), t ≥ 2, exactly mcDSQS(5·4ⁿ), n ≥ 3.
4. **Order 80 separately, Construction 2.1, lines 292–335.** An empty-hole gcDSQS(20,0) is just an mcDSQS(20). Fill all four groups. At x, merge the 10 hole-free classes with the 10 classes of its filled STS(19), retaining the other 30. Supports are disjoint. This gives 40 classes of 1027 triples; every STS(79) requires at least ⌈1027/26⌉ = 40, so they are minimum colourings.

The parent independent filling regenerates every non-comment line of the [explicit 80-point certificate](mcdsqs80-parent-inputs-v1/mcdsqs80_c21_A.txt), SHA256 `49f500c5eedd9a79553e1228d0232e203a749bbda04e3a891c52778dbe9d44a3`. Three independently authored checker routes accept it: the parent definition-first checker (20,540 blocks, all 82,160 triples uniquely covered, all 80 derived STS(79) 40-coloured, profile 26³⁷ 25 24 16; [receipt](mcdsqs80-parent-check-v1/parent-result.json)); the earlier Claude-written generic mcDSQS checker, parent-executed with and without optimization plus wrong-digest, wrong-order and four intended corruptions ([receipt](mcdsqs80-claude-checker-run-v1/parent-run-result.json)); and Codex CLI `gpt-6.1-sol`'s own in-memory verifier, RESULT: VALID with six corruption controls and the counting proof χ′(79) = 40 ([note](mcdsqs80-codex-verify-v1.note.md)). Its Lean certification is separate work, not implied here.

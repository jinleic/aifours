# SW(25,16): parent verdict — verified witness; an earlier public claim exists

## Result

An explicit **symmetric weighing matrix SW(25,16)** is verified: a 25 × 25 matrix W over {−1,0,1} with W = Wᵀ and W·Wᵀ = 16·I. Witness [sw25-16-witness-v1.sw](sw25-parent-inputs-v1/sw25-16-witness-v1.sw), 1459 bytes, SHA-256 `bfadf44596bc5138106432690bf507a8ff9c36cd3c40eb8eeb08195a9b2d4ab0`. It is a 5 × 5 array of 5 × 5 circulants: the unsigned permutation with five 5-cycles is an automorphism, and its block-sum quotient B is an integer symmetric 5 × 5 matrix with B² = 16·I. Trace 20 (20 diagonal entries +1, five 0).

**This does not meet the info-2 gate.** The [frozen contract](../screening/info-2-sw25-16-contract.json) cited Rosin's May 2025 statement that n ∈ {25, 27, 29} were the remaining open weight-16 orders. A post-result priority screen ([report](sw25-priority-screen-v1.md)) found a public preprint, which the parent read directly in its [DataCite record](sw25-prior-claim-datacite-v1.json): Griffin Long, *Symmetric weighing matrices SW(25,16), SW(27,16) and SW(29,16)*, Zenodo, doi:10.5281/zenodo.23404962, issued 2026-10-15 and registered 2026-10-15T20:42:34Z, about 48 hours before our solve ended (2026-10-17 13:48:53 PDT). Its abstract states that explicit certificates for all three orders were found by SAT with a prescribed signed-permutation automorphism. The Zenodo files returned HTTP 403, so the parent did not verify Long's matrices or compare them with ours. Existence of SW(25,16) was therefore already publicly claimed when this lane started; the pre-freeze screen missed the two-day-old record. The same preprint also covers the lane's alternates SW(27,16) and SW(29,16).

## Status

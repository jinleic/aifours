# AME(4,6) support lower bound s ≥ 46: parent verdict

## Result

**Every AME(4,6) state, with arbitrary complex amplitudes, has at least 46 non-zero coefficients in every product orthonormal basis.** This improves the bound s ≥ 42 accepted earlier in this run ([record](ame46-lower42-parent-verdict.md)). Pandya (arXiv:2610.16673v1) states 38 ≤ s_min ≤ 60 and calls the gap open; with the accepted bound, the range is 46..60 (the upper bound 60 is Pandya's, not re-reviewed here). **This is partial progress; it does not decide the frozen phys-2 gate** (minimum support 59 versus 60).

# Protocol amendment 3: residual-symmetry lex leaders for the frozen K3 cubes

To be frozen before the new inputs or searches. This is a symmetry-only strengthening after A/C311 ended UNKNOWN
at its 1800-second cap. A/C321 is UNSAT exploratory and A/C411 is in the existing sequential chunk. No result is
inferred from an UNKNOWN, and no base encoder, vector definition or prior evidence is changed.

## 1. Safe generators preserving each cube
Use the same ordering of all 2646 non-collinear Gram predicates as the frozen encoders. Transform a predicate by
applying the indicated row/column permutation to both of its cells and the square permutation to its square label.
All generators fix row 0, p=(1,0), and the cube's full 18-literal assignment.

For every cube, include the adjacent swaps of rows (2,3), (3,4), (4,5), (5,6).

- C311: adjacent swaps of columns (1,2), (2,3), and the combined operation swapping columns 4/5 AND square labels
  2/3. Column 6 is fixed.
- C321: adjacent swaps of columns (1,2), (2,3), and swap columns 4/5. No square exchange.
- C411: adjacent swaps of columns (1,2), (2,3), (3,4), and the combined operation swapping columns 5/6 AND square
  labels 2/3.

These permutations preserve rows/columns as ONB classes, all G1-G5 implications, cube units, and G6 (which is
already implied by G1/G2 plus the cube). Encoder auxiliaries may be reconstructed after a Gram pattern is
transformed; they need not use the same internal variable permutation.

## 2. Lex constraints and coverage
Let X be the ordered Boolean vector of all Gram predicates and T one of these generators. Append X <=lex T(X),
with 0 < 1, for each generator. Use prefix-equality auxiliaries and the standard implication:

    equal_so_far -> (NOT X_i OR T(X)_i),

with equality of the next prefix forced whenever the current prefix is equal and X_i=T(X)_i. Prefixes need not be
forced true after the first unequal position. Positions that are the same literal on both sides are skipped.

Coverage proof: the finite group H generated by the listed operations preserves the frozen cube. Any actual
configuration's Gram pattern has a lexicographically minimal representative in its H-orbit. That representative
satisfies X <=lex T(X) for every T in H, hence for all the listed generators simultaneously. Therefore these lex
constraints do not discard every representative of any actual configuration. They are not an assumption that all
solutions are symmetric, nor a requirement that the Gram predicates are invariant under T.

## 3. Controls, records, stop rules
Before generating production inputs, a finite control will:
- verify every generator is a bijection of the 2646 predicates and preserves all 18 signed cube units;
- exhaust truth tables of comparator lengths 1 through 4, including all prefix-auxiliary choices, and verify that
  existence of auxiliary values is exactly the Boolean lex comparison.

Leave the base/cube CNFs untouched and append only the lex clauses in a new file, with a new DIMACS header.
Bind the original cube CNF SHA, metadata, generator description, new CNF SHA and wrapper SHA. The first new screen
is A/C311 only, a single native solver with <=1800-second cap, no certificate-pipeline overlap. Apply this
representation to another cube only if it is still UNKNOWN. A SAT is only a support pattern; an UNSAT must later
be independently regenerated and DRAT-checked. No new mathematical exclusion is claimed by this amendment.

## Theorem accepted
Let X be any continuous matrix-exponential law of order at most 3: F(t) = Pr(X < t) = 1 − α e^(At) 𝟙 with α real 1×n, A real n×n, n ≤ 3, density f(t) = −α A e^(At) 𝟙 ≥ 0 on [0, ∞), and no sign structure on α or A (HSTZ 2016 Definitions 1–2). Then

  SCV(X) = m0 m2 / m1² − 1 ≥ v_cos := min over ω > 0, φ ∈ ℝ of SCV(e^(−t)(1 − cos(ωt − φ))),

with equality attained in the equal-damping family D1, and SCV(X) > v_cos strictly for every law in a D2 cone (real pole strictly dominant over the complex pair). Real-exponent laws have SCV ≥ Δ3 > 553/2000. The constant is the exact cosine-family minimum, not the rounded 0.200902; the proof uses only the exact rational witness v_cos ≤ 773/3844 and T = 11/50, never a decimal value of v_cos.

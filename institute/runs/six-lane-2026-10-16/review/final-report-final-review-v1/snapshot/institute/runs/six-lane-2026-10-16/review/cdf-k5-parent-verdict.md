# Cyclic (v,5,1) difference families: parent verdict

## Results

**Cyclic (321,5,1), (501,5,1), (621,5,1), (681,5,1), (801,5,1) and (901,5,1) difference families exist.** Each certificate lists (v−1)/20 base blocks of five residues mod v. Their v−1 ordered differences cover every nonzero residue mod v exactly once. Equivalent forms: a perfect (v,5,1) optical orthogonal code, and a strictly cyclic Steiner system S(2,5,v).

These are exactly the six possible exceptions left by Abel–Costa–Finizio 2004 Theorem 14. **Combined with that published theorem (whose other cases, including its prime and v=21 cases, were not re-verified here), a cyclic (v,5,1) difference family exists for every v ≡ 1 (mod 20) with 21 ≤ v ≤ 1001.** Equivalently, the "except possibly t = 16, 25, 31, 34, 40, 45" list restated in 2025–2026 primary sources is now empty.

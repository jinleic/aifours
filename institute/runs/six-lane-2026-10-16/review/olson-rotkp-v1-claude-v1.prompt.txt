24: A selection with an even number of reflections is given by (i) z_i := (-1)^(number of selected reflections
25: cyclically before R_i, counted from a fixed cut point at the end of S_k), (ii) c_i := number of selected letters of

27: R_i and R_(i+1)], (iii) any sub-multiset of each X_i.  Conversely each such datum with 0 <= c_i <= l_i is realised
28: (choose any c_i letters of the constant block S_i; any chosen rotations).  Because S_i is constant, its c_i selected
29: letters multiply to sigma_(y_i) (c_i odd) or 1 (c_i even); so the ordered product, read from the cut, is
30:     rho_V,  V = sum_i z_i (sum of chosen values in X_i) + sum_{i : f_i = 1} z_i y_i      (mod p),
31: by induction along the word (a rotation rho_x in a zone of sign z contributes z x; a reflection sigma_y entered in
32: a zone of sign z contributes z y and flips the zone sign; the even total makes the product a rotation).

44:       >= p + (2p - k - 1) >= 2p.  A contradiction.

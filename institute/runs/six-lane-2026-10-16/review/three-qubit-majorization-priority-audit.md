> Parent correction, 2026-10-16 PT: the original scout report below is preserved. Its inference that the standard depolarizing Weyl representation satisfies Amosov's condition (8) **literally** is superseded: equal cosets force equal within-coset weights, which that representation fails unless `p=1`. The scout acknowledged this correction. See the [canonical parent assessment](three-qubit-majorization-parent-verdict.md#amosov-prior-work-applicability-check) and [exact countercheck](amosov-composition-parent-check.json). This changes applicability, not the scout's modest-extension calibration.

I found no source dated before 2026-10-16 that states the n=3 qubit result. My verdict is a **modest extension**: an elementary corollary of Song–Chen Prop. 3 plus a one-line symmetry. The n=2 case for p ≤ 1 already follows from published results.

Four points change how the result should be framed:
- **The conjecture itself predates Song–Wang–Tomamichel–Fang (SWTF).** Alhejji–Knill (arXiv v2, 2023-08-07; CMP 2024) already stated it for p ≤ 1 and any dimension d, and observed that the depolarizing output has the compatible-marginal structure. SWTF do not cite them, Song–Chen, or Alhejji.
- **n=3 with p ≤ 1 is essentially Song–Chen's own proof applied to the binomial weights.** Prop. 3 handles the pair block. The other blocks are elementary, and adding the blocks uses the same Ky Fan step Song–Chen use in their eq. (35).
- **The p > 1 reflection has partial prior art.** The one-copy relation between D_p and D_{2-p} appears in Rosati–Giovannetti (2018). The tensorized spin-flip identity is Song–Chen eqs. (29)–(31). I found no source that uses it to reduce the majorization conjecture for p > 1.
- **One 2024 preprint claims something much stronger, with a broken proof.** Amosov (arXiv:2401.05104) claims tensor-product output majorization for a class of Weyl channels. Read literally, the class seems to include depolarizing channels with p ≤ 1, for every n and d. The proof rests on a composition lemma (Prop. 1) that is false in general; I have a two-line counterexample. So it does not establish the result, but a referee could raise it.

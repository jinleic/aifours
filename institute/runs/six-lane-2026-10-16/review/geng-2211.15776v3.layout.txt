                                                                       Families of Perfect Tensors
                                                                                     Runshi Geng




arXiv:2211.15776v3 [math.AG] 6 Dec 2022

                                                                                        Abstract
                                               Perfect tensors are the tensors corresponding to the absolutely maximally entangled states,
                                               a special type of quantum states of interest in quantum information theory. We establish a
                                               method to compute parameterized families of perfect tensors in (Cd )⊗4 using exponential maps
                                               from Lie theory. With this method, we find explicit examples of non-classical perfect tensors
                                               in (C3 )⊗4 . In particular, we answer an open question posted by Życzkowski et al.



                                          1    Introduction
                                          The entanglement of quantum states plays a central role in quantum information theory. For
                                          bipartite states, the entanglement is usually measured by the von Neumann entropies of the partial
                                          traces of the states [2], but its generalization for multipartite states is more complicated. One is
                                          interested in an extremal class of multipartite states: the absolutely maximally entangled (AME)
                                          states – the pure states that are maximally entangled for every bipartitions of the system. The
                                          AME states are closely related to quantum secret sharing [7], quantum error-correcting [8] and
                                          quantum repeaters [1].
                                          The existence of AME states in (Cd )⊗n , denoted as AME(n, d), is an open-problem [11]. For n = 2
                                          and 3, the Bell and GHZ states are AME states. For n = 4, it was proved that AME(4, 2) does not
                                          exist [3, 5], and AME(4, d) can be obtained by orthogonal Latin squares (also known as Graeco-
                                          Latin squares) of order d, denoted as OLS(d) [5]. OLS(d)’s have been constructed for all d > 2
                                          except d = 6 [6, 4], which implies the existence of AME(4, d) for such d. Although OLS(6) was
                                          proved not existing [10], an explicit example of AME(4,6) is found in [9].
                                          The tensors corresponding to the AME states are perfect tensors [8]. An open question is asked
                                          in [12]: for d = 3, 4 and 5, are there nonzero perfect tensors in (Cd )⊗4 that are non-classical, i.e.,
                                          not locally equivalent to a solution corresponding to an OLS(d)? In this paper, we establish a
                                          method with the exponential maps from Lie theory to find parameterized families of perfect tensors
                                          in (Cd )⊗4 for d > 2 and d 6= 6. Particularly we find explicitly parameterized families of perfect
                                          tensors in (C3 )⊗4 and we proved that there are 4-dimensional subsets in those families consisting
                                          of non-classical perfect tensors.

2.1    The Varieties of Perfect Tensors
Let H := H1 ⊗ · · · ⊗ Hn , where each Hi is a d-dimensional complex Hilbert space with the inner
product hΦ|Ψi := Φ† Ψ.
Write [n] := {1, 2, · · · , n}. A bipartition of [n] is a pair of non-empty subsets I, J ⊂ [n] such that
                                                                    ∗
I ∪ J = [n] and I ∩ J = ∅. Identifying HI with its Ndual space HI , anyN  tensor Φ ∈ H can be regarded
as a linear map ΦI,J : HI → HJ , where HI := i∈I Hi and HJ := j∈J Hj .
Definition 1 ([8]). A tensor Φ ∈ H is called a perfect tensor if for every bipartition (I, J) of [n]
with |I| ≤ |J|, ΦI,J is proportional to an isometry.

For even n, let P(n, d) be the set of tensors Φ ∈ H such that for every bipartition (I, J) of [n] with
|I| = |J| = n2 , ΦI,J is unitary.
Proposition 2 ([8]). When n is even, Φ ∈ H is perfect if and only if it is proportional to a tensor
in P(n, d).

That is, the set of all perfect tensors in H is just the cone over the real algebraic variety P(n, d),
which we will mainly study for the rest of this paper.

23:                                           1 Introduction
24: 
25:                                                Let H be any subgroup of a (multiplicative) group G. A left coset of H has the form
26:                                           aH = {ah : h ∈ H} with a ∈ G. [G : H], the index of H in G, is the cardinality of the
27:                                           set G/H = {aH : a ∈ G}. If k = [G : H] < ∞, then we can partition G into k distinct
28:                                           left cosets of H in G.
29:                                                In 2004, Zhi-Wei Sun proposed the following conjecture on disjoint cosets.
30: 
31:                                           Sun’s Conjecture ( [4, Conjecture 1.2]). Let a1 G1 , . . . , ak Gk (k > 1) be finitely many
32:                                           pairwise disjoint left cosets in a group G with [G : Gi ] < ∞ for all i = 1, . . . , k. Then,
33:                                           for some 1 ≤ i < j ≤ k, the greatest common divisor ([G : Gi ], [G : Gj ]) of [G : Gi ] and
34:                                           [G : Gj ] is at least k.
35: 
36:                                               By [4, Remark 2.2] and [4, Remark 1.5(b)], Sun’s conjecture holds when k = 2 or G
37:                                           is a p-group with p a prime. K. O’Bryant [1] proved Sun’s conjecture in the special case
38:                                           G = Z and k ≤ 20.
39:                                               In this paper we confirm Sun’s conjecture for k ∈ {3, 4}.
40: 
41:                                           Theorem 1.1. Let a1 G1 , . . . , ak Gk (k ∈ {3, 4}) be pairwise disjoint left cosets in a group
42:                                           G with [G : Gi ] < ∞ for all i = 1, . . . , k. Then ([G : Gi ], [G : Gj ]) ≥ k for some
43:                                           1 ≤ i < j ≤ k.

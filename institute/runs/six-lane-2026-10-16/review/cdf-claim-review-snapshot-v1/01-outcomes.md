## Headline

**Mathematics met the contract's breakthrough gate.** Large sets of Kirkman triple systems of orders **57** and **87** were constructed explicitly and verified independently ([verdict](review/lkts-57-87-parent-verdict.md)). Wang's 2026 survey of known constructions ([arXiv:2607.21521v1 §4](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.21521v1#S4)) named these as the only admissible orders below 200 still possibly without an LKTS. Combined with that survey, **no admissible order below 200 remains open**. That combined statement inherits the survey's premises at uneven evidence levels; order 147 is the weakest.

The universal question in the book (an LKTS of every admissible order) remains **open**. Priority is **unresolved**: the bounded literature search found no earlier construction, which does not prove novelty. There has been no human peer review or publication. Both finite cases now have **reviewed Lean 4.34.1 existence proofs**. Parent raw-data rebinding, rebuild, statement audit, axiom audit and fresh-kernel replay passed; a cross-vendor Claude semantic review accepted the formalization ([verdict](review/lkts-57-87-parent-verdict.md#finite-lean-existence-proofs)). This is one Lean kernel implementation, not an independent kernel or Comparator.

The contract defines a breakthrough as a complete solution of an explicitly stated open target. It requires independent parent verification, cross-vendor review and a primary-source comparison. LKTS(57) was the frozen target of the math-1 slot, and LKTS(87) is the other order Wang names. Both certificates were accepted by three separately written verifiers: the parent's stdlib checker, cross-vendor Codex `gpt-6.1-sol` running its own in-memory code, and a Claude-written program executed by the parent. Their corruption controls differ. The parent and Claude-written checkers each rejected the same eight targeted mutations per order. Codex ran its own controls: 22 malformed-line parser tests on the 57 file, and five corruptions of the 87 file (point swap, duplicated system, missing class, repeated point, unrecognized line), each rejected for its intended reason. Compact seeds regenerate both certificates byte for byte. The method is an established design-theory technique: prescribed automorphism groups, Kramer–Mesner exact cover, and resolution SAT. Its contribution is the specific groups and orbit-forcing lemmas, not a new general method.

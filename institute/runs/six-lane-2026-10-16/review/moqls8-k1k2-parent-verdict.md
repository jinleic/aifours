## Proof outline

1. **W8 and Theorem A8.** In row-0 standard form the five supports at a cell are disjoint and avoid the column coordinate, so weights are ≤ 3. If all weights are ≤ 2, Lemma 10 makes the other four squares classical; the six resulting line classes form a net, a bipartite 6-regular graph on the 16 cells carrying the symbols a, b is connected, and the weight-1 cell (0,a) forces every weight-2 support class to be empty. So all weights are 1.
2. **Lemma K1-AG.** If a QLS is an orthonormal basis on every line of six direction classes of AG(2,8), its supports along each line of a seventh net direction are pairwise disjoint (GF(8) subspace argument: |rV ∩ r′V| = 1), hence singletons: the square is classical. Egan–Wanless Table 6 gives one paratopy class of 4-MOLS(8), so every K1 configuration is of this form; K1 = ∅.
3. **K2.** In a K2 set both quantum squares have a weight-3 entry in the frame of every line (otherwise A8 or K1 applies after regridding). Transport to one of the 39 Egan–Wanless 3-MOLS(8) representatives and pass to row-0 standard form; necessary support conditions (P0)–(P4) give a fixed CNF per representative. All 39 × 2 CNFs (two independently written encoders) are UNSAT with DRAT proofs.

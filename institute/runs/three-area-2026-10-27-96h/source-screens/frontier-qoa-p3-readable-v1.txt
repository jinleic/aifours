Definition 4.6.
Let $t\geq 3$ . A set of $d^{3}$ $t$ -partite pure quantum states $|\psi_{i,j,k}\rangle\in(\mathbb{C}^{d})^{\otimes t}$ arranged as
[source diagram id=S4.Ex21.m1.g1 src=2111.04055v1/cube2.png; not rendered here]
forms a set of generalized mutually orthogonal quantum Latin cubes of dimension $d$ , denoted by $t$ -GMOQLC $(d)$ , if the following properties hold:
1.
the $d^{3}$ states $\{|\psi_{i,j,k}\rangle\}$ are orthogonal, i.e.
(15)
$\langle\psi_{i,j,k}|\psi_{i^{\prime},j^{\prime},k^{\prime}}\rangle=\delta_{ii^{\prime}}\delta_{jj^{\prime}}\delta_{kk^{\prime}}.$
2.
(16)
$\sum^{d-1}_{i=0}Tr_{l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{t-1}}|\psi_{i,j,k}\rangle\langle\psi_{i,j^{\prime},k^{\prime}}|=\delta_{jj^{\prime}}\delta_{kk^{\prime}}\mathbb{I}_{d},$
(17)
$\sum^{d-1}_{j=0}Tr_{l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{t-1}}|\psi_{i,j,k}\rangle\langle\psi_{i^{\prime},j,k^{\prime}}|=\delta_{ii^{\prime}}\delta_{kk^{\prime}}\mathbb{I}_{d},$
(18)
$\sum^{d-1}_{k=0}Tr_{l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{t-1}}|\psi_{i,j,k}\rangle\langle\psi_{i^{\prime},j^{\prime},k}|=\delta_{ii^{\prime}}\delta_{jj^{\prime}}\mathbb{I}_{d},$
for every subset $\{l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{t-1}\}$ of $t-1$ parties.
3.
(19)
$\sum^{d-1}_{i,j=0}Tr_{l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{t-2}}|\psi_{i,j,k}\rangle\langle\psi_{i,j,k^{\prime}}|=\delta_{kk^{\prime}}\mathbb{I}_{d^{2}},$
(20)
$\sum^{d-1}_{j,k=0}Tr_{l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{t-2}}|\psi_{i,j,k}\rangle\langle\psi_{i^{\prime},j,k}|=\delta_{ii^{\prime}}\mathbb{I}_{d^{2}},$
(21)
$\sum^{d-1}_{i,k=0}Tr_{l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{t-2}}|\psi_{i,j,k}\rangle\langle\psi_{i,j^{\prime},k}|=\delta_{jj^{\prime}}\mathbb{I}_{d^{2}},$
for every subset $\{l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{t-2}\}$ of $t-2$ parties.
4.
(22)
$\sum^{d-1}_{i,j,k=0}Tr_{l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{t-3}}|\psi_{i,j,k}\rangle\langle\psi_{i,j,k}|=\mathbb{I}_{d^{3}}.$
for every subset $\{l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{t-3}\}$ of $t-3$ parties.
Remark 4.7.
If a $t$ -GMOQLC $(d)$ is composed of fully separable states, i.e. $|\psi^{A_{1}A_{2}\cdots A_{t}}_{i,j,k}\rangle\\
=|\psi^{A_{1}}_{i,j,k}\rangle\otimes|\psi^{A_{2}}_{i,j,k}\rangle\otimes\cdots\otimes|\psi^{A_{t}}_{i,j,k}\rangle$ for every $i,j,k\in[d]$ , then the $t$ -GMOQLC $(d)$ is just a $t$ -MOQLC $(d)$ . In fact, Property (4) implies Property (1); Property (2) implies that the arrangement $\{|\psi^{A_{s}}_{i,j,k}\rangle\}$ is a QLC, for every $s,1\leq s\leq t$ , according to Definition 3.3; Property (3) implies that for each corresponding planes of any two cubes from $|\psi^{A_{1}}_{i,j,k}\rangle,|\psi^{A_{2}}_{i,j,k}\rangle,\dots,|\psi^{A_{t}}_{i,j,k}\rangle$ form a pair of orthogonal quantum Latin square, which is consistent with Property (2) of Definition 3.6; and Property (4) implies that the “superimposed” elements of any three cubes form an orthonormal basis of $(\mathbb{C}^{d})^{\otimes 3}$ , which is consistent with Property (1) of Definition 3.6.
Proposition 4.8.
A QOA $(d^{3},t+3,d,3)$ generates a $t$ -GMOQLC $(d)$ , and vice versa.

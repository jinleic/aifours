Appendix H Proof of Proposition 4.8
${\bf Proof:\ }$ Suppose that $|\Phi\rangle$ is the sum of the $d^{3}$ states in the QOA $(d^{3},t+3,d,3)$ . Since $|\Phi\rangle$ can produce a 3-uniform state, we choose the first three subsystems, namely, $i$ , $j$ , $k$ , then
(33)
$|\Phi\rangle=\sum^{d-1}_{i,j,k=0}|ijk\rangle\otimes|\Phi_{i,j,k}\rangle.$
Actually, $\{|\Phi_{i,j,k}\rangle\}$ is the set of arrangements of $t$ -GMOQLC $(d)$ , where $i,j,k\in[d]$ are the indexes of the rows, columns and files of the $t$ -GMOQLC $(d)$ .
To show this, we take an arbitrary subset $S\subseteq\{1,2,\dots,t+3\}$ with $|S|=3$ . Consider the following four cases: (1) $|S\cap\{1,2,3\}|=3$ ; (2) $|S\cap\{1,2,3\}|=2$ ; (3) $|S\cap\{1,2,3\}|=1$ ; (4) $|S\cap\{1,2,3\}|=0$ .
Case 1. When $|S\cap\{1,2,3\}|=3$ , we have
$\displaystyle\rho_{S}$
$\displaystyle=Tr_{4,5,\dots,t+3}\sum_{i,j,k,i^{\prime},j^{\prime},k^{\prime}\in[d]}|ijk\rangle\otimes|\Phi_{i,j,k}\rangle\langle i^{\prime}j^{\prime}k^{\prime}|\otimes\langle\Phi_{i^{\prime},j^{\prime},k^{\prime}}|$
$\displaystyle=\sum_{i,j,k,i^{\prime},j^{\prime},k^{\prime}\in[d]}|ijk\rangle\langle i^{\prime}j^{\prime}k^{\prime}|\langle\Phi_{i^{\prime},j^{\prime},k^{\prime}}|\Phi_{i,j,k}\rangle.$
So, $\rho_{S}=\sum\limits_{i,j,k,i^{\prime},j^{\prime},k^{\prime}\in[d]}|ijk\rangle\langle i^{\prime}j^{\prime}k^{\prime}|\langle\Phi_{i^{\prime},j^{\prime},k^{\prime}}|\Phi_{i,j,k}\rangle=\mathbb{I}_{d^{3}}$ if and only if Eq. (15) holds.
Case 2. When $|S\cap\{1,2,3\}|=2$ . Suppose $S\cap\{1,2,3\}=\{1,2\}$ , and $\{l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{t-1}\}\\
\cap\{1,2,3\}=\emptyset$ , then we have
$\displaystyle\rho_{S}$
$\displaystyle=Tr_{3,l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{t-1}}\sum_{i,j,k,i^{\prime},j^{\prime},k^{\prime}\in[d]}|ijk\rangle\otimes|\Phi_{i,j,k}\rangle\langle i^{\prime}j^{\prime}k^{\prime}|\otimes\langle\Phi_{i^{\prime},j^{\prime},k^{\prime}}|$
$\displaystyle=\sum_{i,j,i^{\prime},j^{\prime}\in[d]}|ij\rangle\langle i^{\prime}j^{\prime}|\otimes\sum_{k\in[d]}Tr_{l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{t-1}}|\Phi_{i,j,k}\rangle\langle\Phi_{i^{\prime},j^{\prime},k}|.$
So, $\sum\limits_{i,j,i^{\prime},j^{\prime}\in[d]}|ij\rangle\langle i^{\prime}j^{\prime}|\otimes\sum\limits_{k\in[d]}Tr_{l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{t-1}}|\Phi_{i,j,k}\rangle\langle\Phi_{i^{\prime},j^{\prime},k}|=\mathbb{I}_{d^{3}}$ if and only if Eq. (18) holds.
If $S\cap\{1,2,3\}=\{1,3\}$ , or $S\cap\{1,2,3\}=\{2,3\}$ , in that case, they hold true if and only if Eqs. (17) or (16) holds true, respectively.
Case 3. When $|S\cap\{1,2,3\}|=1$ . Suppose $S\cap\{1,2,3\}=\{1\}$ , and $\{l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{t-2}\}\cap\{1,2,3\}=\emptyset$ , then we have
$\displaystyle\rho_{S}$
$\displaystyle=Tr_{2,3,l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{t-2}}\sum_{i,j,k,i^{\prime},j^{\prime},k^{\prime}\in[d]}|ijk\rangle\otimes|\Phi_{i,j,k}\rangle\langle i^{\prime}j^{\prime}k^{\prime}|\otimes\langle\Phi_{i^{\prime},j^{\prime},k^{\prime}}|$
$\displaystyle=\sum_{i,i^{\prime}\in[d]}|i\rangle\langle i^{\prime}|\otimes\sum_{j,k\in[d]}Tr_{l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{t-1}}|\Phi_{i,j,k}\rangle\langle\Phi_{i^{\prime},j,k}|.$
So, $\rho_{S}=\sum\limits_{i,i^{\prime}\in[d]}|i\rangle\langle i^{\prime}|\otimes\sum\limits_{j,k\in[d]}Tr_{l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{t-1}}|\Phi_{i,j,k}\rangle\langle\Phi_{i^{\prime},j,k}|=\mathbb{I}_{d^{3}}$ if and only if Eq. (20) holds.
If $S\cap\{1,2,3\}=\{2\}$ , or $S\cap\{1,2,3\}=\{3\}$ , they hold true if and only if Eqs. (21) or (19) holds, respectively.
Case 4. When $|S\cap\{1,2,3\}|=0$ , we have
$\displaystyle\rho_{S}$
$\displaystyle=Tr_{1,2,3,l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{t-3}}\sum_{i,j,k,i^{\prime},j^{\prime},k^{\prime}\in[d]}|ijk\rangle\otimes|\Phi_{i,j,k}\rangle\langle i^{\prime}j^{\prime}k^{\prime}|\otimes\langle\Phi_{i^{\prime},j^{\prime},k^{\prime}}|$
$\displaystyle=\sum_{i,j,k\in[d]}Tr_{l_{1},l_{2},\dots,l_{t-2}}|\Phi_{i,j,k}\rangle\langle\Phi_{i,j,k}|.$
So, $\rho_{S}=\mathbb{I}_{d^{3}}$ if and only if Eq. (22) holds.

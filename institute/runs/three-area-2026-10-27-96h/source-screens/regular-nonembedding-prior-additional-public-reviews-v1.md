# Additional public-source corroboration: regular nonembedding

After the parent’s [Szendrei survey screen](/Users/jinleic/repos/aifours/institute/runs/three-area-2026-10-27-96h/source-screens/szendrei-survey-billhardt-eunitary-nonembedding-prior-v1.md), two further publicly readable sources were inspected on 2026-10-30:

1. [MaRDI / zbMATH review of Billhardt, *On embeddability into a semidirect product of a band by a group*](https://portal.mardi4nfdi.de/wiki/On_embeddability_into_a_semidirect_product_of_a_band_by_a_group), DOI 10.1006/jabr.1997.7390, explicitly states that the 1998 paper solves the E-unitary regular-semigroup embeddability problem negatively and presents an example not embeddable into a band-by-group semidirect product. It defines E-unitary as the idempotents forming a least-group-congruence class. This is a mathematical review, not the original proof.
2. [Miklós Hartmann, *Orthodox semigroups and semidirect products*, dissertation summary](https://doktori.bibl.u-szeged.hu/id/eprint/1183/3/HM_angtezis.pdf), page 5, Theorem 2.14, attributes to Billhardt the existence of such a nonembeddable E-unitary regular semigroup. Its bibliography separately lists Mária B. Szendrei, *A finite non-embeddable E-unitary regular semigroup*, 1998, pp. 202–214. The summary is a secondary report about Billhardt, not a reproduced multiplication table or proof. The 2019 Szendrei survey independently labels a Billhardt example finite; no equivalence between the two original constructions is asserted.

These sources reinforce the reported prior negative **existence** result and the pure-quotient interface. They do not supply an independently checked explicit finite multiplication table, a concrete same-\(q\) collision in the present universal-band presentation, or identity with an unspecified printed original question. No original-proof, exhaustive priority, novelty, human, independent-family or kernel acceptance is claimed.

# Public prior report: E-unitary orthodox semigroups need not embed in band-by-group products

Parent read [Mária B. Szendrei, *Structure theory of regular semigroups*, arXiv:1909.05280v1, §5.1](https://arxiv.org/html/1909.05280v1#S5.SS1) on 2026-10-30. The author explains that an E-unitary orthodox semigroup is a regular semigroup with least group quotient whose identity class is exactly the idempotent band. Thus its quotient onto the group is idempotent-pure at the group-actor interface of the current manuscript.

The survey explicitly reports that **B. Billhardt constructed a finite E-unitary orthodox semigroup that is not embeddable in any semidirect product of a band by a group**. It also reports the positive embedding theorem for E-unitary orthodox semigroups with regular bands. Citation in the survey: B. Billhardt, *On embeddability into a semidirect product of a band by a group*, Journal of Algebra 206 (1998), 40–50. The surveyed counterexample is stronger than failure of a particular prescribed-actor or whole-host extension: it is reported as failure for **any** such band-by-group host.

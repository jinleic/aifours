## Supplementary Material E A single extension of ρ3×3\rho^{3\times 3} is insufficient

As discussed in the main text, after performing a trivial extension on subsystem A of ρ3×3\rho^{3\times 3}, and a single nontrivial extension with |20⟩⟨3|\outerproduct{20}{3} on subsystem B, the resulting state ρ4×4\rho^{4\times 4} still has SN 22. We shall prove this by finding a suitable separable projection on the ρ4×4\rho^{4\times 4} and applying Theorem [2](#Thmobservation2 "Theorem 2. ‣ Scaling Bound Entanglement through Local Extensions").

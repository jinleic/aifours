## Reconstruction 1: fixed-law iff and Volterra/endpoint reduction

S4:12–41. Put `f=Fop w`, `g=Gop w`, `o=Eop w`, `mu(t)=integral_0^t exp(P(t-s)) f(s) ds`, `d=g+Q mu`, `y=x-mu`. Then `dy=P y dt+(Qy+d)dW` and `z=(Eop+O J)w+O y`. The deterministic Volterra/convolution operators are bounded on the finite L2 interval. The registered E2 class in S1 is contained in the author's E1 extension; both have pathwise L2 inputs.

Necessity uses **all** deterministic L2 inputs, not a selected disturbance. With deterministic forcing, standard square-integrable linear SDE theory gives `E y=0`; from universal zero output, taking expectation yields `(Eop+O J)w=0` for every deterministic `w`. Thus `Oy=0` almost surely for a.e. time, and by continuity for all times. For any word `V` in noncommuting `P,Q`, if `OVy=0`, uniqueness of the continuous semimartingale's finite-variation and martingale parts gives `OVPy=0` and `OVQy+OVd=0` a.e. Taking expectation gives `OVd=0`; hence `OVQy=0`, and continuity gives the two next word identities. Countably many words therefore place deterministic `d(t)` in `N=intersection_V ker(OV)` a.e. for every deterministic `w`; equivalently `Pi(Gop+QJ)=0` as a bounded time operator. This reconstructs S4:43–51 without commuting P and Q.

Conversely, for each random path the deterministic operator identities give `(Eop+OJ)w=0` and `d(t) in N` a.e. Since `N` is P,Q invariant and `y(0)=0`, localized strong uniqueness preserves `y in N`, so `z=0` pathwise. No E2 expectation is used in this direction; S4:53–55 also applies to E1 inputs as stated, subject to the usual localized SDE existence/uniqueness import.

For h>0, separate the direct atoms `D1 L0`, `D1 Lh` and `Pi B0 L0`, `Pi B0 Lh` from the locally integrable regular kernels. Normalized pulses in shrinking first/delayed windows isolate the two atoms; approximate-identity inputs supported near 0 then identify the regular kernel at every lag in `(0,T)`. This uses both `S=T-h>0` and `T>h`. For a disturbance coordinate define

`m(t)=exp(Pt) B l0 + integral_0^t exp(P(t-r)) B H(r) dr` for `0<t<h`,

`Astar=[D1; Pi B0]`, `Cstar=[O; Pi Q]`, and `V=m(h)+B lh+C`.

The early regular kernel is exactly `Astar H(t)+Cstar m(t)`, giving `m'=P m+B H`, `m(0)=B l0` and the early Volterra equation. For every later lag it is `[O exp(Ps)V; Pi Q exp(Ps)V]`, `0<s<T-h`. If it vanishes on this nonempty interval, analyticity yields `O P^j V=0` and `Pi Q P^j V=0` for all j. The span of `N` and the P-cyclic span of V is then P,Q invariant and contained in ker O, so maximality of N forces `V in N`; the converse follows by invariance. Thus the entire late continuum is exactly `Pi V=0`, not a finite-sample surrogate. This independently reconstructs S4:57–94. ## Reconstruction 3: all-matrix fixed-law gain lower bound

S6 imports the E1 state-supremum estimate from S3. I reconstructed its stated argument: the pathwise Young inequalities bound the drift and diffusion forcing L2 norms by `a_d||w||` and `a_s||w||`. For continuous vector martingales, the stopping-time good-lambda inequality `P(M*>2l) <= P(sqrt([M])>l/2)+(1/4)P(M*>l)` integrates to `E M* <= 8 E sqrt([M])`, after standard localization. The finite geometric recurrence yields a finite law-dependent constant `C_state` for `E sup_t||x_t|| <= C_state E||w||`. This is an L1 bound and does not turn the printed expectation of a path norm into an RMS criterion. S3:48–101 gives the associated explicit constants; the named stochastic facts remain imports rather than formal machine proofs.

For any direction a and `epsilon<min(h,S)/2`, take `w_epsilon=epsilon^(-1/2) a 1_[0,epsilon]`. On the disjoint windows `[0,epsilon]` and `[h,h+epsilon]`, the direct atoms have exact norm `sqrt(||D1L0 a||²+||D1Lh a||²)`. The expected state contribution is at most `||O||sqrt(2epsilon) C_state ||a||`; the distributed output contribution tends to zero by Young's inequality and L1 absolute continuity of H at the kernel endpoints. Reverse triangle and the supremum over a give `G_E1,G_E2 >= ||[D1L0;D1Lh]||op`. At h=0 the atoms combine and give `>=||D1(L0+Lh)||op`. These deterministic pulses belong to both classes. Thus gamma below the corresponding bound is impossible for **that fixed law**; equality is not disposed of, and there is no controller optimization in this bound. See S6:12–67.

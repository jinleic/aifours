## 3. A band calculation explaining the collision

The following elementary band implication is sufficient.

Let \(x,y,z,t,r,p,v\) belong to any band and suppose

\[
xyz=x,\qquad xtr=x,
\]

\[
zypv=pv,
\]

\[
rtpzxypv=pzxypv.
\]

Then

\[
xypv=xtpzxypv. \tag{B}
\]

To prove this, put

\[
c=pv,\qquad u=xyc,\qquad d=pzu,\qquad k=pz.
\]

First,

\[
zc=z(zyc)=zzyc=zyc=c.
\]

Consequently,

\[
u=xyc=xyzc=xc.
\]

The fourth premise gives \(rtd=d\), and therefore

\[
rd=r(rtd)=rrtd=rtd=d.
\]

It follows that

\[
xtd=xtrd=xd.
\]

Also,

\[
kc=pzc=pc=p^2v=pv=c.
\]

Using the square relation on the nonempty product \(xk\),

\[
xd=xkx c
     =xkxkc
     =(xk)^2c
     =xkc
     =xc
     =u.
\]

Thus \(xtd=u\), which is exactly (B).

This calculation uses only band idempotence and the four displayed premises. In particular, it does not use an imported nonembedding theorem.

### 6.1 A genuine obstruction to a misleading finite-model search

A search restricted to band-by-group models in which R1–R4 already hold cannot produce a separating model for this pair: the proof above forces their images equal in every such model.

In particular, suppose one replaces \(F\) by a finite equivariant band quotient \(F/\beta\), imposes the source equations **as band equations in that quotient**, and then searches in \((F/\beta)\rtimes C_2\). Every such model collapses \(U,V\).

That is not evidence that SEP is false. It shows that this band-closure procedure is unsuitable for proving SEP in the original source congruence.

A useful finite-image procedure must instead:

1. take an equivariant finite band image of the ambient construction;
2. form the actual image of \(S_0\);
3. impose R1–R4 by the least semigroup congruence **inside that source image**.

The program in §8 follows this order.

### 6.2 The exact obstruction to lifting this collision proof into a source equality

Treat a universal generator \((h,s)\) as an arrow

\[
h\longrightarrow h+q(s).
\]

A cocycle expansion of a source element is a composable path.

For example, U1,

\[
XADAPQ,
\]

is composable. In it, \(D=(1,a')\) is an arrow \(1\to0\).

The next step duplicates \(D\):

\[
XADAPQ\longrightarrow XADDAPQ.
\]

The adjacent pair \(DD\) is **not composable**: its first copy ends at \(0\), while its second copy starts at \(1\).

This is a legitimate universal-band square replacement, because universal words need not be composable paths. It is not a multiplication identity in \(S_0/\theta\).

Thus the first exact obstruction to reading the displayed collision derivation as a source equality is U1→U2. This explains a limitation of this derivation; it is **not a proof that no other source equality derivation exists**.

Before imposing \(\theta\), the two ambient first coordinates have different contents: the coordinate of \(V\) contains \(b_0\), and that of \(U\) does not.

But R2 already identifies an element with coordinate \(e_0b_0\) and one with coordinate \(e_0\), both in group grade \(0\). Consequently, an invariant that merely records the presence of \(b_0\) does not factor through \(\theta\).

This is an explicit failure of a proposed simple invariant, not separation in the quotient.

- Disjointness of the pair projections of \(P\) and \(E\).
- A residual \(12\times12\) unitary after removing the 24 singleton rows and columns.

A residual row with one nonzero would force that entry to have modulus one, contrary to its membership in \(E\). Thus every residual row and column has at least two entries. There are twelve rows and exactly 24 entries, so every row and column has exactly two.

Consequently, every physical pair projection \(R_{ij}=\pi_{ij}(E)\) has twelve distinct values, each with exactly two extensions, and
\[
\pi_{ij}(P)=[6]^2\setminus R_{ij}.
\]

### 4.2 Full block closure

Every component is therefore a full \(2\times2\) block. In particular, two residual rows sharing a column share both columns. This applies separately to every balanced cut of the same \(E\).

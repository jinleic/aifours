## 5. Injectivity of \(EF\), without assuming semisimplicity

The following elementary lemma suffices.

**Lemma.** Suppose
\[
[E,F]=H,\qquad [H,E]=2E,
\]
\(E\) is locally nilpotent, and \(Hx=mx\) with \(m>0\). Then
\[
EFx=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x=0.
\]

**Proof.** If \(x\ne0\), let \(N\ge1\) be the least index with \(E^Nx=0\). Commuting \(F\) through \(E^N\) gives
\[
FE^Nx=E^NFx-N(m+N-1)E^{N-1}x.
\]
But
\[
E^NFx=E^{N-1}(EFx)=0,
\]
and the left side is zero. Therefore
\[
N(m+N-1)E^{N-1}x=0,
\]
contradicting minimality of \(N\) in characteristic zero. ∎

## 3. Route A — reconstruction of addendum §§1–4 (LF5–62)

Fix the law, `h>0` (for `h=0` see A5), `a≠0`, `n`, and `ε` admissible. Throughout, `C` denotes constants depending on `(P,Q,T,n,a,‖F‖_{L²},‖G‖_{L²},‖E‖_{L²},a_d,a_s)` but not on `ε`.

**Standard facts used (imports).** [S1] For constant `P,Q` and one scalar Brownian motion the fundamental matrix `F` (`dF=PFdt+QFdW`, `F_0=I`) and its inverse `Ψ=F^{-1}` (`dΨ=Ψ(Q²−P)dt−ΨQdW`) satisfy, for every `p<∞`, `E sup_{t≤T}(‖F_t‖^p+‖Ψ_t‖^p)<∞` and `E‖Ψ_s−Ψ_r‖^p≤C_p|s−r|^{p/2}` (Gronwall + BDG). [S2] For deterministic `f,g∈L²[0,T]`, `E sup_{t≤T}‖x_t‖²≤C_0(‖f‖²_{L²}+‖g‖²_{L²})` (Doob/BDG + Gronwall; f120 Lemma 4.1). [S3] Tightness + convergence of finite-dimensional distributions ⇒ weak convergence on `C[0,T]×R^{n+1}`; a weak limit of centered Gaussian laws with converging covariances is the centered Gaussian law with the limit covariance. [S4] Doss–Sussmann representation for one driver; continuous dependence of linear ODE solutions on a continuous coefficient (Gronwall). [S5] Continuous mapping theorem. [S6] A family bounded in `L²(Ω)` is uniformly integrable; weak convergence + UI ⇒ convergence of expectations. [S7] Itô isometry, BDG, Young's inequality `‖K∗w‖_{L²}≤‖K‖_{L²[0,h]}‖w‖_{L¹}`, `‖K∗w‖_{L¹}≤‖K‖_{L¹[0,h]}‖w‖_{L¹}≤√h‖K‖_{L²}‖w‖_{L¹}`.

**A1 — exact variation of constants with the `−Qg` correction (LF12–17, eq. (1)).** With `d(ΨF)=0` one gets `dΨ=Ψ((Q²−P)dt−QdW)` (the "usual globally linear matrix SDE", LF12). Itô's product rule, `d(Ψx)=dΨ·x+Ψ·dx+dΨ·dx` with `dΨ·dx=−ΨQ(Qx+g)dt`, gives
`d(Ψx)=Ψ[(Q²−P)x+Px+f−Q²x−Qg]dt+Ψ[−Qx+Qx+g]dW=Ψ(f−Qg)dt+Ψg dW`,
hence `x_t=F_t[∫_0^tΨ_s(f_s−Qg_s)ds+∫_0^tΨ_s g_s dW_s]`, which is (1). The cancellation is word-by-word in the free algebra generated by `P,Q,Ψ,x,f,g` — no commutation of `P` and `Q` is used (diagnostic D1, §6). Dropping `−Qg` leaves the residual `−ΨQg dt` (D1). The addendum's "`−Qg` is essential; NOT dropped" (LF17) is correct for the exactness of (1); in the limit that term is itself `O(√ε)` by (2) (addendum LF34 says so), so the limit does not depend on it (A-m5, wording only). ✔

## 3. Normalization and one period

Among the 256 unordered r-pairs in the **whole 512-frequency** domain, let `N_j` count absolute difference j. The permitted differences are 0, 4, 8, 12. `16N₄+64N₈+144N₁₂=16384`, `ΣN_j=256`, hence `5N₁₂=4N₀+3N₄`.  (A)

`N₀(K)≥100−E`, and `N₄(K)=N₁₂(K)=0`.  (B)

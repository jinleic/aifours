A modulus-one entry exhausts both its row and its column in each unitary flattening. Consequently:

1. \(P\) is pair-injective on every physical pair.
2. No pair value occupied by \(P\) can occur in \(E\).
3. Deleting the 24 singleton rows and columns leaves a \(12\times12\) unitary with 24 residual nonzeros.

5. Every residual row and column has degree exactly two.

Hence every residual cut consists of six full nonzero \(2\times2\) blocks. For every physical pair \(ij\),

\[
|R_{ij}|=12,\qquad R_{ij}=\pi_{ij}(E),
\]

each value in \(R_{ij}\) has exactly two \(E\)-extensions, and

\[
\pi_{ij}(P)=[6]^2\setminus R_{ij}.
\]

These are properties of the **same \(P\) and \(E\)** in all six systems—not six independently chosen complements.

\[

For any proposed `K`, let `Q=F_2^30/K`. For vertex `v`, define `D_v` as the 30-bit differences with a nonzero three-bit symbol at `v` and zero symbols at every graph-neighbour of `v`. The quotient images of these sets, `B=union_v image(D_v)`, are **exactly** the incompatible class differences: if a class difference is in `B`, the corresponding difference differs from an isolated-vertex witness by some element of `K`, hence two words in the two full cosets conflict; conversely any conflict supplies such a witness. This checks the load-bearing equivalence at candidate 1 L25, L29, L33 and candidate 2 L33, not merely a sampled syndrome subspace.

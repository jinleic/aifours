## Common mathematical setup and verdict convention

Let `V={1,...,10}`. `N(v)` is the **literal original** neighborhood in 05:22–95 and 15:19–31, including edge 3–4. `T_t` is the ordered triangle at index `t`, and `J` is the selected factor set. `L_J(v)` is the union of the other vertices of the selected triangles through `v`; `L_ALL(v)` uses all ten factors. `Y_v` is the tuple of selected bits at vertex `v`, not a single bit. All entropies are of one finite joint law and the conditions below are almost-sure conditions on that law; fixed-factor conditioning is on positive-probability fibers.

For an affine binary plane with independent direction basis, take its unique coordinates `S=(B0,B1)` and forms `F0=B0`, `F1=B1`, `F2=B0 XOR B1`. A selected factor of color `c` has parity `constant_t XOR F_c`. The plane need not be fully populated. Define `h0(a)=H(Y_a)`, `h1(a)=H(Y_a,S)`, and `h_(2+c)(a)=H(Y_a,F_c)`. The IDs are `5*a+b`, with bit `v-1` for vertex `v`; only ID 0, `H(empty)=0`, is omitted. In a branch, `H_b(m)=h_b(m AND 1023)` when bit 10 of `m` is set and `h0(m AND 1023)` otherwise. It is the ordinary entropy of the subset of `(Y1,...,Y10,Z_b)`, with `Z_1=S` and `Z_(2+c)=F_c`; it is not a binary-entropy replacement for tuple-valued `S`.

`SUPPORTED` below means **the stated formula has the stated sign/equality under all listed guards**, with a derivation from standard entropy identities and the written component model. It does **not** mean that a named archive row has been found, that its image or weight matches, or that a record satisfies the guards. The displayed condition for an equality is exact; a component or visibility predicate is a sufficient way to establish it, not a logically necessary condition for every special law. This distinction is essential for degenerate supports and tautological masks. Source references use the numbered table above; declaration formulas are 16:43–238, with coordinate and weight definitions in 16:9–24 and 16:241–249.

The conditional-fiber mechanism itself is elementary: fixing every complete omitted-factor tuple supplies constants to each original graph decoder, hence the selected `Y` law still has `H(Y_v|Y_N(v))=0` in each positive fiber (14:16–23, 15:61–75). A triangle's actual parity and the other two actual component bits recover its missing component. This latter fact—not the original graph FD alone—proves local component decoders. For a state-visibility row, an incident triangle's parity is recoverable from `Y_N(v)` only after the graph FD recovers `Y_v`; enough such parities must then determine the stated auxiliary. Merely naming a color or visibility set does not establish these conditions.

### Why the singleton guard matters even for an actual graph-FD law

This is a written counterexample, not an archive input or a machine check. Use selected factors `J={1,2,4}`, namely `123,145,239`, with colors `F0,F1,F1` and zero offsets. The ambient affine direction plane has syndrome coordinates `sigma_1=B0`, `sigma_2=sigma_4=B1`, all other syndromes zero. Let `U` be fair, take the law on the line `S=(0,U)`, set only the `239` component at vertex 3 and the `145` component at vertex 4 equal to `U`, and set every other component to zero. All parity identities hold; the only nonconstant vertex tuples are `Y3=U` and `Y4=U`, which recover one another across the literal edge 3–4, so every original-neighbor FD holds. At vertex 3 the incident selected colors are `{0,1}`, `L_J(3)={1,2,9}`, and `F0=0`; hence `H(Y3|Y_L_J(3),F0)=1`, not zero. Full `S` does determine that missing component. This rules out weakening the declared singleton test to mere incidence even in the actual component setting; it does not assert anything about an archive occurrence.

## Weights, signs, transport, and what remains unverified

For a row vector `r` with `n>0` declared actions and top-level rational multiplier `w`, the author's intended contribution is **`(w/n) sum_(k=1)^n action_k(r)` once**. The proper-n9 `name.multiplier` is declared redundant provenance, not an additional multiplier; e.g. `w=2` with a redundant name value 2 contributes `2r` under one identity action, not `4r`. Likewise the already serialized target is not multiplied again by `scope.scale`. This is a review of the intended convention, not an inspection of any actual weight or target vector. Each action must be a valid relabeling of the corresponding law for the row relation to survive; rational arithmetic must preserve signs exactly.

For a target proven `<=0`, nonzero multipliers on `le` rows must be nonnegative and on `ge` rows nonpositive; for a target proven `>=0`, the signs reverse. Equality multipliers may have either sign **only after the row's equality guard is established**. Zero is harmless, but it proves nothing about a missing premise. This applies to the declared n11 `Delta<=0`, functional `3 Delta<=0`, and proper n9 `scale*(2 sum H(single)-3H(joint))>=0`; the scale must be appropriate and the actual serialized target independently checked.

A vertex action `p` must be a bijection and preserve the literal graph where graph FDs are transported. The relevant induced triangle permutation must preserve the ordered factor incidence; for a same-record stabilizer it must preserve `J`, while a source-to-client transport may instead map the source `J` and coloring to the actual client `J` and coloring. It must carry `N(v)` to `N(pv)` and `L_J(v)` to `L_(pJ)(pv)`. Every image used in an average must also preserve or transport the **specific** visibility, factor scope, and singleton-color premises of that row. A factor-preserving graph action alone does not prove those premises.

For a functional action `(p,cp)`, original subset masks move under `p`, branch 0 and full-state branch 1 remain their respective entropy types, and color branch `2+c` moves to `2+cp(c)`. The color map must be a bijection of the three nonzero forms compatible with one invertible binary state-coordinate change; in dimension two every permutation of those three forms is induced by some `GL(2,2)` map. Affine offsets and actual source-to-client syndrome maps must also be compatible; entropy is unaffected by a fixed bit complement. Distinguish color `c=0,1,2` from branch `j=2,3,4`, and check push-versus-pull orientation rather than assuming the declared action table does so.

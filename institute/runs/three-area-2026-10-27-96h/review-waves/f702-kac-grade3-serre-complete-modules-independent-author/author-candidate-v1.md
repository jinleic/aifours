## 3. Character calculation and the actual Serre denominator

For independently reproducible character arithmetic, put \(x=e^{\omega_1}\), \(y=e^{\omega_2}\) and
\[
 N(a,b)=x^ay^b-x^{-a}y^{a+b}-x^{a+b}y^{-b}
       +x^{-a-b}y^a+x^by^{-a-b}-x^{-b}y^{-a}.
\]
Then \(\chi_{p,q}=N(p+1,q+1)/N(1,1)\), while the exterior characters are the coefficients of
\[
 (1+t)^2(1+tx^2/y)(1+ty/x^2)(1+ty^2/x)

\]
Multiplying each requested character by \(N(1,1)\), expansion gives the following complete positive-chamber exponent pairs \((a,b)\), with superscripts denoting coefficients. All other terms are their signed Weyl translates:
\[
\begin{array}{c|l}

 \Lambda^2V&(4,1),(1,4),(2,2)\\

\end{array}
\]
Here \(L(p,q)\) denotes the finite irreducible with **lowest** labels \((p,q)\); the two exterior identities are symmetric under exchanging the labels. The table in particular yields
\[
\begin{aligned}

For degree three, the map \(x\otimes(y\wedge z)\mapsto[x,[y,z]]\) has precisely the Jacobi subspace as kernel:
\[
 L_3(V)\simeq (V\otimes\Lambda^2V)/j(\Lambda^3V),\quad
 j(x\wedge y\wedge z)=x\otimes(y\wedge z)+y\otimes(z\wedge x)+z\otimes(x\wedge y).

Indeed wedge projection after \(j\) is multiplication by three; the ordinary free-Lie tensor embedding identifies the remaining kernel. Thus \(\dim L_3(V)=(8^3-8)/3=168\), and the character table gives
\[

 \oplus2L(2,2)\oplus L(1,4)\oplus L(4,1).

## 7. Why these calculations exclude the actual radical

By the separately imported grade-two theorem and ideal stability,
\[
 F_3(\mathcal R\cap S_3)\subseteq\mathcal R\cap S_2=0.
\]
The full Cartan grading and finite-dimensional \(\mathfrak{sl}_3\)-stability make \(\mathcal R\cap S_3\) a completely reducible Levi submodule. If it were nonzero, it would contain a nonzero lowest vector. The quotient calculation in §3 lists every possible type and multiplicity, while §6 gives an injective actual \(F_3\) map on **each complete lowest-weight multiplicity space**, including both 27s and all three/two adjoints. Thus no such nonzero vector exists, proving the precise conditional claim in §1. This argument neither infers a radical statement from the contraction nor ignores exceptional relation overlap.

## 4. Assessment of the written complex argument (the part finite tests cannot replace)

Each step below was re-derived by the reviewer; "✓" means the step is correct as written over **C** given the finite facts of §3.

**§3.1 pure-H C covector λ, any nonzero u=(u_H,u_K).** Only E_{AC}, E_{CD}, E_{BC} contribute (C∈H, P absent). Blocks: (A-H,D-K)=X with column g = u_K(g)·Q_{AC,g}λ; (A-K,D-H)=Y with row g = u_K(g)·λᵀQ_{CD,g}; (A-K,D-K)=Z diagonal with Z_{gg}=u_Hᵀ Q_{BC,g} λ; (A-H,D-H)=0 (three H indices impossible). u_K≠0: pick g with u_K(g)≠0, a with X_{ag}≠0, d with Y_{gd}≠0; the minor on rows {a,g}, columns {d,g} is [[0,X_{ag}],[Y_{gd},Z_{gg}]] with determinant −X_{ag}Y_{gd}≠0 ✓. u_K=0: if three of the four Z_{gg} vanished, three vectors Q_gλ would lie in the line ker u_Hᵀ, so λ would be a common eigenvector of two distinct non-identity Paulis (Q_a⁻¹Q_b, Q_a⁻¹Q_c ∈ {±X,±Z,±J} up to scalar), which anticommute and are invertible — impossible (ABλ=αβλ=−BAλ forces αβ=0). Hence ≥2 nonzero diagonal entries, rank ≥2 ✓.

**§3.2 pure-K C covector t, u=(u_H,u_K).** Contributors: P (all-K, into (A-K,D-K)=Z), E_{AD} (into F=(A-H,D-H)), E_{AB} (into X, column g = t_g Q_{AB,g}u_H), E_{BD} (into Y, row g = t_g u_HᵀQ_{BD,g}). By sharp 2-transitivity (C2) the 12 off-diagonal entries of Z are √2·e^{iθ}·t_g u_h, one per ordered pair g≠h, each from exactly one P (or P′) term, and Z_{gg}=0 (Z1) ✓ — no inter-term cancellation is possible, so arbitrary phases on S change nothing here ✓. u_H≠0: minor [[F_{ad},X_{ag}],[Y_{gd},0]] with g∈supp t gives rank ≥2 ✓. u_H=0: M=F⊕Z with F=Σ_g t_g u_g Q_{AD,g}; F=0 ⟺ t_g u_g=0 ∀g by linear independence (P1); Z=0 ⟺ supp t = supp u_K = one singleton, in which case F is an invertible Pauli times a nonzero scalar (rank 2). Hence M≠0 always, and rank 1 ⟹ F=0 and rank Z=1 ⟹ u_H=0 and u_g=0 for every g∈supp t ⟹ u∈W_t (dimension 3 for one fixed g). At most 3 of 6 linearly independent B covectors lie in W_t, so the row sum is ≥ 3·1+3·2 = 9 ✓. (Reading note: W_t is a necessary condition only; many u∈W_t give rank 2 — my diagnostic found 72/84 such cases — which is consistent with the text's "all such rank-one B covectors lie in W_t".)

**§4 rank-one ⟹ product saturation.** For the CB|AD flattening F with FF†=2I (I1/B1, all phases, both faces), M = (λ⊗u)ᵀF has ‖M‖_F = √2‖λ‖‖u‖; if rank M = 1 then M = √2·αδᵀ with unit α,δ, giving |⟨φ,T/√72⟩| = √2/√72 = 1/6 for φ = α⊗ū⊗λ̄⊗δ ✓. For every cut, T/√72 = U/6 with U unitary, so |⟨φ,T/√72⟩| = (1/6)|⟨φ̄_{ij}, U φ̄_{kl}⟩| ≤ 1/6 with equality iff Uφ̄_{kl} = e^{iθ}φ̄_{ij}: product saturation on every cut, hence every two-party contraction of φ has rank one ✓. **§5 probability classification.** For an even (odd, on P′) colour permutation (g_0..g_3), the output coordinate (g_i,g_j) in cut ij|kl is fed only by the single S term (Z1: no E term at distinct spectator colours; exactly one all-K feeder), with |U entry| = 1, giving p_i(g_i)p_j(g_j) = p_k(g_k)p_l(g_l) for all three partitions — phases drop out under moduli ✓ (6). **§6 Bloch contradiction.** Exact saturation at matched outputs gives p_k(g)p_l(g) = (1/2)|φ̄_{i,H}ᵀQ_{ij,g}φ̄_{j,H}|², and with (8), |η_iᵀQη_j|² = 1/2 for all four Paulis of every pair (the pair's four blocks are one each of I,X,Z,J up to sign and transpose, E1; signs and transposes do not affect the modulus, and η_jᵀQᵀη_i = η_iᵀQη_j) ✓ (9). ## 6. Scientific and source limits (explicit)

4. **Finite vs. universal.** My checker proves finite statements (transcription, class membership, 2-transitivity, Pauli families, structural zeros, exact Gram/isometry, 17-pattern enumeration, the Bloch polynomial identity by degree-bounded grid evaluation). The complex argument (§4 of this review) is verified by reading and re-derivation only; it is written mathematics, not machine-checked. The exact Q(√2) rank diagnostic and the structured transform supports are consistency evidence, not proof.
5. **Same-harness.** Reviewer and author are agents in the same harness; this review is not cross-model, not human, not kernel-checked. The parent decides adoption; this report alone does not confer it.

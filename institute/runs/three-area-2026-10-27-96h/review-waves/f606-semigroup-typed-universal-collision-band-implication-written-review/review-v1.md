## 3. Reconstruction of the general band implication

The implication in S1 lines 100–171 is valid.

Let \(x,y,z,t,r,p,v\) lie in a band and assume

\[
xyz=x,\qquad xtr=x,\qquad zypv=pv,
\]

\[
rtpzxypv=pzxypv.
\]

Put, as in the candidate,

\[
c=pv,\qquad u=xyc,\qquad d=pzu,\qquad k=pz.
\]

Each intermediate equality has the required direction and context:

1. From \(zyc=c\),

   \[
   zc=z(zyc)=zzyc=zyc=c.
   \]

   This expands \(c\) using the third premise, contracts \(zz\), then uses the third premise forward.

2. Therefore,

   \[
   u=xyc=xyzc=xc.
   \]

   The first change inserts \(z\) immediately before \(c\); the second uses \(xyz=x\).

3. The fourth premise is \(rtd=d\). Hence

   \[
   rd=r(rtd)=rrtd=rtd=d.
   \]

4. Consequently,

   \[
   xtd=xtrd=xd,
   \]

   by inserting \(r\) before \(d\), then using \(xtr=x\).

5. Also,

   \[
   kc=pzc=pc=p^2v=pv=c.
   \]

6. Since \(d=ku\) and \(u=xc\),

   \[
   \begin{aligned}
   xd
   &=xkxc\\
   &=xkxkc\\
   &=(xk)^2c\\
   &=xkc\\
   &=xc\\
   &=u.
   \end{aligned}
   \]

   Here the second line uses \(kc=c\) in reverse, the fourth uses the square relation on the **nonempty product \(xk\)**, and the fifth uses \(kc=c\) forward.

Thus \(xtd=u\), namely

\[
\boxed{xypv=xtpzxypv.}
\]

No cancellation, commutativity, identity element, or source equality is used.

## 6. Consuming the collision correctly

### 6.1 Every host over the prescribed \(q\) identifies the pair

Let

\[
H:S\to K\rtimes C_2,\qquad H(s)=(k_s,q(s)),
\]

where \(K\) is a band and \(C_2\) acts on \(K\).

The consuming map from the universal presentation is

\[
T_h(s)\longmapsto h\cdot k_s.
\]

It respects every word-square relation because \(K\) is a band. It respects a cocycle relation because the homomorphism law gives

\[
k_{st}=k_s\bigl(q(s)\cdot k_t\bigr),
\]

and hence

\[
h\cdot k_{st}
=(h\cdot k_s)\bigl((h+q(s))\cdot k_t\bigr).
\]

Consequently, the complete primitive chain evaluates to \(k_U=k_V\). Since \(q(U)=q(V)\), it follows that

\[
\boxed{H(U)=H(V).}
\]

This verifies the consuming side of the universal collision, rather than merely constructing an equality with no checked host interpretation.

### 6.2 Transfer to arbitrary group actors is conditional on SEP

S4 lines 7–9 provide actor rigidity for a regular source with a prescribed pure onto group quotient. Therefore, **if**

\[

\]

### 6.3 The collision chain is not a source-equality derivation

The noncomposability warning in S1 lines 393–421 is correct.

Read \(T_h(s)\) as an arrow \(h\to h+q(s)\). The word

\[

is composable. But \(D=(1,a')\) is an arrow \(1\to0\), so the next prescribed step

\[

introduces adjacent copies of \(D\) that are not composable.

This square insertion is legitimate in \(C_q\), whose words need not be composable paths. It cannot be read as the corresponding multiplication step in the original source.

This establishes the claimed **first obstruction for this displayed derivation**, not a theorem excluding every possible source-equality derivation. In particular, it proves neither SEP nor its negation.

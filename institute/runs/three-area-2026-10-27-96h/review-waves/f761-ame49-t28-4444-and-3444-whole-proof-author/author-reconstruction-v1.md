## 2. Common P degrees, moments and the five-cube lemma

A used degree-one symbol has only one possible partner in each of the other three coordinates, hence at most one E word, contradicting line multiplicity at least two. Thus d_i(x)>=2.

If a d=2 symbol had h=1, its five words would be five distinct vertices of a binary 3-cube. In each direction the two pair lines have sizes two and three and cannot overlap in exactly one, so they induce at least two cube edges; there would be at least six.     sum_P k = 6 sum_i q_i - 4n,

\[

\[(u,h)(v,k)=(u\tau^h(v),h+k).\]
The actual source \(S_0\) is the subsemigroup generated by
\[
a=(a_0,1),\quad a'=(a_1,1),\quad b=(b_0,1),\quad b'=(b_1,1),\quad e=(e_0,0),\quad f=(f_0,0),\quad g=(g_0,0).
\]
Here and below a prime is part of a **source-generator name**, not an inverse operation. Define \(\theta\) as the least **semigroup congruence on \(S_0\)** containing exactly these generating pairs:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\mathrm{R1}:eaa'=e,&\mathrm{R2}:ebb'=e,\\
\mathrm{R3}:a'afg=fg,&\mathrm{R4}:b'bfa'eafg=fa'eafg.
\end{array}

The proof uses R1 and R4 only, as source congruence pairs with explicitly composable source contexts; it does not impose their coordinate expressions as relations on the ambient free band. ## Both identities hold already in the actual \(S_0\)

Compute coordinates without silently normalizing words. For the first identity,
\[
\begin{aligned}
aa'b&=(a_0\tau(a_1)b_0,1)=(a_0a_0b_0,1),\\
ab'b&=(a_0\tau(b_1)b_0,1)=(a_0b_0b_0,1).
\end{aligned}
\]
Rows I1-left and I1-right reduce both coordinates to \(a_0b_0\); their grades are both 1. Therefore
\[aa'b=ab'b\tag{I1}\]
as elements of \(S_0\), before any quotient relation is imposed.

For the second identity, \(eaf=(w,1)\) with \(w=e_0a_0f_1\). Since \(\tau(a_1)=a_0\),
\[
(eaf)a'(eaf)=(w\tau(a_1)w,1)
=(e_0a_0f_1a_0e_0a_0f_1,1).
\]
Rows I2-expand-yz through I2-contract-yz reduce that exact raw coordinate to \(e_0a_0f_1\); the grade remains 1. Thus
\[(eaf)a'(eaf)=eaf.\tag{I2}\]
Again this is an equality in the ambient semidirect product between two literal source words and therefore is an equality **in the subsemigroup \(S_0\)**. No R1--R4 relation was used in either band calculation.

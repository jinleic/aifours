## 4. Orbit coverage

The three symmetries joined by the union-find are genuine symmetries of the physical twirled moments $X_f=L\cdot\mathrm{avg}_g\,g\cdot Y_f$: (a) two-sided replica permutations by the letter stabilizer ($Y_f=|\ell\rangle\langle\ell|$ with $\ell\in\{aaab,aabb,bbba\}$ is invariant under independent ket/bra permutations of equal letters; $V_\sigma V_wV_\tau^\dagger=V_{\sigma w\tau^{-1}}$ componentwise); (b) simultaneous permutation of the three site components (the twirl averages over $S_3$, legitimate because $Z$ is site-symmetric and PSD/equality properties are preserved); (c) word inversion = operator transpose ($X_f$ is real symmetric because the twirl includes complex conjugation). Soundness needs only that every union-find merge is a true symmetry (a finer partition would still be sound); exactness of the partition is a bonus.

## 4. Reflection transport and forbidden support — SUPPORTED

**Sources:** S1 LF257–300; S5 LF7–10,19.

### The reflection automorphism is derived from the relations

Because \(E_i,F_i\) are locally nilpotent derivations,
\[
\sigma_i=\exp(E_i)\exp(-F_i)\exp(E_i)
\]
is well defined on every element. The derivation binomial identity proves that each exponential preserves brackets, and its inverse is the exponential with opposite sign.

On the universal \(e_i,h_i,f_i\) string,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\exp(E_i)e_i&=e_i,\\
\exp(E_i)h_i&=h_i-2e_i,\\
\exp(E_i)f_i&=f_i+h_i-e_i,
\end{aligned}
\qquad
\begin{aligned}
\exp(-F_i)e_i&=e_i+h_i-f_i,\\
\exp(-F_i)h_i&=h_i-2f_i,\\
\exp(-F_i)f_i&=f_i.
\end{aligned}
\]
Their composition gives
\[
\sigma_i(e_i)=-f_i,\qquad
\sigma_i(f_i)=-e_i,\qquad
\sigma_i(h_i)=-h_i.
\]

For arbitrary \(h\), the element
\[
h-\frac{\alpha_i(h)}2h_i
\]
commutes with both \(e_i\) and \(f_i\). Therefore
\[
\sigma_i(h)=h-\alpha_i(h)h_i.
\]
This calculation uses no parameter-dependent Cartan inverse.

The induced transformation of root characters is consequently
\[
\sigma_i(S_\beta)=S_{s_i\beta},
\qquad
s_i\beta=\beta-\beta(h_i)\alpha_i.
\]
Indeed, applying \(\sigma_i^{-1}\) to a Cartan element in the weight equation produces exactly that character. Invertibility gives equality of the spaces, not merely containment.

## 2. Results claimed, conditional on the imports

**Partial theorem A: four complete grade-four root components.** For every parameter and full Cartan realization in §1,
\[
 \mathcal R\cap S_{a,b,4}=0
 \quad\text{for }(a,b)\in\{(0,0),(1,0),(0,1),(1,1)\}.
\]
Equivalently \(J_{a,b,4}=\tau_{a,b,4}\) for each of those four components. This is a statement about the **whole component**, including all Levi-copy overlap; it is not a seed-only statement. The proof uses \(F_3(\mathcal R\cap S_4)\subseteq\mathcal R\cap S_3=0\), and proves actual \(F_3\)-injectivity on the two nontrivial components; it does not infer a radical conclusion from contraction invertibility.

**Partial theorem B: entire isotypic exclusions.** In the whole finite-dimensional grade-four Levi module, the complete \(L_{\rm low}(5,2)\)-isotypic and \(L_{\rm low}(3,3)\)-isotypic subspaces have zero intersection with \(\mathcal R\). Their respective multiplicities are
\[
 m_{(5,2)}=\begin{cases}1&s\ge4,\\0&s\in\{2,3\},\end{cases}
 \qquad
 m_{(3,3)}=\begin{cases}0&(r,s)=(1,2),\\r&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}
\]
Each statement includes **every copy**, not a preferred copy. No assertion is made about the other isotypics. With S4, type \(L_{\rm low}(6,0)\) is excluded as an imported consequence.

**Actual nonzero kernel obstruction.** For every \(r=3\), \(s\ge4\), and full realization,
\[

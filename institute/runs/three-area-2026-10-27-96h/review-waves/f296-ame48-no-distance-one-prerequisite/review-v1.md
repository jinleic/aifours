## 2. Reconstruction of the setup (S1|7)

### no-AME(4,2) spin-flip (S1|53) — **SUPPORTED** (self-contained)

Each 3-qubit marginal has purity 1/2 (Schmidt across 1|3 with maximally mixed single-qubit marginals), and with ρ_{ijk} = (1/16)Σ_{P on ijk} c_P P one gets (1/8)Σ_{P on ijk} c_P² = 1/2, i.e. weight-3 sum 4 − 1 = 3 per triple, hence total weight-3 Σc_P² = 12 over the four triples. Full purity gives Σ_all c_P² = 16, so the weight-4 sum is 16 − 1 − 12 = 3. For ρ' = σ_y^{⊗4} ρ* σ_y^{⊗4} = (1/16)Σ_P (−1)^{wt(P)} c̄_P P (using σ_y P* σ_y = (−1)^{wt(P)} P), ⟨ψ|ρ'|ψ⟩ = (1−12+3)/16 = −1/2 < 0, impossible for a pure-state overlap. **Independently confirmed**; this is the classical nonexistence of 2-uniform 4-qubit states, re-derived in-packet with no import.

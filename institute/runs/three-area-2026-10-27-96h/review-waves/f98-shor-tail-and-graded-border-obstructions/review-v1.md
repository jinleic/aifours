## Reconstructed common prerequisites

1. **Same-code actual borders.** In a hypothetical finite factor-complete UD code D containing the literal S, some pure-a atom exists because arbitrarily long a-runs occur inside D*, and it is unique: two distinct pure-a words commute and give different atom lists. Call it `a^n`; the literal atom `b` is also in D. Distinct border atoms `a^i b^k` cannot share `i mod n`: equalize a-runs with `a^n` and b-runs with copies of `b`; two distinct atom lists would spell the same word. The reverse padding gives the analogous uniqueness for `b^l a^j`. Thus all old A-heads and Y-tails occupy distinct residues, and multi-b border atoms have distinct residues outside the appropriate old anchors.

2. **Direct coverage.** For two representations `i+j mod n`, select actual `a^i b^k` and `b^l a^j` atoms, pad with b on the outside and with `a^n` in the middle, and obtain two lists spelling the same `b^L a^G b^K`, with positive G. UD forces the same actual border representatives, hence the same pair. For coverage, let H be the maximum D-atom length and choose `g>H` in a prescribed residue class. Factor-completeness embeds `b^H a^g b^H` in D*. The atom containing the last left-guard b has form `b^l a^j`, the one containing the first right-guard b has form `a^i b^k`, and all intervening atoms are `a^n`; hence `g≡j+i (mod n)`. Therefore `P+Q=Z_n` directly and `n=|P||Q|`. The twenty old A+Y representations are forced distinct; direct enumeration shows that this is impossible for n=1,…,25. Thus n≥26. No abstract one-b-only border has been substituted for P or Q.

3. **The one-b layer has n atoms.** Here is an independent elementary derivation of the conservation imported by the packing statements. For 0<t<1 give a word weight `t^(#a)(1−t)^(#b)` and write `f(t)` for the sum over D. UD makes the words in D^k distinct, so `f(t)^k≤kH`: the sum of all binary-word weights of any fixed length is 1, and the k-fold concatenations have lengths at most kH. Thus f≤1. If f<1, the total weight of D* is the finite geometric sum. But factor-completeness lets every word of length N be extended to a D* word by at most H−1 letters at each end. Each extension has at most 2H−1 possible N-length factors, and its weight is bounded below by a fixed positive multiple of the original word's weight. The weight in D* at lengths N through N+2H−2 would therefore be bounded away from zero, contradicting convergence of the D* weight sum as N→∞. Hence f(t)=1 for all 0<t<1. As a polynomial it has zero derivative at t=1. Its unique pure-a contribution differentiates to n, each one-b atom contributes −1, and atoms with at least two b's contribute 0. Thus `|B|=n`.

4. **One-b packing.** UD and padding also rule out two different one-b atoms with the same head and tail residues. For a fixed head h, combine each atom in its tail row with each one-b atom from the zero-tail column: equality of two seam sums would give different two-atom lists with the same padded raw word. Thus `|J_h| c_0≤n`. Summing over the four old heads and using |B|=n yields `N_out≥n−4 floor(n/c_0)`. The reversed argument gives `N_Y≥n−5 floor(n/r_0)` for the head-zero row. These bounds count actual atoms of the *same* D; a zero residue is not confused with literal exponent zero.

5. **Head prerequisite and Fourier filter.** The size-4, size-5 and size-6 P branches were reconstructed from their forced/colliding coverage equations; the size-7 branch was independently checked directly from the 10 literal tables in `frontier-20261028-eight-head-border-obstruction-v1.md`, 59 collision cells, all survivors and both branches. Evaluating the direct-tiling polynomial identity at primitive 2-, 4- and 8-th roots forces `Phi_2 Phi_4 Phi_8=1+X+...+X^7` to divide P, so P is uniform modulo 8; eight heads would give one per class, contradicting the old heads 0 and 8.

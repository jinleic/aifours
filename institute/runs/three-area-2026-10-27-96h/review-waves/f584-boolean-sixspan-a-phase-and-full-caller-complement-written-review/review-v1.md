## 5. Forced \(g\)-signs and saturation

\[

\[

\[
g_E=3\,1_{j=0}-2,\qquad
g_y=q_y(3b_y-2),\quad
b_y=1_{S_y}+2\,1_{F_y},
\]

with \(\sum_z b_y=4\). In particular, \(g=q_y\) on \(S_y\), \(4q_y\) on \(F_y\), and \(-2q_y\) elsewhere in an odd row.

For the E row,

\[

\[
\widehat b_y(n)=0.

\[
\sum_t b_y(0,t)=\sum_t b_y(1,t)=2.

- two F points in one row occupy opposite \(j\)-levels;

## 6. C/B and BB/CC alternatives

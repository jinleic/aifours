## Precise hypotheses and conclusion

Let M be the adjoint sl3 module with its positive Frobenius form, L(M) its free Lie algebra embedded in the tensor algebra, and E_i,F_i,H_i the finite-Levi derivations. Assume homogeneous nonzero lowest seeds q_j of degrees d_j whose **complete** finite-Levi modules are Q_j=U(n_+^sl3)q_j. Let I be the sum of the ideals generated by those complete modules. At fixed degree n and weight alpha, let P be the full simultaneous F1/F2 kernel and Pi its orthogonal projection.

1. E_i is adjoint to F_i; H_i is self-adjoint. Thus Pi E_i=0, and Pi kills other weights.
2. For every subspace W stable under all E,F,H, its orthogonal complement is stable and Pi(W)=P∩W. Full stability is necessary; the ideal generated by the seed line alone cannot replace the complete-module ideal.
3. The degree-n one-seed ideal is spanned by every nested bracket ad(u1)…ad(u_(n-d_j))z with z∈Q_j and every ordered tuple of basis letters u. Jacobi supplies the entire ideal, including mixed-seed brackets. For n<d_j the contribution is zero.
4. Applying Pi to the derivation identity moves each seed raising to the outer letters with a minus sign. Hence P∩I_n^(j) is spanned by Pi ad(u1)…ad(u_(n-d_j))q_j over **all ordered tuples of matching weight**. Sorted tuples alone are insufficient.
5. P∩I_n is the span of the union of those projected families. Use its rank; do not add the separate ranks as though the sum were direct.
6. With inner product linear in its second argument, coordinates of Pi(y) in a complete basis x_i of P are H^-1 b(y), H_ij=<x_i,x_j>, b_i=<x_i,y>. If the additional **unverified actual-source premise** S_n≅L_n(M)/I_n holds, then P→Pbar is surjective with kernel P∩I_n. The invariant orthogonal complement supplies the lift, and dim Pbar=dim P−dim(P∩I_n).

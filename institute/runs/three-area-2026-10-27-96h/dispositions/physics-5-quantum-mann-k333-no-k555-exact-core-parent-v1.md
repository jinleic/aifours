# Quantum Mann dichotomy: no orthonormal L(K3,3,3) in C^6, exact L(K5,5,5) and L(K7,7,7) cores, and a maximal QLS(10) with exactly five orthogonal transversals

**Disposition: same-harness written and exact-arithmetic support at the stated scopes.** An *orthonormal representation of* `L(K_{m,m,m})` *in* `C^{2m}` assigns a unit vector to each of the `3m²` edges of the complete tripartite graph so that the `2m` edges at every vertex form an orthonormal basis (extra orthogonalities between non-adjacent edges are allowed).

1. **Theorem A (adopted, unconditional).** `L(K_{3,3,3})` has no orthonormal representation in `C^6`. Proof chain: the frame identity `Σ_a P_a = (m/2)I`; Lemma 1 (three projections summing to `(3/2)I` satisfy `P_iP_jP_i = P_i/4`, have equal rank `k`, `dim H = 2k`, and are unitarily `|u_a⟩⟨u_a| ⊗ I` with angles `0, π/3, 2π/3`); Lemma 2 (a `3×3` array of unit vectors in `C^3` with orthonormal rows and columns is a phased Latin square of basis vectors); the graph-subspace Steps 3–5 producing `K_b = U Θ_b`, `L_c = −U Θ^c` with `det K_b = det L_c = 1`; and the determinant parity `(−1)^9 = 1` contradiction. Then `L` has no quantum orthogonal mate at any cardinality; already the nine contexts `R0 ∪ C0 ∪ T1` are jointly unrealizable.

# Ordered dihedral words: the projected p+2 odd-run selector

**Disposition: same-harness written support for Theorem P2 only.** For every odd prime p, take a word in the dihedral group with exactly `F=3p−2` reflection occurrences, `R≥p+1` rotation occurrences and singleton cyclic gaps (no two cyclically adjacent rotations). Delete all rotations, form the maximal constant-label **cyclic projection runs**, and suppose exactly `o_π=p+2` of these runs have odd length. Then the original linear word has an identity subsequence of exactly `2p` letters: either all are reflections or exactly two are rotations. Rotation exponent 0 is allowed, and the two rotations need only be distinct occurrences, not distinct values.

This is a projection-run theorem, not a historical actual-block statement. It does not establish L_RES, the ordered-Olson target, an old floor or profile lift, the unreviewed lower projection band, non-singleton gaps, or any case `o_π≥p+4`. ## Parent reconstruction

For `ρ_u=(u,0)` and `σ_a=(a,1)` with `(x,e)(y,b)=(x+(−1)^e y,e+b)`, an even reflection list has rotation exponent equal to its alternating label sum. A cyclic cut of an identity selection changes its product by conjugation, so a valid cut-order selection is still an identity in the original linear order.

For two selected rotation occurrences, split the intervening and complementary reflection lists into constant-label pieces. If the designated odd-selection pieces form T, the smallest selected reflection count is `|T|`; the largest is `F−e_T−(o_pc−o_T)`, and all intermediate counts with that parity are attainable independently by steps of two. Hence the capacity condition `o_T−e_T≥o_pc−p`, together with `|T|≤2p−2`, supplies exactly `2p−2` reflections. With rotation exponents u,v, literal multiplication gives `u+v+alt(T_X)+alt(T_Y)=0` when `|T_X|` is even and `u−v+alt(T_X)−alt(T_Y)=0` when it is odd; the latter sign is load-bearing.

If four odd projection-run labels have alternating sum zero, taking one from each and all `(F−o_π)/2=p−2` equal-reflection pairs gives `2p` reflections with product 1. Otherwise the cyclic `p+2` odd-run labels must consist of one label c three times and every other field element once: four equal labels or a triple plus a double immediately give a zero quadruple; doubled labels must interleave; and, if all multiplicities are at most two, each other doubled b forces a distinct absent value `2a−b`, one more absent value than available. This yields all field labels plus the three-c construction used by the selector.

The candidate and review partition the remaining cases into a run with two occupied gaps and, otherwise, sectors between one spare per odd projection run. The H cases cover both even/odd run parity and the γ boundary where a selected even piece contributes `o_T−e_T=2`. In the sector cases, the number λ of selected even runs split after an odd reflection is 0,1 or 2; both the two-rotations-in-one-sector and at-most-one-per-sector constructions satisfy the capacity and signed-product equations. The λ=2 construction needs six pieces, permitted for p≥5; at p=3 there cannot be two even runs. None of this identifies projected runs with actual blocks separated by occupied gaps.

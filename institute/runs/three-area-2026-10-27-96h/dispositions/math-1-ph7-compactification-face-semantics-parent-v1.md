## Supported implication

Let a nondegenerate six-dimensional dyadic leaf be produced by cyclic axis bisection at depth at least six, let `J` be a closed parameter interval, and suppose an exact tensor-product Bernstein representation of `P` on the closed leaf × `J` has nonnegative coefficients. If every parameter coefficient at every spatial vertex inside `(0,1)^6` is strictly positive, then `P` is strictly positive at every point of the leaf in `(0,1)^6`, for every parameter in `J`, including both endpoints and admissible internal faces.

The parent reconstruction is elementary: each spatial interval has an endpoint in `(0,1)` after its first bisection. Given an admissible point, choose its matching endpoint where it lies on a leaf face, and an admissible endpoint on each remaining axis. The chosen vertex's Bernstein basis factor is positive at that point; its parameter-coefficient combination is positive on all of `J`; all other terms are nonnegative. Degree-zero axes require no exception. Points on excluded `s_i=0` or `s_i=1` faces need not be strictly positive, and the infimum on the actual open domain can be zero. This is a sufficient test, not a necessary condition or a uniform positive bound on a closed leaf touching an excluded face.

The ordered clarification expressly exempts this QO/HO rule from the earlier blanket whole-closed-cell strict-bound sentence. NB, MC, SB and MP remain stated closed-domain obligations; R3's coefficientwise strict rule is a sufficient, potentially conservative rule. No selector implementation is verified here.

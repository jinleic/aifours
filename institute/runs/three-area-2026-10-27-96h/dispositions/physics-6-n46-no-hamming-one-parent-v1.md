# AME(4,6): a no-Hamming-one lemma and one N=46 branch

**Disposition: same-harness written support for the no-Hamming-one lemma, and computer-assisted support for the stated `(4,4,4,4)` branch exclusion.** Both are conditional on the classical no-OLS(6) theorem. The exact input is a normalized complex AME(4,6) state in a product basis with support `N=46` and exactly `t=26` coefficients of modulus `1/6`. Let `E` be the other 20 support positions. Then no two positions of `E` differ in exactly one coordinate. With the additional assumption that each `E` coordinate alphabet has size four, that residual-support branch is excluded by the stated exact finite enumeration. ## Written lemma

In every 2|2 cut the 26 maximal entries form singleton rows and columns. Removing them from the unitary flattening `6ψ` leaves a 10×10 unitary with exactly 20 entries, hence exactly two entries in every residual row and column. Consequently each of the six coordinate-pair projections of `E` has 10 cells, each with exactly two extensions.

If two `E` words differ only in the fourth coordinate, their two values saturate all three base-pair cells. The impossibility of two orthogonal columns sharing exactly one nonzero row propagates those two fourth-coordinate values along three unique partner operations on base triples. Their closure is a simple cubic graph `S`; the corresponding `S×{d₀,d₁}` is an isolated unitary block in all three cuts. Along any graph edge, nonzero coefficient ratios satisfy `r↦−1/conjugate(r)`, a fixed-point-free involution, so the graph is bipartite. Its even order `m` lies in `{6,8,10}`.

For `m=10`, choosing fourth-coordinate values by the bipartition adjoins 10 pair-injective words to the 26 maximal words, giving an OA(36,4,6,2), contrary to the imported no-OLS(6) theorem. For `m=8`, the complementary residual has pair-projection size 2 and the incidence bound `2r≤3(2−r)` forces every coordinate to be constant, contradicting that size. For `m=6`, the complementary residual has pair-projection size 4, is binary in every coordinate and yields an AME(4,2) state. Its self-contained Pauli spin-flip calculation gives the impossible nonnegative overlap `(1−12+3)/16=−1/2`. Thus no distance-one pair exists, independently of the four-alphabet condition.

## Finite `(4,4,4,4)` branch

With four residual symbols at each party, the degree profile at each party is either `X=(2,2,2,4)` or `Y=(2,2,3,3)`, and active-incidence counts permit at most one X party. Given the no-Hamming-one lemma, degree-2 fibers are parity four-subsets of a binary cube; higher fibers are pair-injective. The [independent same-harness review](../review-waves/f127-ame-n46-noh1-standalone/noh1-review-v1.md) reconstructs the normal form and reports its own exact enumeration: 2,975 normalized all-Y incidence patterns yield 40 support candidates, each violating unitary row-overlap necessity in all three cuts; 240 one-X patterns give no compatible eight-point high fiber. Its additional fully labeled enumeration reports 39,304 all-Y patterns with 512 candidates, all failing in every cut, and 3,375 one-X patterns with no matches. Thirty of the 40 normalized candidates contain cross-fiber distance-one pairs but are deliberately retained as a conservative superset, not treated as admissible supports. This branch conclusion relies on the stated reduction and correctness/coverage of the reviewer-written enumerator; it is not a formal proof certificate.

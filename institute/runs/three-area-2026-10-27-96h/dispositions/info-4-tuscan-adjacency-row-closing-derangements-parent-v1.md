## Adopted results

1. **Adjacency-row bridge.** In every T1(n), every ordered distinct pair a,b occurs as an adjacency in exactly one row rho(a,b). For a!=c, (a,c) is missing at distance2 iff rho(a,b)!=rho(b,c) for every b outside {a,c}. This equivalence uses only T1; T2 gives uniqueness of a witnessing middle b when the pair is not missing.
2. **Uniform target completeness.** Under T2, distance-two targets from a are precisely S minus {a} and its missing targets, each occurring once; the dual holds for incoming events. The numbers of misses are the penultimate and second-column multiplicities, respectively, with general-n notation alpha_(n−2) and alpha_1.
3. **Closing pairs as derangements.** In T2(11), for each first symbol c let a=E(c) and define psi_(a,c)=rho(.,c)^−1 composed with rho(a,.). It maps S minus {a} bijectively to S minus {c}, sends c to a, and restricts to a permutation pi_c of the remaining nine symbols. The closing pair (E(c),c) belongs to M iff pi_c has no fixed point. Exactly as many closing pairs are missing as these eleven pi_c are derangements; since |M|=|C|=11, M=C iff all eleven are derangements. No boundary-column-permutation assumption is made.
4. **Distance-sum limitations.** For ANY n permutation rows, sum over unordered pairs and rows of f(row distance) equals n sum_(d=1)^(n−1)(n−d)f(d). For T2(n), the simple classwise per-row extremum relaxation for additive f is affine in the number of distance-two events j; using k1+2k2=n(n−2) makes that relaxation independent of k0. This is NOT a theorem that nonlinear per-pair distance statistics, stronger class-dependent estimates or all distance-based methods cannot distinguish profiles. The mixed-moment attempt remains unproved; a global constant-weight sum U−cT is itself additive and constant.

## Parent reconstruction and corrections

The image intersection gives the two psi domains: if E(c)!=a, remove the successor b0 of a in row c and the predecessor c0 of c in lambda(a); if E(c)=a, the omitted rows coincide.

The complete [Suzuki manuscript](/Users/jinleic/repos/aifours/mathematics/evidence/three-area-20261027-96h/parallel-18/sun-coset8/frontier-20261028-suzuki-simple-family-v1.md) and [small-Ree manuscript](/Users/jinleic/repos/aifours/mathematics/evidence/three-area-20261027-96h/parallel-18/sun-coset8/frontier-20261028-small-ree-simple-family-v1.md) and the full [f57 review](/Users/jinleic/repos/aifours/physics/evidence/three-area-20261027-96h/parallel-18/ppt-schmidt44/frontier-review-f57-suzuki-small-ree-v1.md) were read. The reviewer found 0 fatal/major/minor defects at the explicitly conditional scopes. The parent separately reconstructed the following arguments.

**Suzuki.** If five indices among a hypothetical six, seven or eight disjoint cosets are even, the `Sun_5` import gives a pair gcd at least 5. The group order is not divisible by 3; hence an even gcd at most 7 can only be 2 or 4, a contradiction. At most four indices are even, so at least two are odd. The published order `|G|=q²(q²+1)(q−1)` has full 2-part `q²`. By the exhaustive maximal-subgroup list in [Héthelyi–Horváth–Petényi, arXiv:1404.1523v1, §2 and Theorems 3.1/4.1](https://arxiv.org/html/1404.1523v1), the only maximal type with that 2-part is a Sylow-2 normalizer of index `q²+1`. Every proper odd-index subgroup is contained in some maximal proper subgroup and has full 2-part, so its index is divisible by `q²+1>=65`. Two such indices therefore have gcd at least 65, contradicting the alleged bound of at most 7. This uses the actual cosets and indices, not a projected tuple or a common normalizer assumption.

**Small Ree.** If seven of eight indices are divisible by 3, `Sun_7` applied to those seven actual cosets gives a gcd at least 7, impossible when all pair gcds are at most 7 because a gcd of two multiples of 3 is itself divisible by 3. Thus at least two indices are 3-free. For `q>=27`, [Pierro, arXiv:1410.8702v2, §§2.1–2.2](https://arxiv.org/html/1410.8702v2) gives `|G|=q³(q³+1)(q−1)` and the exhaustive maximal-subgroup types. Only the parabolic has the full 3-part `q³`; its index is `q³+1`. Hence every proper subgroup of 3-free index has index divisible by `q³+1>=19684`, and the two 3-free indices have gcd at least that value. The separately checked [Alavi–Daneshkhah–Mosaed order statement](https://arxiv.org/html/1606.00049v1) agrees. The exceptional subfield `²G₂(3)` is handled only as a smaller maximal-subgroup type, not asserted to be a simple member of the stated family.

The parent independently ran exact integer arithmetic for odd field exponents `3,5,7,9,15` in both families. All stated listed orders divided the respective group order; in each case the claimed Sylow-normalizer/parabolic was the only listed type with the full 2- or 3-part, including the eligible proper subfields. Boundary outputs were `Sz(8): |G|=29120, [G:B]=65` and `²G₂(27): |G|=10073444472, [G:P]=19684`. This is a bounded arithmetic check, not a proof of either maximal-subgroup classification.

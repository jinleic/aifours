## Reconstruction

Positive Perron diagonal similarity makes G a zero-row-sum generator with the same diagonal rates, graph and cycle products; each triangle arc is at most its row sum, so `w=θu₁u₂u₃` with `0<θ<1` (θ=1 would close the triangle, contradicting irreducibility). In the class the only vertex-disjoint cycle families are the empty family, the triangle, and single cycles of length ≥5; hence `[y⁶]=e₁`, `[y⁵]=e₂`, `[y⁴]=e₃−w`, `[y³]=e₄−w(O+t)`, where `O` sums two outside rates and `t,r` are the sum and product of the remaining two. Matching against `D·(y²+py+q)` gives two equations `E4=E3=0` affine in `(t,r)`. Cramer's rule gives `t=tn/den`, `r=rn/den`, and the cubic `N(θ)=−19683(tn²−4rn·den)` is free of `t,r`. Its cubic Bernstein coefficients satisfy `b₀,b₁,b₃>0` and `4b₁b₃−3b₂²>0` for all positive five rates, so `N>0` on `[0,1]`. If `den≠0`, `t²−4r=−N/(19683den²)<0`, impossible for real rates; if `den=0`, `N=−19683tn²≤0`, impossible.

## Mathematical reconstruction

Fix a state and product basis. Two-uniformity makes each of the three 2|2 flattenings `6ψ` unitary. Let `P` be the `t` entries of modulus `1/6`, and `E` the remaining nonzero entries. Every element of `P` exhausts its row and column in **each** cut. Removing those singleton rows and columns leaves an `n×n` unitary, `n=36−t`, in every cut, with no singleton row or column. Thus, for each of the six coordinate pairs, `E` has exactly `n` projected pairs, every occurring pair has at least two extensions in `E`, and the total support satisfies `N ≥ t+2n = 72−t`.

For a coordinate of `E` using `r` values, each fiber must vary in at least two of the other three coordinates. Counting those variations against the `n` pair values for each other coordinate gives `2r ≤ 3(n−r)`. The ≥42 argument excludes residual sizes `n=0,1,2,3,4,5`: respectively by no-OLS(6), the singleton rule, the incidence inequality, a self-contained four-qubit spin-flip contradiction, and a three-fiber 2×2 rectangle/XOR contradiction. The spin-flip calculation gives Pauli weight-square sums `(1,0,0,12,3)` and the impossible overlap `(1−12+3)/16=−1/2`; no separate AME(4,2) nonexistence theorem is imported. Hence `t≤30` and `N≥42`.

If `N=42`, then `t=30`, `n=6`, and `E` has 12 entries. Every residual pair value has exactly two extensions. The incidence inequality bounds each residual alphabet by 3, and at most one alphabet has size 2. With four ternary alphabets, three normalized four-point fibers would require at least six pairwise correlation incidences while at most three are possible. With one binary and three ternary alphabets, the size-six projection conditions force `E=S×{b₀,b₁}` for six triples. Residual unitarity makes the shared-coordinate graph of these triples bipartite: along an edge the nonzero coefficient ratios obey the fixed-point-free involution `r↦−1/conj(r)`. Choosing binary symbols by a 2-coloring adjoins six pair-distinct words to the 30 saturated words. Their 36 pairwise-injective projections produce two orthogonal Latin squares of order 6, contradicting the named import. This explicitly handles the bipartite residual configuration; a support search alone does not eliminate it.

If `N=43`, `t=30` gives a six-dimensional residual with `2n+1=13` entries, impossible because the neighbors of its unique degree-three row must use the same two of its three columns; `t=29` gives an odd-dimensional residual with `2n=14` entries, impossible because a two-regular unitary support is a disjoint union of full 2×2 blocks. These are the only remaining cases. Therefore `N≥44` at the named import.

Then Y satisfies every original FD and, for every vertex subset A,

  H(Y_A)=Σ_{g∈K} H(X_{g(A)}).

Its entropy profile is K-invariant, H(Y_V)=|K|H(X_V), and max_v H(Y_v)≤|K|max_v H(X_v).

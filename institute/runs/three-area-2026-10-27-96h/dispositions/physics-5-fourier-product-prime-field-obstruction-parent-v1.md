# Fourier-product QLS of order 2p over fields where Φ_p is irreducible have no transversal

**Disposition: same-harness written support.** Let `p≥3` be prime, `F⊂ℂ` a conjugation-stable field over which `Φ_p` is irreducible, and `K` any `2p×2p` complex Hadamard matrix with entries in F. The Fourier-product array `ψ_(r,c)(t)=ζ_{2p}^{rt}K_(t,c)/√(2p)` has no three pairwise orthogonal cells in distinct same-parity rows and distinct columns; hence it has no orthogonal transversal. Corollary: for `p∤q`, no `BH(2p,q)` Fourier product has a transversal, since `Φ_p` is irreducible over `ℚ(ζ_q)` by the classical irreducibility of cyclotomic polynomials over ℚ. For order 10 this covers every q not divisible by 5, including q=6, 12, 18 and 42. Nothing follows for `p|q`, reducible `Φ_p`, non-Fourier products, unrestricted QLS, priority or novelty.

## Reconstruction

For orthogonal cells in distinct same-parity rows r,s and distinct columns c,d, put `Δ=s−r=2k` with `k≢0 (mod p)`, `z_t=conj(K_(t,c))K_(t,d)∈F` (conjugation-stability) and `w_u=z_u+z_{u+p}`. K-column orthogonality gives `Σ_u w_u=0`; cell orthogonality gives `Σ_u ζ_p^{ku}w_u=0`. Because k is invertible mod p, the exponents permute the p-th roots; because `Φ_p` is the minimal polynomial of `ζ_p` over F and has degree p−1, every F-linear relation among the p-th roots is a multiple of the all-ones relation, so all `w_u` are equal, and `p·w=0` in characteristic zero forces `w_u=0`. With unimodular entries this reads `h_d(u)=−h_c(u)` for `h_c(u)=K_(u+p,c)/K_(u,c)`. Three such cells on columns c₁,c₂,c₃ would give `h_{c₂}=h_{c₃}=−h_{c₂}`, impossible for unit values. A transversal uses p≥3 even rows, so it contains such a triple.

# Source-bound original definitions — physics-3

Standard definitions (parent statement). States are density operators rho on C^4⊗C^4. Partial transpose Gamma on the second factor; PPT means rho^Gamma>=0. Schmidt rank of a pure vector = its tensor rank as a 4x4 coefficient matrix; Schmidt number SN(rho)=min over pure-state decompositions of the max Schmidt rank. SN(rho)>=3 iff there is a Hermitian W with <v|W|v> >= 0 for EVERY complex v of Schmidt rank <=2 and Tr(W rho)<0. Original target: an exact 4x4 PPT state with SN>=3 together with such a separator W, or a proof that EVERY 4x4 PPT state has SN<=2.

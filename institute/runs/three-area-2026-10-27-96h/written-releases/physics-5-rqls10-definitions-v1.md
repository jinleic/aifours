# Source-bound original definitions — physics-5

Standard/registered definitions (parent statement). A QLS(10) is a 10x10 array of unit vectors in C^10 whose every row and every column is an orthonormal basis. Its cardinality is the number of distinct cells up to global phase. Target: a QLS(10) with 100 pairwise nonparallel rays (maximal cardinality) that is 1-resolvable: the 100 cells can be partitioned into ten transversals (each meeting every row and column once) each of which is itself an orthonormal basis; or an unrestricted proof that no such array exists.

Primary admitted for definition confirmation and PRIOR-CLAIM screening ONLY: Zhang and Cao, arXiv:2608.29201v2, “Resolvable quantum Latin squares with maximal cardinality” — read ONLY the abstract, the definitions section and main-theorem STATEMENTS (which orders are settled).

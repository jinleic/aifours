EA-invariance (why one bent f1 per EA class suffices for Lemma 6):
Let f1' = f1∘L ⊕ l and f2' = f2∘L ⊕ l, where L is an affine permutation of F_2^6 and l is an affine function. Then f1' is bent, d(f1', f2') = d(f1, f2) and nl(f2') = nl(f2), because nl is invariant under affine permutations of the input and under adding affine functions. So the statement "d(f1,f2) in {4,5,6} => nl(f2) <= 26 for every bent f1" holds for all f1 iff it holds for one representative of each EA class of 6-variable bent functions.

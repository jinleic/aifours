--- Verbatim transcription (zh), book p.403 ---
非线性度和弹性是用于对称密码系统的布尔函数最重要的两个密码学指标，它们彼此之间存在着相互制约关系，不可能同时达到最优。弹性函数的非线性度的紧上界是目前悬而未决的公开难题。

我们把平衡函数看做弹性函数的一个特殊情形。Seberry等[2]曾给出具有偶数个变元的n元平衡布尔函数非线性度的一个上界2^{n−1}−2^{n/2−1}−2，但至今没人证明这个界是紧的。n≥8时，这个界是否能达到至今还不知道。8元(10元)平衡布尔函数的非线性度能否达到118(494)是目前受到关注的公开问题[3]。当n为奇数且n≤7时，布尔函数的最大非线性度是2^{n−1}−2^{(n−1)/2}，并且可以是平衡的；当n为奇数且n≥9时，n元平衡布尔函数的最大非线性度还不知道。

关于偶数个变元的高非线性度平衡布尔函数的一般构造方法，目前最好的结果由Seberry[2]和Dobbertin[4]分别独立给出，十几年过去了，这个问题没有大的突破。如何构造非线性度严格大于2^{n−1}−2^{(n−1)/2}的奇数个变元的非Patterson-Wiedemann[5]型平衡布尔函数也是目前颇受关注的问题。

--- English translation ---
p.403: Nonlinearity and resiliency are the two most important cryptographic criteria for Boolean functions in symmetric ciphers. They constrain each other and cannot both be optimal. The tight upper bound on the nonlinearity of resilient functions is an unresolved open problem.
We regard balanced functions as a special case of resilient functions. Seberry et al. [2] gave the upper bound 2^{n−1}−2^{n/2−1}−2 on the nonlinearity of balanced n-variable Boolean functions for even n, but nobody has proved that it is tight. For n ≥ 8 it is still unknown whether the bound can be attained. Whether the nonlinearity of balanced 8-variable (10-variable) Boolean functions can reach 118 (494) is an open problem currently attracting attention [3]. For odd n ≤ 7, the maximum nonlinearity of Boolean functions is 2^{n−1}−2^{(n−1)/2} and can be attained by balanced functions; for odd n ≥ 9, the maximum nonlinearity of balanced n-variable Boolean functions is still unknown.
For even n, the best general constructions of highly nonlinear balanced functions were given independently by Seberry [2] and Dobbertin [4]; more than ten years later there has been no major breakthrough. How to construct balanced Boolean functions of an odd number of variables that are not of Patterson–Wiedemann [5] type and have nonlinearity strictly greater than 2^{n−1}−2^{(n−1)/2} is also a problem attracting much attention.
p.404: Reference [10] holds that the Sarkar–Maitra bound can be improved and conjectures that for m < ⌊n/4⌋ (n even, f m-resilient), N_f ≤ 2^{n−1}−2^{n/2−1}−2^{⌊n/4⌋+m−1}. Definitions: N_f is the minimum Hamming distance from f to the 2^{n+1} affine functions; a balanced m-th order correlation-immune function is m-resilient; a balanced function is 0-resilient.

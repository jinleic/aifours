## Exact comparison
- **Standard form [INFERENCE, parent-checked].** C_(2p)=C_2xC_p, so LS concerns even-length zero-sums of a C_p sequence. If |Y|>=3p-1, EGZ in C_p twice gives two disjoint p-term C_p-zero-sums whose union is a length-2p zero-sum of Y, forbidden by (ZP_kappa) because kappa<=p-1. Hence (ZP_kappa) forces |Y|<=3p-2, and G-LS only concerns 3p-2e+1<=|Y|<=3p-2.
- **Structure theorems do not reach this range [INFERENCE from quoted scopes].** Savchev-Chen (n-zero-free in Z_n, length >3n/2-1), the Gao-Li-Yuan-Zhuang manuscript (length >=3p at n=2p) and inverse EGZ (length 4p-2) all start above the cap. The length-set invariants screened (s_L, s_dN, eta, s_<=k, E_k, disc, the Fan et al. property) assert existence of some zero-sum, not the conditional LS implication.
- **Normal-form attribution.** Zeng-Yuan (EJC 30(4) P4.21, 2023) quotes Gao-Peng-Wang (JCTA 118 (2011) 613-622): "Under the assumption that |Σ(S)| < min{|G|,2|S|−1}, Gao, Peng and Wang showed that such a sequence S must be strictly behaving." Lev v3 does not cite GPW. Whether GPW's hypotheses (including any length/zero-sum restriction h(S)) cover G-LS's application is unverified, since GPW itself was not read.

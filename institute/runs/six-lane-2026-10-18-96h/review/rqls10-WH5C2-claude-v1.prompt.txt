147: **Lemma 7 (Kramers parity).** Let Theta be antiunitary on C^n. Then Theta^2 is unitary and commutes with Theta;
148: Theta maps the lambda-eigenspace of Theta^2 onto the conj(lambda)-eigenspace; the (-1)-eigenspace E_(-1) is
149: Theta-invariant and has even dimension; and if n is odd, +1 is an eigenvalue of Theta^2.
150: Proof. Theta^2 (Theta y) = Theta(Theta^2 y) = Theta(lambda y) = conj(lambda) Theta y. On E_(-1), Theta is antiunitary
151: with Theta^2 = -1; for y != 0, <y, Theta y> = <Theta(Theta y), Theta y> = <-y, Theta y> forces <y, Theta y> = 0, so
152: span{y, Theta y} is a Theta-invariant plane whose orthogonal complement in E_(-1) is again Theta-invariant: induction.
153: The non-real eigenvalues pair off with equal multiplicities, so dim E_(+1) = n mod 2. QED

`<prod z_j^a_j, prod z_j^b_j> = delta_(a,b) * prod a_j! * w_j^a_j`.

### Derivation of normalization and phase

Let `p_i` and `d_a` be Gram–Schmidt raw states before normalization, so their Fock squared norms are `v!*hp_i` and `(v-1)!*hd_a`. Their raw creation overlap is `v!*(Y_k)_(i,a)`. Thus

`<normalized parent| a†_(f-j) |normalized daughter> = sqrt(v)*(Y_k)_(i,a)/sqrt(w_j*hp_i*hd_a)`.

At positive weight `fv-5`, the multiplicity of that total spin equals the difference of adjacent weight-space dimensions. For degrees `v=3,4,>=5`, those dimensions give `5-4=1`, `6-5=1`, and `7-5=2`. 3. **Seniority.** The scalar pair annihilator preserves total spin but lowers particle degree by two. The target weight `fv-5` exceeds the maximum weight `f(v-2)` because `2f>5`; its action is therefore zero. All parent covariants are harmonic and have seniority `v`. The same reasoning holds for daughters with deficit at most five. Equivalently, derivatives commute with the constant-coefficient pair annihilator.
4. **No missing daughters.** Coupling a removed spin `f` to total spin `L=fv-5` requires `L'>=L-f=f(v-1)-5`, while any `(v-1)`-boson state has `L'<=f(v-1)`. Thus only deficits `0..5` occur. Deficit one has no highest-weight state; the exact low-degree dimensions give the domain table. At `N=v`, the derivative of a harmonic parent is harmonic, so no lower-seniority daughter sector has been omitted.
5. **Completeness.** The number identity `sum_m a†_m a_m=v` and Clebsch–Gordan orthogonality imply `sum_k R_k R_k^T=I` in the orthonormal multiplicity basis. A separate exact rational identity checked by the script is

## The problem in one paragraph

Coefficients of fractional parentage (CFPs) expand a symmetric N-particle state into one
particle (or a pair) times states of the remaining N−1 (N−2) particles. With them, one- and
two-body matrix elements become simple sums. The book points out that there is no general
analytic formula. CFPs are generated by a recursion in N that becomes unmanageable for the
mesoscopic range N ≈ 10–1000. Only spin-1 bosons have a closed form (Bao & Li 2005; the j = 1
result is older, Marić & Popović-Božić 1980). Other cases are called "very hard". The goal is to
crack the recursion beyond spin 1.

## Precise formulations attacked (and what they do not settle)

For N spin-f bosons in total-spin eigenstates labelled by seniority v (the O(2f+1) irrep), a
missing label α, and total spin L:

1. **The N-recursion, every f.** Closed-form N-dependence of all one-body CFPs (Theorem A).
2. **Infinite families as functions of v.** These are
   (a) near-stretched states L = fv − 2, fv − 3 for every f (Theorem B), and the first
       multiplicity family L = fv − 4 for every f ≥ 3 (Theorem B');
   (b) spin 2, L = 2v − k for k ≤ 8 (Claim C);
   (c) spin 2, low total spin L ≤ 5 at any seniority (Theorem E1, Conjecture E2).
3. **The multiplicity question.** Can the missing label be chosen so that CFPs keep the Bao–Li
   product form? For spin 2, in the five two-dimensional families k = 6, 8, 9, 10, 11, no
   rational direction does it with linear factors from an explicit finite set T (Theorem D).
   For every spin f ≥ 3, the pair-first basis does it in the first multiplicity family (Theorem B').

**Not settled:** a uniform closed form for every (v, α, L) of spin 2; general f beyond the
near-stretched families; spin-3/2 fermions (not attacked); universal signs in the older
low-spin phase convention. **E2's squared coefficients and both block weights are now
proved for all admissible v.** Two-body CFPs follow from the one-body ones by the verified
chain formula where all required one-body coefficients are available (RESULTS Claim G).

## Key formulas

* Theorem A (D = 2f+1): c(N v → N−1, v−1) = √[v(N+v+D−2)/(N(2v+D−2))]·r, and
  c(N v → N−1, v+1) = (−1)^{L+L'} √[(N−v)(v+D−2)/(N(2v+D−2))]·√[dim_v(2L'+1)/(dim_{v+1}(2L+1))]·r',
  where r and r' are N-independent O(D) ⊃ SO(3) isoscalar factors.
* Theorem B, e.g. r²(fv−2 → f(v−1)) = 2(fv−1)/(v(2fv−2f−1)). Full table in RESULTS §B.
* Theorem E1: total weight of a scalar parent = (2v+D−2)(v+D−3)/(Dv(2v+D−4)).
* Theorem D, k = 6: no rational direction has Bao–Li-type CFPs over T. The trio-first basis
  attains one irreducible quadratic, 18v²+91v−190. It is minimal only within the class whose
  linear support lies in T.
* Theorem B': for f ≥ 3 and L = fv − 4, every CFP in the pair-first basis is a product of factors
  linear in v. The only non-obvious factor is g = f(2f−1)v + 3(4f²−10f+5).

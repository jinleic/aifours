https://doi.org/10.1088/0253-6102/53/6/01 | Symmetries and Algebras of a (2+1)-Dimensional MKdV-Type System
ABSTRACT: In this paper, a (2+1)-dimensional MKdV-type system is considered. By applying the formal series symmetry approach, a set of infinitely many generalized symmetries is obtained. These symmetries constitute a closed infinite-dimensional Lie algebra which is a generalization of w∞ type algebra. Thus the complete integrability of this system is confirmed.

https://doi.org/10.1088/1674-1056/19/5/050202 | A new (2+1)-dimensional supersymmetric Boussinesq equation and its Lie symmetry study
ABSTRACT: According to the conjecture based on some known facts of integrable models, a new (2+1)-dimensional supersymmetric integrable bilinear system is proposed. The model is not only the extension of the known (2+1)-dimensional negative Kadomtsev–Petviashvili equation but also the extension of the known (1+1)-dimensional supersymmetric Boussinesq equation. The infinite dimensional Kac–Moody–Virasoro symmetries and the related symmetry reductions of the model are obtained. Furthermore, the traveling wave solutions including soliton solutions are explicitly presented.

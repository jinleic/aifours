Abstract A novel (2+1)-dimensional Sawada-Kotera type system is considered. The
existence of three-soliton and four-soliton solutions with constraints on wave num-
bers is confirmed. Other intriguing solutions, such as the long-range interaction be-
tween a line soliton and a y-periodic soliton, are also presented based on the Hirota
formalism. By extending the multilinear variable separation approach to the fifth-
order nonlinear evolution equation, various localized excitations are introduced, in-
cluding solitoff, dromion, and an instanton excited by three resonant dromions. In
addition to these localized excitations, the general fusion or fission type N-solitary
wave solution is obtained, and the resonant Y-shaped soliton and the resonant T -type
soliton interaction in shallow water are explored graphically.
Keywords Hirota bilinear form · Multisoliton solution · Multilinear variable
separtion approach · Localized excitations · Fusion and fission phenomena

also referred to as Sawada-Kotera (SK) equation, is a system of crucial importance in
soliton theory [1, 2]. As a member of the Liouville field hierarchy [3], the SK equation
has played a significant role in conformal field theory, quantum gravity and shallow
water flows [4]. It has been widely and profoundly studied. For instance, Satsuma
and Kaup derived its Bäcklund transformation and conservation quantities [5], while
Oevel established the bi-Hamiltonian structure and recursion formula [6]. Recently,
a supersymmetric SK equation has been proposed [7], demonstrating remarkable in-
tegrability through the existence of a Lax representation.
    Konopelcheno and Dubrovsky introduced the (2+1)-dimensional generalization
of the SK system in the form of [8]
                                                      Z              Z
       wt + (w xxxx + 15ww xx + 15w3 + 5w xy + 15w wy dx) x − 5 wyy dx = 0. (2)

It is straightforward that if w is independent of the variable y, then the above equation
(2) would reduce to the well-known SK equation (1). The integrability of Eq. (2) has
undergone comprehensive examination from various perspectives. Geng [9] provided
a rigorous investigation, deriving both Darboux and Bäcklund transformations. Addi-
tionally, the exploration of the connection between the singularity manifold equation
and corresponding pseudopotential equations was undertaken in [10]. Lou [11] intro-
duced a novel Bäcklund transformation and a related quintic linear equation, result-
ing in the discovery of a new hierarchy of multiple soliton solutions. Furthermore,
the analysis of the transitions and mechanisms of nonlinear waves governed by Eq.
(2) was conducted using characteristic line and phase shift analysis by Tian [12].
     In this work, we deal with another type of (2+1)-dimensional Sawada-Kotera
(2DSK) system

                       ut + u xxxxx + 5(uv xx + 2u xx v + 3uv2 ) x = 0,
                       u x − vy = 0.                                                         (3)

It is obvious that setting u = v = w(x, y, t) and rescaling y → x would degenerate the
2DSK system (3) into the SK equation (1).
     The paper is organized as follows. In section 2, the existence of an N-soliton so-
lution is conjectured based on the confirmation that at least a four-soliton solution
exists with additional conditions on wave numbers. In section 3, the periodic soliton
solution as well as the interaction process between a line soliton and a y-periodic
soliton are considered. In section 4, the multilinear variable separation approach is
applied, localized excitations, such as the solitoffs, dromions and dromion-like in-
stantons are constructed. Furthermore, general fusion or fission type N-solitary wave
solutions and solitary waves on moving cnoidal wave background are also obtained.
Section 6 is devoted to the summary and discussions.

5. Summary and discussions

In conclusion, a new (2+1)-dimensional generalization of the SK system is proposed.
By employing the Hirota bilinear method, the existence of three-soliton and four-
soliton solutions, with constraints on wave numbers {ki , li }, is confirmed. Further-
more, the multilinear variable separation approach is extended to the 2DSK system
to generate a variable separation solution. It is found that the arbitrary function q
is determined by a nonlinear Riccati equation, and the physical field u is precisely
expressed by the universal formula applicable to all multilinear variable separable
16                                                       J.Y. Wang, Y.Q. Yang, X. Y. Tang and Y. Cheng


systems. By choosing the arbitrary function Y appropriately, abundant localized ex-
citations, such as solitoffs, dromions, and instantons, can be constructed. In addition
to localized excitations, fission and fusion phenomena, as well as solitary waves on a
cnoidal wave background, are also revealed. Based on our study, we can conjecture
that the nonlinear version of the following bilinear system

                             Dy (D2n+1
                                  x    + Dt ) f · f = 0,        (n ≥ 1)

is solvable under the multilinear variable separation approach. It is noted that n = 1
and n = 2 correspond to the (2 + 1)-dimensional ANNV equation and the 2DSK
system, respectively.

## 4. Lemma 20 (doubled triangles; e = 0)
**Statement.** Let ψ be an AME(4,6) state with e = 0, with non-classical support N. Fix a coordinate i and two values
a ≠ a′. Write points as (x_i; z), with z ∈ [6]³ the other three coordinates. Let z1, z2, z3 satisfy:
- z1 and z2 agree in coordinate c1 and differ in c2 and c3;
- z2 and z3 agree in c2 and differ in c1 and c3;
- z1 and z3 agree in c3 and differ in c1 and c2;
where {c1, c2, c3} are the three coordinates other than i. Then the six points (a; z_m), (a′; z_m), m = 1, 2, 3, do
not all lie in N.

*Proof.* Suppose they do.
- **Facts.** At e = 0, every line meeting N contains exactly two points of S, both in N (L42-9, L42-2(a)). Every
  component of a reshaping that meets N is a 2×2 block (L42-7(a), (c)).
- **A closed block for the pair (z1, z2).** Take the reshaping whose row pair is (i, c1).
  - The row (a, z1_{c1}) contains (a; z1) and (a; z2), since z1 and z2 agree in c1. So it is a hole line with
    exactly these two points.
  - Likewise the row (a′, z1_{c1}) contains exactly (a′; z1) and (a′; z2).
  - The column of (a; z1), i.e. its values in coordinates c2, c3, contains (a; z1) and (a′; z1), so it holds exactly
    these two. Likewise the column of (a; z2) holds exactly (a; z2) and (a′; z2).
  - Hence the four points form a closed 2×2 component.
- **Its congruence.** By L42-10, φ(a; z1) − φ(a; z2) − φ(a′; z1) + φ(a′; z2) ≡ π (mod 2π), up to a global sign,
  which does not matter since −π ≡ π. With δ(z) := φ(a; z) − φ(a′; z), this reads δ(z1) − δ(z2) ≡ π.
- **The other pairs.** In the same way, the pair (z2, z3), using the reshaping with row pair (i, c2), gives
  δ(z2) − δ(z3) ≡ π. The pair (z1, z3), with row pair (i, c3), gives δ(z1) − δ(z3) ≡ π.
- **Contradiction.** (δ1 − δ2) + (δ2 − δ3) − (δ1 − δ3) = 0, but the three congruences give π + π − π ≡ π
  (mod 2π). ∎

**Encoding.** `exp_triangle.triangle_clauses` emits, for every coordinate i, every pair a < a′ in V_i, and every
triangle (z1, z2, z3) inside Π_{k≠i} V_k with the agreement pattern above, the clause
¬(a;z1) ∨ ¬(a′;z1) ∨ ¬(a;z2) ∨ ¬(a′;z2) ∨ ¬(a;z3) ∨ ¬(a′;z3).
- The orientation c1, c2, c3 is fixed as the other coordinates in increasing order. Every triangle with one shared
  coordinate per edge, the three distinct, is emitted exactly once: name its vertices so that the c1-edge is z1z2.
- Points outside Π V_i are never in N (L42-8(c)), so restricting to Π V_i loses nothing.
- Self-check at 15:2x (exploration): for profile {6,6}{2⁶}³ at t = 24, all 108,000 clauses have the stated shape.

## 5. Completeness with J and W
This is the realizable-support argument of `review/SUPPORT_LOWER48.r2.md` §3, extended.
- Let ψ be a normalized AME state with parameters (t, e, profile), with support N and officers T.
- For g ∈ H = ⟨Γ⟩, g·ψ is AME with support g(N) and officers g(T).
- Let N* = g(N) be lex-least in H·N. Then (N*, g(T)), with base auxiliaries, line indicators and lex chains,
  satisfies the base, J (§3), W (Lemma 20, if e = 0) and Lex_Γ.
- If the final CNF is certified UNSAT, N* is a recorded solution, and it is excluded by an exact check. That
  contradicts realizability.

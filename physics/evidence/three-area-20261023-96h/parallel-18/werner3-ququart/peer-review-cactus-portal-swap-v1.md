## 2. Exact original model and all pair classes

The frozen objective is endpoint-inclusive coverage of ALL original shortest paths on a finite simple connected UNWEIGHTED cactus, with unrestricted selectable vertices and arbitrary exact k. For the SW graph, all item/fixture interiors are disjoint and their only common vertex is v, initially unselected. A shortest path between different interiors MUST go through v. Its geodesics are precisely Cartesian products of local shortest paths to v; total and avoiding path counts multiply, including every local tie. Internal shortest paths never become shorter by an excursion through another branch: that excursion must return to v and has positive length.

Every unordered pair belongs to exactly ONE of these classes:

1. Both endpoints in one H_i, possibly one endpoint v: counted in beta_i.
2. Both endpoints in F, possibly one endpoint v: counted in beta_F.
3. Endpoints in two DISTINCT item interiors: contribution alpha_i alpha_j.
4. One fixture-interior and one item-interior endpoint: contribution z_F alpha_i.

A pair {v,x} belongs only to x's own piece, not to every piece containing the shared portal. The nonexistent pair {v,v} is never inserted. Summing these disjoint classes proves S2(SW2), including the full `Q+z_F S` cross mass.

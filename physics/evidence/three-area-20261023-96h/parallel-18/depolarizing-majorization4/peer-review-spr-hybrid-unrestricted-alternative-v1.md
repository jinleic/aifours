## 3. Literal original box, polynomial pairing and all-corner reduction

**Reconstructed: LF23–41.**

At s=iw with t=w², the denominator is `a(iw)=E_a(t)+iw O_a(t)`. The alternating signs in the source follow directly from powers of i: the real coefficients are−a1,a3,−a5,a7 and the iw coefficient is−t³+a2t²−a4t+a6. The same decomposition for an unrestricted numerator gives `b(iw)=E_b(t)+iw O_b(t)`. Multiplication by the conjugate denominator therefore has real part `E_a E_b+t O_a O_b`, exactly P_(a,b). A Hurwitz denominator has no imaginary-axis zero, so its modulus-squared denominator is strictly positive for every finite t>=0.

For fixed b and t, P is affine in the seven freely varying denominator coefficients; the degree-seven leading denominator coefficient is fixed at1. A box with finite closed coordinate intervals is the convex hull of its corners, including degenerate intervals and duplicate removal. Its convex weights depend only on the selected a. Thus strict positivity of P at every corner and every t gives strict positivity for every a and t using the SAME numerator. Conversely every corner belongs to the box. No frequency-dependent numerator or correlated-family hull is substituted.

All coefficients of a real monic Hurwitz polynomial are positive: each real negative root contributes s+c with c>0, and each conjugate pair contributes s²+2c s+(c²+d²) with c>0. Their product has positive coefficients. Hence m1>0 and all midpoint anchors are defined. In particular a7(v)>0 at every corner.

## 6. Exact hybrid alternative and at-most-five atoms

**Reconstructed: LF139–175. Named imports: finite-dimensional compactness, nearest-point separation and Carathéodory.**

The generator set is a finite union of continuous images of[0,1], hence compact. Its finite-dimensional convex hull Z_K is compact. Linear images commute with convex hull, so

`T^T Z_K=conv{W4(v,x)}`.

Zero belongs to this image iff some point of Z_K belongs to ker(T^T), giving exactly the intersection condition in(H).

If zero is in the compact convex image, Carathéodory in ambient R4 gives a representation by at most five actual generators. After dropping zero weights, all remaining weights are positive and sum to1. The atoms remain actual corners and compactified frequencies; endpoints and repeated atoms are permitted. Rank deficiency of T can reduce the necessary atom number but does not invalidate the safe ambient bound5.

If zero is outside the compact convex image, choose a nearest point y. For any point w in that set, the segment from y toward w stays in the set, and nonnegative derivative of squared distance at its minimum gives `y dot(w−y)>=0`. Thus `y dot w>=||y||²>0`. Theta=y and eta=||y||² give the common strict separator for all generators. Evaluation at infinity makes its leading coefficient positive; positive rescaling gives the claimed normalization.

A zero convex combination of W4 contradicts every strict separator by summing its inequalities. The alternatives are consequently exclusive and exhaustive. This proves the written hybrid feasibility alternative; it does NOT show that all-member Hurwitzness excludes its obstruction branch.

**Conic-versus-convex check.** Z_K is the compact CONVEX hull, not a cone whose origin is present by definition. H0 has nonnegative weights, not all zero, normalized to total1. A nontrivial finite nonnegative conic balance can indeed be normalized to such a convex balance, but neither the trivial conic origin nor a closure-only limiting origin is an obstruction certificate here. The nearest-point proof is on the compact convex image and does not rely on closure of an unbounded conic hull.

### Soft-channel expansion

For any mean-zero real f on the finite positive-probability support, choose `|epsilon| ||f||_infinity < 1` and define

`P(W=+|X=x) = (1+epsilon f(x))/2`.

Then W is unbiased. In bits,

`H_2((1+t)/2) = 1 - t^2/(2 ln 2) + O(t^4)`.

Finite support gives a controlled remainder as epsilon tends to zero. Hence

`I(W;X_V) = epsilon^2 E[f^2]/(2 ln 2) + O(epsilon^4)`.

Conditioning on `X_i` replaces f by `E[f|X_i]`, so the same expansion applies to each `I(W;X_i)`. Dividing the HC inequality by its positive quadratic factor and taking the limit gives exactly

`2 sum_i ||E[f|X_i]||_2^2 <= 3 ||f||_2^2`.

For nonconstant `X_i,X_j`, take mean-zero unit functions u and v and choose the sign of v so `r=E[uv]>=0`. For `f=u+v`,

`||f||_2^2 = 2+2r`,

`||E[f|X_i]||_2^2 >= |E[fu]|^2 = (1+r)^2`,

with the analogous j bound. The other terms are nonnegative. Therefore

`4(1+r)^2 <= 6(1+r)`.

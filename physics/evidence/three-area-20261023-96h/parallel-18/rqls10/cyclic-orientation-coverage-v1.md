## 2. An explicit safe symmetry reduction gives at most21 orientation cases

### Burnside calculation

The group elements have these cycle types on the eight nonzero labels:

- identity: one element, `1^8`;
- ±90-degree rotations: two elements, `4^2`;
- inversion: one element, `2^4`;
- reflections: four elements, `1^2 2^3`.

Numbers of perfect matchings fixed by these types:

1. Identity:105.
2. `4^2`: either both4-cycles are matched internally by their opposite pairs (one choice), or they are matched to each other by an equivariant offset (four choices), total5. A mixed internal/external choice cannot be invariant because each4-cycle is transitive on its vertices.
3. `2^4`: each2-cycle is internally matched or two2-cycles are cross-matched in one of two ways. The count is `1 + binom(4,2)*2 + 3*2² = 25`.
4. `1²2³`: the two fixed vertices must be paired with each other. The three2-cycles contribute `1+binom(3,2)*2=7` matchings.

Thus the number of orbits under this explicit8-element stabilizer is

    (105+2*5+25+4*7)/8 = 168/8 = 21.

## 2. Theorem GLX: partner exclusion at EVERY sparse intersection, isoclinic or not

Proof. Since S_v commutes with P_C, rangeP_C = span(p,p') with p = rangeP_C cap S_v and p' = rangeP_C cap S_v-perp. These two lines are exactly the two actual C+ cells at v.

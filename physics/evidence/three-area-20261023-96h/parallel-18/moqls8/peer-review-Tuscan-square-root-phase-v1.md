## 6. Pair and every-proper-subset capacity constraints

Each P_j alternates the two Q_j blocks A_j,B_j. Thus an unordered pair u,v can receive either orientation only from a class separating the pair. Covering u→v and v→u in one Hamilton12 class would make a2-cycle, impossible. At least two classes must separate each pair. Encoding the balanced block partitions by binary words gives pairwise Hamming distance at least2, but it supplies neither orientations nor compatible phases and is only necessary.

For every nonempty proper S⊂Ω, put a_j=|S∩A_j| and b_j=|S∩B_j|. In P_j at most min(a_j,b_j) internal arcs can go from A to B, and the same bound holds from B to A. Moreover a directed Hamilton12 cycle induces no directed cycle on a proper subset S: its internal edges form disjoint paths, so at most |S|−1 are internal. Hence

`Σ_j min(|S|−1, 2 min(a_j,b_j)) ≥ |S|(|S|−1)`.

The right side is exact because the P arc cover contains every ordered pair internal to S once. This argument handles **every** size1 through11, without assuming Q arc-disjointness.

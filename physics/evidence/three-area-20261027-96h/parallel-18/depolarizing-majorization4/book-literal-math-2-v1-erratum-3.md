## Base v1 U1 — character before 列

**RESOLVED: 子**, not 序. Image PDF158/p142, body line B06; see crop `p158-b06` and its pixel rectangle above. The visible phrase is **“零和子列”**. This resolves the uncertainty mark attached to base v1 LF44/LF107/LF134. The bold \(\mathbf Z_n\) typography below incorporates the already separate erratum-1 correction.

**Corrected verbatim Chinese, B05–B07:**

> Grynkiewicz证明了下述加权EGZ定理：设 \(a_1,\cdots,a_{2n-1}\in\mathbf Z_n\) 且 \(w_1,\cdots,w_n\) 为 \(\mathbf Z_n\) 中元的零和子列，则有 \(n\) 个不同的下标 \(i_1,\cdots,i_n\) 使得加权和 \(w_1a_{i_1}+\cdots+w_na_{i_n}\) 等于零。

**Corrected literal English, same span:**

> Grynkiewicz proved the following weighted EGZ theorem: let \(a_1,\cdots,a_{2n-1}\in\mathbf Z_n\), and let \(w_1,\cdots,w_n\) be a zero-sum subsequence of elements in \(\mathbf Z_n\); then there are \(n\) distinct indices \(i_1,\cdots,i_n\) such that the weighted sum \(w_1a_{i_1}+\cdots+w_na_{i_n}\) equals zero.

**Corrected quoted inventory span, B06:** “设 \(a_1,\cdots,a_{2n-1}\in\mathbf Z_n\) 且 \(w_1,\cdots,w_n\) 为 \(\mathbf Z_n\) 中元的零和子列”

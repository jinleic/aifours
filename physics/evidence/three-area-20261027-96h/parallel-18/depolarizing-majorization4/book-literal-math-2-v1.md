## Sentence-aligned literal English

[EN-P1 ↔ CN-P1 / B01–B02]
[The printed opening is the incomplete fragment “合方法”; leading wording is not present.] … method proved the Kemnitz conjecture put forward in1983: an element sequence of length \(4n-3\) in the additive group \(\mathbb Z_n\oplus\mathbb Z_n\) must contain a zero-sum subsequence of length \(n\).

[EN-P2-S1 ↔ CN-P2-S1 / B03]
The EGZ theorem has many different generalizations.

[EN-P2-S2 ↔ CN-P2-S2 / B03–B05]
Olson proved that, for any element sequence \(a_1,\cdots,a_{2n-1}\) of length \(2n-1\) in a multiplicative group \(G\) of order \(n\), one can always find \(n\) distinct indices \(i_1,\cdots,i_n\) such that the product \(a_{i_1}\cdots a_{i_n}\) equals the identity element of \(G\).

[EN-P2-S3 ↔ CN-P2-S3 / B05]
He further conjectured that one can additionally require \(i_1<\cdots<i_n\).

[EN-P2-S4 ↔ CN-P2-S4 / B05–B07]
Grynkiewicz proved the following weighted EGZ theorem: let \(a_1,\cdots,a_{2n-1}\in\mathbb Z_n\), and let \(w_1,\cdots,w_n\) be a zero-sum subsequence[U1, AMBIGUOUS glyph] of elements in \(\mathbb Z_n\); then there are \(n\) distinct indices \(i_1,\cdots,i_n\) such that the weighted sum \(w_1a_{i_1}+\cdots+w_na_{i_n}\) equals zero.

[EN-P2-S5 ↔ CN-P2-S5 / B07–B10]
Sun Zhiwei obtained the following covering-form generalization of the EGZ theorem: suppose the system of residue classes \(\{a_1(\bmod n_1),\cdots,a_k(\bmod n_k)\}\) covers each integer a number of times equal to \(2q-1\) or \(2q\), where \(q\) is a prime power; then, for any given \(c_1,\cdots,c_k\in\mathbb Z_q\), there is a nonempty subset \(I\) of \(\{1,\cdots,k\}\) such that \(\sum_{i\in I}c_i=0\) and \(\sum_{i\in I}1/n_i=q\).

[EN-P2-S6 ↔ CN-P2-S6 / B10–B11]
Note that, when \(k=2q-1\) and \(n_1=\cdots=n_k=1\), this result is the EGZ theorem on \(\mathbb Z_q\).

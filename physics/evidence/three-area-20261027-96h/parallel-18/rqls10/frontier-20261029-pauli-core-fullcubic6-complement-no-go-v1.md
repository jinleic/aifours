# The positive Pauli 4D core has no 6D complement in the full cubic-phase alphabet

**COMPUTER-ASSISTED AUTHOR CANDIDATE, UNREVIEWED.** Native `/bin/date`: **Thu Oct 29 01:04:02 PDT 2026**. This rules out a larger concrete 6D alphabet for ONE specific positive 4D parity core. It does NOT rule out arbitrary C6 vectors, other 4D cores, or general 1RQLS10.

## Claim and target graph

Take the 30-ray/15-basis 4D configuration and its three groups of five in `frontier-20261029-pauli60-positive-tripartite-4d-core-v1.md` SHA **734e0ff4397534dd8e486f9375984ddb6b3bcbdc51530e57505621726571af96**. Its context graph G4 has degree4 and is a subgraph of K5,5,5; let G6 be the complementary degree6 subgraph of K5,5,5.

There are NO 45 distinct rays in ANY unitary copy of the projective 6D alphabet `{0,1,omega,omega²}^6`, `omega³=1`, that assign one ray to each G6 edge and form an ONB at each vertex. Consequently this literal 4D parity core cannot be completed to a 4+6 balanced-quotient parity core with a 6D sector from that alphabet.

The tripartition matters. `frontier-20261029-pauli60-core-tripartition-uniqueness-v1.py` SHA **bb391357ee6d687e864d78f44b2cb5c3a1e3280c6b1abfe4591ec2dc9db7cb80**, result SHA **de00d6f656fc98256432669ca079213b8f93f5eaadfdb59ee7b55f74d2088fe2**, exhausts all graph 3-colorings: 30 after fixing vertex0's color, but only two are balanced 5+5+5 and they differ by permuting the other two colors. An independent SMT replay `frontier-20261029-pauli60-balanced-color-smt-replay-v1.py` SHA **357bafa42dcd3c314c8ceeeb187542963e5aad02675305b2955d4ceb9dedd774**, result SHA **2bd74ff429d769ac5b57eb6cb806957e3419a14dc2799be47922741b7e19f3d3**, recovers those two. Thus the balanced tripartition is unique up to family permutation, and the exclusion is not an artifact of the particular R/C/T naming. The initial development assertion that ALL 3-colorings were balanced was false and corrected before publishing; the output records this distinction.

## Exhaustive proof and independent replay

Projective normalization gives **1365** distinct source rays. Exact orthogonality regenerates **1609** six-ray bases, involving834 rays. The source symmetry subgroup generated by coordinate permutations, coordinate multiplications by omega and conjugation has five basis orbits with sizes `1,60,972,90,486`; the 90-basis orbit has no single-ray-intersection neighbors and cannot represent any degree6 target vertex. The remaining four root representatives cover all possible images of a fixed target vertex.

For each representative, the proof search assigns source bases to all15 target vertices. Target adjacency requires exactly one common source ray; nonadjacency requires none. It excludes a source ray from appearing at a third vertex, as required for the 0/2-incidence core, and never assigns a source basis twice. `frontier-20261029-cubic6-fullphase-complement-proof-v1.py` SHA **f1a319178b9ca6ddab794082abd329a46e0cc38b0492b97fcc612551782d35e8**, result SHA **246ceea122d922db1882023772d9dd99274d3def1f91ad2c30bf3223d71004f7**, records an explicit failure tree for every possible assignment under all four roots: **51246 nodes**, no solution.

The independently written `frontier-20261029-cubic6-fullphase-proof-replay-v1.py` SHA **15eff89d8c23e7da6149ef8ac39ffc8e2a7fb7fd53ce9986c6aee34899ce9f1f**, result SHA **1f4bdc6034c90f9f5b030b02ce99e0f2965d43bb2c18d31b2e02bfaad379402d**, generates source rays directly from support/relative-phase choices, computes Grams in `Z[omega]` and all bases with pivoted Bron–Kerbosch, independently reconstructs the symmetry orbits including all six phase generators, derives G6 directly from the independent original-4D-vector replay, and checks EVERY child of EVERY literal proof-tree node. The new computation excludes the LARGER full cubic-phase alphabet but only for this one 4D context graph.

## Boundary

No 6D complement has been constructed or ruled out beyond these stated scopes.

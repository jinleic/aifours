## Property dictionary and attribution limits

- **IF:** conditional first frequency moment equals phase derivative. The book explicitly calls this a useful Wigner property; LPA94 §III-D equations(30) and adjacent prose formulates it and explicitly warns it conflicts with positivity and marginals for many analytic signals.
- **GD:** conditional first time moment equals minus spectral phase derivative. The book names it; LPA94 §III-B gives the Gaussian conditional peak/mean interpretation. The complete general formula and sign are proved in `../proof.md` using the stated Fourier convention.
- **Strong finite support:** zero wherever either marginal vanishes, including internal holes; LPA94 §II, p.2698, explicitly defines this and distinguishes it from classical finite-support properties. We use the invariant measure/a.e. formulation, not arbitrary point values.
- **Weak finite support:** no mass outside a containing interval of signal/spectral support. This is weaker than strong support and follows from nonnegativity plus marginals. The exact terminology in C89 has not been independently inspected.
- **Time/frequency shift covariance and reciprocal scaling covariance:** explicitly defined and proved here. These are standard desirable transformation laws, but until C89/C95 are read we do not attribute a specific numbered Cohen axiom or claim a verbatim list from those sources.
- “Clear physical meaning” is not equivalent to any one of these lists by fiat. IF is a particularly faithful *candidate* because the book singles it out, but LT97 explains why it need not represent local frequency content in a multicomponent signal.

### F1 audit (all items pass)
- **Recurrence.**
  - Since `r(0)=0`, `e_i − θe_i(0) = r − Ju_i − φa_i`, so `u_{i+1} = Au_i + ℓ(r − φa_i)`.
  - Using `ℓJ·Σ_{k<i}A^k = I − A^i`, the physical-error formula is exact.
  - The fixed-start telescoped form `A^i(r−φa) − θa` is also exact.
- **Bessel identity.**
  - I re-derived it independently. Termwise integration gives `Γ(n+m+α+1)/(m!Γ(m+α+1))·(−x)^m`, which is absolutely summable.
  - Kummer's identity `M(n+α+1,α+1,−x) = e^{−x}M(−n,α+1,x)` together with `L_n^{(α)} = C(n+α,n)·M(−n,α+1,x)` closes it.
  - So nothing rests on the DLMF citation.
  - The `2^{−1/2}` factor is right. `Γ(n+7/4)/n! = O((n+1)^{3/4})` and `Γ(n+3/4)/n! = O((n+1)^{−1/4})` are right.
- **Binomial identities.** Each checks coefficient by coefficient:
  - `A^n(t²/2) = Σ_k C(n,k)(−ℓ)^k t^{k+2}/(k+2)! = t²L_n^{(2)}(ℓt)/((n+1)(n+2))`
  - `A^n1 = L_n(ℓt)`
  - the kernel of `A^nJ` is `L_n(ℓ(t−s))`
- **Uniformity at t=0.** Multiplying `(ℓt)^{−5/4}` by `t²` leaves `t^{3/4}e^{ℓt}`, and `s^{−1/4}` is integrable. The bounds hold uniformly on `[0,T]`, and no Laguerre asymptotic is used.
- **Delayed quadratic.** `Jφ = (f − D_h f)/h` checks on both pieces. `D_h` commutes with J and is a sup-norm contraction, so `S ≤ (2C/h)·Σ(n+1)^{−5/4}`.
- **Nominal convergence.** `‖A^nJv‖∞ ≤ C'(n+1)^{−1/4}‖v‖∞`, and this holds even for `v ∈ L∞`.
- **Fixed-start tail.** `φ = Jφ'` with `φ' ∈ L∞`, so `A^iφ → 0` uniformly and `e_i → −θa` uniformly.
- **BV remark.** Correct: `Jφ = φ'(0+)f + ∫D_σ f dμ(σ)`.

### F2 audit (all items pass)
- **Common initial interval.** Every `ξ_j` lies in X, so all vanish on `[0,τ/2]`.
- **Continuous noise.** `X ⊂ C[0,T]`.
- **Single infinite sequence.** UBP is applied on the Banach space `ℓ∞(X)`.
- **Measured vs. physical output.** They differ only by the bounded term `ξ_N(t_*)`.
- **Norm lower bound.** `‖F_N‖ ≥ Σ_{k≤N}|h_{q,k}(t_*−τ)|` is correct. It needs `0 ≤ ψ_ε ≤ 1`, which is unstated and should be added.
- **Stronger than stated.**
  - Because `e_i(0) ≡ 0`, the proof covers any correction `R_i(e_i(0))` with `R_i(0)=0`. That includes nonlinear, trial-varying, or discontinuous θ.
  - Read the other way, the rectifier is a bystander. F2 is Theorem B for the P base law, transported to measurement noise.

### Design classification
- **Not a finite-basis design: confirmed.** The update acts on full traces. The fixed shape θ restricts neither the target nor the input.
- **Not a full-state design: true but vacuous here.**
  - It is true as an information pattern: the law uses only the measured `e_i(t)` and `e_i(0)`.
  - But for `ẏ=u`, and for the diffeomorphic example `y = x + α sin x`, `y(0)` *is* the initial state.
  - So F1 gives no evidence about plants where the output differs from the state.
  - At q≥2 the initial derivative errors `y^{(k)}(0)` are invisible to `e_i(0)`.
- **Not a full arbitrary-noise robustness claim: confirmed.** F1 is noise-free and F2 is a negative result. The only overreach is the §1 sentence about process noise (Obj. 1).

## Substantive objections

### 1. §1 says Section 4 rules out removing the *process*-noise exclusion. It does not.

**The gap.**
- F2 covers measurement noise only.
- Its ramp witness does not carry over to process noise. An input disturbance `ẏ_i = u_i + d_i` gives `u_{i+1} = Au_i + ℓ(r − φa_i − Jd_i)`. That is exactly measurement noise `ξ_i = Jd_i`, which is equi-Lipschitz.
- For ℓ>0, a step in d yields coefficients `−ℓA^{k−1}(t²/2)(t_*−τ) = O(k^{−5/4})`. These are summable, so the construction proves nothing.

**Repair at q=1** (all ℓ≠0, all θ):
- Take zero starts and r=0. Then `e_i(0)=0`, so the rectifier is inactive.
- The kernel of `−ℓA^{k−1}J²` is `−ℓ(A^{k−1}J1)(σ) = h_{1,k}(σ)`. Hence

```
y_N(t_*) = ∫₀^{t_*} d_N + Σ_{k=1}^{N} ∫₀^{t_*} h_{1,k}(t_*−s) d_{N−k}(s) ds
```

- Each trial's `d_j` is chosen independently. So on `ℓ∞(X)` the functional's norm is the **sum** of the per-trial L¹ norms.
- By Tonelli and Theorem B:

```
‖F_N‖ = (t_*−τ/2) + Σ_{k=1}^{N} ∫₀^{t_*−τ/2} |h_{1,k}(σ)| dσ  →  ∫₀^{t_*−τ/2} Σ_k |h_{1,k}(σ)| dσ = ∞
```

- UBP then gives one bounded sequence of continuous disturbances, all vanishing on `[0,τ/2]`, with `sup_N|y_N(t_*)| = ∞`.
- For q≥2 the kernel becomes `J^{q−1}h_{q,k}`, which needs its own argument.
- Either add this argument, or restrict the §1 sentence to measurement noise.

*Byproduct:* with `ξ_j = Jd_j`, the measured output is this same functional. So at q=1, F2 has a witness with `sup_j‖ξ_j'‖∞ < ∞`, which is uniformly Lipschitz and hence equicontinuous. The §4 disclaimer about equicontinuity is unnecessary at q=1.

### 2. "Sharp noise obstruction" is unsubstantiated

F2 shows failure on the whole continuous class, but it locates no boundary. The manuscript's own §2 estimate supplies one at q=1, ℓ>0.

Suppose `ξ_j = J²g_j` with `sup‖g_j‖∞ ≤ M`. Equivalently, process noise `d_j = Jg_j`. Then `ξ_j(0)=0`, and

```
r − y_N = A^N r − a_N + ℓ Σ_j A^{N−1−j} Jφ a_j + ℓ Σ_j A^{N−1−j} J³ g_j
```

The kernel of `A^kJ³` is `A^k(t²/2)`. Therefore:

`‖r−y_N‖∞ ≤ ‖A^N r‖∞ + (1+ℓS)ε₀ + ℓMT·C·Σ(k+1)^{−5/4}`

Combined with Obj. 1, the threshold sits between noise in `J(L∞)`, which fails, and noise in `J²(L∞)`, which is tolerated. Either state this, or drop the word "sharp".

### 3. The fixed-start wording understates the residual

The limit `e_i → −θa = −(1−t/h)a` is nonzero on all of `[0,h)`, not only at the uncontrolled point t=0. It should read: "exact on [h,T]; residual ramp on [0,h)."

### 4. The robustness constant cannot be made uniform in h, and this is intrinsic

- `‖Jφ_h − J1‖∞ ≤ h/2`, and each `A^n` is bounded on C. So `‖A^nJφ_h‖∞ → ‖A^nJ1‖∞` as h→0.
- By Fatou and Theorem B: `liminf_{h→0} S_h ≥ ℓ^{−1}·Σ_n|h_{1,n+1}(t)| = ∞`.
- This is not slack in the bound. With r=0, for each finite i, exactly: `sup_{|a_j|≤ε₀}|e_i(t)| = ε₀(1 + ℓΣ_{k<i}|A^kJφ_h(t)|)`.
- The same Fatou argument sends the supremum over i to ∞ as h→0.
- So the layer width h is a genuine trade-off between accuracy and robustness. F1 cannot be pushed to exact tracking on (0,T].

### 5. "Nominal convergence" is output-only; inputs fail to converge and can be unbounded

This matters only if the original specification includes input boundedness or input convergence.

- **No uniform input convergence when v(0)≠0.** `θ(0)=1` gives `u_{i+1}(0)=u_i(0)`, so `u_i(0) ≡ 0`. Uniform `u_i→v` would force `v(0)=0`.
- **Fixed start with a≠0 and h<T.**
  - A uniform limit `u_∞` would satisfy `Ju_∞ = r − φa`.
  - That means `u_∞ = v − aφ'` a.e., which jumps by `a/h` at t=h.
  - This contradicts continuity of a uniform limit.
- **Possible unboundedness, even nominally.**
  - `‖A^n‖_{C→C} = 1 + Var_{[0,ℓT]}L_n`.
  - This is unbounded, growing like `n^{1/4}` by Fejér's asymptotic. That asymptotic is not one of the manuscript's tools.
  - Since nominally `u_i = (I−A^i)v`, Banach–Steinhaus gives a continuous v with `sup_i‖u_i‖∞ = ∞`.

### Minor
- Theorem D and the DLMF references cannot be checked from the embedded text. F1 does not depend on them.
- The "genuinely nonlinear" plant has input–output map exactly `ẏ=u`. The transfer of F1 to it is tautological, and it adds no evidence about nonlinear plants. The symbol α is also overloaded with the Laguerre index.

`★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────`
- **One regularity ladder explains all three results.** P-recursion responses decay like `k^{−3/4}` when the forcing is *one* integral of a delta or bounded function. Examples: the pure-P step `a·1`, the F2 ramps, and Lipschitz noise `Jg`. They decay like `k^{−5/4}` at *two* integrals: the rectified ramp `φ` and noise `J²g`. Only the second rate is summable. Rectification works by lifting the initial shift exactly one rung; noise that vanishes at t=0 is never lifted, hence F2.
- **Why pointwise divergence suffices for UBP.** Trials are independent, so a functional `Σ_j λ_j(ξ_j)` on `ℓ∞(Y)` has norm `Σ_j‖λ_j‖`. Pointwise divergence plus Tonelli then forces `sup_N‖F_N‖=∞`. A single fixed φ gets no per-k sign freedom, which is why the manuscript rightly does not claim its BV condition is necessary.

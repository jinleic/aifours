**Steps checked:**

  - Bounded sets in L^∞=(L^1)^* are weak-\* sequentially compact because L^1 is separable.

`★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────`
- The hard case q=m=1, a>0 contains classical P-type ILC, K=γz/(1−z).
  - There, H = e^{γt/2}·exp(−(γt/2)(1+z)/(1−z)), which is a constant times the atomic **singular inner function**.
  - H is bounded on D, has radial limit 0 at z=1, and has modulus e^{γt/2} everywhere else on the circle. It lies in H^∞ but not in the disk algebra.
  - That is why boundedness alone cannot settle this case and the horocycle (continuity) argument is genuinely needed.
  - As a consistency check, h_n(t)=L_n^{(−1)}(γt) decays only like n^{−3/4}, which is not summable.

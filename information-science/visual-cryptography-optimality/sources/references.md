# Primary-source ledger

Checked 2026-10-14 (PT). A successful metadata/abstract fetch is not a full-text proof audit. No claim of exhaustive literature coverage or novelty clearance is made. Search results are discovery aids, not decisive evidence. The exact finite claims are certified locally, without importing an inaccessible paper's theorem.

## Authoritative problem and mathematical baselines

1. **Liu Feng**, “视觉密码方案的最优化问题 / On the Optimization of Visual Cryptography Schemes,” in *10000 Selected Problems in Sciences: Information Science*, Science Press (2011), printed pp.409–410, PDF pp.430–431. **Verified visually against both page images**, copied to `book/pdfpage-0430.png` and `book/pdfpage-0431.png`. The five requested components (four extrema plus attaining constructions/trade-off) agree with the assignment transcription. The article itself does not define basis versus arbitrary collections or a contrast normalization; those must be resolved against its references.

2. **G. Ateniese, C. Blundo, A. De Santis, D. R. Stinson**, “Visual Cryptography for General Access Structures,” *Information and Computation* **129** (1996), 86–106. DOI [10.1006/inco.1996.0076](https://doi.org/10.1006/inco.1996.0076). **Full author manuscript fetched and read** from [Stinson's page](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~dstinson/visual.html), [PostScript](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~dstinson/papers/IC.ps). Local files: `ateniese-1996.ps/.pdf/.txt`. Load-bearing locations: Definitions2.1/2.2 (collections/basis); Section2 (strong versus weak structures and relative difference); Section3 (n-out-of-n, expansion 2^(n-1)); Theorem7.3 (2-out-of-n, minimum m with binomial(m,floor(m/2))>=n); Section9/Table1 (small structures). The text extraction of Table1 row13 is malformed, so that row is not a comparison anchor.

3. **C. Blundo, A. De Santis, D. R. Stinson**, “On the Contrast in Visual Cryptography Schemes,” *Journal of Cryptology* **12** (1999), 261–289. DOI [10.1007/s001459900057](https://doi.org/10.1007/s001459900057). [ePrint 1996/013](https://eprint.iacr.org/1996/013); [author PostScript](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~dstinson/papers/contrast.ps). **Full author manuscript fetched and relevant proofs read**, local `blundo-1999.ps/.pdf/.txt`. Theorems4.1–4.2 give alpha*=floor(n/2)ceil(n/2)/(n(n-1)) for 2-out-of-n, even allowing weak/collection schemes. Theorems4.4–4.9 characterize optimum-contrast expansion using designs; Theorem4.7 gives, for n=3 mod4, m>=n with equality iff a Hadamard matrix of order n+1 exists. Section6 contains the concatenate-collections-to-basis argument for contrast; Theorem6.4 closes the two 1996 n4 gaps at m=6 and alpha=1/6. Our general-access concatenation proof is an elementary extension of that argument, not a claimed new discovery.

4. **C. Blundo, P. D'Arco, A. De Santis, D. R. Stinson**, “Contrast Optimal Threshold Visual Cryptography Schemes,” *SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics* **16** (2003), 224–261. DOI [10.1137/S0895480198336683](https://doi.org/10.1137/S0895480198336683). [Author PostScript](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~dstinson/papers/COTVCS.ps). **Full author manuscript fetched**, local `blundo-2003.ps/.pdf/.txt`; Section3's LP description and Lemma3.5 were checked. It gives a symmetry-reduced threshold LP (2n+2 variables before further simplification), not a proof that arbitrary-access finite-expansion trade-offs are all resolved. No numerical formula extracted from damaged PDF math is used without an independent check.

5. **M. Krause, H. U. Simon**, “Determining the Optimal Contrast for Secret Sharing Schemes in Visual Cryptography,” *Combinatorics, Probability and Computing* **12** (2003), 285–299. DOI [10.1017/S096354830200559X](https://doi.org/10.1017/S096354830200559X). [Primary ECCC TR00-003 record](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2000/003/) and [PDF](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2000/003/download). **Primary ECCC abstract verified**: threshold bounds 4^(-(k-1)) <= C(k,n) <= 4^(-(k-1))*n^k/[n(n-1)...(n-k+1)], asymptotic fixed-k limit, and an approximation-theory connection. PDF saved as `krause-simon-2000.pdf`; its extracted text is severely garbled and is **not** proof-audited. This source is threshold-specific; do not cite it as settling arbitrary access structures.

Its improvements must not be imported into the book's black-white deterministic model.

12. **M. Cati, D. V. Pasechnik**, “A database of constructions of Hadamard matrices,” [arXiv:2411.18897](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.18897) (2024). **Primary abstract verified**: real Hadamard existence at every multiple of four is presented as a conjecture, with checked constructions/tables over specified ranges. Together with Blundo et al. Theorem4.7 this supplies a concrete existing design-existence barrier, not a proof from failed literature searches that the entire 2026 VCS literature has no solution. The 2026 preprint [arXiv:2608.18053](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18053), whose abstract was also checked, concerns classification of **complex** order-six Hadamard matrices and does not resolve this real all-orders conjecture.

## Deliberate nonclaims

- No primary-source reduction establishing NP-hardness/NP-completeness for the exact present model and representation was verified. We do not make that claim. An ILP encoding is not a hardness proof.
- No verified published conjecture is refuted by the n<=5 tables. The 2-out-of-4 trade-off is already classical.
- A source saying a numerical optimum is “computable by LP” is not a full structural solution of the original book question.
- The search covered primary threshold/general-access baselines and representative 2011–2026 developments. It is not an exhaustive negative survey; the overall OPEN assessment is evidence-limited.

Printed p.731:
“所谓排队的镇定问题，是指在系统有随机外部服务请求到达时如何设计调度策略，在最大化系统服务能力的同时保证系统内所有队列长度均值的有界。”
Translation: The queue stabilization problem asks how to design a scheduling policy, in the presence of random exogenous service requests, that maximizes the system's service capability while keeping the mean lengths of all queues bounded.

The arrival-rate formula is lim_{t→∞} (1/t) sum_{τ=0}^{t−1} E{A_i(τ)} = X_i.

Printed p.732, equation (1):
Q_i(t+1) = max{Q_i(t) − μ_i I_(σ_i=1), 0} + A_i(t).
The immediately following text calls μ_i(t) the current service rate of link i, depending on the time-varying channel, and sets it to zero if the link is not scheduled. The displayed equation omits the time argument on μ_i; the proof uses the unambiguous potential-service notation c_i(t)σ_i(t) instead of relying on that notational redundancy.

Printed p.732:
“定义[2] 一类排队系统{Q_i(t)}如满足下式，被称作具有强稳定性（strong stability）。”
Equation (2), read from the image:
limsup_{t→∞} (1/t) sum_i sum_{τ=0}^{t−1} E{Q_i(τ)} < ∞.
Translation: A queueing system {Q_i(t)} is strongly stable if the limsup of the time average of the sum of expected queue lengths is finite.

“针对无线网络这类排队系统设计调度算法，同时保证系统稳定与资源有效利用，其难点在于：①实际网络系统中，[X_i]与[C_i]无法精确已知，因此，保证排队系统强稳定的充分和必要条件[2]无法直接验证；②满足镇定队列、资源有效利用的调度算法必须是简单的、分布式的、无记忆的策略。”
Translation: The difficulty in designing scheduling algorithms that provide stability and efficient resource use is that (1) arrival and service rate vectors are not known exactly, so strong-stability conditions cannot be directly checked; (2) a policy that stabilizes queues and efficiently uses resources must be simple, distributed and memoryless.

Equation (3): maximize over σ the sum_i Q_i(t) μ_i(t), subject to σ_i∈{0,1} for every i, and σ_i σ_j=0 whenever i interferes with j. The prose identifies the combinatorial independent-set optimization and its centralized/exponential-complexity difficulty.

Printed p.733:
“有别于确定性调度算法，随机接入方式是另一类可以分布式逼近问题（3）的解的一类算法。最近，人们发现节点接入概率可以基于局部队列信息自适应调整，同时达到镇定队列、降低冲突的目的[5]。这类算法，网络节点接入概率取决于局部队列长度，而节点接入概率影响链路服务速率，从而影响队列动态演变，这种相互作用致使其成为一个关联系统，其网络镇定问题归结为寻找合适的Lyapunov函数刻画该关联系统的稳定性。目前，结果大多集中在假设式（1）中的μ（链路物理带宽，受信道状态影响）为常值。在时变链路速率情况下的基于队列的随机接入算法及其稳定性还未被人们解决。”
Translation: Unlike deterministic scheduling, random access can approximate (3) in a distributed manner. Access probabilities can adapt to local queue information to stabilize queues and reduce collisions. The feedback between access probabilities, service rates, and queues yields an interconnected system, whose stabilization requires an appropriate Lyapunov function. Most existing results assume that μ in (1), the physical link bandwidth affected by channel state, is constant. Queue-based random access algorithms and their stability under time-varying link rates have not yet been resolved.

The subsequent future directions, distinct from the main target, ask for (1) fast or finite-time convergence; (2) distributed scheduling with delayed/inaccurate channel information, contrasting it with currently known instantaneous CSI; (3) retransmissions, real-time behavior and reliability.

Source-model limits: these four pages specify expected long-run arrival/service means and the strong-stability formula, but no iid assumption, no finite arrival bound, no finite-state Markov law, no sensing latency, no control clock bound, and no within-slot fading law. Those assumptions must therefore be declared additions, not attributed to the book. The faithful local-channel idealization is motivated by the book's explicit contrast with delayed/imperfect CSI, not a claim that actual radios provide exact CSI.

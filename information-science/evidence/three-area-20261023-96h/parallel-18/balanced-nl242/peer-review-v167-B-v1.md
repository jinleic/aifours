## 2. Physical identities used throughout (each checked once from the original equations)

Q, R, S, T are any four ordinary MOQLS8: complex unit entries, all row and column ONBs, and all six pair-product ONBs. A product ONB for Q and X is equivalent to

- **COMPLETE:** Σ_z P_{Q_z} ⊗ P_{X_z} = I ⊗ I.

Its consequences are:

- **(P2)** For x ≠ y, ⟨Q_x,Q_y⟩⟨X_x,X_y⟩ = 0. So equal Q-rays force X-orthogonality, and so does any nonzero Q-overlap. The same holds for the companion pairs RS, RT and ST.

- **(P5) Purity.** Intrinsic components span mutually orthogonal spaces. Each row or column restricted to a component is an ONB of its span. A fixed ray is the same thing as a singleton component and as multiplicity 8.
- **(P6) Rank-one purity.** Every actual projector lies in the real span S. Writing an operator's off-diagonal part relative to an actual E, edge/cross-block constraints force each actual ray into the stated cones.

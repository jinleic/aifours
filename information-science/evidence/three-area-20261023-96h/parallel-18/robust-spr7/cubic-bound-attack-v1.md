## Exact independent-coefficient control, not a synthesis counterexample

For every real `R>20`, fix

`E(t)=-(t-1)(t-3)(t-5)=-t^3+9t^2-23t+15`,

and let

`O(t)=-t^3+A t^2-B t+C`,

with the three coefficients varying **independently** in

`A in [R+6,R+7]`, `B in [6R+8,6R+14]`, `C in [8R,8R+8]`.

Then every corresponding monic degree-seven denominator

`a(s)=E(-s^2)+s O(-s^2)`

is strictly Hurwitz. Indeed, uniformly over the complete coefficient box,

`O(1) >=3R-9>0`,

`O(3) <=20-R<0`,

`O(5) >=3R-45>0`,

while `O(t)` tends to negative infinity. Thus its three roots are simple, one each in `(1,3)`, `(3,5)`, `(5,infinity)`, giving exactly the strict degree-seven Hermite–Biehler order with the fixed even roots `1,3,5`. All signs and inequalities are symbolic and cover every coefficient choice, not just vertices.

The member

`O_R(t)=-(t-2)(t-4)(t-R)`

belongs to that same box. Its last odd root is exactly `R`, and can be arbitrarily large while the entire even polynomial remains fixed. Hence there is **no finite bound on the final odd root depending only on the even-root data**. This is a control against an invalid direct reuse of the sextic bounded-gap construction, not a counterexample to an improved construction using the actual odd coefficients.

This class has fixed even part, whose SPR synthesizability is already classical. The control is not claimed novel or significant in isolation; the parity difference itself is already implicit in the old dossier's Hermite–Biehler statements. Its value in this bounded branch is to prevent silently omitting an infinity-dependent ingredient while trying to generalize Lemma3.2.

Necessary arc filter (proof, not heuristic)
For any two projected columns c,d, if four rows share beta_i(c)-beta_i(d), their full column differences can have only three F3 digits. Two of those rows then give an equal full row-pair difference at both columns, contradicting a DM. Thus every such fibre has size<=3. For this family this is exactly: for f1, f2 and f1-f2, for every slope s in F7 the seven values f(i)-s*i have no multiplicity>3. Call these normalized functions ARC. Enumerate all 7^5 functions (last five entries), not a sample. The zero function is not ARC. Catalog correctness/completeness is independently checked by the C consumer.

Exact equivalences/canonicalization
G has252 actions (a,b,l), a,l in1..6, b in0..6:
 T(f)(i)=l*(f(a*i+b)-f(b)-i*(f(a+b)-f(b))) mod7.
It preserves normalization and ARC. Derivation: row i->a*i+b; subtract row b columnwise; multiply all DM entries by the CRT unit L=1 mod3,l mod7; reindex c in each block by c'=l*a*c+l*(f_t(a+b)-f_t(b)). These are validity-preserving cyclic-DM operations, not CFR row-isotopy claims.
Choose f1 minimal in its G orbit; map f2 by the same normalizing action; minimize f2 under the complete stabilizer of f1. Also quotient permutations of the three blocks: for s in S3 replace (f1,f2) with (f_s1-f_s0, f_s2-f_s0), with f0=0, then normalize the first function under G and the second under its stabilizer. Retain only the lexicographically least pair of catalog IDs among the six block orders. All transformations are common to the whole projected matrix.

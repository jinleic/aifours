I propose a canonical candidate obtained by imposing

  E_N in span{E_+, E_-}.

These are two additional linear conditions. They select a unique nonzero line in the contact kernel. I can prove useful reductions for this candidate, but not its universal feasibility.

Write
  (alpha,a,b,c,d,e,f) = (x0,x1,x2,x3,x4,x5,x6),
  p=L_+, q=L_-, k=p/q,
and define
  A=(a-t)(b-t)(e-t), B=(alpha-t)(c-t)(d-t),
  U=(a-t)(d-t)(e-t), V=(b-t)(c-t)(f-t).

Thus E_+=pA, E_-=qB, O_-=U, O_+=V.

The box conditions are exactly

  k_min <= k <= k_max,

where
  k_min=max{alpha*c*d/(a*b*e), (alpha+c+d)/(a+b+e)},
  k_max=min{1, [alpha*c+alpha*d+c*d]/[a*b+a*e+b*e]},

together with
  b*c*f >= a*d*e,
  a*d+a*e+d*e >= b*c+b*f+c*f,
  b+c+f >= a+d+e.

These explicit inequalities are the hypotheses still needed in the unproved steps below.

Canonical formula.

Define five positive numbers
  T1=(a-alpha)/[a(d-a)(e-a)],
  T2=(b-alpha)/[b(f-b)(c-b)],
  T3=(c-alpha)/[c(f-c)(c-b)],
  T4=(d-alpha)/[d(d-a)(e-d)],
  T5=(e-alpha)/[e(e-a)(e-d)],

and put
  lambda=(T3+T4)/(T1+T2+T5).

Let
  D(t)=(t-a)(t-b)(t-c)(t-d)(t-e),
  theta=(lambda*T1, lambda*T2, -T3, -T4, lambda*T5),

with z=(a,b,c,d,e), and define the polynomial
  g(t)=sum_j theta_j D(t)/(t-z_j).

The candidate is
  E_N=pA+lambda*qB,
  O_N=p*q*g.

Everything here is rational in the supplied parameters.

Proved: this is a well-defined, unique canonical line.

Since sum_j theta_j=0, g has degree at most three. Substitution at the five nodes verifies all five contact equations.

One useful identity behind that verification is
  A(t)B(t)=(alpha-t)D_*(t),
  U(t)V(t)=(f-t)D_*(t),
where D_*=(a-t)(b-t)(c-t)(d-t)(e-t).

Conversely, writing E_N as a combination of pA and qB, cubic interpolation for O_N has one compatibility equation. It is precisely
  lambda(T1+T2+T5)=T3+T4.
Thus the additional conditions leave exactly one line.

Moreover, E_N has exactly one root in each of
  (alpha,a), (b,c), (d,e).

The signs of g at the contact nodes are
  g(a)>0, g(b)<0, g(c)<0, g(d)>0, g(e)>0.

Define
  kappa=c*T3+d*T4
        -lambda(a*T1+b*T2+e*T5).

The leading coefficient of g is -kappa. Consequently, if kappa>0, g has exactly one root in each of
  (a,b), (c,d), (e,infinity).

Thus kappa>0 gives the required component interlacing. Establishing it from the box conditions is an explicit unresolved inequality for this candidate.

Proved: four explicit residuals suffice.

Put H=(a-t)(e-t). After normalization by p*q, the residuals are

  R_+-=[k*A^2+lambda*A*B+t*U*g]/H^2,

  R_++=[k*A^2+lambda*A*B+t*V*g]/(b-t)^2,

  R_-+=[A*B+(lambda/k)*B^2+t*V*g]/(c-t)^2,

  R_--=[A*B+(lambda/k)*B^2+t*U*g]/(d-t)^2.

All divisions are exact polynomial divisions. Their degrees are at most 3,5,5,5 respectively. Their nonnegativity on the stated binding sets is equivalent to this candidate being closed.

There is also a useful reduction in the leading-coefficient ratio. For fixed nodes,

  dR_+-/dk=(b-t)^2,
  dR_++/dk=H^2,

whereas differentiation with respect to 1/k gives

  dR_-+/d(1/k)=lambda(alpha-t)^2(d-t)^2,
  dR_--/d(1/k)=lambda(alpha-t)^2(c-t)^2.

Therefore, to prove this candidate works throughout the admissible k interval, it suffices to check:

  R_+-, R_++ at k=k_min;
  R_-+, R_-- at k=k_max.

This reduces the continuous ratio parameter to two boundary values, with only the branches defining the maximum and minimum remaining.

Proved: the cubic has a particularly simple form.

Exactly,
  R_+-(t)=k(b-t)^2+(d-t)S(t),

where
  S(t)=-kappa*t^2+sigma*t+tau,
  tau=lambda*alpha*b*c/(a*e),
  mu=lambda*(b-alpha)*(d-b)/(f-b),
  sigma=(mu-tau+kappa*b^2)/b.

In particular,
  S(0)=tau>0, S(b)=mu>0,
and
  R_+-(d)=k(d-b)^2>0.

If kappa>=0, S is concave, so S>0 throughout [0,b]. Hence

  P_+-,N >= 0 on [0,x2]

is proved conditional only on kappa>=0. No further inequality is needed for this whole binding interval.

For the remaining binding ray [d,infinity), a simple sufficient condition is

  S(d)<=0.

Indeed, concavity and S(b)>0 then imply S(t)<=0 for every t>=d, making both terms in R_+- nonnegative.

When S(d)>0, the exact remaining test requires at most one stationary minimum. Write

  R_+-=kappa*t^3+C2*t^2+C1*t+C0,

where
  C2=k-sigma-d*kappa,
  C1=-2*k*b+d*sigma-tau,
  C0=k*b^2+d*tau.

Assuming kappa>0, set
  Delta=C2^2-3*kappa*C1.

If Delta>0 and
  t_+=(-C2+sqrt(Delta))/(3*kappa)>d,
one must check R_+-(t_+)>=0. Otherwise the positive endpoint value at d already proves nonnegativity on the ray.

Also, conditional on kappa>0, component signs immediately prove P_-+,N>0 on [0,alpha].

Conjectured, not proved.

My proposed universal statement is that this canonical N is closed for every coefficient box in the question. The missing obligations are:

  1. The box inequalities imply kappa>0.
  2. They imply the cubic’s remaining tail test.
  3. They imply the three quintic residual inequalities on their remaining binding sets, at the two extremal k values above.

I have not established those implications. In particular, the component interlacing proved conditionally above does not establish the quintic inequalities.

This yields an explicit candidate, a complete elimination of the ratio parameter, and a quadratic reduction proving the first substantial binding interval conditional on one scalar inequality. It does not prove the fully-closed conjecture, and failure of this particular candidate would not establish kernel infeasibility.

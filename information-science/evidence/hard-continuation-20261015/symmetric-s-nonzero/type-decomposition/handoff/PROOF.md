# Exact balanced 3-DSBF support-type decomposition

This is a correspondence proof for three finite exact-search questions, not a nonexistence theorem. It uses known Walsh residue tools, not a new general mechanism. No covering-radius theorem from a 2026 preprint is imported, and no Lean verification is claimed.

## 1. The actual group and all possible support types

Inputs are integers `x=0,...,511` with coordinates `x_i=(x>>i)&1`. Let `R` rotate the nine coordinates left by three positions and let `J` reverse all nine coordinate positions. Both are linear permutation matrices over `F_2`. The matrices are stored explicitly in `inputs/decomposition.json`, with rows and columns labeled by these coordinates.

A 3-DSBF is a Boolean function invariant under the group generated by `R,J`. Its independent truth values are the104 input orbits of this action. The checker independently constructs each orbit using the six possible images `R^i x` and `J R^i x`, for `i=0,1,2`, and checks the entire512-point partition.

For `s(f)=XOR_(x:f(x)=1)x`, invariance implies `R s(f)=s(f)` and `J s(f)=s(f)`, because linear maps commute with XOR and permute the support. Rotation fixes vectors whose bits are constant on each of `{0,3,6}`, `{1,4,7}`, `{2,5,8}`. Reversal interchanges the first and third sets and fixes the second. Thus the fixed space has dimension two, with basis

- `146 = 2^1+2^4+2^7`,
- `365 = 2^0+2^2+2^3+2^5+2^6+2^8`.

Its four vectors are exactly `{0,146,365,511}`. This is also derived computationally by applying the actual two matrices to **all512 vectors**, rather than assuming the type list. Every individual orbit XOR lies in this fixed space; their binary span is the whole fixed space. The nonzero possibilities are therefore exactly **146,365,511**. No branch is skipped using an assumed transformation: all three receive the requested exact visit. An explicit equivalence of two branches is proved next and is not used to launch any additional visit.

### 1.1 A checked linear equivalence of types146 and511

Put `u=365`, `v=146` and define the linear shear

`M(x)=x+u(v.x)`, `M^T(a)=a+v(u.a)` over `F_2`.

Since `u.v=0`, `M^2=I`; it is a bijective input change. Both `u,v` are fixed by rotation and reversal, so `M` commutes with both actual group matrices. Consequently `g(x)=f(Mx)` is a3-DSBF whenever `f` is. It is balanced exactly when `f` is, and `g(0)=f(0)`. Its support vector and Walsh transform are

`s(g)=M s(f)`, `W_g(a)=W_f(M^T a)`.

Here `M(146)=511`, `M(511)=146`, and `M(365)=365`. Also `a.511=(M^T a).146`, so the24/28 residue bands map exactly. Thus the balanced type146 and511 questions are linearly equivalent.

The artifact `inputs/type-equivalence.json` records the entire512-point map and dual map, and their explicit permutations of the104 input orbits and104 frequency orbits. It verifies every10816 entry of the character matrix under these permutations. The independent checker constructs the matrix by its columns, constructs its transpose from coordinate dot products, and verifies every262144 character pairing. It additionally maps the **actual parsed** Walsh-PB assertions, balance equality, fixed-type equations and normalization of branch511 to branch146, rather than trusting only a fitted-spectrum match.

This yields recorded groups `{146,511}` and `{365}`; it does **not** prove type365 inequivalent under every possible transformation. No solver timeout is thereby turned into an exclusion. All three requested visits are retained even though two questions have this recorded isomorphism.

Transporting the genuine type146 balanced control through `M` supplies another genuine balanced type511 control with peak36. The original independently constructed type511 peak40 control is preserved unchanged; the transported table is stored separately and checked directly and by FWHT.

## 2. Why the zero type is irrelevant to the target

The following is the accepted elementary degree/spectral restriction, recalled so the decomposition does not depend on an unrestricted preprint theorem.

For a balanced nine-variable function let `T=support(f)`, `w=256`, `m_i=sum_(x in T)x_i`, and `m_ij=sum_(x in T)x_i x_j`. Expanding the parity character over ordinary integers gives, modulo16,

`W_f(a)=512*1_(a=0)-2w+4 sum_i m_i a_i-8 sum_(i<j)m_ij a_i a_j (mod16)`.

If `s(f)=0`, every `m_i` is even. Every Walsh coefficient is divisible by eight, and

`q(a)=W_f(a)/8 mod2`

is a quadratic Boolean function. The terms `64*1_(a=0)` and `-w/4=-64` vanish modulo two. Balance gives `q(0)=0`, so `1+q` is a nonzero polynomial of degree at most two. A nonzero degree-at-most-two Boolean polynomial on nine variables has at least128 ones: this is the elementary minimum-weight bound `2^(n-d)`, proved by writing `p=p0+x_n p1` and inducting on the number of variables.

Consequently at least128 Walsh positions have `q=0`. Under a peak bound28, divisibility by eight lowers every magnitude to at most24, while those128 positions are divisible by16 and have magnitude at most16. Parseval would require

`262144=sum_a W_f(a)^2 <=128*16^2+384*24^2=253952`,

a contradiction. Thus a balanced peak<=28 function has nonzero support XOR. In particular a hypothetical balanced 3-DSBF target lies in exactly one of the three branches above. This does **not** exclude any nonzero branch.

## 3. Forced residue bands from exact balance and fixed type

For any Boolean function put `I(a)=#{x in T:a.x=1}`. Directly,

`W_f(a)=512*1_(a=0)-2w+4I(a)`.

For exact balance `w=256`, the first two terms vanish modulo eight and `I(a) mod2=a.s(f)`. Therefore

`W_f(a)=4(a.s) mod8`.

When `a.s=0`, the coefficient is divisible by eight; a requested magnitude<=28 is exactly the same as magnitude<=24. When `a.s=1`, it is four modulo eight and the largest allowed magnitude is28. Thus each branch imposes the logically exact bands

- `|W_f(a)|<=24` if `a.s=0`,
- `|W_f(a)|<=28` if `a.s=1`.

For each nonzero type there are256 frequencies in each band. The numbers of frequency *orbits* in the bands can differ; the checker verifies both the orbit counts and the total512-frequency counts. The fixed-type condition ensures `a.s` is constant across a frequency orbit.

The residue/support-sum method is prior art: Lou–Xu–Wang, [arXiv:2610.17897v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.17897v1#Thmproposition1), Proposition1's proof, and Wang–Lou–Wang, [arXiv:2610.17871v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17871), Lemma2 and Proposition1, contain stronger low-bit forms. The present object is an explicit balanced dihedral application and bounded exact computation, not a priority claim.

## 4. Complete104-Boolean weighted-PB question

Let `O_j` be the104 input orbits and `x_j=f(O_j)`. For a frequency `a`, define the integer character sum

`C[a,j]=sum_(y in O_j)(-1)^(a.y)`.

Then exactly

`W_f(a)=sum_j C[a,j](1-2x_j)`.

Since coordinate permutations preserve the character pairing and the input table is group-invariant, the Walsh transform is invariant under the same action on frequencies. Thus one representative of each of the104 frequency orbits covers every512 coefficient. The checker reconstructs all512 character rows directly and checks this orbit constancy; it does not trust a producer's matrix hash as mathematical evidence.

For a row `C`, put `L=sum_j |C_j|`. Define a Boolean literal `b_j=x_j` if `C_j>0`, and `b_j=1-x_j` if `C_j<0`; zero coefficients are omitted. The exact identity is

`W=L-2 sum_j |C_j| b_j`.

At a local cap `c` (24 or28), impose both integer weighted thresholds

`ceil((L-c)/2) <= sum_j |C_j| b_j <= floor((L+c)/2)`.

Clipping these thresholds to `[0,L]` removes only tautological bounds on a sum known to lie in that range. The independent checker instead expands every parsed SMT constraint as `constant+sum coefficient_j*x_j` and compares it to the directly derived signed-`C` inequalities. It verifies both the coefficients and right-hand side, not merely a producer's symbolic expression or solver response.

Exact balance is the separate weighted equality

`sum_j |O_j| x_j=256`.

For each of the nine coordinates, the XOR of the `x_j` whose orbit XOR has that coordinate one is required to equal the corresponding bit of the chosen `s`. This fixes **all nine support bits**, not merely `s!=0`. These conditions contain only two independent type directions, but their redundant coordinate equations are retained explicitly.

Output complementation preserves dihedral invariance, weight256, support XOR (the XOR of all512 inputs is zero), and all Walsh magnitudes. Thus the harmless normalization `f(0)=0`, represented by `x0=false`, loses no solution up to complementation.

Each branch has104 independent Boolean variables and219 assertions:208 Walsh thresholds, one exact-balance equality, nine fixed-support equations and one complement normalization. An independently written S-expression parser checks every declaration and every assertion in the actual compressed SMT input. It rejects extra assertions or altered coefficients, balance or type; it imports no producing code or solver library.

These facts prove both directions: any balanced3-DSBF target of type `s` (possibly after output complement) satisfies the branch, and any SAT orbit assignment expands to a balanced3-DSBF target with that type and peak<=28. A positive table is independently checked by all512 direct character sums and a separate FWHT. Solver-only UNSAT is **not** a checked exclusion.

## 5. Genuine relaxed controls and discriminators

The three planted controls are constructed by changing complete invariant orbits of the published `dsbf40` table. Exactly six input points are changed before an optional global output complement. They are actual512-bit tables, not substituted SAT assignments or relaxed integer solutions. All are balanced, invariant under the actual generators, degree eight, have `f(0)=0`, and have their declared support type.

The producer obtains peaks36,40,40 for types146,365,511 respectively. For a nominal relaxed bound `B`, the allowed cap is the largest nonnegative integer<=B with residue `4(a.s)` modulo eight. Thus the type146 control uses bands32/36; the other two use40/36. The independent checker verifies the tables directly, evaluates every relaxed SMT assertion on their true orbit bits, and verifies that the target28 questions reject them only at Walsh inequalities—not at balance, support type or normalization.

The checker also evaluates each control against the other branches' fixed-support equations: the number of failing coordinate equations is exactly the Hamming weight of the difference of the two support vectors. This is a test of the fixed-type equations specifically; it is not a claim that all the wrong branch's Walsh caps would also pass.

Deliberate corruptions independently change a nonzero-frequency PB coefficient, a support-type bit, the exact-balance value or a recorded Walsh coefficient. Each must fail the corresponding exact assertion rather than failing for a missing file.

## 6. Interpretation of outcomes

A bounded visit is not an exhaustive enumeration of `2^104` orbit assignments. An UNKNOWN, timeout, memory guard or solver-only UNSAT has no exclusion force. A complete negative claim would still need an independently checked proof/certificate plus this question-to-encoding correspondence. The three branches are a complete **formulation** of the balanced3-DSBF subclass, not a completed proof of its infeasibility. They do not cover all balanced nine-variable Boolean functions.

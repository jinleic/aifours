## 1. Statement and computable slab

Let an independent monic septic coefficient box be all Hurwitz. Denote the ordered odd root bands by \([a,b],[c,d],[e,f]\), and let its pointwise lower even envelope factor as
\[
E_-(t)=\ell_6(\rho-t)(r_2-t)(r_3-t),\quad
0<\rho<a,\quad b<r_2<c,\quad d<r_3<e,\quad \ell_6>0. \tag{1}
\]
Consider any five increasing positive squared frequencies with
\[
b<t_1<c<t_2<t_3<d<t_4<t_5<e. \tag{2}
\]
There is a strictly positive, explicitly computable \(\delta\), depending **only** on the odd box and these frequencies, such that if \(a-\rho<\delta\), no positive-weight aggregate of actual denominator atoms at those five nodes, even allowing zero and infinity atoms, has all eight monomial coordinates strictly negative.

To compute \(\delta\), take the eight odd-moment interpolation rays with supports \((1,2,3,4)\), \((1,2,3,5)\), each indexed by \(k=0,1,2,3\), from the author-written reduction `frontier-20261029-three-strict-pattern-eight-ray-reduction-v1.md`, SHA-256 `8591da67f304472da00e7afb7bf1ddfc1c2a7970802f7c844cf8cd495ee93686`. For each ray and each \((y,z)\in\{b,c\}\times\{d,e\}\), take the worst odd endpoint independently at each support node, as specified in §2, and let \(P_{y,z,k,S}(x)\) be its pairing with
\[
E_x(t)=(x-t)(y-t)(z-t),\qquad F(t)=(-t)(b-t)(e-t). \tag{3}
\]
The same positive ray normalization must be used for \(P(a)\) and \(P(0)\). The boundary lemma below proves all 32 values \(A_{y,z,k,S}:=P(a)\) strictly positive. Put \(B_{y,z,k,S}:=P(0)\) and define
\[
\delta=\min\!\left(\frac a2,\quad
\min_{y,z,k,S}\frac{aA_{y,z,k,S}}{2\bigl(A_{y,z,k,S}+|A_{y,z,k,S}-B_{y,z,k,S}|\bigr)}\right)>0. \tag{4}
\]
For rational odd bands and nodes, (4) is a finite rational expression. If \(a-\rho<\delta\), linearity in \(x\) gives \(P(\rho)>A/2\); multi-affinity in \((r_2,r_3)\) extends this to every admissible even lower envelope. This is a sufficient slab, not a necessary characterization.

## 2. Boundary lemma at \(\rho=a\)

Use the test \(F\) in (3). It is positive at all nodes in (2), and has nonnegative even numerator coefficients \((0,be,b+e,1)\). Thus replacing every actual even value by \(E_-\) lowers its pairing. Any hypothetical direct certificate's odd sign sequence is necessarily \((- ,+,-,+,+)\): the outside-gap signs are forced, and the required oriented odd subsequence forces the two inside-band signs. Its four nonpositive odd moments are generated by the eight rays in the named import. Their unnormalized finite weights on a four-node support \(u_1<u_2<u_3<u_4\) are
\[
w_i^{(k)}=\frac{e_{3-k}(\{u_j:j\ne i\})}
{u_i O_i(u_i)\prod_{j\ne i}(u_i-u_j)}>0. \tag{5}
\]
Here \(u_1=t_1,u_2=t_2,u_3=t_3\), and \(u_4=t_4\) or \(t_5\).

For each even vertex \((y,z)\), the signs of \(E_a(u_i)\) are fixed, and the worst odd endpoints in (5) are
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
(y,z)&O_1&O_2&O_3&O_4\\\hline
(b,e)&O_-&O_+&O_-&O_+\\
(b,d)&O_-&O_+&O_-&O_-\\
(c,e)&O_+&O_+&O_-&O_+\\
(c,d)&O_+&O_+&O_-&O_-.
\end{array} \tag{6}
\]
At the two nodes inside \((c,d)\), the even value is positive, so the same bounds remain valid if actual odd values approach zero with the required sign. At the other nodes, the reciprocal odd magnitude ranges between the two endpoints. Pairings are affine in each reciprocal; consequently the endpoint choices in (6) are worst. In particular, choosing them independently is a relaxation, not an assumption that all nodes use one denominator.

For a support, let \(H(t)=\prod_{i=1}^4(t-u_i)\), \(m=3-k\), and
\[
J_k(g)=\sum_i\frac{e_m(\widehat u_i)g(u_i)}{H'(u_i)}
=(-1)^{3-k}[t^k]I_g(t), \tag{7}
\]
where \(I_g\) is the degree-at-most-three interpolant on the four nodes and \(g(u_i)=E_a(u_i)F(u_i)/(u_i O_i(u_i))\). We prove \(J_k(g)>0\) in all four cases.

### Case \((y,z)=(b,e)\)

Every \(E_a(u_i)>0\), and every ray weight is positive. No cancellation is needed.

### Case \((b,d)\)

Use \(O_-\) as a formal baseline at all four nodes. Its reduced function is \(g_-(t)=-(b-t)^2\), so the four \(J_k\) values are \((b^2,2b,1,0)\). At node 2, replacing the formal negative \(O_-\) by the actual positive \(O_+\) adds a strictly positive term; hence all four values are strict.

### Case \((c,e)\)

Use \(O_+\) as baseline. Then
\[
g_+(t)=\frac{(t-a)(e-t)^2}{f-t}.
\]
At node 3, replacing formal positive \(O_+\) with actual negative \(O_-\) adds a strictly positive term. For the baseline, write \(D=H(f)>0\), \(s_j=e_j(u_1,\ldots,u_4)\), and \(A=(f-a)(f-e)^2>0\). The four coefficients of \(I_{g_+}\) have signs \((- ,+,-,+)\): the leading one is \(A/D>0\); the quadratic coefficient is \(-1+(A/D)(f-s_1)<0\), using \(D\ge f^3(f-s_1)\) when the latter is positive and \(A<f^3\); the linear coefficient obeys
\[
2e-f+a+\frac A D(f^2-fs_1+s_2)
\ge \frac{e^2+2ae}{f}-\frac{ae^2}{f^2}>0;
\]
the constant is \(-ae^2/f-A s_4/(fD)<0\). The inequalities used are the first and second Bonferroni bounds for \(\prod_i(1-u_i/f)\), valid because \(0<u_i<f\). Thus (7) is strictly positive for all \(k\).

### Case \((c,d)\)

Use the formal \(O_-\) baseline. It gives \(g_-(t)=-(b-t)(c-t)\), whose four \(J_k\) values are \((bc,b+c,1,0)\). Switching nodes 1 and 2 to \(O_+\) changes \(g\) by
\[
D_0(t)=\frac{\Delta(t)}{f-t},\qquad
\Delta(t)=O_+(t)-O_-(t)=\delta_0+\delta_1t+\delta_2t^2, \tag{8}
\]
with all \(\delta_j\ge0\) by independent coefficient intervals and \(0\le\delta_0=bcf-ade<bcf\). The correction equals
\[
\frac{w_2D_0(u_2)G_2-w_1D_0(u_1)G_1}{u_2-u_1},\quad
w_1=e_m(u_2,u_3,u_4),\quad w_2=e_m(u_1,u_3,u_4),\quad
G_i=\frac1{(u_3-u_i)(u_4-u_i)}. \tag{9}
\]
For each \(t^j\) in \(\Delta\) with \(j\ge1\), this correction is nonnegative: \(u_2w_2-u_1w_1=(u_2-u_1)e_m(u_3,u_4)\ge0\), while \(G_2/(f-u_2)>G_1/(f-u_1)\).

For the constant term, set \(G(t)=1/[(u_3-t)(u_4-t)(f-t)]\), \(A_m=e_{m-1}(u_3,u_4)\) with \(e_{-1}=0\), and \(B_m=e_m(u_3,u_4)\). The correction coefficient of \(\delta_0\) is \(A_m K+B_m J\), where
\[
K=\frac{u_1G(u_2)-u_2G(u_1)}{u_2-u_1},\qquad
J=\frac{G(u_2)-G(u_1)}{u_2-u_1}>0. \tag{10}
\]
Strict convexity of \(G\) gives \(-K<G(0)=1/(u_3u_4f)\). If \(K<0\), the first three baseline values dominate the worst possible negative constant-term correction, respectively because
\[
bc>\delta_0/f,\quad
b+c>bc(1/u_3+1/u_4),\quad 1>bc/(u_3u_4).
\]
If \(K\ge0\) they are immediate. For \(k=3\), \(m=0\), the baseline is zero but \(D_0(t)G(t)(f-t)=\Delta(t)G(t)\) is strictly increasing: \(G\) increases and \(\Delta\) is a nonzero polynomial with nonnegative coefficients. Thus this last pairing is also strict.

These four cases prove all 32 boundary values in (4). The required nonzero odd-width polynomial \(\Delta\) follows from the strict distinct odd bands in (2).

# Heterogeneous example is outside the uniform sector-radius theorem

In `frontier-20261029-heterogeneous-radius-cone-theorem-v1.md`, the explicit center is \(a=(s+1)^7\), with coefficient radius \(r_1=5/2\). Any scale \(c\) satisfying the real-root or sector-damped hypotheses of `frontier-20261029-sector-damped-center-uniform-box-v1.md` must have \(c\le1\). The required common scaled radius for the \(s^1\) coefficient is then
\[
\frac{r_1}{c^{7-1}}\ge\frac52>1.
\]
Thus the example is outside **every** instance of that theorem, not merely outside its \(c=1\) presentation, yet its independent exact replay proves stability and common numerator \(a\). This is a coverage comparison between sufficient conditions, not an arbitrary-box result or novelty claim.

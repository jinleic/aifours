# Rectangular corollary of the resonant root localization

For the wide septic box in `frontier-20261029-resonant-root-localization-v1.md`, every common numerator has at least two upper-half-plane roots \(z\), counted with multiplicity, satisfying the explicit rectangular bounds
\[
-\frac{197}{500}<\operatorname{Re}z<0,\qquad
\frac{253}{500}<\operatorname{Im}z<\frac{747}{500}.
\]
Their conjugates obey the reflected lower-half-plane bounds. This is a direct, weaker consequence of the proven distance \(<197/500\) from \(i[9/10,11/10]\) and strict Hurwitz stability of the literal numerator. It does not assert that every point in this rectangle lies within the segment neighborhood or that the reduced transfer function retains the roots after cancellation.

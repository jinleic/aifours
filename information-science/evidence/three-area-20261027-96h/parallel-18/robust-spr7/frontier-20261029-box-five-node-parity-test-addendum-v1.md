# Addendum: parity-member dual tests and a refuted simple proof route

**Status: WRITTEN reduction plus WRITTEN-checked-in-kernel finite counterexample to a simple inequality; conjecture still open; not parent adoption.** This supplements `frontier-20261029-box-five-node-conjecture-evidence-v1.md` (SHA-256 `0031ced22ea54e307bdae7a9715dee09fd3bc8e1b72da1c7b8c299042def6e05`).

## A box-native dual family

For an eligible box, every \(b=(\gamma E_\theta,O_\varphi)\), with \(E_\theta=(1-\theta)E_-+\theta E_+\), \(O_\varphi=(1-\varphi)O_-+\varphi O_+\), \(\theta,\varphi\in[0,1]\), \(\gamma>0\), has strictly positive coefficients. Therefore, if its pairings with all five atoms of a proposed strict certificate are nonnegative, that certificate is impossible. These \(b\) form exactly the cone generated by the four half-corner polynomials \((E_\pm,0)\), \((0,O_\pm)\). By the linear alternative, **this whole dual family fails** if and only if some positive atom weights make all four half-corner pairings strictly negative. That is a necessary condition for any strict certificate.

For the pattern \((2,1,3,2,4)\), with mandatory corners \(O_-\) at \(t_1\), \(O_+\) at \(t_2\), \(E_+\) at \(t_3\) and \(E_-\) at \(t_4\), the choice \(\theta=\varphi=0\) gives automatic positivity at nodes 1, 4 and 5. It succeeds exactly when
\[
\frac{t_2O_+(t_2)|O_-(t_2)|}{|E_{s_2}(t_2)||E_-(t_2)|}
\le
\frac{t_3|O_{s_3}(t_3)||O_-(t_3)|}{E_+(t_3)|E_-(t_3)|}.
\]
In the worst actual corner choices this is \(|F(t_2)|\le|F(t_3)|\), where \(F=tO_+O_-/(E_+E_-)\). In an unfrozen exploratory run of 600 random pattern samples, each of the two choices \(\theta=0\) and \(\theta=1\), with \(\varphi=0\), succeeded every time.

## The simple inequality is false

A proof by \(\sup_{O_1}|F|\le\inf_{E_2}|F|\) is impossible. The exact frozen eligible box in `frontier-20261029-F-separation-counterexample-frozen-v1.json` (SHA-256 `b8229be24f03feb0d43475b6efefef94d18584c29b4699404ae8f3593a01c6d6`) is built from exact interlaced parity roots and checked nonnegative box-difference coefficients. It has rational \(t_2=179751/818068\in O_1\), \(t_3=3657385/963877\in E_2\), and \(|F(t_2)|>8|F(t_3)|\). The standalone exact checker `frontier-20261029-F-separation-counterexample-independent-kernel-v1.py` (SHA-256 `63b7a8e549d5507308c2f7916bb4c2391f33cfbf210c6301c95ff080ee7ba24d`) verifies all seven positive widths, the band signs and the inequality. Interlacing and Kharitonov give all-member stability as written mathematics.

On that same box, a targeted exact z3 sweep over 3888 pattern-\((2,1,3,2,4)\) configurations, using these worst nodes, found no strict certificate. A 248-frequency float grid LP was clearly negative, at \(-0.0033\). Thus other dual tests handle the failure; the conjecture survives but needs the larger family or another argument. These sweep results are evidence, not proof, and were not separately frozen.

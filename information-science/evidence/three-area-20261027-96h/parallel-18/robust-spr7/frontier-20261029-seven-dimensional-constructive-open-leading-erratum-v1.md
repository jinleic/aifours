# Leading-coefficient clarification for the moving seven-generator numerator

In `frontier-20261029-seven-dimensional-constructive-open-v1.md`, the phrase “The monic leading coefficient remains positive because the two odd generators have fixed monic degree-seven leading coefficients” is imprecise: the two displayed odd-basis weights are not individually nonnegative. The exact conclusion is stronger and follows by direct addition: each odd endpoint generator has leading coefficient one, all even endpoint generators and midpoint derivatives have degree at most six, and the displayed fixed weights satisfy
\[
c_2+c_3=1.
\]
Therefore every perturbed moving-span numerator has leading coefficient **exactly one**, regardless of the signs of \(c_2,c_3\). This clarification does not change any other inequality or result; the coefficient identity follows directly from the printed rational values. No new formal or cross-vendor verification is claimed.

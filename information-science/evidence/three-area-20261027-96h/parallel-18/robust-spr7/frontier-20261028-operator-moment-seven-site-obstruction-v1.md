## Exact cone identity

At one fixed actual denominator \(a\) and \(t=\omega^2\), the \(I+hD\) pairing from the preceding operator theorem is
\[
P_h=P_0+hP_1+h^2P_2,\qquad h\ge0,
\]
where \(P_2\) is the pairing of the unnormalized derivatives. The nonnegative cone generated by all \(I+hD\) rows and the separately allowed derivative row is exactly the set of triples
\[
(\mu_0,\mu_1,\mu_2)\quad\text{with}\quad
\mu_0,\mu_1,\mu_2\ge0,\quad \mu_0\mu_2\ge\mu_1^2.
\]
Necessity follows from Cauchy–Schwarz. Conversely, if \(\mu_1>0\), use an \(I+hD\) row at \(h=\mu_2/\mu_1\) with weight \(\mu_1^2/\mu_2\), and a row at \(h=0\) with weight \(\mu_0-\mu_1^2/\mu_2\). If \(\mu_1=0\), use the \(h=0\) row with weight \(\mu_0\) and the derivative row with weight \(\mu_2\). This handles boundary cases without pretending a finite \(h\) realizes a pure derivative row.

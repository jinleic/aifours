# Conjecture: independent coefficient boxes exclude five-node strict certificates

**Status: CONJECTURE with nonverdict exact-sign sampling evidence; not a theorem, not parent adoption.** The stable-polytope packet `frontier-20261029-stable-polytope-separated-bands-negative-ineligible-v1.md` (SHA-256 `572738478e99522433dffa9dba31667c589643455099044f2f0a92be793a4795`) exhibits a five-node strict negative certificate for a compact convex all-Hurwitz septic polytope with separated root bands. Its independent coefficient hull is unstable. The following conjecture isolates the difference.

**Conjecture.** If \(K\) is an eligible full-width independent monic septic coefficient box, there is no finite actual-corner atom collection with exactly five distinct positive squared frequencies whose aggregate numerator coordinates are all strictly negative.

Every such certificate must have one of the three classified phase patterns and the mandatory oriented corners, by `frontier-20261029-strict-certificate-oriented-corner-obstruction-v1.md` (SHA-256 `cfcd21119ca1bf523bb4bd726baffc1faa4a44f4d1d6d160be3b04b0ba4eba53`). A plausible mechanism is the **box-difference monotonicity**: for a box, \(E_+-E_-\) and \(O_+-O_-\) are polynomials in \(t=\omega^2\) with nonnegative coefficients and thus increase on \(t\ge0\). In the stable-polytope counterexample, two odd vertices differ by \((o_i-o_j)(t-9/2)(t-7)\), whose coefficients have mixed signs, and its even scalings vary by more than a factor \(10^5\). For boxes, the same nonnegative-coefficient conditions tie a large ratio \(u_6/l_6\) to widened even root bands. No inequality chain proving the conjecture has been written.

## Exact-sign sampling evidence, not proof

`frontier-20261029-five-node-box-pattern-sampling-kernel-v1.py`, SHA-256 `eca398ad51d1315bbf88dc86c29459e380233922179a779875e143e2442c7d3c`, constructs 300 exact-rational eligible boxes from ordered parity-root bands, after checking all box-difference coefficient signs. It places random rational nodes in the classified regions, uses the mandatory corner orientations and other random corners, and solves the five-atom strict certificate LP exactly with z3. As a control, it first recovers the pinned stable-polytope certificate. In one replay it tested 3000 pattern-\((2,1,3,2,4)\) samples, 1899 pattern-\((2,1,3,5,4)\) samples and 2403 pattern-\((2,4,3,5,4)\) samples, finding **zero** certificates. An earlier exploratory run gave the same outcome on 9169 further samples.

A separate exploratory float hill-climb on the LP margin for the first pattern only drove configurations toward degenerate limits, such as a fifth node approaching an even root, where the margin tends to zero from below. That behaviour is consistent with the proved impossibility of four positive nodes plus an endpoint, but it is not evidence of a certificate or a proof of the conjecture. A float grid screen of 100 band-structured boxes, `frontier-20261029-band-structured-box-screen-kernel-v1.py` (SHA-256 `9f9f81f2e9b1e1145d02ef660bb6188af58e5edbaf19c3ed178945c78f57e530`), also found no grid certificate with any number of nodes; its float values carry no verdict.

Sampling cannot exclude rare configurations. The original universal interval-box question remains open.

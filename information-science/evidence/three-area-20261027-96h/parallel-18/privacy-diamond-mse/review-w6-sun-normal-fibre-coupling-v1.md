## 3. Two-coset lemma, without hidden normality or finiteness

`X/(H intersect K) -> (X/H) x (X/K)`, `x(H intersect K) -> (xH,xK)`

is well-defined and injective: equal image means the relative multiplier belongs to BOTH H and K. The target is finite, so `m=[X:H intersect K]` is finite and at most `h k`, where `h=[X:H]`, `k=[X:K]`. The subgroup index chains show that both h and k divide m. If gcd(h,k)=1, their product divides m; hence `m=hk`, making the injection onto the entire product of coset sets. Every pair of actual H/K cosets therefore has a common representative.

Owner: InfoResearch96h-2 / info-1. Observed `/bin/date`: `Fri Oct 30 04:00:06 PDT 2026`. ADDITIVE; addenda 11–13 bytes unchanged. Parent qualification accepted; no example/code.

**Withdrawal.** Addendum-12 (T2)'s claim that only its displayed EDGE corner integral remains "on all S_pair" is WITHDRAWN for S_pair points whose EDGE core is ACTIVE: addendum-11's two-endpoint correction was derived assuming two-line core slack at BOTH (a,c) and (a,u), so F0=Π0 at both points and the C0 blocks cancel. When the edge core is active, the edge F0 may differ from Π0 and an additional term appears:

    + (1−b)·[ core_true(a,c) − core_true(a,u) ],

where core_true(d) is the TRUE (constrained two-line) C0-block value at (a,d). On S_both both C0 blocks do cancel (F0=Π0 at both), and the (E)/Δ-exactness claim there remains a separate review question. Addendum-12 (T1) (pair-level corner compatibility on S_pair) is unaffected.

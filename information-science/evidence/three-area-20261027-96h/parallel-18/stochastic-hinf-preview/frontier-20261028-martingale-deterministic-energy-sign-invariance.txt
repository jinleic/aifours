1. Exact physical statement

For T>h>=0,S=T-h, use the original given law

 dx_t=w_t dW_t,     z_t=x_t,    x0=0,

with scalar progressively measurable input w supported on [0,S]. Suppose its temporal magnitude is deterministic: there is a fixed q in L2[0,S], q>=0, such that

 |w_t(omega)|=q(t) for dt x P-almost every (t,omega).       (M)

The sign of w may depend arbitrarily and progressively on the ENTIRE observed Brownian past; no independence or deterministic sign is assumed. Then the complete output PATH LAW is identical to that generated by the deterministic input q, and

 E||z(w)||_L2[0,T] / E||w||_L2[0,S]
    = E||z(q)||_L2[0,T] / ||q||_L2[0,S]                 (1)

for q!=0. Thus this law's adaptive disturbance advantage cannot come from changing only a sign while retaining a deterministic energy profile. In particular, any input that strictly beats ALL deterministic inputs must have a genuinely random temporal magnitude on a positive dt x P set.

2. Proof via Brownian invariance

Set sigma_t=w_t/q(t) where q(t)>0, and sigma_t=1 where q(t)=0; choose a predictable representative. By (M), |sigma_t|=1 dt x P-almost everywhere. The actual continuous martingale

 B_t=integral_0^t sigma_s dW_s

has quadratic variation [B]_t=t and B0=0. Lévy's characterization gives that B is Brownian with respect to the same filtration. The original state is

 x_t=integral_0^t q(s) sigma_s dW_s=integral_0^t q(s)dB_s.

Because q is deterministic, the ENTIRE continuous process x has the same Gaussian law as the state driven by deterministic input q against W. Also ||w||_path=||q|| deterministically. This proves (1), not merely equality of second moments or an RMS objective. Equivalently, a deterministic cumulative energy clock A_t=integral_0^t w_s²ds for all t forces (M) by differentiating A almost everywhere.

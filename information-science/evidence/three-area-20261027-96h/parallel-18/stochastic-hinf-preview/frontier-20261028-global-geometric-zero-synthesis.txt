1. Actual original existence question

Fix arbitrary finite real matrices A,A0,B,B0,C,D0,D1 and scalar Brownian W. For any T>h>=0, with zero initial state and every progressive vector E1 disturbance supported on [0,T-h], use the original SDE and registered deterministic full-preview family

 dx=(Ax+Bu+Cw_(t-h))dt+(A0x+B0u)dW,
 z=D0x+D1u,
 u=Kx+L0w_t+Lh w_(t-h)+integral_0^h H(r)w_(t-r)dr,

where K,L0,Lh,H are freely chosen finite matrices/L2 kernel, without stability or magnitude constraints. The pinned global zero-reduction theorem says universal z=0 exists iff there are a subspace M and matrices K,L making M common invariant for A+BK,A0+B0K, invisible to D0+D1K, with BL+C and B0L ranging in M and D1L=0. The construction below decides precisely that condition and returns a pure delayed law.

2. Maximal joint output-nulling controlled subspace

Start V0=R^n and iterate

 V_(j+1)={x in Vj: there exists u with
           A x+B u in Vj,  A0 x+B0 u in Vj,
           D0 x+D1 u=0}.                                  (1)

Each Vj is a real linear subspace, the sequence decreases and stabilizes after at most n strict dimension drops. Let Vstar be the stable subspace. At stability, one can choose a SINGLE linear feedback Kstar on Vstar such that BOTH closed state matrices preserve Vstar and the closed output vanishes on it: for each basis vector choose one feasible u from (1), then extend linearly.

For an explicit recursion, let Rj have orthonormal columns spanning Vj^perp and define

 Cj=[Rj^T A;Rj^T A0;D0],
 Dj=[Rj^T B;Rj^T B0;D1].

Then (1) is exactly

 V_(j+1)=Vj intersect ker[(I-Dj Dj^dagger) Cj].            (2)

All operations are finite-dimensional linear algebra; empty complements/subspaces are allowed.

3. Exact feedforward decision and explicit K,L

At Vstar let R span Vstar^perp and define

 Dstar=[R^T B;R^T B0;D1],
 Cstar=[R^T A;R^T A0;D0],
 Fstar=[R^T C;0;0].

Then a universal zero-output law exists in the ENTIRE original deterministic full-preview class, with arbitrary K, if and only if

 (I-Dstar Dstar^dagger) Fstar=0.                           (DECISION)

When DECISION holds, one concrete complete choice is

 Kstar=-Dstar^dagger Cstar Pi_Vstar,
 Lstar=-Dstar^dagger Fstar,
 u_t=Kstar x_t+Lstar w_(t-h),                             (3)

where Pi_Vstar is the orthogonal projector onto Vstar. Set L0=0,H=0,Lh=Lstar (for h=0 the single direct tap is Lstar). The definition of Vstar guarantees Cstar x in range(Dstar) for every x in Vstar; DECISION guarantees Fstar in range(Dstar). Thus

 (A+B Kstar)Vstar subset Vstar,
 (A0+B0 Kstar)Vstar subset Vstar,
 (D0+D1 Kstar)Vstar=0,
 range(B Lstar+C),range(B0 Lstar) subset Vstar,
 D1 Lstar=0.

The actual zero-start stochastic state stays in Vstar for EVERY progressive E1 input, so z=0 pathwise. Hence (3) is strictly feasible for every gamma>0, but the original strict inequality is never satisfied at gamma=0 by any law for nonzero w.

4. Necessity and exact scope

Suppose ANY full-preview law with arbitrary K has universal zero output. The pinned global reduction theorem supplies some common invariant output-invisible M and matrices Khat,L satisfying its condition (S). By induction M subset Vj for every j: for x in M choose u=Khat x in (1), and all three constraints remain in M. Hence M subset Vstar. Since B L+C and B0 L lie in M and D1L=0, the SAME L solves Dstar L=-Fstar. Therefore DECISION is necessary.

If DECISION fails, no original registered full-preview law with any constant K has exactly zero output on all inputs, for ANY finite T>h>=0. This no-go is only about exact zero. It does NOT establish infeasibility for a given positive gamma, an optimal literal gain, a bound on controller norms, stable/robust synthesis, or infinite-horizon behavior. The conclusion also does not apply if K or taps are constrained, or to unregistered nonlinear/time-varying controllers.

1. Actual plant, law and theorem

For arbitrary finite n,m,p,r, T>h>=0, S=T-h, take the original physical model with scalar W and vector w in R^m:

 dx=(Ax+Bu+Cw_(t-h))dt+(A0x+B0u)dW,
 z=D0x+D1u,                   u=Kx+L w_t,

x0=0, w supported in [0,S] and w progressively measurable with E||w||_L2[0,S]<infinity. All coefficients are constant, of their natural dimensions, with

 B0 L=0,    D1 L=0.                                        (R)

Define P=A+BK, Q=A0+B0K, b=BL, c=C, D=D0+D1K. Thus P,Q may be arbitrary noncommuting n x n matrices, b,c are n x m, D is p x n, and the physical closed law is dx=(Px+bw_t+c w_(t-h))dt+Qx dW,z=Dx.

Assume ONE of the following explicitly different sufficient cases (so necessarily p>=m):

 (i) h>0 and rank(D b)=m;
 (ii) h>0, every column of b has D-invisible common (P,Q)-invariant span, and rank(D c)=m;
 (iii) h=0 and rank[D(b+c)]=m.

Then deterministic Gamma,H and a model-dependent epsilon>0 below satisfy, for ALL such E1 vector disturbances (and hence E2),

 E||z||_L2[0,T] <= (1-epsilon)Gamma E||w||_L2[0,S]
                 <= (1-epsilon)H E||w||_L2[0,S].          (A)

Gamma is an expected OPERATOR-column bound, not asserted equal to the true literal gain. Therefore gamma=Gamma is uniformly strictly feasible for this actual feedback/preview law; its true literal gain is >0. The gain endpoint, arbitrary rank-deficient early response, additive control diffusion B0L!=0, direct feedthrough D1L!=0, general preview synthesis and multidimensional W remain outside this claim. epsilon is defined by a guaranteed positive-probability flow event, not numerically evaluated in closed form.

2. Operator-valued physical columns

Let Phi(t,s) solve the actual fundamental Itô equation d_t Phi=P Phi dt+Q Phi dW, Phi(s,s)=I. For each s, define the random linear map K_s:R^m -> H_out=L2([0,T];R^p) by

 (K_s v)(t)=1_(t>=s)D Phi(t,s)b v +1_(t>=s+h)D Phi(t,s+h)c v.

The actual original output is z=integral_0^S K_s w_s ds. Set

 Y_s=||K_s||_(R^m -> H_out),  Cbar(s)=E Y_s,
 Gamma=(integral_0^S Cbar(s)²ds)^(1/2),
 R=integral_0^S ||K_s w_s||_H_out ds,
 Rbar=integral_0^S Y_s |w_s| ds,  O=||z||_H_out.

O<=R<=Rbar. K_s, Y_s depend only on W increments after s, so Y_s is independent of F_s even though the FULL operator is random. Since w_s is F_s measurable,

 E Rbar = E integral_0^S Cbar(s)|w_s|ds <= Gamma E||w||.  (1)

All expressions are finite for E1 inputs by the deterministic moment bounds below. This is the original unsquared expected-path criterion, not an RMS substitution.

3. Deterministic moments and shape lower bound

Define the covariance semigroup M_t'(X)=P M_t+M_t P^T+Q M_t Q^T, M_0(X)=X, and

 X_h=M_h(bb^T)+(e^(Ph)b)c^T+c(e^(Ph)b)^T+cc^T,
 a_b(t)=tr[D M_t(bb^T)D^T],  a_h(t)=tr[D M_t(X_h)D^T],
 m2(s)=integral_0^h a_b(t)dt+integral_0^(S-s)a_h(t)dt,
 H²=integral_0^S m2(s)ds
   =S integral_0^h a_b(t)dt+integral_0^S (S-t)a_h(t)dt. (2)

Here m2(s)=E||K_s||_HS², where ||K_s||_HS²=sum_j ||K_s e_j||_H_out². Formula (2) follows from independent increments and is valid for noncommuting P,Q. Rank in (i)–(iii) gives m2(s)>0 for s<S and H>0. Since ||K_s||op<=||K_s||HS, Jensen gives Gamma<=H.

Let b_j,c_j denote columns and set

 M4=8Tm integral_0^T sum_(j=1)^m
       [E||D Phi(t,0)b_j||^4+E||D Phi(t,0)c_j||^4]dt.

All fourth moments are finite and given by deterministic finite-dimensional tensor ODEs. The bound E||K_s||HS^4<=M4 follows from the time Cauchy inequality, (x+y)^4<=8(x^4+y^4), and (sum_j x_j²)^2<=m sum_j x_j^4. In these nonzero rank cases M4>0. Pointwise ||K_s||op>=||K_s||HS/sqrt(m) and Hölder give

 Cbar(s)>=m2(s)^(3/2)/(sqrt(m) sqrt(M4)).

Thus f=Cbar/Gamma has unit L2 norm and deterministic positive lower envelope

 f(s)>=f0(s):=m2(s)^(3/2)/(sqrt(m) sqrt(M4) H),  s<S.   (3)

4. A uniform vector-direction prefix on a positive future event

Set delta=S/8, s0=S/4, u0=3S/4. In case (i) use v=b,dstar=0 and L0=min(h/2,S/16); case (ii) use v=c,dstar=h,L0=S/16; case (iii) use v=b+c,dstar=0,L0=S/16. The K_s prefix on [s+dstar,s+dstar+L0] is D Phi(t,s+dstar)v; in case (ii) the b-output is identically zero by invariant-subspace invisibility.

Because Dv has full column rank, for each Brownian path and each fixed s the prefix map v_input -> D Phi(t,s+dstar)v v_input is injective as a map R^m -> L2(prefix): at its initial time it is Dv, and path continuity preserves positive least singular value over some positive interval. Thus both anchors s0,u0 have positive minimum prefix singular value almost surely.

Almost-sure continuity of the linear Itô flow in both time arguments, and a countable choice of deterministic closed I,J around s0,u0, positive rational lambda_I,lambda_J and finite rational M0, yield one positive-probability event E0 such that

 sup I+L0<inf J,
 inf_(s in I) inf_(|v_input|=1) ||1_prefix K_s v_input|| >=lambda_I,
 inf_(u in J) inf_(|v_input|=1) ||1_prefix K_u v_input|| >=lambda_J,
 sup_(v_time in I union J) ||K_(v_time)||op <=M0.          (4)

Choose I,J inside (delta,S). Because all columns in (4) use Brownian increments after delta, E0 is independent of F_delta; put p0=P(E0)>0. Existence is not a claim to have evaluated p0 or its constants numerically. Define xi=1-sqrt[1-(lambda_I/M0)^2]>0.

For actual q_s=K_s w_s, the first prefix lies strictly before the earliest possible support of q_u. On E0,

 ||1_prefix q_s||>=lambda_I |w_s|,  ||q_s||<=M0 |w_s|,
 ||q_u||>=lambda_J |w_u|,
 |<q_s,q_u>| <= (1-xi)||q_s||||q_u||.                     (5)

The prefix lower bound is UNIFORM in the unknown vector direction w_s, which is why full column rank is needed. No assumption that K_s itself is positive or scalar is made.

5. Weighted nonanticipation and strict gain gap

Define

 b0=(integral_0^delta f0²)^(1/2), bI=integral_I f0, bJ=integral_J f0,
 eta=min{bI/(2sqrt(|I|)),bJ/(2sqrt(|J|))},
 cI=lambda_I bI/2, cJ=lambda_J bJ/2,
 epsilon=min{p0² b0²/[128(1+p0)²], p0 eta²/16,
             xi cI cJ p0²/(8H²)} >0.                      (6)

All terms are deterministic; (4) guarantees existence of the event constants. Normalize U=||w|| by EU=1, let q=1-EO/Gamma, and e=|| |w|/U-f || when U>0 (zero otherwise). Because (1) is an exact factorization for Rbar,

 E[U e²]<=2q.

The F_delta-measurable variable ||w 1_[0,delta]||/||f 1_[0,delta]|| differs from U in L1 by at most sqrt(2q)/b0. Therefore

 E[U 1_E0]>=p0-(1+p0)sqrt(2q)/b0.                         (7)

On E0 and e<=eta, the f>=f0 shape bound gives integral_I |w_s|ds>=bI U/2 and integral_J |w_s|ds>=bJ U/2. Equation (5) and the vector-output Hilbert pairwise identity then imply

 R²-O² >=2xi cI cJ U².                                    (8)

The U-weight of e>eta is <=2q/eta² and that of Rbar>(4H/p0)U is <=p0/4. If q is below the first two terms in (6), at least p0/2 U-weight remains on the good event. There R+O<=2R<=2Rbar<=8HU/p0, so (8) gives R-O>=xi cI cJ p0 U/(4H). Since Gamma-EO >= E(Rbar-O) >=E(R-O), q>=xi cI cJ p0²/(8H²). If either preliminary bound fails, q already exceeds epsilon. This proves (A) for every progressive E1 vector input.

6. Positivity and boundary

Full-rank Dv makes the physical kernel nonzero on a positive time set with probability one. A fixed countable dense family of deterministic vector L2 inputs cannot all lie in the kernel; one has positive-probability nonzero output and finite expected norm by (1). Thus the actual literal gain is >0. This is an existence proof, not selected-reference optimization. It does not identify that gain or resolve its endpoint.

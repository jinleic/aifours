1. Actual original plant and two-tap law

Take any finite constant matrices, T>h>=0, S=T-h, scalar Brownian W and progressive vector E1 w supported on [0,S]. The actual original dynamics and controller are

 dx=(Ax+Bu+Cw_(t-h))dt+(A0x+B0u)dW,
 z=D0x+D1u,                 u=Kx+L0w_t+Lh w_(t-h),

x0=0. Distributed H is zero here; B0,D1 may be arbitrary and nonzero. Fix K and define

 P=A+BK, Q=A0+B0K, O=D0+D1K,
 F0=BL0, G0=B0L0, E0=D1L0,
 Fh=BLh+C, Gh=B0Lh, Eh=D1Lh.

Let N be the largest common P,Q-invariant subspace of ker O, computed by the finite kernel-closure recursion in the pinned predecessor.

2. Necessary AND sufficient exact fixed-law conditions

For h>0, z=0 in L2[0,T] a.s. for EVERY progressive E1 w if and only if

 E0=Eh=0,
 range(F0),range(G0),range(Fh),range(Gh) subset N.          (Z+)

For h=0, the two disturbance taps are the SAME signal and must be combined first: z=0 for every input iff

 D1(L0+Lh)=0,
 range[B(L0+Lh)+C],range[B0(L0+Lh)] subset N.             (Z0)

The actual literal gain is exactly zero in these cases. Consequently every gamma>0 is strictly feasible for this actual law; gamma=0 is never strict for a nonzero disturbance. No claim is made about necessity of (Z+) or (Z0) for ANY positive-gain threshold.

3. Proof for h>0: causality separates the taps

Sufficiency follows because all drift/diffusion forcings lie in the P,Q-invariant hidden subspace N and both direct-output maps vanish.

For necessity, choose an arbitrary deterministic step w=a on [0,epsilon], zero otherwise, with 0<epsilon<min(S,h). Before h, the delayed signal is identically zero, so on [0,epsilon] the system is precisely the pinned predecessor's zero-start input-on problem with F0,G0,E0. Universal zero output and its semimartingale word induction force E0=0 and range(F0),range(G0) subset N.

For every input, the state component x0 driven by the current tap alone now remains in N and has no output. By linear SDE superposition, subtract x0 from the full state: the remaining component is driven only by Fh,Gh times w_(t-h), starts at zero and has output O xh+Eh w_(t-h). Since the full output is universally zero and the current component is hidden, the pinned predecessor applies to this pure delayed component, forcing Eh=0 and range(Fh),range(Gh) subset N. This is a universal argument over all input directions, not a selected reference-gain optimization.

When h=0, w_t=w_(t-h) and the closed dynamics/output depend only on L0+Lh; the predecessor directly proves (Z0). The distinction h>0 versus h=0 is essential; separate zero conditions on L0,Lh at h=0 would be too strong.

4. Complete fixed-K construction and no-go for extra current preview

Let R be an orthonormal basis of N^perp and define

 A_stack=[R^T B; R^T B0; D1],   C_stack=[R^T C; 0; 0].

For h>0, (Z+) is exactly

 A_stack L0=0,         A_stack Lh=-C_stack.                (1)

Thus perfect zero-output is attainable within ALL two-tap static laws at this fixed K iff

 (I-A_stack A_stack^dagger) C_stack=0.                     (2)

When (2) holds, the complete controller family is

 L0=(I-A_stack^dagger A_stack)Y0,
 Lh=-A_stack^dagger C_stack+(I-A_stack^dagger A_stack)Yh.

In particular L0=0 is always an available choice, so adding a current preview tap cannot create a zero-output solution if pure delayed matching is impossible. For h=0, (2) applies to Lsum=L0+Lh and ANY split of a feasible Lsum is allowed.

This is an exact no-go/solvability statement only for ZERO output with fixed K and H=0. Current preview may reduce positive gain, and nonzero H or other laws might change feasibility. No optimization over K is claimed.

5. Exact diffusion-matching obstruction

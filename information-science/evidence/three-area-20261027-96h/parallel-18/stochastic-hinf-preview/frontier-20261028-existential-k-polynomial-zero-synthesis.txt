1. Original physical existence problem

Fix arbitrary finite real matrices A,A0,B,B0,C,D0,D1, dimensions n state, r control, m disturbance, p output, one scalar Brownian W, and T>h>=0. The original plant and law are

 dx=(Ax+Bu+Cw_(t-h))dt+(A0x+B0u)dW,
 z=D0x+D1u,                x0=0,
 u=Kx+L0w_t+Lh w_(t-h)+integral_0^h H(s)w_(t-s)ds.

Here K,L0,Lh are arbitrary constant real matrices and H in L2[0,h] is deterministic; w ranges over every progressively measurable vector E1 input supported on [0,T-h]. The question HERE is whether SOME K,L0,Lh,H make z=0 almost surely for every such w. If yes, every gamma>0 satisfies the actual strict expected-path-L2 inequality with the same constructed law; gamma=0 does not. This section does NOT claim that a law satisfying a given positive gamma must have zero output.

2. Finite polynomial system for h>0

Range over integer dimensions 0<=d,v<=n and 0<=k<=r. The unknowns are

 K (r x n), N (n x d), V (n x v), Z (r x k),
 Pn,Qn (d x d), Av (v x v), Bv (v x k), Theta (r x v),
 Lh (r x m), Y (d x m), and X_j (k x m) for 0<=j<v.

Zero-dimensional matrices have the usual empty conventions. Define polynomial expressions

 P=A+BK, Q=A0+B0K, O=D0+D1K, Pi=I-N N^T,
 Astar=[D1;Pi B0],   Cstar=[O;Pi Q].

The following finite system is NECESSARY AND SUFFICIENT for the physical zero-output existence problem for EVERY h>0,T>h:

 N^T N=I_d,    V^T V=I_v,    Z^T Z=I_k,                   (A)
 P N=N Pn,     Q N=N Qn,      O N=0,                       (B)
 Astar Theta+Cstar V=0,       P V+B Theta=V Av,            (C)
 Astar Z=0,    B Z=V Bv,      Astar Lh=0,                  (D)
 C+B Lh+V sum_(j=0)^(v-1) Av^j Bv X_j = N Y.             (E)

These are ordinary real polynomial equalities in finitely many unknown entries, including the matrix-power products; no exponential, inverse, pseudoinverse, norm or strict inequality appears. The union over finite d,v,k is part of the statement. Pi is an orthogonal projector because (A) makes N orthonormal; N need NOT be specified as the maximal invariant hidden subspace in the certificate.

3. Sufficiency and actual full-preview controller

Equations (A)–(B) make range N common P,Q invariant and output-invisible. Let

 rho=sum_(j=0)^(v-1) Av^j Bv X_j.

It lies in the finite controllability subspace of (Av,Bv). For the given h>0 define its deterministic finite-horizon Gramian

 Wv(h)=integral_0^h exp(Av t)Bv Bv^T exp(Av^T t)dt.

Its range is exactly the controllability subspace: zero quadratic form is equivalent by analyticity to orthogonality to all Av^j Bv. Set

 L0=0,
 v_in(t)=Bv^T exp[Av^T(h-t)] Wv(h)^dagger rho,
 xi(t)=integral_0^t exp[Av(t-s)]Bv v_in(s)ds,
 H(t)=Theta xi(t)+Z v_in(t),       0<=t<=h.                (F)

Then xi(0)=0, xi(h)=rho. Put the deterministic auxiliary impulse state m(t)=V xi(t). By (C)–(D),

 m'=P m+B H,      Astar H+Cstar m=0,

and Astar L0=Astar Lh=0. Equation (E) makes m(h)+B Lh+C lie in the invariant invisible range N. Hence the early regular output/diffusion-residual kernels and the late endpoint satisfy the exact zero-output equations in the pinned general-matrix source; they are STRONGER than needed if range N is smaller than the maximal hidden subspace. Therefore the actual original stochastic law with the constructed H produces z=0 for every progressive E1 w. H is a fixed deterministic continuous/L2 function, not adaptive feedback from m.

4. Necessity from the fixed-K controlled-invariant result

Suppose some actual law with h>0 produces universal zero output. Take its K and let N be the maximal (P,Q)-invariant subspace of ker O, represented by an orthonormal basis. The named horizon-independent source shows that a zero-output law exists also with L0=0 and gives the exact controlled-invariant/reachable-subspace endpoint condition.

Choose V as an orthonormal basis of the corresponding largest controlled invariant subspace. For each basis vector of V, the source's controlled-invariance and algebraic range conditions permit a control column Theta satisfying the two equations in (C); define Av as the coordinates of P V+B Theta in V. Choose Z as an orthonormal basis of ALL control directions z satisfying Astar z=0 and B z in range V, and define Bv by BZ=V Bv. Then (A)–(D) hold.

The source's endpoint condition says there is Lh with Astar Lh=0 and an auxiliary reachable state rho_state such that C+B Lh+rho_state lies in range N. Since the zero-start constrained dynamics are V times the controllable pair (Av,Bv), rho_state=V sum_(j=0)^(v-1) Av^j Bv X_j for suitable matrices X_j. Taking Y as the coordinates of that hidden endpoint establishes (E). Thus every universally zero-output physical law yields at least one solution of the finite polynomial system.

5. Coincident preview h=0

For h=0, H has empty support and the atoms L0,Lh merge as L=L0+Lh. Range over 0<=d<=n and unknown K,N,Pn,Qn,L,Y with

 N^T N=I_d,  P N=N Pn,  Q N=N Qn,  O N=0,
 D1 L=0,    B0 L=N Y0,    B L+C=N Y1.                    (G)

Y0,Y1 are free d x m matrices. This finite polynomial system is necessary and sufficient by the pinned exact h=0 zero-output theorem. The positive-h system can be feasible when (G) is not feasible for a FIXED K; a global all-K separation is NOT claimed here.

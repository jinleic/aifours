3. Every pair of nonzero vector columns is noncollinear

Shrink I,J so that, for every s in I,u in J, all of these open intervals have positive length and contain the corresponding observed subwindows:

 (s,u),       (u,s+h),       (s+h,u+h),       (u+h,T).       (2)

Suppose for nonzero input directions a,d and scalar t one has K_s a=t K_u d as L2 output paths. On the constructed event:

 * On (s,u), only the early b-term from K_s is present. Its observed prefix has kernel Nb, so a in Nb.
 * On (u,s+h), the b-term from K_s is identically invisible and its c-term has not started; only K_u's b-term remains. Thus d in Nb (if t=0, injectivity below already rules out the equality).
 * On (s+h,u+h), both b-terms are invisible and only the delayed c-term from K_s is present. The observed c-prefix is injective on Nb, so a=0, a contradiction.

The interval (u+h,T) contains the observed c-prefix for K_u; it similarly guarantees K_u d!=0 for every d!=0. The corresponding b/c tests make K_s injective too. Therefore, for ALL s in I,u in J and all nonzero a,d,

 K_s a and K_u d are nonzero and not collinear.             (3)

This is a geometric all-direction statement; no sampled test inputs are used.

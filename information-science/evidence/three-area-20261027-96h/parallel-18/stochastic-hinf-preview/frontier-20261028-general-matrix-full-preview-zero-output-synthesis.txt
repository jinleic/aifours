1. Actual full physical model

 dx=(Ax+Bu+Cw_(t-h))dt+(A0x+B0u)dW,

 P=A+BK, Q=A0+B0K, O=D0+D1K,

 G0=B0L0, Gh=B0Lh, G(r)=B0 H(r),

All P,Q including noncommuting pairs, all additive diffusion taps G and direct output taps E are admitted. Define the actual deterministic causal time operators f=Fop w, g=Gop w, o=Eop w from these current/delayed/distributed coefficients. The closed physical law is dx=(Px+f)dt+(Qx+g)dW,z=Ox+o.

2. Deterministic mean trajectory and stochastic residual

For each path define the ordinary finite-variation trajectory

 mu=J w,       mu_t=integral_0^t exp[P(t-s)] f_s ds,
 Top=Eop+O J,  Dop=Gop+Q J.

J,Top,Dop are deterministic bounded L2 time operators, and mu is progressive. Put y=x-mu. Its actual SDE and output are exactly

 dy=P y dt+(Qy+Dop w)dW,     z=Top w+O y.                 (1)

Let N be the largest common P,Q-invariant subspace of ker O and Pi the orthogonal projection onto N^perp. N is found by the finite kernel-closure recursion, independently of w.

3. Full necessary-and-sufficient zero-output theorem

The actual output is zero in L2[0,T] a.s. for EVERY progressive E1 input if and only if

 Top=0   and   Pi Dop=0 as deterministic L2 operators.      (Z)

The exact fixed-law literal gain is then 0, and every gamma>0 is strictly feasible (gamma=0 is not). Conversely failure of (Z) proves the law is not universally zero-output; it does NOT exclude feasibility at any particular positive gamma. For fixed K, (Z) is affine linear in the full tap triple L0,Lh,H.

4. Necessity for arbitrary noncommuting P,Q

First take ALL deterministic L2 inputs w. They are E2. For each, the deterministic forcing d(t)=(Dop w)(t) is L2 and the zero-start residual y in (1) has Ey_t=0. Therefore E z=Top w, so universal zero implies Top=0. Then O y=0 a.e. almost surely; continuity of y makes O y identically zero in time.

For any word V(P,Q), suppose O V y is identically zero. Its continuous Itô semimartingale decomposition is

 d(O V y)=O V P y dt+[O V Q y+O V d(t)]dW.

Uniqueness of the finite-variation/martingale components gives O V P y=0 and O V Q y+O V d=0 dt x P-a.e. Taking expectations in the latter and using Ey=0 gives O V d=0 a.e. Hence O V Q y=0 too; continuity extends both new residual identities to all times. Starting V=identity and inducting over every finite word shows O V d(t)=0 a.e. for every V, so d(t) lies in N. Because this holds for EVERY deterministic L2 input, Pi Dop=0 as a bounded operator. This argument uses the entire deterministic input space for an all-input property; it does not optimize a selected reference or assume an endpoint attainer.

5. Sufficiency for E1-only inputs

For any random input path w(omega) in L2, the deterministic operator identities imply Top w(omega)=0 and Dop w(omega) lies in N for almost every time. The residual y in (1) starts at zero, its P,Q coefficients preserve N, and its additive diffusion forcing lies in N. Strong uniqueness therefore keeps y in N pathwise, yielding O y=0 and z=0. This is a pathwise/local argument; no E2 moment of w is needed.

6. Explicit impulse equations; h>0

For 0<tau<T define the two ordinary matrix kernels

 k(tau)=1_(tau<h) E(tau)+O exp(P tau)F0
       +1_(tau>=h) O exp[P(tau-h)]Fh

The first is the regular kernel of Top and the second that of Dop. As T>h and S>0, deterministic approximate-identity input pulses near zero identify all ordinary lags in (0,T) and the two distinct direct atoms at 0,h. Consequently (Z) is equivalent to

 E0=Eh=0,                k(tau)=0 a.e. on (0,T),

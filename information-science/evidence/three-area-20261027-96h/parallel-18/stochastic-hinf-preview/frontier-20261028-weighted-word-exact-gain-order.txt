2. Exact ordered-chaos kernel

Fix a weight k, deterministic input v and a noise count j; let i=(k-j)/2 be a nonnegative integer. Different j give different homogeneous Wiener chaoses and are orthogonal in L2(Omega). For a fixed j, distribute the i drift integrations among the j+1 chronological gaps: alpha=(alpha0,...,alphaj), sum alpha_l=i.

After deterministic/stochastic Fubini, the corresponding ordered j-fold Itô kernel, before integration against v(r), is the finite polynomial

 P_alpha(r,t1,...,tj,t) = [ product_(l=0)^j (t_(l+1)-t_l)^(alpha_l)/alpha_l! ]
       * D A^(alpha_j) Q A^(alpha_(j-1)) Q ... Q A^(alpha_0) C,

where t0=r and t_(j+1)=t. For j=0 the same formula has no Q. Summing over alpha gives exactly the j-chaos component of T_k: every Itô word has a unique allocation of its drift letters among the noise gaps. There is no cross-chaos cancellation and no missing Itô contraction, since the words are NESTED LINEAR Itô integrals rather than products of integrals.

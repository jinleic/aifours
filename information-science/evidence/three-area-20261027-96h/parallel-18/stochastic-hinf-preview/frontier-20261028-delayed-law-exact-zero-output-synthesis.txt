FRONTIER INFO-3: NECESSARY AND SUFFICIENT ZERO-OUTPUT SYNTHESIS FOR A PURE DELAYED PREVIEW LAW

Status: NEW WRITTEN CANDIDATE, UNREVIEWED AND UNADOPTED. Native prewrite observation Wed Oct 28 18:30:03 PDT 2026. This is an exact all-matrix necessary-and-sufficient result for the explicitly restricted pure delayed law, including nonzero control diffusion and direct output feedthrough. It is not a global arbitrary-controller synthesis theorem, a selected-reference optimization, or a novelty claim.

1. Actual original model and closed law

Let T>h>=0, S=T-h>0. Take arbitrary finite real matrices, scalar Brownian W, vector w and the original plant

 dx=(Ax+Bu+Cw_(t-h))dt+(A0x+B0u)dW,
 z=D0x+D1u,              x0=0.

Let w be any progressively measurable E1 input, supported in [0,S], and fix arbitrary constant K,Lh in the ACTUAL pure delayed law

 u_t=Kx_t+Lh w_(t-h).                                      (L)

This is a legitimate subclass of the registered deterministic linear preview family, with current and distributed taps zero. It uses the given delayed disturbance channel, not an anticipative input. Put

 P=A+BK, Q=A0+B0K, O=D0+D1K,
 F=BLh+C, G=B0Lh, E=D1Lh.

The actual closed dynamics are dx=(Px+F w_(t-h))dt+(Qx+G w_(t-h))dW, z=Ox+E w_(t-h). All matrices P,Q are allowed to be noncommuting, and G,E may initially be nonzero.

2. Exact zero-output criterion for EVERY E1 input

Define the deterministic largest common P,Q-invariant subspace in ker O:

 N=intersection_(all finite words V) ker[O V(P,Q)].

It is found by N0=ker O, N_(j+1)=Nj intersect P^(-1)Nj intersect Q^(-1)Nj until stable, with at most n strict dimension drops.

**Theorem.** For fixed K,Lh, the following are equivalent:

 (i) z=0 in L2[0,T] almost surely for EVERY progressive E1 disturbance w;
 (ii) E=0, range(F) subset N, and range(G) subset N.       (Z)

Consequently the actual literal gain is exactly 0 iff (Z) holds. In this case the ORIGINAL strict criterion is satisfied for every gamma>0 and every nonzero E1/E2 input; it is NOT strict at gamma=0. If (Z) fails, this theorem makes no claim that a given positive gamma is infeasible or about its exact gain.

3. Sufficiency, including noise-driven hidden state

If (Z) holds, the drift and diffusion both preserve N and all input-forcing columns lie in N. By strong uniqueness the zero-start solution x_t remains in N pathwise for every progressive path-L2 input; E=0 and O|_N=0 give z=0. This permits nonzero F and G and a genuinely noise-driven hidden state, not merely the special equation F=G=0.

4. Necessity from ALL input directions and semimartingale identities

Fix an arbitrary vector a and a deterministic step input w_t=a on [0,epsilon] and 0 afterward, with 0<epsilon<S. It is E2 and hence among the allowed E1 inputs. Before time h, w_(t-h)=0 and x=0 by homogeneous uniqueness. On (h,h+epsilon), the output is continuous and equals Ox_t+Ea. Assumption (i) says it vanishes almost everywhere; continuity makes it vanish for all t in the interval, almost surely. Taking t down to h gives Ea=0. As a is arbitrary, E=0, and Ox_t=0 on the step interval.

Now apply Itô's equation to the identically zero continuous semimartingale Ox. Uniqueness of the finite-variation and martingale parts gives

 O(Px_t+Fa)=0,   O(Qx_t+Ga)=0

for almost every t, and continuity extends these to the entire step interval. At t=h, x_h=0, so OFa=OGa=0 and hence OPx_t=OQx_t=0. Repeat this argument for every row O V(P,Q), inductively over word length: from O V x identically zero, its drift/diffusion identities at t=h give O V F a=O V G a=0 and then O V P x=O V Q x=0. Thus O V F a=O V G a=0 for every finite word V. Varying a over a finite basis proves range(F),range(G) subset N.

This is a universal all-directions necessity proof. The deterministic step family is used only as legitimate test inputs for a claimed universal property; it is not a selected reference gain, a calibration/optimization branch, or an assertion about stochastic maximizers.

5. Closed-form exact controller construction for fixed K

Choose an orthonormal basis R of N^perp and stack the deterministic matrices

 A_stack=[R^T B; R^T B0; D1],
 C_stack=[R^T C; 0; 0].

Then (Z) holds exactly when

 A_stack Lh = -C_stack.                                    (1)

There exists a pure delayed zero-output law iff

 (I-A_stack A_stack^dagger) C_stack=0.                     (2)

When (2) holds, ALL such controllers are

 Lh= -A_stack^dagger C_stack + (I-A_stack^dagger A_stack)Y,

with arbitrary Y; taking Y=0 gives a complete explicit actual control law. Empty N^perp is allowed. Equations (1)–(2) are an exact linear-algebra decision for zero output with this fixed K; they do not optimize K, and failure does not rule out nonzero-gain feasibility or other preview controllers.

6. Exact physical hidden-noise example

Take n=2,m=r=p=1, K=0, O=[1,0],

 P=[[-1,0],[1,-2]],  Q=[[1/2,0],[1,1/3]],
 B=[1,0]^T, B0=[0,1]^T, C=[2,3]^T, D1=0.

Here N=span(e2). The exact solution Lh=-2 gives F=BLh+C=[0,3]^T and G=B0Lh=[0,-2]^T. Both forcing channels are nonzero, but they lie in N. The first state satisfies dx1=-x1 dt+(1/2)x1 dW with x1(0)=0, so z=x1=0 for every input, while the second state can be noise driven. Exact Python Fraction arithmetic checked P e2=[0,-2], Q e2=[0,1/3], F=[0,3], G=[0,-2]. This is an actual original stochastic law, not a statement of general solvability.

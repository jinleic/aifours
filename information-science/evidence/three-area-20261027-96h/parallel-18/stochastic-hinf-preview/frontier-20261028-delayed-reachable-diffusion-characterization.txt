2. A finite explicit noise-range matrix — including cancellation and h=0

The L2 H kernel and finite-horizon exponential make K_B square-integrable; hence the last integral operator is Hilbert–Schmidt. The paired delayed drift coefficient is B Lh+C, NOT two independently variable channels.

Define the n-by-n positive semidefinite matrix

The weight is positive for every 0<r<T, so lK_B=0 a.e. Conversely these three conditions plainly annihilate Bcalligraphic. The nullspace of Gamma is exactly those rows, yielding (5). No frequency sample, hidden independence of the tied input channels, or infinite spectral optimization is assumed.

3. Sufficiency for actual RANDOM inputs

For every progressive input w, solve pathwise the deterministic ODE x^d_w with the same w. Since w(omega)∈L2(J) a.s., the definition of D0 shows Bcalligraphic w(t,omega)∈D0 for almost every (omega,t). The actual deviation eta=x−x^d obeys

4. Necessity from actual deterministic callers, every delay/kernel term

For ANY finite word W in P,Q, project W eta onto N-perp. If it is zero, the zero semimartingale decomposition gives W P eta∈N and `W Q eta+W Bcalligraphic v∈N` a.e. Induction gives W Bcalligraphic v(t)∈N for every finite word W and all t for this smooth v.

Smooth compact inputs are dense in L2(J), and Bcalligraphic is a bounded deterministic L2 operator. Thus W times every vector in (D0) lies in N. Their word span is D, so D⊂N.

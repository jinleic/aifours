2. Proof

The exact global zero-rejection theorem says eta_global>0 iff NO registered deterministic full-preview law can have universally zero output, even with arbitrary unconstrained K and taps. The bounded-class attainment theorem says the minimum actual E1 gain g_B is attained. Since a nonnegative gain equals zero exactly when that law's output is zero for every input, eta_global>0 rules out g_B=0. Hence g_B>0. This argument does not assume a worst-case disturbance exists and does not replace expected-path norms with RMS norms.

The controller budget, finite T and hmax<T are essential hypotheses of this compactness proof. No positive floor is inferred over unbounded K/L/H, over horizons h approaching T, or for infinite horizon.

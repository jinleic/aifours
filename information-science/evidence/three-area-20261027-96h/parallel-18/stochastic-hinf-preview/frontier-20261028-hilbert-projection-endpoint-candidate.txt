3. Exact vector normal equation at the actual E1 attainer

For EVERY v in H, v is also E1. For sufficiently small real epsilon, w+epsilon v has positive E1 norm. The actual SDE is linear and the common gain is gamma, so

 F(epsilon)=gamma E||w+epsilon v||-E||z+epsilon T_2 v|| >=0,

Because U>0 a.s., the input norm has the same derivative from both sides, E<s,v>. The difference quotient is dominated by ||v|| and E||v||<infinity. The output norm derivative on V>0 is <p,T_2v>; on V=0 the right/left derivatives are +/−||T_2v||. The output difference quotient is dominated by ||T_2v||, also integrable. Thus at the minimum,

 E[1_{V=0}||T_2v||]=0,
 E<p,T_2v> = gamma E<s,v>,       for EVERY v in H.                (1)

4. A strict quantitative nonprogressivity obstruction

The projected normalized input r is NONZERO. Indeed, for any positive energy cutoff n, take the actual progressive stopped input w_n=w 1_{t<tau_n}; it is E2. Then

 <s,w_n>_H = E[||w_n||_J^2/U] >0,

because U>0 a.s. and positive energy is accumulated before a positive cutoff. The expectation is finite since it is <=E U. If r were zero, the H inner product with every w_n would vanish, contradiction.

Use v=r in (1)–(2). It is a NONZERO actual E2 input, so strict E2 feasibility gives

 gamma ||r||_H^2 = E<p,T_2r>
                   <= E||T_2r||_I
                   < gamma E||r||_J.

Cauchy-Schwarz on probability and projection contraction give the complete chain

 **0 < ||r||_H^2 < E||r||_J <= ||r||_H <= 1.**                (3)

In particular ||r||_H<1. Since P_prog is an orthogonal projection and ||s||_L2=1,

 **||s-P_prog s||_L2(P x J)^2 = 1-||r||_H^2 >0.**              (4)

Thus EVERY hypothetical full-support E1-only endpoint attainer under strict E2 performance has a normalized input direction w/||w|| that is GENUINELY NONPROGRESSIVE in product-L2, by a strictly positive distance. This is stronger than merely saying that a chosen regularizer's multiplier may vary; it gives a canonical Hilbert-space obstruction and an actual E2 test input r.

If s itself were progressive, then r=s, ||r||_H^2=E||r||_J=1, and (3) would fail; equivalently (2) shows the actual E2 input s attains gamma. No uniform numerical lower bound on the projection distance is inferred, and (H+) is essential to the all-direction differentiability argument. Inputs which vanish as a full path on a positive-probability event remain a separate unproved case.

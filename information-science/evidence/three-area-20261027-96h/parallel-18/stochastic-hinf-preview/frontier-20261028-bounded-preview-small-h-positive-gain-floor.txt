1. Actual physical family, finite budget and claim

Fix finite T>0, scalar Brownian W, arbitrary finite real original plant matrices A,A0,B,B0,C,D0,D1 and a fixed state-feedback K. For each 0<h<T use the actual original law

 dx=(Ax+Bu+Cw_(t-h))dt+(A0x+B0u)dW,
 z=D0x+D1u,
 u=Kx+L0(h)w_t+Lh(h)w_(t-h)+integral_0^h H_h(r)w_(t-r)dr,

x0=0; w ranges over progressive vector E1 inputs supported on [0,T-h]. Let gamma_1(h;L0,Lh,H) be the ACTUAL supremal E||z||_L2/E||w||_L2, not an RMS gain. It is finite by the named all-matrix source.

Fix any finite M>0 and constrain the allowed family only for this theorem by

 ||L0(h)||_F<=M,  ||Lh(h)||_F<=M,
 ||H_h||_L2([0,h];F)<=M.                                  (B)

Let N be the largest common (P,Q)-invariant subspace in ker O for P=A+BK,Q=A0+B0K,O=D0+D1K; Pi projects onto N^perp, Astar=[D1;Pi B0], Z spans ker Astar and Bbar=BZ. Assume

 Pi C not in range(Pi Bbar) columnwise.                    (NZ)

By the named exact h=0 source, (NZ) is precisely the failure of any fixed-K zero-output law at h=0.

**Theorem.** For every finite M there exist deterministic numbers h_M>0 and c_M>0 such that

 gamma_1(h;L0,Lh,H) >= c_M                                 (A)

for EVERY 0<h<h_M and EVERY actual preview law satisfying (B). Thus sufficiently small preview cannot make the TRUE literal expected-path gain arbitrarily small under a uniform finite tap budget when (NZ) holds. The proof guarantees existence but does NOT evaluate c_M, h_M or the sharp gain. The budget is an extra stated hypothesis and is not silently imposed on the original synthesis problem.

2. Exact limit of bounded full-preview laws on any fixed E2 input

Suppose contrariwise that h_j downarrow 0 and laws satisfying (B) have gamma_1(h_j)->0. Finite-dimensional compactness gives a subsequence, not relabeled, with L0(h_j)->L0*, Lh(h_j)->Lh*. Set L*=L0*+Lh*; no convergence of H_h as a function on shrinking intervals is assumed.

Take ANY fixed progressive w in L2(Omega x [0,T]) whose time support is contained in [0,T-epsilon] for some epsilon>0. For j large it belongs to the actual input space [0,T-h_j]. Extend w by zero outside [0,T]. Standard strong continuity of time translations in L2(Omega x R) gives

 ||w(.-h_j)-w||_L2(Omega x [0,T]) ->0.

Pathwise Young plus Cauchy–Schwarz gives

 ||integral_0^(h_j) H_hj(r) w(.-r)dr||_L2(Omega x [0,T])
   <=sqrt(h_j) M ||w||_L2(Omega x [0,T]) ->0.             (1)

Consequently all actual closed drift, diffusion and direct-output forcing signals converge in L2(Omega x time) to those of the h=0 law u=Kx+L*w. This includes nonzero B0,D1 and arbitrary noncommuting state noise. The zero-start linear Itô SDE is continuous from L2 forcing into L2(Omega;C[0,T]); hence the actual output paths satisfy

 E||z_j-z_0||_L2 ->0.                                     (2)

Equation (2) is a conventional E2 continuity argument applied ONLY to a fixed E2 test input; the gains and final conclusion remain the original E1 expected-path quantities.

3. Contradiction with the exact h=0 criterion

Since w is an admissible E1 input for all large j,

 E||z_j|| <= gamma_1(h_j) E||w|| ->0.

Combining with (2) gives z_0=0 almost surely for EVERY fixed progressive E2 input with support away from T. Such inputs are dense in progressive E2 under the standard L2(Omega x time) norm by truncation before T; the h=0 linear SDE/output map is continuous in that norm. Hence the h=0 law with L* has zero output for ALL progressive E2 inputs. In particular it has zero output for the entire deterministic L2 input space; the named h=0 semimartingale criterion then forces the same exact matrix conditions for universal E1 zero output. This contradicts (NZ).

The subsequence of taps was selected by finite-dimensional compactness BEFORE quantifying over the arbitrary w, so the conclusion is simultaneous and does not depend on choosing or optimizing a reference disturbance. The contradiction proves (A): otherwise pick h_j<1/j and budget-feasible laws with gain <1/j. The zero-input controller class is nonempty, but even if a numerical infimum is not attained this selection is valid by the definition of infimum.

1. Original model and inputs of the decision

Use the same actual full finite-horizon model and law as the pinned source:

 dx=(Ax+Bu+Cw_(t-h))dt+(A0x+B0u)dW,
 z=D0x+D1u,                 x0=0,
 u=Kx+L0 w_t+Lh w_(t-h)+integral_0^h H(r)w_(t-r)dr.

All coefficient matrices are arbitrary real finite-dimensional, scalar W, vector progressive E1 w supported on [0,S], S=T-h>0, H in L2[0,h], and K is FIXED. Define

 P=A+BK,  Q=A0+B0K,  O=D0+D1K,
 N=maximal common (P,Q)-invariant subspace of ker O,
 Pi=orthogonal projection onto N^perp,
 Astar=[D1;Pi B0],   Cstar=[O;Pi Q].

N is computed by finite kernel iteration. Astar has p+n rows and r control columns; it may be arbitrarily rank deficient. Let Z be an orthonormal basis matrix for ker Astar and Bbar=B Z.

2. Replace the Volterra equation by a finite-dimensional constrained system

For h>0 and one disturbance-coordinate column, define the DETERMINISTIC impulse state

 m(t)=exp(Pt) B l0 + integral_0^t exp[P(t-s)]B H(s)ds,

where l0 is the corresponding column of L0. This is an auxiliary design trajectory, NOT the stochastic plant state or a feedback signal. The imported source proves universal zero output iff Astar L0=Astar Lh=0, the early Volterra equation holds, and Pi[m(h)+B Lh+C]=0.

The early equation is exactly

 m'=P m+B H,       Astar H+Cstar m=0,       m(0)=B l0.

Define the deterministic state subspace and reduced drift

 X=ker[(I-Astar Astar^dagger) Cstar],
 Pbar=P-B Astar^dagger Cstar.                              (1)

Since l0=Z a0, every solution has m(t) in X and can be written

 m'=Pbar m+Bbar u(t),
 H(t)=-Astar^dagger Cstar m(t)+Z u(t),
 m(0)=Bbar a0.                                             (2)

Conversely every L2 solution of (2) remaining in X satisfies the early Volterra equation, and Astar L0=0. The equality m in X follows from the algebraic range condition; m is continuous, so an a.e. subspace condition holds for every time.

3. Finite controlled-invariant recursion

Set V0=X and recursively

 V_(j+1)=X intersect {v: Pbar v in Vj+range(Bbar)}.        (3)

These subspaces decrease and stabilize at V after at most n strict dimension drops. V is the largest subspace of X controlled-invariant for (Pbar,Bbar). Every absolutely continuous trajectory of (2) staying in X lies in V at all times: if it lies in Vj, its derivative lies in Vj a.e., so (3) and continuity put it in V_(j+1); iterate.

Choose ANY linear F:V -> R^k such that (Pbar+Bbar F)V subset V, and extend it linearly off V. Such F exists from controlled invariance by choosing solutions on a basis. Let W have columns spanning the finite input subspace

 Ustar={u in R^k: Bbar u in V},

and put Ac=Pbar+Bbar F, Bc=Bbar W. All admissible dynamics inside V are then exactly

 m'=Ac m+Bc v(t),                                          (4)

because any u satisfying Pbar m+Bbar u in V decomposes into Fm plus an element of Ustar. The allowed initial states are exactly

 I=range(Bbar) intersect V,                                (5)

since l0 can be any ker-Astar control coefficient.

4. Exact finite range decision, every h>0

Let Rch=span{Ac^j Bc:0<=j<n}, choose basis matrices UI for I and UR for Rch, and form

 Dreach=[Pi exp(Ac h) UI,  Pi UR,  Pi Bbar].               (6)

Then there exists a full original deterministic linear preview law with this FIXED K and zero output for all E1 inputs if and only if

 (I-Dreach Dreach^dagger) Pi C=0.                          (DECISION)

This is a finite matrix/range criterion after the subspace recursion and a matrix exponential. It is exact for all real finite matrices, arbitrary rank Astar, any h>0 with T>h, additive/multiplicative control noise, direct feedthrough and distributed L2 H. The criterion is a mathematical real-arithmetic statement; no numerical conditioning or decidability for arbitrary uncomputable real inputs is claimed.

Proof: the exact reachable states of (4) at h from initial m0 in I are exp(Ac h)I+Rch; the delayed atom Lh=Z ah adds range(Bbar) to the endpoint. The imported zero-output endpoint is Pi[m(h)+Bbar ah+C]=0. These are exactly the columns spanned in (6). The familiar reachability equality range(Wh)=Rch follows without an asymptotic argument: for the finite-horizon Gramian

 Wh=integral_0^h exp(Ac t) Bc Bc^T exp(Ac^T t)dt,

v^T Wh v=0 iff Bc^T exp(Ac^T t)v=0 for all t in [0,h], which by analyticity is equivalent to v perpendicular to every Ac^j Bc. Thus no infinite-time limit, genericity, or controllability assumption is used. Different admissible choices of F,W give the same reachable endpoint set because (4) parameterizes ALL trajectories staying in V.

5. Explicit controller construction when DECISION holds

Solve the finite linear endpoint equation columnwise for deterministic matrices M0,Rho,ah with

 columns(M0) in I, columns(Rho) in Rch,
 Pi[exp(Ac h)M0+Rho+Bbar ah+C]=0.

Choose a0 with Bbar a0=M0, set L0=Z a0 and Lh=Z ah, and define the explicit deterministic input function

 v(t)=Bc^T exp[Ac^T(h-t)] Wh^dagger Rho,
 m(t)=exp(Ac t)M0+integral_0^t exp[Ac(t-s)]Bc v(s)ds,
 H(t)=-Astar^dagger Cstar m(t)+Z[F m(t)+W v(t)].            (7)

All constructions apply columnwise to the m disturbance dimensions. Because Rho lies in range(Wh), the integral reaches Rho at time h. The trajectory stays in V and hence X; (2) verifies the early Volterra equation, and the finite endpoint choice verifies the late kernel. Formula (7) is a FIXED deterministic continuous, hence L2, kernel in the actual registered law, not an adaptive use of the auxiliary state m.

Consequently z=0 pathwise for every actual progressive E1 disturbance; any prescribed gamma>0 is strictly feasible by this constructed law, while gamma=0 is not strict. If DECISION fails, NO law in this full deterministic linear preview class with the given K has identically zero output, but positive-gain feasibility is not ruled out.

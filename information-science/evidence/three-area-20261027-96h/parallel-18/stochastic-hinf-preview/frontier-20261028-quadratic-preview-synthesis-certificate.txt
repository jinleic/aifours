1. Original physical plant and design question

For arbitrary finite matrices, scalar Brownian W, vector w in R^m, T>h>=0,S=T-h, consider the original plant

 dx=(Ax+Bu+Cw_(t-h))dt+(A0x+B0u)dW,
 z=D0x+D1u,                  x0=0,

with progressive E1 disturbance supported in [0,S]. Fix ANY real state-feedback matrix K. We choose the static current-preview matrix L in

 u=Kx+Lw_t

subject to B0L=0,D1L=0; other preview taps are zero. These restrictions describe the admitted constructive subclass only, not all possible controllers. Define P=A+BK,Q=A0+B0K,D=D0+D1K,b=BL,c=C.

For this fixed K, we compute a real feasible L minimizing the EXACT deterministic Hilbert–Schmidt kernel upper certificate H(L). This is an auxiliary sufficient upper bound for the actual literal criterion E||z||/E||w||, NOT its RMS replacement and NOT necessarily the minimal true literal gain.

2. Deterministic matrix ODEs and the quadratic certificate

For symmetric X let M_t(X) solve M'=P M+M P^T+Q M Q^T, M_0=X; its adjoint M_t^* satisfies W'=P^T W+W P+Q^T W Q. Set

 W(t)=M_t^*(D^T D),
 Wc=integral_0^S (S-t)W(t)dt,
 Gb=S integral_0^h W(t)dt + M_h^*(Wc),
 J=e^(P^T h) Wc.

All matrices are deterministic and W(t),Wc,Gb are positive semidefinite; no commutativity or stability assumption is used. In the original kernel's exact stochastic second-moment formula,

 H(L)^2=tr[b^T Gb b]+2 tr[b^T J c]+tr[c^T Wc c].         (1)

Derivation: early current-preview contribution is S integral_0^h tr[b^T W(t)b]dt. The late factor Phi_h b+c has second moment M_h(bb^T)+e^(Ph)b c^T+c b^T e^(P^T h)+cc^T; pairing it with Wc yields (1). This is an EXACT HS bound on the actual physical future columns, including their noncommuting-noise cross term, not a sample estimate. When h=0, Gb=J=Wc and (1)=tr[(b+c)^T Wc(b+c)].

3. Closed-form constrained solution, including singular directions

Stack F=[B0;D1] and let Z be a deterministic orthonormal basis for ker F. Every permitted L is L=Z X, with arbitrary X in R^(k x m), k=dim ker F. Put E=BZ and

 M=E^T Gb E,    N=E^T J c,    Hc=tr[c^T Wc c].

Then M is positive semidefinite and

 H(ZX)^2 = tr[X^T M X]+2tr[X^T N]+Hc.                    (2)

Necessarily every column of N lies in range(M): if v in ker M, inserting X=t v e_j^T into the nonnegative exact quantity H² for all real t forces v^T N_j=0. Therefore the full set of minimizers and minimum are

 X*= -M^dagger N + (I-M^dagger M)Y,   L*=Z X*,
 Hmin²=Hc-tr[N^T M^dagger N] >=0,                         (3)

where M^dagger is the Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse and Y is arbitrary. The simple deterministic choice Y=0 gives a complete actual law. If k=0, only L=0 is permitted and Hmin²=Hc. Formula (3) is exact, including a singular M; it does not infer that L* is optimal for the true literal gain.

1. Actual original law and coefficient conditions

Fix arbitrary original matrices and a constant K; put P=A+BK,Q=A0+B0K,O=D0+D1K. For each finite S=T-h<=h and a constant feedforward L use the original pure-delayed law

 u_t=Kx_t+L w_(t-h),

so the actual closed input coefficients are F=BL+C,G=B0L,E=D1L. The original vector progressive E1/E2 disturbance is supported on [0,S] and the criterion is E||z||/E||w||. K,L and plant matrices remain FIXED as S downarrow zero.

For a word W in P,Q define wt(W)=2 #(P)+#(Q), with the empty word weight0. For integer k>=1 define S_k as the set of all matrices L satisfying the FINITE affine system

 D1 L=0;
 O W B0 L=0              for EVERY word with wt(W)<=k-2;
 O W (B L+C)=0           for EVERY word with wt(W)<=k-3.   (1)

A negative weight cutoff means no equations. Set S_0 to the entire L-space. By the pinned operator-zero theorem, L in S_k iff T0,...,T_(k-1) all vanish in the ACTUAL revealed-window expansion. Each S_k is an affine linear solution set, and S_(k+1) subset S_k.

2. Finite decision and exponent-optimal constant law

For fixed K, evaluate these nested linear systems up to k=2n+1, n=state dimension. They are solvable by ordinary matrix/range operations; a pseudoinverse may be used after stacking coefficients columnwise.

* If S_(2n+1) is nonempty, any L in it makes the ACTUAL output identically zero for every input and every horizon, as the named finite reachability theorem shows.
* Otherwise define kstar=max{k:0<=k<=2n and S_k is nonempty}. For EVERY L in S_kstar the first nonzero weighted coefficient is exactly kstar, and BOTH actual E1/E2 literal gains obey

 gamma_i(S;L)=c_kstar(L) S^(kstar/2)+O(S^((kstar+1)/2)),
 c_kstar(L)>0,  i=1,2.                                    (2)

Any fixed L outside S_kstar has an earlier nonzero coefficient and a strictly WORSE asymptotic exponent. Thus a solution of (1) at kstar is an exponent-optimal FIXED constant pure-delayed law for this fixed K. The algorithm makes no claim about minimizing c_kstar or about a family L=L(S) that changes/diverges with S; those may trade coefficients across orders. It also does not assert that the coefficient norm is attained when input diffusion is present.

3. Why the finite cutoff is complete

A common (P,Q)-reachable state subspace generated by F or G stabilizes within n-1 word-length steps. Every word of length at most n-1 has weight at most 2(n-1). Hence if T0,...,T_2n all vanish, both reachable forcing subspaces are output-invisible and E=0, proving universal zero output. Conversely if S_(kstar+1) is empty while S_kstar is nonempty, no member can also have Tkstar=0. The pinned all-order gain remainder and exact operator-zero theorem then give (2).

4. Exact scalar demonstration: noise defeats naive drift cancellation

Take the actual scalar plant dx=(u+w_(t-h))dt+u dW,z=x, with K=0 and pure delayed u=L w_(t-h). Here E=0,G=L,F=L+1,O=1,P=Q=0. Equation (1) gives

 S_2={L: L=0},
 S_3={L: L=0 and L+1=0}=empty.

Thus kstar=2, and the FIXED choice L=0 gives the exact deterministic Volterra literal gain 2S/pi. Every fixed L!=0 has a nonzero additive-noise coefficient at weight1 and hence a positive-order sqrt(S) leading gain; in particular the seemingly natural drift cancellation L=-1 leaves u dW and is asymptotically worse. Exact Python Fraction arithmetic checked the two incompatible scalar equations. This is a structural example, not a claim about an S-dependent optimized law or the global original problem.

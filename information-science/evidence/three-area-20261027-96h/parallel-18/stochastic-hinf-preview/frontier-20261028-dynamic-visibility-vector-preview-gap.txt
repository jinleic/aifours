2. Why common unobservability is the right finite condition

N is invariant under P and Q since appending either letter to any word remains a word. Put R=P-Q²/2. A subspace is invariant under P,Q iff invariant under R,Q. Therefore the SAME N is obtained using words in R,Q. Finite dimension gives a deterministic closure algorithm: start N0=ker D, iterate Nk+1=Nk intersect P^(-1)Nk intersect Q^(-1)Nk until stable; at most n strict dimension drops.

If every column of b is in N, the actual fundamental Itô flow preserves N and D Phi(t,s)b=0 for all s<=t almost surely, so case (ii) really has only a delayed output column. If N_in(v)={0}, v is necessarily injective and every nonzero initial vector v a is outside N.

3. Constructing a full-rank finite-time prefix without Db full rank

The following argument is deterministic and applies to any prefix length L0>0. For a smooth scalar driving path beta, the corresponding homogeneous Stratonovich-controlled flow solves

 dF/dt = (R+beta'(t) Q) F,     F(0)=I.

Claim: if x is outside N, there exists a piecewise-linear beta of arbitrarily small total duration and a time t in that duration such that D F(t)x!=0. Otherwise, for every finite number of segments, every real choice of slopes kappa_j and every sufficiently small positive segment duration t_j, the analytic expression

 D exp[(R+kappa_k Q)t_k] ... exp[(R+kappa_1 Q)t_1] x

would be zero. Mixed derivatives at all t_j=0 and polynomial coefficients in the independent kappa_j would then give D word(R,Q)x=0 for every word, contradicting x outside N. This construction requires no noncommutativity assumption and no chosen disturbance.

Now start with observed-input kernel S_0=R^m. Given a controlled prefix beta on [0,t], let

 S_t={a: D F(s)v a=0 for all 0<=s<=t}.

If S_t contains nonzero a, then v a is outside N. Every controlled flow and its inverse preserve N, so F(t)v a is also outside N. Append the arbitrarily short control segment from the previous paragraph to make D F(s)v a nonzero at some later s. Continuity makes the extended output Gramian strictly positive on that a, so S_t loses at least one dimension. Repeat at most m times, each time within an assigned subinterval shorter than L0/(2m). The resulting finite piecewise-linear beta satisfies

 integral_0^L0 [D F(t)v]^T[D F(t)v]dt >0                 (positive definite). (1)

Thus a single admissible deterministic driver makes the entire vector-prefix map injective, even if instantaneous Dv is rank deficient.

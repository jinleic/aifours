# Addendum: all three-point subsets of the corresponding affine plane

For the binary syndrome theorem `frontier-20261028-three-syndrome-bipartition-theorem-v1.md` SHA-256 `7217a2ebe34f1f7be40b61b1ddcd7ae90c62739f3d2fcc0f8d3a08e63f35ca0f`, the support may be **any** three-point subset of any affine translate of the two-dimensional plane generated by the disjoint masks `u={1,2,5,6,8,9}` and `v={3,4,7,10}`. Every three-point subset is a translate of `{0,u,v}`: if its missing plane element is `d`, translate the whole plane by `d xor (u xor v)` to make the missing element `u xor v`. Bitwise factor translations are bijections and preserve FDs and entropies. The one- or two-point subsets are also included as subsets of an appropriate three-point set.

The orbit and bicover checker `frontier-20261028-three-syndrome-bipartition-symmetry-check.py` SHA-256 `1c31e7c249ae856e91d003727e623c2e73b98b8a52254613dffde365e4dc58bc`, receipt SHA-256 `781ea9ffb4b8494f456f5453ca15f26dd5bfdf736536fce1728fe308efdcdd88`, checks the factor-preserving graph automorphisms preserve this unordered partition, every vertex meets both factor classes, and all four three-point subsets of a two-dimensional binary plane translate to the canonical three-point set. This does **not** establish the result for all four points or a different syndrome plane; the exact entropy theorem remains pending independent scientific review.

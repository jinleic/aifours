### 3.2 Game and alphabet

§2.5 uses an arbitrary integer s>1, independent uniform pre-game hat values in {1,...,s}, no communication after the game starts, and a joint all-correct winning event. Definition 2.13 makes each decoder a TOTAL function on the original neighbour tuple. Mixed strategies cannot outperform the best deterministic one by the weighted-average argument.

Definition 2.14 gives fixed-alphabet gn(G,s) as the log_s of the largest actual winning-configuration count. Definition 2.17 separately defines the asymptotic limit and states it equals sup_(s>=2) gn(G,s).

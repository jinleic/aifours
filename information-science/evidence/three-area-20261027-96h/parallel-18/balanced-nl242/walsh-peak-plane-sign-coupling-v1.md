Two distinct pairs with the same nonzero difference are disjoint and form a unique affine 2-flat. Each 2-flat has exactly three nonzero directions, hence

    P=(1/3)Σ_{b≠0} binom(e_b,2)
      ≥ (1/6)(m²/255−m)=m(m−255)/1530,

by Cauchy–Schwarz. Since n≥86, m>255; P is a positive integer. The bound concerns all possible peak sets, not a constructed or selected plane.

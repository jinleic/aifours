**Lemma.** A subset S of an affine 8-space with no affine 3-flat has at most 90 points.

Let K be the 8-dimensional direction space. For every nonzero b∈K, let e_b be the number of unordered pairs of S separated by b. These pairs are disjoint. Any two distinct such pairs form an affine 2-flat whose direction contains b. There are exactly 127 two-dimensional direction spaces containing b, because K/⟨b⟩ has 127 nonzero vectors.

If two distinct resulting 2-flats had the same direction, they would be distinct disjoint parallel affine 2-flats. Their union would be a complete affine 3-flat contained in S. Thus, under the no-3-flat assumption, the map from unordered pairs of b-edges to their 2-flat directions is injective. Hence

    binom(e_b,2)≤127,  so e_b≤16,                                  (1)

for **every** nonzero b. Counting all unordered point pairs by their difference gives

    binom(|S|,2)=Σ_{b∈K\{0}}e_b≤255·16=4080.

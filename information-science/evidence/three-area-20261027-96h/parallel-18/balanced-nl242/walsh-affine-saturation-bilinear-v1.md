## Proof

By the affine-restriction paper, equality forces η≠0 and, for some ε∈{±1}, all extensions a∈A_η satisfy

    k(a)=ε·χ_a(x₀)·m(a),  m(a)=6 if a·T=0,  m(a)=7 if a·T=1.

If h,g∈H∩T^⊥, the four frequencies a,a+h,a+g,a+h+g all lie in A_η and have the same parity, hence the same m. The two group differences of the coherent character contribute factors χ_h(x₀)−1 and χ_g(x₀)−1, whose product is divisible by 4. Thus

    Δ_hΔ_g k(a) ≡0 (mod 4).

## Claim

For any actual balanced peak≤28 object and any affine 3-flat E=a₀+H all of whose frequencies are peaks, consider the eight affine source cosets of U=H^⊥. Choose any linear section y↦x_y of V/U and define their coherently oriented local coefficients

    C(y)=χ_{a₀}(x_y) Σ_{x∈x_y+U} (-1)^{f(x)}χ_{a₀}(x).          (1)

Exactly one of these alternatives holds:

1. one C(y) is ±28 and the other seven are zero;

A change of linear section multiplies the C-signs by a character of V/U, preserving their support and, in alternative 2, the product of the four signs. This is a conditional necessary source/global coupling, not an assertion that any such E or f exists.

Its total squared magnitude over y∈V/U is 8·8=64 by character orthogonality. Hence it has either one nonzero value of magnitude 8 or four of magnitude 4, proving the two magnitude patterns in (1).

In the four-value case let P⊂V/U be the support and σ_y∈{±1} its transform signs. Fourier inversion on H gives

    ε(h)=(1/2)Σ_{y∈P}σ_yχ_h(x_y).

Equation (2) identifies the C-signs with σ_y. If the linear section is changed, the signs are multiplied by a character on V/U; its product over the affine 2-flat is +1, so the conclusion is unchanged. ∎

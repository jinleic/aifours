## Claim

## Proof

Put U=H^⊥. For each of the 2^d source cosets x_y+U choose a linear section y↦x_y and define

    C(y)=χ_{a₀}(x_y)Σ_{x∈x_y+U}(-1)^{f(x)}χ_{a₀}(x).

Write W(a₀+h)=28ε(h), h∈H, with ε(h)∈{±1}. The all-affine restriction identity gives

    C(y)=(28/2^d) Σ_{h∈H} ε(h)χ_h(x_y).                           (1)

For 1≤d≤8, each source coset has even cardinality, so every C(y) is even. Since 28=4·7 and 7 is odd, (1) forces every H-Walsh coefficient of ε to be divisible by 2^{d−1}. For d≥2 it is therefore 0, ±2^{d−1} or ±2^d. Parseval gives total squared magnitude 2^{2d}; the only possibilities are one coefficient of magnitude 2^d or four of magnitude 2^{d−1}. For d=1 the direct two-sign transform has one coefficient of magnitude 2.

One nonzero transform value makes ε an affine character and produces one C-value of magnitude 28. In the four-value case the C-values have magnitude 14. If their labels are y_i and transform signs σ_i, inversion gives

    ε(h)=(1/2)Σ_{i=1}^4 σ_iχ_{y_i}(h).

This sum is ±2 at every h, so the product of its four summand signs is −1 everywhere. Thus the four labels have XOR 0 and form an affine 2-flat P, and ∏σ_i=−1. A change of linear section multiplies their signs by a character, whose product over P is +1.

For a fully peak E, all its frequencies are odd under the v1 parity identity, so H⊆T^⊥. The peak sign is a quadratic phase induced by the **same** global 𝔅. In the one-value case its second differences vanish, so 𝔅|_H=0. In the four-value case let L be the 2-dimensional direction of P⊂H*. The inverse formula shows the sign changes by a constant under every translation in L^⊥, so L^⊥ lies in the radical of 𝔅|_H. Conversely, if a direction r is in that radical, the peak sign changes by a constant under r, forcing all transform-support labels to agree on r; hence r annihilates every difference of labels and lies in L^⊥. The radical is exactly L^⊥, of dimension d−2. No global rank or target branch has been selected.

Finally, |C(y)| cannot exceed the source-coset size 2^{9−d}. If d≥6 this is at most 8, smaller than 14, so neither pattern is possible. If d=5 it is 16, so magnitude 28 is impossible and only the four ±14 pattern remains. ∎

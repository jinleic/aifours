## Proof from the original Boolean caller

Put `k=W_f/4`, let M be the 256-point one-support of f, and let `T=⊕_{x∈M}x`. For `a∈F₂⁹`, write `n_a=#{x∈M:a·x=1}`. The Walsh definition gives k(0)=0 and `k(a)=n_a−128` for a≠0; hence **for every a**, `k(a)≡n_a (mod 4)` and `k(a)≡a·T (mod 2)`.

Let `K=T^⊥`, of dimension d=8 if T≠0 and d=9 if T=0. All k(a) are even on K. Define `q(a)=k(a)/2 (mod 2)` there. We show q is a Boolean polynomial of degree at most two in coordinates on K. Choose a basis `b₁,…,b_d` of K and put `r_i(x)=b_i·x∈{0,1}`. Since each b_i is orthogonal to T, `m_i=Σ_{x∈M} r_i(x)` is even. For `a=Σ_i ε_i b_i`, the integer parity identity modulo four is

    ⊕_i ε_i r_i = Σ_i ε_i r_i − 2 Σ_{i<j} ε_i ε_j r_i r_j  (mod 4).

Summing over M and dividing the resulting even quantity by two modulo two yields

    q(a) = Σ_i (m_i/2) ε_i + Σ_{i<j} (Σ_{x∈M} r_i(x)r_j(x)) ε_i ε_j  (mod 2).

Thus q has degree≤2 and q(0)=0. Let `h=1+q`; it is a nonzero Boolean polynomial of degree≤2. The elementary minimum-support bound for such a polynomial in d variables gives `|supp(h)|≥2^(d−2)`.

For completeness, that bound follows by induction: write any nonzero degree≤r Boolean polynomial as `p(x',x_d)=p₀(x')+x_d p₁(x')`. If p₁=0, double the support of p₀. Otherwise `wt(p₀)+wt(p₀+p₁)≥wt(p₁)` and deg p₁≤r−1. The base d=r is immediate. No classification or numerical case search is needed.

We first rule out T=0, independently of the earlier adopted T≠0 result. Then d=9 and `|supp(h)|≥128`. On those positions q=0, so, since k is even and |k|≤7, `|k|≤4`; on the other at most 384 positions `|k|≤6`. Parseval would imply

    2¹⁴ = Σ_a k(a)² ≤ 128·16 + 384·36 = 15,872 < 16,384,

a contradiction. Hence T≠0 and d=8, so `|supp(h)|≥64`. On K, `h(a)=1` exactly when k(a) is 0 or ±4. Outside K, k is odd and cannot have either magnitude. Thus `|supp(h)|=Z+F`, proving the theorem. ∎

The bound 64 is sharp **for the isolated quadratic minimum-support lemma**: on F₂⁸, `q=x₁+x₂+x₁x₂` has q(0)=0 and exactly 64 zeros. This does not assert a Boolean target realizes that q.

## Claim

In the feature-relation space for peak signs defined in `walsh-peak-sign-moment-code-v1.md`, every nonzero relation has either weight 8 or weight at least 12. A weight-8 relation is exactly a full affine 3-flat. Every weight-12 relation, if one occurs, has affine span of dimension exactly 5. No existence of either weight is claimed.

## Proof

The previous circuit-geometry proof applies to **any** finite distinct-point relation in the odd affine 8-space, whether or not every point lies in the peak set: it uses only the constant, linear and quadratic feature sums. It gives even weight at least 8 and the self-orthogonal rank bound

    1+dim aff(R)≤|R|/2.                                            (1)

Suppose |R|=10. Ten distinct points cannot lie in an affine 3-flat, and (1) puts them in an affine space of dimension at most 4; hence aff(R) is an affine 4-flat F. Every multilinear polynomial of degree at most 2 has even sum over all 16 points of F, so the entire F is a feature relation. Its complement F\R is then a 6-point feature relation, contradicting the universal weight-at-least-8 result. Thus weight 10 is impossible.

If |R|=12, distinctness gives affine dimension at least 4 and (1) gives at most 5. Dimension 4 would make its 4-point complement in its affine 4-flat a feature relation, again impossible. Therefore its affine span has dimension exactly 5. The weight-8 characterization was proved in the prior circuit-geometry paper. ∎
